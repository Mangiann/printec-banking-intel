Management (17/09/2026) wants the 2027 budget presented in the categories of the Printec budget Excel (`budget/budget_clean.xlsx`: country → HW / SW / SV / OUT → subcategory such as ATM, EFT-POS, AML) with waterfall charts for the group and per country, plus explanations. Static first; a movable ("tailoring") version is a later decision.

**Engine:** `weekly-intelligence/scripts/budget_categories.py` reads the Excel, `budget/budget_2027_board.json`, `budget_actions.json` and `budget_2027.json`, and writes `budget/budget_2027_categories.json` (embedded in the encrypted budget payload by build_artifact.py and make_test_build.py) and `budget/budget_2027_by_category.xlsx` (the 2026 Excel's layout: entity → category → subcategory; columns 2026, lowest acceptable, budget, maximum, RP, RM%, product lines). Run it after `board_2027.py plan --phase texts` and before build_artifact.py. The plan numbers are never re-estimated: totals reconcile to the plan within €2k.

**Allocation rules the user agreed (answers of 17/09/2026):** (1) installation sits in SV, one SV figure; (2) NO invented deal-mix percentages — a dated order or share win is split the way the country's 2026 revenue for that subcategory already splits across HW/SW/SV/OUT (the Excel's own structure); model recurring growth → SV/OUT in 2026 ratio; model deals+outlook → full mix; hires → SV (OUT for managed services); capability → SW; pricing/efficiency → SV/OUT; closing facts → full mix; balance → full mix; (3) a first outsourcing contract with no 2026 row lands in OUT under subcategory ATM (EFT-POS when about terminals); (4) 2027 categories and subcategories are the same as the 2026 second submission; (5) Other and retail flat; (6) static first. Small combinations under €50k that the plan file has no cell for are held at the model's base estimate; a new-business combination that already has Excel revenue carries that revenue flat under the plan's new number (this is how the plan totals count them). Profit holds 2026 margin per country×category×subcategory → budget profit €95.9m vs the line view's €94.3m (noted on the page).

**Other inside its category (user, 17/09/2026 afternoon):** budget rows with no product name ("Other", "Other Point of Sale solutions"…, EUR 5.6m) are no longer pooled with retail: each stays flat at its 2026 value but inside its own category (a pseudo-cell `other_flat` per country; OUT "Other" was already managed services). Only retail (EUR 7.74m) is the separate flat tile/row. The moves grid column "Retail and other, carried flat" is unchanged (it is by product line). Artifact v80 (personal), build 17/09/2026 16:29; the Printec copy needs a republish. Category totals at €180.0m: HW 61.7, SW 37.8, SV 54.1, OUT 18.6, retail 7.7.

**Retail inside its category too (user, 17/09/2026, later):** retail rows also go flat into their own category (pseudo-cell `retail_flat`), so there is no separate flat bucket, tile or row any more (the tile and row render only if a flat bucket exists). 2026 category totals now equal the Excel's own: HW 57.62, SW 28.38, SV 48.61, OUT 14.05; at €180.0m: HW 64.7, SW 38.0, SV 58.2, OUT 19.1. Five tiles. Artifact v81 (personal), build 17/09/2026 16:44.
