Internal Printec reference sheet listing two named **partners** (Namirial, IMTF) and one named **competitor** (Payten / Asseco SEE), each with website, profile, product/service scope and certification/trust signals. Updated version (supersedes the 2026-06-23 digest): IMTF is newly added as a partner and all entries carry richer descriptions.

## Key points
- **Competitor — Payten / Asseco SEE** (payten.com, Competitors sheet): payments brand of Asseco South Eastern Europe Group; end-to-end card and cardless payments for banks, FIs, merchants and e-commerce. Overlaps Printec on **payment gateways, acquiring/issuing infrastructure, merchant payment acceptance, ATM and POS services, SoftPOS/mPOS, terminal management and processing outsourcing**. Strongly relevant where the comparison is payment gateway / acquiring-issuing / merchant acceptance / processing.
- Payten trust signals (Competitors sheet): Member of ASEE Group; **PCI DSS Level 1**; **ISO 27001:2022** and **ISO 22301:2019** reported for Payten/Paratika in Türkiye; **PCI 3DS Core Security Standard** for its ePayment platform.
- **Partner — IMTF** (imtf.com, Partners sheet): Swiss **RegTech** anti-financial-crime / compliance software. Flagship **Siron®One** platform covering AML transaction monitoring, KYC/CDD/EDD, customer-lifecycle management, sanctions screening, fraud detection, case management, regulatory reporting and digital onboarding. Trust signals: **37 years' expertise, 300 anti-financial-crime experts, 1,000+ customers, 90+ countries**; **acquired/transitioned the Siron compliance business from FICO in 2022**.
- **Partner — Namirial** (namirial.com, Partners sheet): digital trust services provider — e-signatures, digital identity, onboarding, registered email, e-invoicing, digital archiving. Trust signals: **eIDAS**, **Qualified Trust Service Provider**, ISO certifications.
