- Average value of cash withdrawal per ATM: 2018 = 2.7; 2019 = 2.8; 2020 = 2.4; 2021 = 2.6; 2022 = 2.9. CAGR ~2.9%. (USD row: 2018 = 3.2; 2019 = 3.2; 2020 = 2.7; 2021 = 3.1; 2022 = 3.0.)
- Cash withdrawal value as a proportion of GDP: 2018 = 6.9%; 2019 = 6.3%; 2020 = 5.5%; 2021 = 5.9%; 2022 = 5.4%. CAGR ~ -4.2%.
Narrative: Close to 1.2 billion cash withdrawals were made at ATMs in France in 2022. Total number fell sharply during 2017, with 2021 the long-term trend (down from 2017). Increase in withdrawals in 2021 partly a recovery from the significant COVID-19 pandemic impact in 2020. Hygiene concerns and lower visits to ATMs/retailers resulted in a 26% drop in cash withdrawals that year. Total number of cash withdrawals fell by 0.1% in 2022, marking a return to the trend of declining cash use. Customers continued to use cards in 2022; contactless usage for everyday purchases. Reduced use of e-commerce and mobile payments as cash multiplies; cash methods became more engrained in habits as a result of the pandemic. By contrast, 2022 saw an increase in the average number of cash withdrawals per ATM per month for the second year in a row. This can be seen as a result of ATM numbers falling at a faster rate than the number of cash withdrawals. Cash withdrawals represent an increasing share of ATM transactions. Online and mobile banking has been growing in France and these methods are increasingly used for non-cash banking services. With widespread internet access, many French customers prefer not to spend too long in front of an ATM for security reasons, when there is the option to perform other transactions digitally at home.

### p.219 — 4.5 Cash withdrawals narrative (continued) (France: Page 19)
No figure/table on this page — narrative only.
Narrative: In France, growth in the total annual value of cash withdrawals at ATMs has been uneven between 2018 and 2022. Although the total annual value has fallen by 6%, from EUR 151 billion (2018) to EUR 142 billion (2022) (or a 17% decrease from USD 179 billion to USD 149 billion), this overall decrease hides a different trend. The total value of cash withdrawn from ATMs has actually grown each year, except between 2019 and 2020, when hygiene concerns and restrictions associated with the COVID-19 pandemic saw cash use decrease in France. Furthermore, the rate of growth has increased, from 1% between 2018-2019 to 5% from 2020-2021, and 6% from 2021-2022. Similarly, the average value of a cash withdrawal has increased each year between 2018 and 2022. Several factors have contributed to the increase in the total and average value of cash withdrawals, including inflation, fewer ATMs, and fewer visits to ATMs (with higher values of cash withdrawn each time). The period 2019-2020 saw the highest increase in the average withdrawal value, which rose by nearly EUR 12, marking a ~12% increase (an increase of around USD 16, or +14%). Owing to COVID-19 restrictions limiting movement, and concerns regarding the hygiene of ATMs, customers visited ATMs less frequently in 2020 and took out more cash each time. Historically, the ATMs of BRED have seen the highest average withdrawal values; these ATMs are mostly based in the more affluent regions of Île-de-France and Normandy. In 2022, each ATM in France had an average value of EUR 2.9 million (USD 3.0 million) of cash withdrawn. Cash withdrawals represented 5.4% of GDP, a proportion which has been falling in recent years as more and more cashless payments are made.

Narrative: Four manufacturers supply ATMs to the French market: Diebold Nixdorf, NCR, Glory and Hyosung Innovue. ATM deployers in France typically use multivendor hardware purchasing strategies and all of the large deployers use a combination of terminals from both Diebold Nixdorf and NCR in their fleets. Diebold Nixdorf has the largest share of the market, accounting for 59% of ATMs in France at end-2022. The manufacturer supplies terminals for over half of the estates of several major deployers, including La Banque Postale, Crédit Agricole, and Euro-Information. Machines of the manufacturer's Diebold series and the CINEO series are very commonly reported in France. NCR represents most of the remainder of the market, or 40% of terminals. All of Oney Bank's terminals are supplied by NCR, as well as most of the terminals of BRED and Crédit du Nord. The most commonly reported NCR model in the market is the SelfServ 25. Glory and Hyosung Innovue both have a very small presence in the market. The former entered the market in 2014 and supplies some of the terminals deployed by the Caisses d'Epargne. These ATMs had been transferred to Brink's by end-2022. Hyosung Innovue entered the market in 2015 as Hyosung TNS, with a small number of its terminals being deployed by the Banques Populaires; similarly, these were also transferred to Brink's. Hyosung Innovue was announced in 2022 as the new trading name of Hyosung TNS, although the firm's legal name remains the same.
