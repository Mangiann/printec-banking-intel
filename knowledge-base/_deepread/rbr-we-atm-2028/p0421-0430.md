- Text: Approximately 22,600 ATMs are forecast to be shipped to Italy between 2023 and 2028, averaging ~3,800 per year. ~96% of shipments are replacements (replacement of existing terminals), expected to grow over the forecast period; new installations remain flat at 150/year. Proportion of replacements projected higher in latter part of forecast period. Some new installations will be shipped throughout the forecast period even as installed base contracts, since some deployers will expand their fleets.
