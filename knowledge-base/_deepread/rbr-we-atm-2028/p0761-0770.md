### p.762 — UK, Fig 4.8 Value of cash withdrawals (2018-2022)
- Body: number of cash withdrawals made from UK ATMs rose in 2022 for the first time since 2018, by 6%. Modest rise (1.3 billion); however total number of cash withdrawals still lower than 2020 level. COVID-19 pandemic, legal limits on travel/business closures/hygiene caused cash-handling decline; some recovery. Decline attributed to growth in non-cash transactions; Apple Pay/Android Pay etc. Cash withdrawals as a proportion of GDP fell sharply 2019->2022. ATM channel being only limited; more customers move to remote banking.

### p.763 — UK, cash-withdrawal commentary (text only)
- Single paragraph (rest of page blank): Falling real incomes amid the 'cost of living crisis' in late 2021 and throughout 2022 led individuals/families to limit spending on non-essential items. In spite of high inflation in 2022, the average value of a cash withdrawal barely changed, suggesting customers were not able to withdraw larger amounts of cash in response to higher prices. The average value withdrawn per ATM did rise as a result of ATM closures.

### p.764 — UK, Section 5 Manufacturer market shares + Fig 5.1 Manufacturer market shares (2018-2022)
- Figure 5.1 "Manufacturer market shares, 2018-2022" (stacked bar, share %; legend GRG / Hyosung Innovue / Triton / Diebold Nixdorf / NCR). Companion installed-base table (ATM units):
  - NCR: 41,167 / 40,128 / 37,434 / 36,617 / 34,071; CAGR -4.6%.
  - Diebold Nixdorf: 16,075 / 16,021 / 14,080 / 13,476 / 12,868; CAGR -5.4%.
  - Triton: 4,794 / 4,596 / 3,672 / 3,433 / 3,202; CAGR -9.6%.

  - Total: 63,586 / 62,057 / 56,378 / 54,481 / 51,036; CAGR -5.3%.
  - Source: UK ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
- Body: NCR and Diebold Nixdorf supply the vast majority of ATMs in the UK market, with a collective share of ~92%. UK bank estates are commonly weighted towards one or the other, while IADs often have more mixed estates due to a history of mergers and acquisitions. NCR has the largest market share by a significant margin, at 67% of the installed base in 2022 (NCR/Atleos branding). NCR were known to have installed QR-code readers at their ATMs; NFC, contactless readers more widely used, at 7% of base. Several major bank deployers such as Lloyds Banking Group, Santander and Nationwide Building Society exclusively deploy NCR ATMs. The Selflers 25 and Selflers34 are the most commonly deployed NCR ATM models, while some older models from the Personas range are also in use. IADs in particular often purchase, refurbish and deploy large numbers of second-hand ATMs. Diebold Nixdorf accounts for the second largest share of the UK market, with 25% of the installed base at end-2022. HSBC and Travelex commonly deploy Diebold Nixdorf ATMs, including the CINEO range. The most commonly deployed Diebold Nixdorf models include the ProCash 2050 and ProCash 2100xe. Triton has a small presence in the market, typically deployed by IADs.
