### p.401 — Italy, Section 4 (Cash withdrawals) — Figure 4.8: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2022
Line chart with two series (annual value EUR bn left axis; per-ATM EUR right axis), axis 0-300.
Table (values per year 2018 / 2019 / 2020 / 2021 / 2022, with CAGR):
- Annual value of cash withdrawals — EUR bn: 236 / 246 / 219 / 230 / 241 (CAGR 0.5%); USD bn: 280 / 270 / 250 / 272 / 253 (CAGR -2.5%).
- Average value per cash withdrawal — EUR: 200 / 202 / 232 / 258 / 238 (CAGR 4.5%); USD: 237 / 227 / 258 / 275 / 251 (CAGR 1.4%).
- Average value withdrawn per ATM — EUR mn: 4.9 / 5.0 / 4.6 / 5.0 / 5.4 (CAGR 2.5%); USD mn: 5.8 / 5.6 / 5.3 / 5.9 / 5.7 (CAGR 0.4%).
- Cash withdrawal value as a proportion of GDP — USD: 13.7% / 13.4% / 13.2% / 13.1% / 12.6% (CAGR -1.6%).
Narrative: value of withdrawals fell 5% in 2020 to EUR 219 bn (USD 253 bn) after dropping 5% in 2021, then recovering 5% in 2022 to EUR 241 bn (USD 253 bn); the rise stemming from the high inflation rate experienced in Italy in 2022. Cash as a proportion of GDP declined slightly in 2022, indicating Italy is becoming a gradually less cash-centric economy. (Italy: Page 14)

### p.403 — Italy, Section 5 (manufacturer narrative, text only)
- Glory accounted for 6% of the market at end-2022, down by three percentage points since end-2021. All Glory terminals are assisted self-service terminals (ASSTs). Glory's main customers in Italy are Gruppo UniCredit and Gruppo Intesa Sanpaolo.
- Hyosung Innovue ATMs deployed by several banks incl. Gruppo Intesa Sanpaolo and Gruppo MPS. The number of KEBA ATMs installed in Italy more than trebled between end-2021 and end-2022, rising to over 200 units, accounting for less than 1% share. Hyosung Innovue announced in 2022 as the new trading name of Hyosung TNS (legal name unchanged).
- Gruppo Intesa Sanpaolo also deploys ARCA ATMS; the group deployed 156 ATMs from this manufacturer at end-2022. All ARCA terminals are ASSTs. (Italy: Page 16)

### p.406 — Italy, Section 6.1 — Figures 6.5 & 6.6

### p.409 — Italy, Section 6.2: Service and maintenance — Figure 6.11a: Outsourcing of cash replenishment, 2022
Stacked-bar chart (In-house light / Outsourced dark) for Branch vs Off-site:
- Branch: In-house 37,726 (96.3%), Outsourced 1,434 (3.7%).
- Off-site: In-house 34 (0.7%), Outsourced 4,630 (99.3%).
- Total: Outsourced 6,064 (13.8%), In-house 37,760 (86.2%).
Narrative: cash replenishment is outsourced for 14% of ATMs in Italy but is performed in-house for the vast majority of branch-based terminals. Some ATM deployers contract a third party for cash replenishment for some of their branch ATMs, including Gruppo Intesa Sanpaolo, Gruppo UniCredit and BNL. BNL has chosen to do so in order to increase security in some of its branches located away from the city centre. (Italy: Page 22)
