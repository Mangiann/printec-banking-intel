Narrative text: Number of bank branches in Belgium fell in both recent years and to 3,710 (~10.3% decline since 2020); ATM installed base declined ~8.9% over 2021-2022, reaching 5,502 ATMs in 2022, a decrease of ~9% from the end of the previous year. The Belgian ATM installed base is set to contract over the forecast period, accelerated by the launch of a bank-neutral ATM network ("Batopin"). The initiative had already begun in 2021 with ING, Belfius, BNP Paribas Fortis and KBC Group. The four large banks aim to operate a shared neutral network of ~472 ATMs per million people, and its installed base continues to decline as a result of the shift to digital channels. As a densely populated country, Belgium had the third-highest density of ATMs to area in Western Europe, with 180 per 1,000 km². There were ~139 million cash withdrawals at Belgian ATMs in 2022, a fall of ~18 million from 2021. Cash withdrawal value continues to decline, with ~2,008 cash withdrawals made per ATM per month on average, representing a ~2% drop from the previous year (a ~1% fall from 2020). In 2022 the total value of cash withdrawals from Belgian ATMs was EUR 24 billion (USD 25 billion), decreasing from EUR 28 billion (USD 33 billion) in 2021. The average value of a cash withdrawal also fell from EUR 178 (USD 210) to EUR 170 (USD 179) in 2022. The lobby remains the most common location for ATMs, with ~77% of the installed base located here. At end-2022, 84% of ATMs in Belgium were located at the branch; one percentage point lower than the end of 2021, as the share of off-site ATMs increased due to expansion of the Batopin project. Additional facilities at ATMs are limited: in Belgium ~92% of the installed base allows PIN change but dynamic currency conversion (DCC) is rare; 78% of ATMs have a touch screen and NFC readers are present on ~58% of ATMs. 58% of ATMs accepted automated note deposit at end-2022, all of which were recyclers. ### p.110 — Belgium, Section 1 Market overview (narrative continued)
Prose page (no figures):
- 69% of ATMs are fitted with an anti-skimming device; 39% have note degradation; only 5% have a built-in or integrated security camera.
- Diebold Nixdorf was the largest supplier to the Belgian market in 2022, accounting for 68% of the installed base at year-end; NCR represented the remaining 32%.
- IBM is the most common central network hardware; majority of ATMs driven by proprietary solutions. Diebold Nixdorf is the most common provider of application software (over a third of ATMs with software); proprietary solutions constitute 37%.
- Operating systems: 32% run Windows 7; 65% migrated to Windows 10; a few remain on Windows XP.
- Proprietary solutions most common for message protocol, management/monitoring, CRM and cash forecasting management. Diebold Nixdorf's SCCM is the most commonly used remote software distribution solution. Dedicated security software present on the majority of the installed base.
- Outsourcing: cash replenishment outsourced for 69% of ATMs (all off-site ATMs replenished by third parties except Loomis machines); first line maintenance outsourced for 68% (all off-site outsourced except Loomis); second line maintenance outsourced for all ATMs except Loomis installations; help desk services outsourced for 75% of the installed base.
