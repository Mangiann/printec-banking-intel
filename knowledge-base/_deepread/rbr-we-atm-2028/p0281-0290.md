### p.289 — Germany Page 38 — Section 7: Market forecasts; 7.1 Forecast installed base (Figure 7.1)
Figure 7.1 "Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028" table:
- ATMs: 49,800 (2023), 48,400 (2024), 47,000 (2025), 45,600 (2026), 44,200 (2027), 42,800 (2028); CAGR -3.0%
- Absolute growth: -1,460 / -1,400 / -1,400 / -1,400 / -1,400 / -1,400; CAGR N/A
- Percentage growth: -2.8% / -2.8% / -2.9% / -3.0% / -3.1% / -3.2%; CAGR N/A
- ATMs per million people: 597 / 581 / 564 / 547 / 530 / 513; CAGR -3.0%
Text: ATM installed base in Germany forecast to fall 17% by 2028, to 42,800. Implies a density of 513 ATMs per million inhabitants in 2028, significantly below the end-2022 ratio of 615 per million. Installed base anticipated to fall by ~1,400 machines per year 2023-2028, driven mainly by continuing mergers and estate rationalisations at the savings and co-operative banks, plus periodic but still significant rationalisations at commercial banks. Commercial banks likely to continue streamlining branch networks (removal of lobby and TTW ATMs); some lobby structures remain after the associated branch closes (also common among savings banks). Germany's IAD sector expected to grow 2023-2028 but not fast enough to offset the contraction of the bank ATM sector; IADs face the same trend toward lower cash usage but still seek opportunities to install ATMs. Entry of new IADs still possible but less likely than before 2020. Germany remains a "cash-heavy" society relative to most of Europe, but this is changing as young consumers adopt contactless/mobile wallets; cash usage expected to remain higher in Germany than in most European markets for the foreseeable future, converging in the long term. Alternative deployment models (bank-IAD partnerships, pooling of ATM ownership) may be explored. Branch closures and resilience of the non-bank ATM sector mean off-site/non-branch ATMs rise as a proportion of the total — expected to exceed 40% by 2028.

### p.290 — Germany Page 39 — Number of ATMs 1995-2028 (Figure 7.2) + market commentary
Figure 7.2 "Number of ATMs, 1995-2028" — bar chart, y-axis 0 to 60,000 in 10,000 steps, years 1995-2028. Dark bars = historic/current; lighter bars (2023-2028) = forecast. Rises from ~37,000 (1995) through the 2000s, peaks around 2008-2017 in the high-50,000s (~57,000-58,000), then declines steadily; ~51,260 at 2022 and tapering down through the lighter forecast bars toward ~42,800 by 2028 (consistent with Fig 7.1). Source: Deutsche Bundesbank, German ATM deployers, banking associations and data centres, and RBR analysis.
Text: Bank branch numbers have been falling for many years and the trend is certain to continue; branch total forecast to fall to 15,800 by 2028. This implies a density of 259 ATMs (including non-branch ATMs) per 100 branches, up from 238 in 2022 — a trend caused by branch numbers falling faster than ATM numbers. German ATMs have a fairly limited range of additional functions, unlikely to change much; bill payment/statement printing where offered is mainly via separate monofunction terminals in self-service lobbies. Automated note deposit (already common in the ATM channel) will become more widespread; cash-recycling ATMs to comprise more than half the national total by 2028. NFC-reader ATMs expected to keep growing to support contactless transactions; touch screens becoming more common as banks replace older ATMs. ATM security now a priority after several hundred explosive attacks on non-branch terminals; federal government mandating banks increase the proportion of ATMs protected by security cameras and note-degradation systems; this is expensive and may push some banks to remove ATMs rather than upgrade. Proportion of ATMs with anti-skimming modules likely to rise slowly (contactless growth reduces the need).
