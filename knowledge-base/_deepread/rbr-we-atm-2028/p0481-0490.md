### p.487 — Norway, Section 1 (continued) — narrative only
Norwegian installed base forecast to slowly contract to 1,100 ATMs at end-2028. Rising use of cashless/digital payments has cut cash demand; some merchants refuse cash. Norwegian government has proposed mandating cash acceptance at physical outlets, which may make the fall in ATM numbers and cash usage more gradual. Banks expected to keep closing branches and removing terminals; IADs likely to partially offset this as they expand fleets.

### p.489 — Norway, Section 3: ATM networks, sharing and services (3.1 ATM networks and sharing) + Figure 3.1
Narrative: First ATMs installed in Norway in 1979 (savings banks via Fellesdata data centre); by end-1979 installations had risen to 90. National network miniBank founded in 1982. ATM provision peaked in 2008 (over 2,358 ATMs installed); number has declined every year since, including 2022 which saw Norway's ATM market contract 5% to 1,166 terminals. IADs (e.g. Nokas, Loomis) taking over machines from banks. Total ATMs in Norway fell at ~7% annual CAGR 2018-2022. History: 1999 EDB founded Fellesdata and EDB Novit, later forming processing centre EDB Fellesdata (handled ATM transactions for all banks except DNB). 2012 EDB Business Partner merged with EDB Fellesdata to form EVRY; 2019 EVRY acquired by Finnish IT firm Tieto, becoming Tietoevry. Nets established 2009 (after Danish PBS merged with Banshees BetalingsSentral/BBS); Advent International, ATP and Bain Capital acquired Nets from a consortium of Norwegian banks (incl. DNB and Norges Bank) in a DKK 17 billion (USD 2.7 billion) transaction.
- Figure 3.1: Domestic sharing agreements, 2018-2022 (columns 2018/2019/2020/2021/2022/CAGR). Source: Norwegian ATM deployers and networks, RBR analysis.
  - Tietoevry: 1,225 (2018); 1,276 (2019); 1,272 (2020); 1,159 (2021); 1,131 (2022); CAGR -2.0%.
  - Nets: 315 (2018); 169 (2019); 163 (2020); 43 (2021); 0 (2022); CAGR -100.0%.
- Narrative below table: Nearly all of Norway's ATM transactions processed through Tietoevry, with Euronet the only deployer operating outside the network. Until mid-2022 there were 43 off-site DNB ATMs still processed by Nets; these were acquired by Nokas and now form part of the Tietoevry network.

Narrative: Nokas remains the largest ATM deployer in Norway, fleet stable at 305; IAD present since acquiring Point's fleet in 2012; in 2022 incorporated the remaining DNB ATMs (43 machines, primarily in airports — the last terminals processed by Nets) that Nokas agreed to acquire in 2021. Loomis is the next largest IAD with 115 ATMs at end-2022, many in transport hubs; Loomis has owned Nokas' cash-handling service since early 2020; its fleet grew slightly in 2022. Euronet (the only other IAD) added 15 new machines. The IAD market has seen strong growth, with independent deployers buying ATMs previously deployed by banks.
