Majority of ATMs deployed in bank branches; **through-the-wall (TTW) most common location >half of installed base** at end-2022; lobby ATMs relatively common ~**26%** of installed base; hall ATMs **11%**; off-site terminals **10%**; two drive-through ATMs in the country. Annual number of cash withdrawals at Italian ATMs increased **2% in 2022 to 1 billion**, averaging **1,890 withdrawals per ATM per month**. Cash withdrawals annual value **EUR 241 billion (USD 253 billion)**; average value per withdrawal **EUR 238 (USD 251)**. Cash withdrawals **accounted for 74% of ATM transactions in 2022**. **Diebold Nixdorf is the largest ATM manufacturer with 43% market share at end-2022; NCR 35%; domestic vendor Sigma 14%.**

### p.389 — Italy: Section 1 continued — software, services, forecast (text)
Footer: "Italy: Page 2". Body text:
- **72% of ATMs in Italy run from IBM network central hardware, remainder run from HP.** Auriga's WWS ATM is the most common terminal driving software and the most common ATM application software, followed by KAL's K3A. **Nearly 60% of ATMs run Windows 7**, but deployers migrating to Windows 10.
- In-house developed software most common for ATM management/monitoring and cash forecasting/management. Software can be distributed remotely to all ATMs; Auriga's WWS ATM, CA's UniCenter and IBM's BigFix most common solutions. Auriga's WWS One to One OMNIA most common CRM solution.
- Most common cash forecasting management solutions are proprietary. Dedicated ATM security software tends to be third-party; **GMV's Checker ATM Security most common solution.**
- **Cash replenishment outsourced for 14% of ATMs; first line maintenance outsourced for 46%.** Main cash replenishment providers are CIT companies (Mondialpol, BTV, Sicuritalia, Fidelitas); first line maintenance providers include the ATM manufacturers and some CIT companies. Second line maintenance mostly outsourced to hardware vendors.
- **Forecast: Italy's installed base forecast to contract at an average 1% per year between 2023 and 2028, to 41,600 ATMs.** This would give a density of **705 ATMs per million people in 2028, down from 742 in 2022** (based on 2022 population).
