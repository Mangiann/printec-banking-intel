### p.539 — Portugal, SLM provider split
- Text: all Portuguese deployers outsource SLM; case for the last five years as it is not economically viable for Portuguese banks to have in-house SLM teams.
- **Figure 6.13c: Second line maintenance providers, 2022** (pie + table). Provider / ATMs / Share:
  - Diusframi — 5,989 — 43.2%
  - Diebold Nixdorf — 4,159 — 30.0%
  - BOS — 1,815 — 13.1%
  - Glory — 1,176 — 8.5%
  - NCR — 554 — 4.0%
  - Prosonic — 112 — 0.8%
  - Cronotécnica — 35 — 0.3%
  - Minsait — 22 — 0.2%
  - Total — 13,862 — 100.0%
  - Pie labels: Diusframi 43%, Diebold Nixdorf 30%, BOS 13%, Glory 8%, NCR 4%, Others 1%.
  - Text: largest SLM provider is Diusframi, a national company focusing on banking-automation maintenance; it is the largest provider for all major bank deployers, while also servicing a minority of SIBS ATM Express terminals. Hardware manufacturers also present: SLM for 42% of the installed base; Diebold Nixdorf is the manufacturer with the largest share (30%), followed by Glory (8%) and NCR (4%). All three vendors service varying proportions of the fleets of all major bank deployers, as well as some SIBS ATM Express machines. BOS, another Portuguese company, has the third largest share of SLM market; BOS provides SLM for all major bank deployers, plus servicing a few SIBS ATM Express machines. Prosonic services majority of Millennium bcp's proprietary fleet; while a minority share is held by Cronotécnica (services a minority of Montepio's estate) and Minsait.
