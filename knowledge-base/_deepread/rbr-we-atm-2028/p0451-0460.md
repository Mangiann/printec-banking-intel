### p.451 — Netherlands intro: ATM pooling (Geldmaat)
Body text only (no chart). Western Europe context: there were 19 ATM pools in the region as of end-2022. Netherlands ATM market overview:
- IADs (independent ATM deployers) ~150 ATMs, mostly at points of interest (e.g. Cardpoint/Cashzone-type sites). VVV tourist offices removed all their ATMs in 2021.
- Cardtronics (now NCR) entered the Dutch market in 2016; many ATMs rebranded.
- **3.2 Pooling:** Geldmaat is the single national ATM pool jointly owned by ING, Rabobank and ABN AMRO. Began deploying pooled ("white-label") ATMs in 2019; rollout largely completed by end of 2022. By 2022 Geldmaat operated essentially all bank-owned ATMs in the Netherlands. Geldmaat 50/50 JV with Brink's previously; majority of cash-in-transit by Geldmaat.

### p.452 — Geldmaat background (text only)
- Geldmaat (formerly Geldservice Nederland) established by the three largest Dutch banks in 2011 to cut costs and improve efficiency in cash collection, counting and distribution.
- Cash withdrawal limits set by the card-issuing bank, not the deploying bank; ING/Rabobank/ABN AMRO customers can make balance enquiries at any Geldmaat ATM.
- Central bank's National Forum on the Payment System (NFPS) supports Geldmaat and its **five-kilometre radius requirement** (max distance any resident should travel to reach an ATM). Three core components: affordability, availability, accessibility.
- ATMs previously deployed by banks mostly **rebranded rather than replaced** to cut hardware cost; some removed in high-density areas, some redeployed to lower-density areas. Migration of Geldmaat ATMs to a unified software platform continuing in 2023.

### p.455 — Locations commentary (text only)
- Nearly all Dutch ATMs are off-site, located away from bank branches; share rose from 93% to 96% during 2022 as ATMs migrate to/are attributed to off-site as bank branches close (≈150 ATMs from external walls of closing bank branches reclassified off-site).
- Dutch Banking Association announced Dec 2019 that all bank and Geldmaat ATMs would have to cease operating between 11pm and 7am with immediate effect; the fact that less than 2% of cash withdrawals occur in that window meant impact was limited. By July 2019, ≈25 explosive attacks in 2019 had triggered the measure.
- As of early 2023, Geldmaat still committed to retaining the five-km radius rule despite ATM closures; majority of terminals can no longer be used overnight. To ensure Geldmaat ATMs are nearer customers, number of terminals installed through external walls of branches has reduced (smaller TTW count). TTW installations operated at 49% of Dutch installed base at end-2012; 7% of remaining ATMs are shut overnight; convenience-store/retailer-located ATMs operate exception/24h hours.
