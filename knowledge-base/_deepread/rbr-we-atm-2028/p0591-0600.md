Figure 7.1 "Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028" (Source: RBR analysis):
- ATMs: 43,900 (2023), 42,700 (2024), 42,100 (2025), 41,700 (2026), 41,400 (2027), 41,000 (2028); CAGR -1.4%.
- Absolute growth: -1,333 / -1,200 / -600 / -400 / -300 / -400; CAGR N/A.
- Percentage growth: -2.9% / -2.7% / -1.4% / -1.0% / -0.7% / -1.0%; N/A.
- ATMs per million people: 923 / 898 / 885 / 877 / 871 / 862; CAGR -1.4%.
Text: installed base forecast to decline over forecast period at annual avg ~1%, to stand around 41,000 ATMs by end-2028 (a decline of ~3% on 2022 base); ATMs per million people based on 2022 population figures, forecast to 2028. Contraction attributed to: increased customer preference for digital banking, continued migration toward cashless card payments vs cash; COVID-19 accelerated this. Sizeable mergers in 2021 (Spanish retail banking) indicate market ripe for further consolidation, which could dramatically change the ATM landscape.
Figure 7.2 "Number of ATMs, 1995-2028" (bar chart, y-axis 0-70,000): historical bars peak ~64,000-65,000 around 2008-2009, then steady decline; forecast bars (lighter, 2023-2028) flatten around 41,000-44,000. (Source: Spanish ATM deployers and RBR analysis.)

### p.594 — Spain, 7.2 Forecast ATM shipments (Spain p.35)
Continuation text on contraction drivers (digital banking, cashless migration; COVID). Notes a campaign by a large ageing population (>600,000 signatures) pushing banks to provide in-person banking services (face-to-face support, training on websites/apps) — could moderate ATM number declines somewhat.
Figure 7.3 "Forecast ATM shipments, 2023-2028" (Source: RBR analysis):
- Shipments: 4,500 (2023), 3,900 (2024), 5,000 (2025), 6,000 (2026), 6,100 (2027), 5,600 (2028); CAGR 4.5%.
- of which new installations: 800 / 600 / 480 / 460 / 350 / 140; CAGR -29.4%.
- of which replacements: 3,700 / 3,300 / 4,520 / 5,540 / 5,750 / 5,460; CAGR 8.1%.
- Replacements as % of shipments: 82% / 85% / 90% / 92% / 94% / 98%; CAGR 3.5%.
Text: ~5,300 ATMs/year shipped on average (avg ~5,200 ATM shipments/year between 2023 and 2028). Forecast to rise gradually through the period. Increasing replacements expected to drive shipments, especially ATM deployers replacing equipment installed ~2008-2010 reaching end of ~10-year life. Replacements rise to 98% of shipments by 2028.

### p.595 — Spain, 7.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number (Spain p.36)
Figure 7.4 "Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028" (combo: bars = annual number million [left axis 0-1,600]; line/diamonds = average number per ATM per month [right axis 0-2,500]):
- Annual number (million, approx from bars): ~1,290 (2018), ~1,335 (2019), ~960 (2020), ~1,040 (2021), ~1,105 (2022), then forecast (lighter) declining: ~1,050 (2023), ~1,000 (2024), ~950 (2025), ~900 (2026), ~850 (2027), ~800 (2028).
- Average number line peaks ~2018-2019 (~2,150-2,200), dips 2020, then declines to ~just over 1,600/month by 2028.
Text: number of cash withdrawals forecast to continue decreasing 2023→2028, reaching around 800 million in 2028. ATM usage not predicted to return to pre-pandemic levels (consumers familiar with cards/mobile/digital; COVID accelerated contactless). Average withdrawals/ATM/month follows same pattern, contracting to just over 1,600/month as both installed base and withdrawals fall.

### p.596 — Spain, 7.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal value (Spain p.37)
Figure 7.5 "Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028" (combo: bars = annual value EUR bn [left axis 0-250]; line/diamonds = average value EUR per withdrawal [right axis 0-250]):
- Annual value (EUR bn, approx): ~167 (2018), ~173 (2019), ~150 (2020), ~191 (2021), ~202 (2022); forecast (lighter): ~206 (2023), ~205 (2024), ~203 (2025), ~198 (2026), ~193 (2027), ~188 (2028).
- Average value line rises steadily from ~128 EUR (2018) to ~234 EUR (2028).
Text: annual value increased again in 2022 (same trend as prior year). Total value forecast to remain stable until 2024, slight decrease, then gradual consistent decline to 2028, ending around EUR 188 billion (USD 197 billion at end-2022 exchange rate). Trend of fewer ATM visits but larger withdrawals continues; average cash withdrawal value forecast to rise to EUR 234 (USD 246 at end-2022 rate) by 2028.
