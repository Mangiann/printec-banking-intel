### p.12 — Introduction: Page viii — "Methodology"
Narrative methodology page (no figures/numbers).
- Data gathering: statistical information obtained via a survey RBR carried out Jan–May 2023; questionnaire sent to major banks and independent ATM deployers. RBR complemented survey responses with other sources incl. previous ATM surveys, network operators, central banks and RBR's existing market knowledge.
- For population data, RBR used the CIA World Factbook and GDPs from the IMF. Some statistics calculated to a greater level of accuracy than presented (minor discrepancies between sum of components and total may occur).
- Analysis and forecasting: forecasts via cross-checks of multiple sources; key factors and assumptions used in each country's forecast described in the relevant country chapter.

### p.13 — Introduction: Page ix — "Terminology" (definitions, no numbers)
Defines key terms:
- ATM: self-service banking terminals dispensing cash; may offer other facilities (cash/cheque deposit, transaction enquiry, printed statement, account-to-account transfer, bill payment or PIN change). Defined as a generic term covering both multifunctional terminals and monofunctional cash dispensers.
- Independent ATM deployer (IAD): IADs serve one or more (typically non-bank) cardholders.
- Sharing arrangements: most ATMs shared either via a network managed by an interbank organisation, or via bilateral/multilateral agreements. Proprietary ATMs available only to the deployer's own cardholders and not shared via payment schemes.
- Branches: total number of branches excludes post offices and other banking offices unless otherwise stated.
- Usage patterns: average monthly number of transactions per machine; average value of a cash withdrawal; average value of cash withdrawals per machine per year.
- ATM location types: hall (within the bank branch, not accessible outside normal opening hours); lobby (within the bank branch, accessible during normal opening hours); through-the-wall / TTW (on exterior wall of bank branch, accessed from the street); off-site (away from the bank branch, e.g. shopping centre or railway station); drive-through (accessible from a vehicle).

### p.14 — Introduction: Page x — "Service and maintenance" + "Glossary and abbreviations"
Definitions only (no figures).
- Service & maintenance: cash replenishment (provision of bank notes); first line maintenance (minor tasks e.g. clearing obstructions, replacing consumables like printer paper); second line maintenance (remedial/preventative maintenance, replacing parts); help desk (receiving ATM incident reports, managing response).
- Glossary/abbreviations: CAGR = Compound Annual Growth Rate; IAD = Independent ATM Deployer; PIN = Personal Identification Number; NFC = Near-Field Communication; Not-on-us = transaction at an ATM deployed by an institution other than the card issuer; On-us = transaction at an ATM deployed by the card issuer; Disloyalty fee / Foreign fee = fee paid by cardholder to card issuer for using an ATM owned by another institution; Interchange fee = fee payable by the card issuer to the ATM owner; Surcharge = fee paid by cardholder to the ATM owner for using an ATM owned by another institution; N/A = Not applicable; n.a. = Not available; neg. = Negligible.
