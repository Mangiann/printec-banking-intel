### p.142 — Belgium, Section 7: Market forecasts — 7.1 Forecast installed base
- **Figure 7.1: Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028** (table; cols 2023-2028 + CAGR):
  - ATMs: 5,000 / 4,600 / 4,300 / 4,100 / 3,950 / 3,850 — **CAGR -5.1%**
  - Absolute growth: -502 / -400 / -300 / -200 / -150 / -100 — CAGR N/A
  - Percentage growth: -9.1% / -8.0% / -6.5% / -4.7% / -3.7% / -2.5% — N/A
  - ATMs per million people: 429 / 395 / 369 / 352 / 339 / 330 — **CAGR -5.1%**
  - Source: RBR analysis.
- Narrative: Belgian installed base set to fall ~30% over the forecast period, declining from **5,502 at end-2022 to 3,850 by end-2028**; this is ~330 ATMs per million people by 2028 vs **472 ATMs per million in 2022**. Batopin project (begun 2021) consolidating estates of the four largest deployers (Belfius, BNP Paribas Fortis, ING, KBC Group) and relocating machines over the next ~4 years; most ATMs being deployed off-site. Bank-neutral; aim to keep 95% of citizens within an ATM within 5km. Most off-site. Majority of branch ATMs to remain in lobby (JoFiCo initiative shares management of ATM estates; deployers decide on number/location of their ATMs, retaining branding). IAD (independent ATM deployer) sector expected to grow gradually over the forecast period.

### p.143 — Belgium, Section 7: Number of ATMs chart + 7.2 Forecast shipments
- **Figure 7.2: Number of ATMs, 1995-2028** (bar chart, y-axis 0-10,000). Historical rises from ~3,800 (1995) to a peak of ~9,400 around 2014, then declines; 2022 ~5,500; forecast bars (lighter blue) continue declining to 3,850 by 2028. Source: Belgian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
- Narrative: More ATMs projected to be equipped with QR code and NFC readers by end-2028; contactless cash withdrawals to become more widespread (NFC-reader penetration of installed base higher than service offered in 2022). Widening of facilities likely counteracted by tendency to deploy non-cash kiosks/purpose-specific terminals; several deployers plan to replace ATMs with such devices. Assisted self-service unlikely to be introduced over the forecast period.

### p.144 — Belgium, 7.3 Forecast cash withdrawal number & value (two charts)
- **Figure 7.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (dual-axis combo). Left axis (bars, annual number, million) 0-300; right axis (line, average number per ATM per month) 0-3,000. Bars: 2018 ~270m, 2019 ~278m, drop to 2020 ~169m, 2021 ~157m, 2022 ~139m, then forecast (lighter bars) declining to ~90m by 2028. Line (avg per ATM/month) ~2,950 in 2019, dropping to ~2,000 from 2020 onward, ending ~1,920 in 2028. Source: NBB, Belgian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
- Narrative: Significant fall in withdrawals in 2020 (COVID-19); 2021/2022 continued downward, expected to remain so as cashless payments increasingly preferred. Est. **90 million cash withdrawals in 2028 vs 139 million in 2022**. Average number per ATM per month to fall gradually; **~1,900 per ATM per month by 2028 vs 2,008 in 2022**.
- **Figure 7.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (dual-axis combo). Left axis (bars, annual value EUR bn) 0-45; right axis (line, average value per withdrawal EUR) 0-250. Bars: 2018 ~41bn, 2019 ~37bn, 2020 ~29bn, 2021 ~28bn, 2022 ~24bn, then forecast declining to ~18bn by 2028. Line (avg value per withdrawal) rises from ~145 (2018) to ~202 by 2028. Source: NBB, Belgian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
