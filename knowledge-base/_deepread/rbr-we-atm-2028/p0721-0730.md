### p.721 — Turkey, Section 7: Value of cash withdrawals (Figure 7.5)
Figure 7.5 "Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028" — combo chart. Bars = Annual value TRY bn (left axis, 0-8,000); line = Average value TRY (right axis, 0-4,000). Bars rise steadily: 2018 ~800, 2019 ~1,000, 2020 ~1,050, 2021 ~1,200, 2022 ~1,950 (dark/actual), then forecast (light) ~2,900 (2023), ~3,900 (2024), ~4,900 (2025), ~5,700 (2026), ~6,600 (2027), reaching ~7,750 (2028). Average-value line climbs from ~550 (2018) to ~3,650 (2028).
- Body text: total annual value of cash withdrawals from Turkish ATMs projected to grow at an average **22% per year between 2023 and 2028**, to total **nearly TRY 7.8 trillion (USD 468 billion, at the 2022 exchange rate)** at end of forecast period.
- Implies an **average cash withdrawal value of TRY 3,642 (USD 220, at the 2022 exchange rate)** in 2028.
- Rise driven primarily by **high inflation** in Turkey, expected to remain comfortably **above 15%** for the entire forecast period.
- Sources: BKM, Turkish ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
