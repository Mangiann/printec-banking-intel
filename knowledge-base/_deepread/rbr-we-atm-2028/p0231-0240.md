### p.231 — France, Figure 6.13a: Outsourcing of second line maintenance, 2022
Second line maintenance (includes repairs). Stacked bar by location with table:
- Branch ATMs: 28,361 outsourced (74.6%) / 9,663 in-house (25.4%).
- Off-site ATMs: 9,988 outsourced (93.0%) / 748 in-house (7.0%).
- Total: 38,349 outsourced (78.6%) / 10,411 in-house (21.4%).
Outsourced for the majority of ATMs; performed in-house for just 21% of machines at end-2022. Off-site ATMs more often outsourced (93%) than branch (75%), but more branch ATMs are outsourced in absolute terms (more branch-based terminals overall). La Banque Postale outsources some branch ATMs but keeps >half in-house; Euro Information uses in-house team for both branch and off-site.

### p.234 — France, Figure 6.14a: Outsourcing of help desk, 2022
Help desk = first point of call for ATM issues/faults; distributes work rather than carrying it out. Stacked bar by location (note: the bar reuses the 9,663/28,361/748/9,988 graphic, but the data table differs):
- Branch ATMs: 7,843 outsourced (20.6%) / 30,181 in-house (79.4%).
- Off-site ATMs: 3,471 outsourced (32.3%) / 7,265 in-house (67.7%).
- Total: 11,314 outsourced (23.2%) / 37,446 in-house (76.8%).
Help desk outsourced for just 23% of terminals; more commonly performed in-house. Crédit Agricole, La Banque Postale and Euro Information perform it in-house for entire fleets; BRED and Crédit du Nord outsource help desk for all their ATMs.

### p.237 — France, Section 7: Market forecasts — 7.1 Forecast installed base, Figure 7.1: Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028
Table (2023→2028, CAGR):
- ATMs: 47,300 / 46,000 / 44,900 / 44,000 / 43,200 / 42,600 — CAGR -2.1%.
- Absolute growth: -1,460 / -1,300 / -1,100 / -900 / -800 / -600 — N/A.
- Percentage growth: -3.0% / -2.7% / -2.4% / -2.0% / -1.8% / -1.4% — N/A.
- ATMs per million people: 732 / 712 / 695 / 681 / 668 / 659 — CAGR -2.1%.
French installed base forecast to keep contracting 2023-2028; total falls to 42,600 by end-2028, i.e. 13% fewer ATMs than at end-2022. Avg ~2%/yr decline, faster at the start of the period. ATM density falls to 659 per million people by end-2028 (based on end-2022 population). Long-term factors: ATM pooling and bank mergers expected to weigh on numbers, with more impact in earlier years; The IAD (independent ATM deployer) sector is a surge of growth in 2022 (large proportion of bank ATMs transferred across to IADs); IADs can use a business case in installing machines in underserved areas. Brink's took over BPCE Group; meaningful recovery in travel/tourism likely to boost IAD growth at end of forecast, following the impact of COVID-19.

### p.239 — France, 7.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value, Figure 7.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028
Combo chart: bars = annual number of withdrawals (millions, left axis ~0-1,800) + line = average number of withdrawals per ATM (right axis ~0-3,000). Cash withdrawals dipped/peaked around 2020 with COVID-19 impact; recovery 2021 onward; the total number of cash withdrawals peaked in 2020. Steep drop in cash withdrawals in 2020 (COVID-19 and restrictions/hygiene inhibited cash use); total number fell to around 1 billion in 2020. The average number of withdrawals per ATM increased between 2020 and 2022 — partly recovery in transaction numbers, partly fewer ATMs over which to spread them; expected to fall at similar rates. Cash still relatively widely used in France; SMEs and merchants are largely required to accept cash up to certain amounts. Cash remains an increase in card use (particularly contactless, for everyday transactions of lower value, traditionally cash). It is understood common to use cash in France; ATM use is decreasing but cash use is also recovering after COVID-19. Few years owing to increased use of accessible-for-people-with-disabilities. ATM behaviour and use of cash by health and hygiene concerns during COVID-19.

### p.240 — France, Figure 7.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028
Combo chart (both axes 0-180): bars = annual value of withdrawals (EUR bn, left axis); line/diamonds = average value per withdrawal (EUR, right axis). Annual value bars: 2018 ~150, 2019 ~153 (high point of actuals), dip 2020 ~127, 2021 ~134, 2022 ~141, then rising forecast 2023 ~146 / 2024 ~150 / 2025 ~152 / 2026 ~155 / 2027 ~158 / 2028 ~161. Average value per withdrawal (line) rises steadily from ~90 (2018) to ~155 (2028).
Following a 2020 drop (COVID-19 restrictions/hygiene inhibited cash use), total value of cash withdrawals rose in France in 2021 and again in 2022. Annual value expected to increase steadily, with **EUR 161 billion (USD 169 billion) of withdrawals projected for 2028**. Falling ATM numbers and fewer ATM visits expected to push up the average value of a cash withdrawal: rising to **EUR 155 (USD 163) in 2028, up from EUR 119 (USD 125) in 2022**.
