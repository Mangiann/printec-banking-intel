Note (paraphrased): figures are the share of the ATM and cash-withdrawal market that ATM/cash-withdrawal numbers cover; primary markets = certain headline markets; rank = position vs. Western Europe.
- Narrative: At end of 2022, total number of ATMs in Spain stood at 45,233, a fall of 5% from the prior year; a further ~1,300 bank branches were closed in Spain, continuing to focus on digitalisation of banking services and removal of physical branch services. Share of ATMs found in bank branches stood at 78% (end-2022), although the share of ATMs found in off-site settings continues to increase on-site... rising percentage of off-site ATMs and reflecting branch closure. Spain is a significant contributor to the closing of bank branches in the country, a significant contributing factor in the further deployment of off-site ATMs (further deployment). Automated note deposit can be made at 54% of the installed base; all ATMs offer a printed statement, while most offer other services such as PIN change, mobile phone top-ups, bill payments and fund transfers. Biometric identification is an innovative technology that features at more than 1,400 terminals, all of which deployed by CaixaBank in 2022. A total of 1,109 million cash withdrawals were carried out during 2022, a 6% increase from 2021. The value of withdrawals rose to EUR 203 billion (USD 213 billion), 6% higher than a year earlier. Despite slowing due to fluctuating exchange rates, this is actually a decrease of 2% in USD terms from 2021. NCR supplies more than half of Spanish ATMs, while Fujitsu provides over a quarter of the market. Diebold Nixdorf manufactures the majority of the remaining terminals, with Glory retaining a small presence. The main network central hardware provider within the Spanish market is IBM, with 68% of ATMs connected to this vendor. The vast majority of Spanish ATMs use proprietary solutions for terminal driving software, ATM application software, message protocol, management/monitoring, software distribution, customer relationship management and cash forecasting and management software solutions. Just over two thirds of Spanish ATMs run over Windows 7. Dedicated ATM security software tends to be provided by Auriga and GMV's Checker (covers over 70% of Spain's installed base).
