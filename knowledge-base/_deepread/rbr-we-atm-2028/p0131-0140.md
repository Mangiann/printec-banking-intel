### p.131 — Belgium: cash replenishment outsourcing trend, 2018-2022
Figure 6.11b "Outsourcing of cash replenishment, 2018-2022" (100% stacked bar, In-house light / Outsourced dark). Outsourced share rising each year: 2018 32%, 2019 47%, 2020 51%, 2021 66%, 2022 69% (In-house mirror: 68%, 53%, 49%, 34%, 31%).
Table (ATM counts):
- In-house: 5,477 (2018) / 3,952 / 3,320 / 2,031 / 1,687 (2022); CAGR -25.5%
- Outsourced: 2,542 / 3,495 / 3,448 / 4,007 / 3,815; CAGR +10.7%
- Total: 8,019 / 7,447 / 6,768 / 6,038 / 5,502; CAGR -9.0%
Text: outsourced share steadily rising, driven by largest deployers; Batopin project launch saw Belfius, ING and KBC Bank outsource some/all branch ATMs to the bank-neutral network.

### p.135 — Belgium: FLM providers, 2022
Figure 6.12c "First line maintenance providers, 2022" (pie). Shares: Diebold Nixdorf 32%, In-house 32%, CELDS 16%, Cennox 9%, Brink's 8%, Merchant 3%.
Table (ATMs / Share):
- Diebold Nixdorf: 1,772 / 32.2%
- In-house: 1,760 / 32.0%
- CELDS: 863 / 15.7%
- Cennox: 481 / 8.7%
- Brink's: 441 / 8.0%
- Merchant: 185 / 3.4%
- Total: 5,502 / 100.0%
Text: FLM carried out in-house for 32%, mostly attributable to expansion of Batopin (common third-party greater since 2021 due to Batopin). Diebold Nixdorf, while outsourcing cash replenishment to Brink's, outsources FLM to Diebold Nixdorf; ING outsources cash replenishment to Loomis but uses CELDS for FLM. Diebold Nixdorf also contracted by Crelan, making it the most common third-party provider for FLM in Belgium. Manufacturer's share expected to increase with Jof.Co project, whereby it will manage all ATMs hardware, software, service, maintenance and management solutions for Crelan, bpost, Argenta, AXA Bank and vdk bank in the next few years.

### p.136 — Belgium: second line maintenance (SLM) outsourcing by location, 2022
Figure 6.13a "Outsourcing of second line maintenance, 2022" (stacked bars Branch vs Off-site). Branch: Outsourced 4,602, In-house 0. Off-site: Outsourced 880, In-house 20.
Table:
- Branch ATMs: 4,602 outsourced (100.0%) / 0 in-house (0.0%)
- Off-site ATMs: 880 outsourced (97.8%) / 20 in-house (2.2%)
- Total: 5,482 outsourced (99.6%) / 20 in-house (0.4%)
Text: SLM universally outsourced regardless of location, except Loomis' fleet. ING was the only deployer to do SLM in-house via CELDS in 2020; once CELDS became a third-party provider, entire Belgian base outsourced SLM in 2021 until Loomis entered the market in 2022.

### p.140 — Belgium: help desk outsourcing trend, 2019-2022
Figure 6.14b "Outsourcing of help desk, 2019-2022" (100% stacked). Outsourced share: 2019 51%, 2020 52%, 2021 76%, 2022 75% (In-house: 49%, 48%, 24%, 25%).
Table (ATMs):
- In-house: 3,686 / 3,244 / 1,472 / 1,379; CAGR -34.8%
- Outsourced: 3,761 / 3,524 / 4,566 / 4,123; CAGR +8.2%

Text: Belgian deployers gradually outsourcing help desks. In 2021, as with FLM and SLM, ING's sale of CELDS accounted for the 24 pp jump in outsourcing. In 2022, help desk outsourcing share fell one pp as those deployers contracted their fleets faster than those who kept the service in-house.
