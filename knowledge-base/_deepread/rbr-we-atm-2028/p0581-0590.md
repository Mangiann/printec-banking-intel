### p.590 — Spain second line maintenance providers, 2022 (Spain Page 31)
Text intro: Second line maintenance market in Spain unchanged in 2022; all ATMs have this service outsourced. High intricacy means deployers almost certain not to have facilities in-house.
Figure 6.13c: Second line maintenance providers, 2022 (pie — Fujitsu 46%, NCR 36%, Diebold Nixdorf 14%, Tecnocom 4%).
Table — Provider / ATMs / Share:
- Fujitsu — 20,620 — 45.6%
- NCR — 16,494 — 36.5%
- Diebold Nixdorf — 6,498 — 14.4%
- Tecnocom — 1,618 — 3.6%
- Glory — 3 — neg.
- Total — 45,233 — 100.0%
Text: ATM manufacturers conduct the vast majority of second line maintenance. Increase in share of Fujitsu ATMs in 2022 makes Fujitsu the most common SLM provider. Fujitsu SLM software used both on Fujitsu ATMs and ATMs from other manufacturers, so its SLM share exceeds its manufacturer share of the ATM market. ABANCA contracts Tecnocom for its ATM fleet; Glory provides this service for the small number of its machines remaining in Spain.
