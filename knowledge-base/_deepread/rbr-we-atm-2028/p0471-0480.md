### p.476 — Netherlands p.32. Section 7: Market forecasts — 7.1 Forecast installed base
- **Figure 7.1: Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028** (table):
  - ATMs: 2023 4,950, 2024 4,800, 2025 4,650, 2026 4,550, 2027 4,450, 2028 4,350 — CAGR -2.6%.
  - Absolute growth: 2023 -167, 2024 -150, 2025 -150, 2026 -100, 2027 -100, 2028 -100 — CAGR N/A.
  - Percentage growth: 2023 -3.3%, 2024 -3.0%, 2025 -3.1%, 2026 -2.2%, 2027 -2.2%, 2028 -2.2% — CAGR N/A.
  - ATMs per million people: 2023 282, 2024 273, 2025 265, 2026 259, 2027 253, 2028 248 — CAGR -2.6%.
- Text: installed base expected to contract at an avg annual rate of 3% over forecast; reach 4,350 ATMs by 2028. Bank branches in the country expected to fall at a faster rate, to ~480 outlets by end-2028. Density of ATMs per 100 bank branches in country rises from 882 (2022) to 906 (2028). Cash usage expected to keep falling as Dutch consumers grow comfortable using alternative/digital payment channels (Geldmaat to evaluate ATM locations in urban areas, potentially rationalize off-site fleet which is already well-served by ATMs; off-site ATMs already make up vast majority of installations in the Netherlands). Number of IADs expected to fall over forecast period; many installations no longer as profitable; IADs reliant on inbound tourism, many tourists choose to withdraw cash on arrival in the Netherlands. The contraction in the Dutch market likely moderated by Geldmaat's obligation (Social Consultation on the Payments System / MOB) to provide sufficient cash provision to the public via its ATMs — threshold: no ATM within five kilometres of their home address; unless this threshold amended, scope for further consolidation of installed base is limited.

### p.477 — Netherlands p.33. Number of ATMs chart + 7.2 Forecast ATM shipments
- **Figure 7.2: Number of ATMs, 1995-2028** (bar chart, y-axis 0-10,000; rises to peak ~9,000+ around 2007-2010, then declines; forecast years 2023-2028 shown in lighter shade declining toward ~4,350). (Sources: DNB, Dutch ATM deployers and RBR analysis.)

### p.478 — Netherlands p.34. 7.3 Forecast cash withdrawal number and value
- **Figure 7.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo: bars = annual number, million [left axis 0-350]; line = average number [right axis 0-3,500]). Annual number declines from ~300m (2018) toward ~76m (2028); average number per ATM declines steadily.
- Text: annual number of cash withdrawals forecast to fall throughout forecast period, reaching 76 million in 2028 — a 41% decrease from the 108 million seen in 2022. Fall caused by increased uptake of cashless payment methods and consequent reduced demand for cash (contactless payments incl. mobile wallets continue to displace cash; rise in wearable payment technology such as watches and rings may also reduce demand). Average number of cash withdrawals per ATM per month forecast to gradually fall from 2,157 (2022) to 1,439 (2027).
- **Figure 7.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo: bars = annual value EUR bn [left axis 0-45]; line = average value EUR [right axis 0-300]). Annual value declines from ~36-40 (2018) toward ~18-20 (2028); average value per withdrawal rises steadily.
