### p.571 — Spain, off-site / branch deployment trends (Spain: Page 12)
Prose only, no figure. Key points:
- Banco Santander, BBC, Deutsche Bank and Banca Pueyo, along with many other smaller banks, deploy more than 50% of their ATMs in this location, whilst Euronet and RSI have their entire installed base in TTW locations. The share of TTW installations has remained largely stable from 2018; a general consolidation of ATM numbers in Spain, regardless of location, in this period, means the total number of ATMs deployed here has fallen by an average of 2.4% per year between mid-2018 and end-2022.
- The location which has experienced the largest year-on-year reduction in total ATM numbers is the branch lobby, exacerbated by continued branch closures throughout this period. ATMs placed in bank branch halls also fell slightly but still represented a 9% share of the total installed base.
- Historically, CaixaBank and BBVA have been the biggest deployers of terminals in bank lobbies, and this trend continued in 2022. CaixaBank does, however, once again no longer deploy the majority of their ATMs in this location, as the influx of newly acquired Bankia ATMs means that they deploy slightly more in TTW locations, the same as 2021. The benefits of deploying ATMs in branches are felt after the 24-7 service, and it is often perceived as more secure than the through-the-exterior wall of the bank branch.
- The only ATM location that has experienced a year-on-year average growth since 2018 is off-site. Continued branch closures have certainly contributed to this, as banking services are continually becoming more and more remote and the increased variety of functionalities offered by ATMs mean that they can, to a certain extent, operate as a branch themselves for an increasing number of customers. Additionally, expansion by IADs — namely Euronet and Cashzone — has contributed to this trend. By definition, IADs do not have branches and thus deploy all ATMs in off-site locations.

### p.573 — Figure 4.5: ATMs and SATs with automated deposit functionality, 2022 + prose (Spain: Page 14)
Figure 4.5 table (ATMs / SATs):
- Automated note deposit 24,393 ATMs / 0 SATs
- of which note recycling 8,797 / N/A
- Automated cheque deposit 10,517 / 0
- Total 24,393 / 0
Prose:
- The number of ATMs offering additional services increases year-on-year as deployers look to offer customers a wider array of services, especially as bank branches are increasingly closing their doors. The decline in bank branches and ATMs means that the facilities available at ATMs are more important than ever and Spanish banks recognise this. The focus of Spanish banks for improving the capabilities of their fleets, instead of merely installing new terminals, also contributes to a general reduction in the number of ATMs available in the country.
- Numerous services are offered as standard across all or close to all ATMs in Spain. This includes printed statements, which can be found at 100% of Spanish ATMs. 89% of terminals offer mobile phone top-ups, whilst cardless cash withdrawals (83%), prepaid card reloading (78%) and bank transfers, both to accounts at the same bank (82%) and different banks (88%) are the capabilities offered at more than 75% of ATMs.
- Other functionalities are less common but are becoming increasingly more popular. The share of ATMs accepting automated note deposits rose to 54% in 2022, whilst the share of ATMs which recycle deposited notes for withdrawal also increased, to 19%. Dynamic currency conversion (DCC) provides a lucrative source of revenue for deployers carrying foreign cardholders, and is available at 69% of ATMs, although only 15% of Spanish ATMs offer multi-currency dispensing. Assisted self-service remains limited in Spain.
- The availability of certain functions differs across deployers. Multi-currency deposits, for example, are only available at around 1,000 Euronet ATMs. Similarly, automated cheque deposit capabilities are not common for Spanish ATMs, with CaixaBank being the only deployer that offers them. Cheque usage is low in Spain meaning there is little appetite among deployers for this functionality to be introduced. At end-2022, automated cheque acceptance accounted for 9% of CaixaBank's ATM fleet, despite only being available at 23% of terminals across the country.
- Bill payment is possible at 85% of ATMs via the reading of a barcode, while 63% allow this via credit transfer to a pre-arranged payee. The share of ATMs providing voice guidance for visually impaired customers with visual impairment rose to 52% in 2022, up from 51% the previous year.

Prose:
- NFC readers had been fitted at 70% of Spanish ATMs by end-2022. Nearly two thirds of the installed base are equipped with QR code readers, and the vast majority of CaixaBank terminals now feature a QR code reader. Nearly three quarters (72%) of the installed base have a touch screen.
- At more than 1,400 ATMs deployed by CaixaBank, consumers can confirm their identity using facial recognition. Facial recognition has been developed in conjunction with Fujitsu and FacePhi, enabling cardless cash withdrawals bypassing the need for customers to enter a PIN number. No other deployer in Spain currently offers any form of biometric identification.
- The majority of Spain's ATMs have been fitted with anti-skimming devices and over 60% of ATMs have built-in or integrated security cameras. Note degradation techniques are less common, featuring at only 9% of ATMs.

Prose:
- The number of cash withdrawals witnessed growth of 6% during 2022, as the overall number of withdrawals increased by just under 100 million during the year, helped by returning consumer confidence in cash as cash withdrawal numbers continue to rebound post-pandemic. Despite this, the number of withdrawals has still yet to return to the levels seen in 2019 and earlier.
- The average number of cash withdrawals per ATM per month increased to 1,990, further rebounding from the low of 1,605 in 2020 but still not back to the 2,149 average of 2019. Spanish consumers have trended away from cash and towards card payments in recent years, largely because of the COVID-19 pandemic. In the future, the average ATM is expected to see more usage, due to the overall reduction in ATM numbers. Customers are expected to continue to use digital wallets and payments apps in higher numbers, thereby reducing dependence on cash, as well as the need for such a widespread ATM network.
- CaixaBank saw the highest average number of cash withdrawals per ATM per month in 2022, standing at 2,004.

### p.577 — Section 5: Manufacturer market shares — Figure 5.1: Manufacturer market shares, 2018-2022 (Spain: Page 18)

- Total: 52,503 (2018) → 49,468/45,468 (2022); CAGR -3.5%.
Prose:
- At end-2022, the largest ATM manufacturer in Spain was once again NCR, providing 25,793 ATMs, 57% of the market. This increase and percentage point from end-2022, despite the overall number decreasing, as the number of NCR ATMs fell at a lower rate than the total number of ATMs in the country. NCR supplies most ATMs for a number of major deployers including RSI (89%), Banco Santander (80%), Euronet (70%), Unicaja (54%), and BBVA (53%). The most common NCR models deployed in Spain are the SelfServ 34 (6634) and SelfServ 32 (6632).
- Fujitsu is the second largest supplier of ATMs to the Spanish market, holding a 27% share at the end of the year. The share is an increase of almost three percentage points from end-2021, as CaixaBank, the largest single deployer of Fujitsu ATMs, increased their installed base further during 2022. Fujitsu was the only manufacturer to see growth in the number of their ATMs in Spain in 2022, as more terminals are deployed by CaixaBank. Fujitsu deploys the majority of these with its preferred Fujitsu models, both increasing the share of Fujitsu ATMs, whilst also contributing to a decrease in the share of Diebold Nixdorf ATMs. CaixaBank deploys its own, customised Fujitsu models, called proCash. Ibercaja is also a majority Fujitsu deployer, with this vendor constituting 97% of its fleet at end-2022.
- Diebold Nixdorf's share of the ATM market fell its three percentage points in 2022, dropping to 16%. The largest contributor to this shift was the 1,000 ex-Bankia Diebold Nixdorf ATMs which CaixaBank removed throughout 2022. The largest deployer of Diebold Nixdorf ATMs in 2022 was BBVA, which operates 2,255, followed by Euronet (1,049) and Banco Sabadell with 962, the majority of which are the Opteva 760 model. CS 4080 (CINEO C4080) is another common model.
- Glory's share of the Spanish ATM market continues to be negligible; there were three Glory ATMs installed at the end of 2022. BCC remains the only deployer of ATMs from this manufacturer.
