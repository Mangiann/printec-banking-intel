### p.468 — Netherlands p.24 — 6.2 Service and maintenance (continued)
- Narrative: Number of ATMs with cash replenishment outsourced increased rapidly 2018-end-2022 (avg ~28% CAGR); corresponding share of installed base rose from ~26% to virtually 100% (2018-2021). Driven by decreasing number of ATMs deployed directly by banks, since ATMs were typically refilled by bank staff. Bank ATMs were pooled under the Geldmaat scheme (from June 2019). As Geldmaat outsources the service, the number of ATMs for which cash replenishment is carried out in-house in the Netherlands fell sharply. Migration to the Geldmaat project was completed during 2021 and as a result, cash replenishment of Dutch ATMs is now virtually all outsourced.
- **Figure 6.11c: Cash replenishment providers, 2022** (pie + table):
  - Brink's — 4,346 — 84.9%
  - Merchant — 384 — 7.5%
  - VTS — 373 — 7.3%
  - In-house — 14 — 0.3%
  - Total — 5,117 — 100.0%
  - Note: Cash replenishment market for ATMs is highly concentrated, Brink's accounting for ~85% share (largest third-party provider; refills all Geldmaat ATMs as well as some IAD machines). VTS represents ~7% of ATMs contracted by IADs such as In2retail, GWK Travelex and YourServices. ~8% of machines refilled by retail merchants on their premises — only the case at ATMs deployed by IADs. Only a small number of ATMs (~0.3%, all GWK Travelex) use cash replenishment performed in-house.

### p.470 — Netherlands p.26 — 6.2 Service and maintenance (continued)
- Narrative: As with cash replenishment, number of ATMs with first line maintenance performed in-house declined rapidly 2018-2022 (annual avg ~77% decline). By end-2022, virtually 100% of ATM first line maintenance was outsourced. Prior to the Geldmaat project/pooling of bank ATMs, all banks had carried out FLM in-house; consolidation of bank ATMs started in 2017 but accelerated by the migration of their ATMs to the Geldmaat joint venture from 2019. Migration completed and Dutch banks no longer deploy own ATMs; the small number of ATMs maintained in-house belong to GWK Travelex.
- **Figure 6.12c: First line maintenance providers, 2022** (pie + table):
  - Brink's — 4,346 — 84.9%
  - Merchant — 384 — 7.5%
  - VTS — 373 — 7.3%
  - In-house — 14 — 0.3%
  - Total — 5,117 — 100.0%
  - Note: Shares of FLM providers mirror those of cash replenishment, with Brink's accounting for the vast majority, holding an ~85% market share at end-2022. VTS has ~7% of market on account of providing service to several IADs, while other IADs (In2retail) account for the merchant share. ~8% of machines are refilled by retail merchants on their premises (only the case at IAD-deployed ATMs). GWK Travelex uses its own staff to carry out first line maintenance on a small number of its ATMs.
