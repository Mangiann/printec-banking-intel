### p.191 — Finland, Section 4.5 cash withdrawals (narrative, no figures)
Body text on Finnish ATM cash withdrawals (Finland: Page 11):
- Number of cash withdrawals fell during 2022, but the total VALUE of those withdrawals rose by 5% — the first year of growth in this metric in the last five years.
- Increase attributed mainly to short spikes in March and May 2022 tied to the outbreak of war in Ukraine; citing potential Russian cyberattacks that could disrupt payment systems, the Bank of Finland advised citizens to keep cash at home, so many Finns stockpiled cash (withdrew as much as possible in as few ATM visits).
- Average value of a cash withdrawal (local currency) has increased every year since 2019. 2022 saw the most significant increase: from EUR 106 (USD 126) to EUR 119 (USD 125).
- Average withdrawal value also inflated by stockpiling among Russian citizens at border locations, but with Russian cards blocked relatively early in 2022 this was less impactful than Finnish stockpiling.
- Other than the March/May peaks, the rest of the year saw withdrawal values in line with 2021.

Source: Finnish ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Narrative: Only two ATM manufacturers present in the Finnish market — NCR and Diebold Nixdorf. NCR's market share gradually increased year on year between 2018 and 2022, standing at 72% at end of period. Despite overall market contraction, the number of NCR terminals increased by 13 in 2022. NCR machines are being replaced mainly by Automatia (which moved toward an increasing majority-NCR fleet, ~86% area est.); Nokas, on the other hand, deploys a roughly even split of NCR and Diebold Nixdorf machines (54%), but tends to replace DN terminals with new ones during the year. Forex Bank replaces all ATMs with NCR machines. Deployed model families referenced: NCR SelfServ 26 (6626) and SelfServ 36 (6636); Diebold Nixdorf CS 4060 (CINEO 4060).

- ATMs: 1,660 / 1,630 / 1,600 / 1,570 / 1,540 / 1,510 / CAGR -1.9%.

- ATMs per million people: 300 / 294 / 289 / 283 / 278 / 273 / CAGR -1.9%.
Source: RBR analysis.
Narrative: Finnish installed base expected to continue falling over the forecast period at an average ~2% per year. ATM rationalisation is a driver; some terminals to be excluded from rationalisation programmes because they offer automated deposit / cash-recycling. ATM-as-a-Service and ATM pooling discussed (a growing recycling/deposit functionality, partnerships among banks). Bank of Finland still views cash infrastructure as important; legislation on cash access being considered. Number of new ATM customers in Finland (via ADTs) rising even as overall base declines.

### p.194 — Finland, Section 6 cont.: Figure 6.2 (number of ATMs chart) + 6.2 Forecast ATM shipments (Figure 6.3)
Narrative (top): Proportion of ATMs with automated-deposit and cash-recycling functionality is forecast to grow, as these terminals are largely excluded from rationalisation programmes. Very little deployment of new ADTs expected; Automatia's deployment of new ADTs slowed to just two machines in 2022. Automated deposit transactions continue to rise in Finland, attracting new customers to the ATM channel.
Figure 6.2: Number of ATMs, 1995-2028 (bar chart; dark bars = historical to 2022, light bars = forecast 2023-2028; y-axis 0-3,000 in steps of 500). ### p.195 — Finland, Section 6.3: Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value (Figure 6.4 + 6.5)
Figure 6.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028 (combo chart). Left axis = annual number, million (0-140, step 28); right axis = average number per ATM (0-7,000, step 1,400). Dark bars 2018-2022, light forecast bars 2023-2028.
- Annual number (million), approx from bars: ~116 (2018), ~110 (2019), ~77 (2020), ~62 (2021), ~58 (2022); forecast declining gently to ~50 by 2028.
- Average number per ATM (line, right axis): ~6,500 (2018) → ~5,500 (2019) → ~3,700 (2020) → ~3,000 (2021), then roughly flat ~2,800-2,900 through 2028.
Narrative: Number of cash withdrawals projected to keep falling at ~3%/year as consumers prefer card and mobile payments even for low-value transactions. Number of cash withdrawals forecast to total just 50 million in 2028 — 80% lower than the peak in 2001. Decline rate expected to slow over the forecast period as demand for cash plateaus.
Figure 6.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028 (combo chart). Left axis = annual value, EUR bn (0-12, step 2); right axis = average value, EUR (0-180, step 30). 
- Annual value (EUR bn) approx: ~11.5 (2018), ~10 (2019), ~7.5 (2020), ~7 (2021), ~7 (2022); forecast roughly flat ~7.5 through 2028.
- Average value per withdrawal (line, right axis): rising from ~100 EUR (2018) to ~155-160 EUR (2028).
Source: Finnish ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Narrative: Total annual value of cash withdrawals at Finland's installed base projected to increase over the forecast period at an annual average rate of ~3% (2022 value EUR 7bn). Average value of a withdrawal forecast to keep rising as cash is increasingly held as a store of emergency value; average value of a cash withdrawal expected to reach EUR 156 (USD 164 at 2022 exchange rate) by 2028.
