### p.183 — Section 3: ATM networks and sharing (3.1)
- 1,686 terminals at end-2022, -2% YoY; installed base has contracted almost every year since peaking at just over 3,000 ATMs in 1992.
- History: first Finnish ATMs installed 1971 by Finnish Bankers Association; savings banks began 1976, Union Bank of Finland 2 years later. 1980s: two networks — POLT (Kansallis Osake Pankki, Union Bank of Finland, Bank of Helsinki, Samlink) and Otto/Automatia (regional savings banks, Skopbank, co-operatives). POLT and Samlink interoperable 1989. 1994: Union Bank of Finland, Kansallis Osake Pankki, Okobank, Postipankki founded "Otto" (Automatia Pankkiautomaatit / "Automatia"); same banks sold ATMs to the new network, also acquired POLT. 2004: Automatia acquired Samlink + a private fleet from Alandsbanken, becoming a unified network.
- 45% of Finnish terminals removed between 1993 and 2007, at which point declining trend was temporarily interrupted as Swedish IAD Nokas entered Finland; total ATMs grew in 2008 and 2009.
- Nokas grew strongly late 2010s after two deployment deals (retail giant S Group; then Citycon managing shopping centres across Scandinavia); fleet expanded almost tenfold between end-2016 and end-2019 before slowing. Nokas has no deployment deals with Prima, Apela, ABC fuel convenience stores; its terminals are branded Nosto in Finland.
- Late 2020: Swedish cash-handling firm Loomis acquired Automatia from Danske Bank, Nordea and OP Financial Group (previously bank-owned ATM network); Automatia became an IAD, ending last element of bank ATM ownership. Entire installed base deployed by IADs until 2022, when Re:Cash lost its Forex Bank deal and exited Finland; Forex Bank now deploys directly under an ATM-as-a-service agreement with Diebold Nixdorf.

- Body: early adopter of cashless infrastructure → one of least dense WE markets; 304 ATMs/million at end-2022 (down from 309 a year earlier), less than half the regional average. Low provision vs land area: just 5 terminals per 1,000 km², far below WE average of 52.

- Body: 84% of ATMs are off-site; proportion rose every year 2018-2022 (negligible in latter year). - Functionality: ATMs basic, cash-withdrawal focused, few functions added over the years. Automatia's fleet has widest range; Nokas terminals offer fewer; Forex Bank's estate more specialised (foreign currency services). Printed statements at ~two thirds of base = entire Automatia fleet. Mobile top-ups and paid-for third-party advertising at 56% of Finnish ATMs (Automatia only deployer offering these, at majority of its fleet). Speech capability for visually impaired at 54% of base = majority of Automatia + all four Nokas terminals (all at shopping centres); audio guidance also on all speech-capable Automatia machines. Foreign exchange rate info available at all Nokas and Forex Bank machines = 34% of base.

- Cash withdrawal value as a proportion of GDP: 2018 5.0% | 2019 4.4% | 2020 3.2% | 2021 2.7% | 2022 2.7% | CAGR -14.6%
- Body (4.5 cont.): further stabilisation in 2022 — total and average monthly withdrawals fell just 6% and 5% (vs steeper declines earlier in decade pre-COVID). Longer-term: card payments increasingly adopted; mobile payments grown (Apple Pay, Google Pay both present); digital wallets gaining traction (MobilePay, Pivo, Siirto are three major local wallets) — displacing cash for everyday purchases, reducing ATM demand. Other factors: increase in UnionPay-card withdrawals near the Russian border (Russian cardholders using the scheme to store cash for EU-wide payments); Nokas paused UnionPay withdrawals in response. Withdrawals = 80% of ATM transactions in 2022 (broadly stable last 5 yrs); with Finnish ATMs offering limited additional services, share of transactions that are withdrawals is higher than any other market in Europe (also inflated by Nokas' fleet, where 100% of transactions are withdrawals).
