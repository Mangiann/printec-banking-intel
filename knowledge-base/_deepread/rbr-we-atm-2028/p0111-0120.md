### p.112 — Belgium: Banking structure (cont.)
Body text only (no figures). Key data points:
- End-2022 Belfius held assets worth EUR 179 billion (USD 192 billion); second largest by branch network (482 branches), 45 fewer than end-2021; accounting for 13% of Belgium's total.
- Fourth largest bank by assets: ING held EUR 127 billion (USD 136 billion) at end-2022; network of 357 outlets.
- Argenta = fourth largest in branches, with 400 branches (a decline of nine since end-2021); ninth largest bank by assets, EUR 33 billion (USD 35 billion) in total assets.
- Batopin initiative opened 220 branches in 2022; only deployer to open new branches; accounting for 6% of Belgian branches in total.
- Beobank reinstated in 2013 following acquisition of Citibank's consumer franchise by Crédit Mutuel Nord Europe (CMNE); 206 branches at end-2022 (3% fall since end-2021).

- bpost offered financial services through network of 658 post offices by end-2022; in 2022 BNP Paribas Fortis acquired bpost's 50% stake in bpost bank, becoming 100% shareholder; initiating a seven-year exclusive commercial partnership between bpost and BNP Paribas Fortis.

### p.113 — Belgium: ATM networks and sharing (Section 3)
**Figure 3.1: Domestic sharing agreements, 2018-2022.** Table (sharing agreement count by year):

Text: First ATMs deployed in Belgium in 1978. Mister Cash established 1977. ATM network in Belgium followed by Bancontact in 1978. In 1990 both networks merged to create the Bancontact/Mister Cash brand (~60 member banks). In 1979 Belgian post office (now La Poste / bpost) established a proprietary ATM network, named Postomat. In 1999 Banksys took control of its machines. bpost bank then took over operation of Postomat in 2005 (management of machines transferred back to parent company La Poste in 2008). Until 2005, all ATMs installed by the exterior wall of the bank branch (TTW) were required to carry the Bancontact/Mister Cash brand. Atos Worldline acquired Banksys in 2006, retaining the latter's branding until 2013, when point-of-sale and ATM businesses split into separate operations across Europe. Worldline acquired SIX Payment Services. First Data Baltics and Swedish GIRWP. In 2017 and 2018: in February 2022 Worldline integrated Alipay+; wide range of digital payment methods. In September 2021 Batopin network was launched with the aim to install 2,240 cash points by 2024; 481 had already been deployed by end-2022. Source: Belgian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.

### p.116 — Belgium: ATM Pooling + Current ATM market size & growth (Section 4)
**Section 3.2: ATM Pooling** — In 2021 the Belgian ATM Optimisation Initiative (Batopin) was launched by Belfius, BNP Paribas Fortis, ING and KBC Group. Bank-neutral ATMs in the process of being deployed; ~95% of the population has access to an ATM within 5km from their home or workplace. Any ATM deployer in Belgium is free to join the centres.

Text: 2022 marked the eighth consecutive year of decline in the Belgian ATM market. Having peaked at 9,400 in 2014, the market began to contract as both cashless payments and digital banking became ever more widely used, enabling deployers to rationalise their branch networks and ATM fleets. The majority of ATMs are deployed within the bank branch in Belgium; there is a strong correlation between branch closures and removals of ATM installations. The decline in ATMs has also been influenced by the launch of the Batopin project, with the aim of reducing the number of duplicate ATMs in close proximity. Batopin was the only deployer to open new branches and accounting for 6% via more convenient locations; Loomis also added machines as it entered the Belgian market in 2022.

### p.117 — Belgium: Locations (Section 4.3)
Text: At end-2022 there were 472 ATMs per million people in Belgium, lower than the average for the region (WE average 691). Represents a fall from 520 at the end of the previous year; result of the continued contraction in the installed base. With the Batopin and JofiCo projects merging of major bank ATM fleets, concerns have been raised over access to cash, particularly for the elderly, as the number of ATMs continues to fall in the country. Density of ATMs to bank branches also declined from 158 per 100 bank branches at end of 2021 to 148 at end-2022, as the contraction in the installed base outpaced that of bank branches. Belgium had the lowest density in the region for this measure at end-2022. Being a small country, Belgium's density of ATMs relative to area was the third highest for the region, standing at 180 ATMs per 1,000km² at the end of 2022.

Off-site deployment only began from 2005, when control of ATMs was transferred from Banksys to individual deployers. In 2021, for the first time the proportion of off-site ATMs overtook that of TTW-located terminals.

### p.118 — Belgium: Locations (cont.)
Body text only (no figures). Key data points:
- As a result of Banksys legacy, the bank lobby has long been the preferred location for ATMs in Belgium. At end-2022, 77% of installations were located in the lobby, a drop of eight percentage points from the previous year. KBC Group, Belfius and ING are among the deployers with more than 90% of their fleet installed in the lobby.
- TTW is the second most common branch-based location. Europabank has by far the highest proportion at 80% of such ATMs (owing to its small fleet size). Around 20% of the estates of bpost and Crelan are located here.
- Due to historically low usage of ATMs in bank halls and concerns over cost efficiency, only a handful of machines deployed in this location. bpost deploys the largest share of ATMs in hall locations, comprising 2% of its estate. ING has 1% of its fleet located in halls, the only other deployer to install machines here.
- 16% of ATMs located off-site at end-2022, jumping eight percentage points from the previous year due to Batopin's expansion of only off-site machines. Of the largest deployers, BNP Paribas Fortis has the highest proportion of its fleet located here, with 9% of its total fleet situated off-site. Beobank also has a relatively high share away from branches (owing to terminals in metro stations). All IAD fleets are entirely off-site, with Travelex primarily deploying ATMs in airports and train stations. There have never been any drive-through ATMs in Belgium.

Source: Belgian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Text:
- Limited range of additional facilities available at Belgian ATMs, as some deployers prefer to offer non-cash services like fund transfers or bill payments at specific non-cash kiosks to avoid queues. These non-cash kiosks are usually deployed in the lobby alongside ATMs to help migrate customers away from the teller.
- Statement printing becoming less frequently offered, owing to environmental concerns; proportion of installed base offering statement printing fell to 15% by end-2022, with some deployers charging a fee for this service. Only Travelex and Loomis ATMs offered dynamic currency conversion (DCC) and foreign exchange rate information in Belgium. Loomis = the only known deployer to offer multi-currency cash withdrawal.
- PIN change remains universally available at Belgian ATMs offering this service, with most deployers offering it. Funds transfers possible at 35% of Belgian ATMs, usually to another account at the same bank; transfers to accounts at a different bank are offered by Belfius, Crelan, Beobank and Travelex. ING Bank ATMs no longer offer funds transfers due to its integration with their parent. Bill payment is not available at ATMs in Belgium.
- All KBC Group's ATMs and the majority of bpost ATMs offer cardless cash withdrawal, with customers able to use their banking apps to scan a QR code on the ATM screen in order to carry out the transaction. KBC Group are also the only deployers to offer contactless cash withdrawal at all of its ATMs.
- 58% of the installed base offered automated deposit at the end of 2022, up from 55% in 2021; all of which recycle the deposited notes. The main deployers of deposit ATMs include ING, BNP Paribas Fortis, Belfius, KBC Group and Batopin. No ATMs accept cheques or coins; and stand-alone deposit terminals (SATs) are not deployed in the country.
- Audio guidance for the visually impaired is available at all ATMs of Belfius, BNP Paribas Fortis and Batopin.
