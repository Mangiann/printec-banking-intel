- ATMs per million people: 323 | 315 | 306 | 298 | 293 | 289 | CAGR -2.2%

Narrative: ATM installed base in Denmark fell ~2% in 2022; reduction expected to continue. ATM numbers seen as a consumer behaviour issue, with banks driving the move to cashless. Marginal interest-rate rises imposed in Denmark may slow withdrawals. Forecast period sees a reducing cash-withdrawal base. (Footer: Denmark: Page 16.)

### p.172 — Denmark, end of 6.1 (Figure 6.2) + 6.2 Forecast ATM shipments
**Figure 6.2: Number of ATMs, 1995-2028** (bar chart, y-axis 0 to 3,500 in steps of 500). Dark bars = historical (1995-2022), light bars = forecast (2023-2028). Series rises from ~1995 to a peak around the early/mid-2000s (~3,000+) then declines steadily to 1,700 by 2028. Source: Danish ATM deployers and networks and RBR analysis.
Narrative: Number of bank branches in Denmark expected to fall to **810 by the end of 2028**. As ATM number reduces, additional functionality and security features expected on remaining machines; in particular more recyclers expected over the next five years (both deposit/withdrawal and recycling), built-in security cameras and anti-skimming devices. ATMs in the market will be fitted with these features over the forecast period.
**Figure 6.3: Forecast ATM shipments, 2023-2028** (columns 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | CAGR):
- Shipments: 140 | 180 | 190 | 170 | 150 | 110 | CAGR -4.7%
- of which new installations: 20 | 20 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | CAGR -12.9%
- of which replacements: 120 | 160 | 180 | 160 | 140 | 100 | CAGR -3.6%
- Replacements as % of shipments: 86% | 89% | 95% | 94% | 93% | 91% | CAGR 1.2%
Source: RBR analysis.
Narrative: Between 2023 and 2028, ~900 ATMs forecast to be shipped to Denmark, at an average of ~160 per year. Expected that 91% of forecast shipments will be replacements, with banks seeing little incentive to install new ATMs. IADs looking to expand their installed base in the country a small number of these shipments are likely to be new installations. (Footer: Denmark: Page 17.)

### p.173 — Denmark, 6.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value
**Figure 6.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo chart). Left axis = Annual number, million (0-60); right axis = Average number per ATM/month (0-2,400). Bars dark = historical (2018-2022), light = forecast (2023-2028). Annual number falls from ~55-56m (2018) to ~22m (2028). Average-number line declines steadily from ~2,100 (2018) toward ~1,070 by 2028. Source: Danish ATM deployers and networks and RBR analysis.
Narrative: Cash withdrawals in Denmark increased slightly in 2022 (lockdown restrictions removed, more in-person transactions). Next five years expected to return to the prior trend of lower cash usage. In **2028, 22 million cash withdrawals expected** — a **fall of 38% from the 2022 equivalent**. Trend driven by consumers turning to digital payment methods, diminishing cash demand; cash for small in-person transactions expected to become rarer. (Footer: Denmark: Page 18.)

### p.174 — Denmark, 6.3 continued (value of cash withdrawals)
**Figure 6.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo chart). Left axis = Annual value DKK bn (0-100); right axis = Average value DKK (0-2,500). Bars dark = historical, light = forecast. Annual value falls from ~84 DKK bn (2018) to ~46 DKK bn (2028). Average-value line rises steadily from ~1,500 DKK (2018) to ~2,100 DKK (2028). Source: Danish ATM deployers and networks and RBR analysis.
Narrative: Annual value of cash withdrawals in Denmark expected to fall to **DKK 46 billion (USD 7 billion at 2022 exchange rate) by 2028** — a **25% decrease from 2022**. Average value of a cash withdrawal expected to grow slightly to **DKK 2,106 (USD 298 at 2022 rate) in 2028**, primarily driven by inflation; as the installed ATM base contracts, Danes expected to withdraw larger amounts when they reach an ATM, especially in areas with lower ATM provision. (Footer: Denmark: Page 19.)
