### p.351 — Greece p.31. Figure 6.14a: Outsourcing of help desk, 2022
Stacked bar (In-house vs Outsourced) plus table, by ATM location.
- Branch ATMs: Outsourced 1,084 (44.3%); In-house 1,363 (55.7%).
- Off-site ATMs: Outsourced 1,239 (25.7%); In-house 3,575 (74.3%).
- Total: Outsourced 2,323 (32.0%); In-house 4,938 (68.0%); fleet = 7,261.
- Text: Most Greek deployers keep help desk in-house. Main exceptions Alpha Bank and Eurobank, which outsource for their entire fleets. Because those two have a higher share of branch ATMs than the overall base, the end-2022 share of branch ATMs with outsourced help desk (44%) is higher than for off-site machines (26%).
- Source: Greek ATM deployers and RBR analysis.

### p.354 — Greece p.34. Section 7: Market forecasts. 7.1 Forecast installed base. Figure 7.1: Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028
Table (years 2023-2028, plus CAGR):
- ATMs: 2023 = 7,400; 2024 = 7,450; 2025 = 7,400; 2026 = 7,350; 2027 = 7,300; 2028 = 7,250; CAGR -0.4%.
- Absolute growth: 2023 = 139; 2024 = 50; 2025 = -50; 2026 = -50; 2027 = -50; 2028 = -50; CAGR N/A.
- Percentage growth: 2023 = 1.9%; 2024 = 0.7%; 2025 = -0.7%; 2026 = -0.7%; 2027 = -0.7%; 2028 = -0.7%; CAGR N/A.
- ATMs per million people: 2023 = 713; 2024 = 717; 2025 = 713; 2026 = 708; 2027 = 703; 2028 = 698; CAGR -0.4%.
- Source: RBR analysis.
- Text: Greece's installed base projected to slowly decline over the forecast, contracting to 7,250 ATMs at end-2028, a density of 698 ATMs per million people — broadly stable vs 699 at end-2022. Slow contraction follows expansion in 2023 (recovery of IAD ATMs withdrawn/temporarily closed during COVID-19, returning to operation in 2023 — not new installations). Bank branch consolidation expected to reduce branch-ATM numbers; expansion of available off-site IAD/independent ATM locations to partly counteract. Off-ATM cash deployment (cashback, agent banking, retailer cash deposit) expected to grow slowly, limiting cash-withdrawal reliance and contributing to gradual fleet decline. Note: distinguishes branch ATMs (declining) vs off-site/IAD.

### p.355 — Greece p.35. Figure 7.2 (chart) "Number of ATMs, 1995-2028" + 7.2 Forecast ATM shipments / Figure 7.3

  - Shipments: 2023 = 900; 2024 = 800; 2025 = 750; 2026 = 700; 2027 = 650; 2028 = 600; CAGR -7.8%.

In 2023, installed-base growth outpaces new-installation shipments because a small number of IAD ATMs withdrawn/temporarily closed during COVID-19 return to operation (not counted as new installations).

### p.356 — Greece p.36. 7.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value. Figures 7.4 & 7.5
- **Figure 7.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo chart). Left axis = Annual number (million) 0-360; right axis = Average number [per ATM per month] 0-3,600. Bars (annual number, million, historical dark / forecast light): 2018 ≈ 270; 2019 ≈ 302 (peak); 2020 ≈ 231; 2021 ≈ 237; 2022 ≈ 248; then forecast declining gently 2023 ≈ 240 down to 2028 ≈ 227. Line (average per ATM/month): peaks 2019 ≈ 3,590, drops 2020 ≈ 2,650, then broadly flat declining to ~2,600 by 2028.
  - Text: Total number of cash withdrawals projected to shrink at annual average of 1% between 2023 and 2028, reaching 227 million at end of forecast — a decline of 8% from 2022 total. Decline slower than installed base, so average withdrawals per ATM per month falls to 2,600 in 2028 — down 9% from 2,862 in 2022.
  - Source: Greek ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
- **Figure 7.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo chart). Left axis = Annual value (EUR bn) 0-80; right axis = Average value EUR [per withdrawal] 0-280. Bars (annual value EUR bn): 2018 ≈ 52; 2019 ≈ 60; 2020 ≈ 52; 2021 ≈ 51; 2022 ≈ 55; forecast rising gently 2023 ≈ 56 to 2028 ≈ 61. Line (average value per withdrawal EUR) rises steadily from ~190 (2018) to ~270 (2028).
  - Text: Total value of cash withdrawals anticipated to increase over the forecast, annual average +2% to reach EUR 61 billion (USD 64 billion, at 2022 exchange rate) by 2028. Average value per withdrawal forecast to rise steadily over the forecast, from EUR 224 (USD 235) in 2022 to EUR 267 (USD 281, at 2022 exchange rate) by 2028, taking inflation into account.
  - Source: Greek ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
