### p.495 — Norway Page 10 — 4.4 Functionality and hardware features; Figure 4.4: ATMs and SATs with automated deposit functionality, 2022
Table (Type of deposit / ATMs / SATs):

- Automated cheque deposit: 0 / 0
- **Total: 354 / 90**
(Source: Norwegian ATM deployers and networks, RBR analysis.)
Text: Norwegian ATMs generally have limited functionality outside of cash withdrawal — multi-currency dispensing and deposit at deposit ATMs is one of the more common additional features, with 24% of machines offering this. As Norway is not part of the Eurozone, beneficial for tourists to have the option of withdrawing various currencies. The most commonly offered foreign currency at Norwegian ATMs is the euro, as well as those from other Scandinavian countries (Swedish krona / Danish krone). Few euros begin dispensing several ATPs (~ATMs) in country which offer a wide array of currencies. All eight of their machines can be found in Oslo, and the additional currencies dispensed include the US dollar, sterling, Croatian kuna, Polish zloty and Turkish lira. Dynamic currency conversion (DCC) is also common at Norwegian ATMs, with both Nokas and Nordea offering this feature at all of their terminals. Auto-deposit cheques are not available. A small number of ATMs are equipped with NFC reading technology. However, as at end-2022, NFC cash withdrawal was not offered at Norwegian ATMs, owing to a lack of software supporting this development.
The proportion of ATMs offering automated note deposit in Norway was almost unchanged at 30% during 2022. All 354 ATMs with this feature also recycle the deposited notes. Automated note deposit is offered at 48% of bank ATMs, but very few IAD ATMs deployed by IADs perform this function. The feature is most common in Eika banks' ATMs, with 67% of the group's machines able to accept deposits. There are no ATMs in Norway deployed by IADs which accept cash deposits at end-2022. There are a small number of stand-alone deposit terminals (SATs) in Norway, with the number gradually decreasing; Nordea is one of the largest SAT deployers, with 21. All SATs in Norway accept note deposits only. Several security features are common at Norwegian ATMs, the most common of which are anti-skimming devices (found in nearly all machines). Note degradation (ink-staining) technology is used at 42% of ATMs, whilst security cameras are incorporated into 27% of terminals.

### p.496 — Norway Page 11 — 4.5 Number and value of cash withdrawals; Figure 4.5: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2022
Combined bar (annual number, millions, left axis 0–40) + line (average number per ATM per month, right axis 0–2,000). Data table:
- Annual number of cash withdrawals (million): 2018 = 35; 2019 = 30; 2020 = 20; 2021 = 16; 2022 = 17; CAGR = -16.7%
- Average cash withdrawals per ATM per month: 2018 = 1,881; 2019 = 1,697; 2020 = 1,127; 2021 = 999; 2022 = 1,189; CAGR = -10.8%
- Cash withdrawals as share of ATM transactions: 2018 = 78%; 2019 = 78%; 2020 = 78%; 2021 = 78%; 2022 = 83%; CAGR = +1.5%
(Source: Norges Bank, Norwegian ATM deployers and networks, RBR analysis.)
Text: Since 2018 cash usage has rapidly declined in Norway; number of cash withdrawals falling at an annual average rate of 17%. Decline driven by widespread availability and acceptance of card and digital payment methods, as well as the 2020 launch of Norway's national in-store cash withdrawal/deposit service. Although Norwegian consumers were moving away from cash payments before 2020, this transition accelerated massively after the emergence of COVID-19; consumers encouraged to use digital and contactless payment methods due to hygiene concerns. There were also some cases of ATMs being inaccessible due to branch closures enforced by lockdowns in both 2020 and 2021. As the first year since the pandemic to be unaffected by lockdowns and social distancing restrictions, 2022 saw the number of cash withdrawals rise to 17 million, up by 7% (vs 2021). This is the first time that the number of withdrawals has increased year-on-year since 2005. Although only a small percentage of POS transactions in Norway are carried out using cash, the reopening of shops and continued reopening of public spaces resulted in increased cash usage. Cash withdrawals continued to be the most common type of ATM transaction in Norway; rose to 83% in 2022 (was 78% other years). Number of cash withdrawals were able to grow from a low base (adding 1 million to total).

Text: NCR and Diebold Nixdorf are the only two ATM manufacturers present in Norway. Suppliers' shares remained broadly stable during 2022. At end-2022 NCR made up 66% of the Norwegian installed base, with Diebold Nixdorf supplying the remaining 34% of the market. IAD Nokas is the largest customer of NCR, deploying 229 of the supplier's machines at end-2022. Nordea deploys 125 NCR terminals, making up 87% of all of its fleet, having installed some additional Diebold Nixdorf machines in 2019. Euronet is the only deployer whose fleet is comprised entirely of NCR ATMs. The most common NCR model is the SelfServ 22, which made up 30% of all NCR ATMs at end of 2022. The next most commonly reported is the SelfServ 25 model, making up 10% of NCR machines. Diebold Nixdorf is the only other supplier in the market, with 34% of the installed base. The Eika banks are exclusively Diebold Nixdorf customers, having removed their remaining NCR terminals during 2022. The banking group Sparebank 1 Alliance also predominantly deploys Diebold Nixdorf machines, with them making up 80% of their combined fleets at end-2022. The country's largest deployer Nokas accounts for 20% of Diebold Nixdorf ATMs. The most common Diebold Nixdorf model in Norway is the CS 4060, followed by the ProCash 2050xe. NCR is the largest vendor of ATMs to bank deployers and IADs; it supplies 61% of ATMs deployed by banks, compared to 75% of IADs' fleets. Diebold Nixdorf accounts for the rest of the market, supplying 39% and 25% of banks' and IADs' estates respectively.
