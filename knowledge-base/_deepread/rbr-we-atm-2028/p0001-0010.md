### p.6 — Introduction: Global ATM Intelligence Service (page ii)
Describes the service. Key points: the report & database examine the evolution of the worldwide ATM market and forecast the pattern of future deployment. The report gives a picture of current status and recent growth of the ATM market, and a view on potential development of ATM numbers and usage and expected ATM shipment levels over a **six-year forecast period**. Database includes all data appearing in charts/tables. Other service components: a regular video series; RBR's monthly research newsletter "Banking and Payments Bulletin"; ongoing access to RBR's expert analysts. No numeric market data.

### p.7 — Introduction: Structure of the report (page iii)
Methodology/scope text. Key facts:
- Research carried out between **January and May 2023**.
- Report consists of six volumes. Volume 1 = Global Analysis (commentaries and comparative tables highlighting similarities and differences by region), also includes an Annex with country-level data tables for **108 countries**. Statistics presented for **75 smaller markets**.
- Data presented for the year ending **31 December 2022**.
- Country chapters of different types: **63 primary country markets, of which 31 are "enhanced" and 32 "standard"; 45 secondary country markets**.
- All country chapters provide data and commentary on the banking industry and branches by bank, ATM installed base size and growth, ATM by deployer; levels of ATM provision (based on various density measures), and forecasts to **2028** for ATM installed base and shipments.
- Coverage of development and structure of ATM sharing arrangements, locations, facilities, hardware and security features, usage (including forecasts to 2028) and manufacturers.
- Enhanced primary chapters additionally include analysis of software, and service and maintenance.
