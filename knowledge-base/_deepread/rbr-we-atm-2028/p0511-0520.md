### p.511 — Portugal, banks (end of Section 2 prose)
Body text only, no figures/tables.
- 2017: Banco BIC rebranded to **EuroBic** after a lawsuit over likeness to Banco BIG (a Portuguese investment bank); prompted a new strategy targeting higher-income private customers and SMEs.
- Feb 2020: Spanish bank **ABANCA agreed to purchase 95% of EuroBic**.
- EuroBic had **165 branches at end-2022**, three fewer than a year earlier.
- Portugal historically high branch-to-population ratio (face-to-face preference). Branches per million inhabitants: **320 at end-2022**, down sharply from **343 at end-2021**.

### p.512 — Section 3: ATM networks, sharing and services (3.1 ATM networks)
Body text only (dense prose; many figures partly obscured by watermark).
- Number of ATMs in the Portuguese market contracted yearly 2011–2018, then grew/stabilised; small recovery, but 2022 the number of ATMs fell ~5% (with 2021 having fallen ~5%).
- Domestic interbank sharing operates via the **Multibanco** network (run by **SIBS**). Multibanco had ~700 terminals at end-2022.
- Various deployer-level network details (Caixamática/CGD, Crédito Agrícola, Chave 24, etc.) discussed; several figures within the prose are obscured by the "NCR" watermark.
- Sep 2020: SIBS bought Romcard/Supercard (formerly Wirecard Romania), thereby expanding presence in the central and eastern European acquirer market (also references PayU/PaytelI Paytell).

### p.520 — Portugal ATM features prose (deposit / SAT functionality)
Body text only, continuing functionality discussion.
- 89% of Portuguese ATMs offer cardless cash withdrawal, as well as other cardless transactions. This is done through Multibanco's mobile application called "MB WAY". Proprietary cardholders can download the app onto their smartphone, generate a token, and withdraw cash at any Multibanco ATM. The MB WAY app also allows users to send and request money, as well as use other Multibanco ATM features without their bank card by using the app to scan a QR code on the ATM screen. Crédito Agrícola's proprietary network also allows cash withdrawal via QR code.
- All Multibanco ATMs also offer the Portuguese cardholders the ability to pay bills by credit transfer to a pre-arranged payee — it is very common in Portugal for consumers to pay regular bills at Multibanco ATMs.
- 24% of ATMs in Portugal offer automated note deposit. Crédito Agrícola has the largest proportion of this type of terminal, with 900 at end-2022; ~24% of Portuguese ATMs enabled in this function. Banco favours the lobby as it offers customers enhanced security and access to banking services outside of branch hours, while also protecting the bank's machines against vandalism. The vast majority of ATMs that form part of banks' proprietary networks are installed in bank lobbies. Crédito Agrícola is the deployer with the highest percentage of its fleet located in lobbies, at 50% — with all its branch-based terminals installed in this favoured location.
- 23% of all Portuguese ATMs offer automated cheque deposit. This figure reflects the fact that cheques are still relatively widely used in Portugal, in comparison with much of Western Europe. The majority of these (ASSTs) offering cheque deposit are operated by deployers with proprietary fleets, with 238 of these also accept automated note deposits; Novo Banco also deploys a handful of ASSTs.
- 4% of ATMs in Portugal are assisted self-service terminals (ASSTs), offering assistance when an, or in person. The majority of these ASSTs correspond to Millennium bcp's proprietary fleet. Novo Banco also deploys a handful of ASSTs.
