### p.775 — Figure 6.12a: Outsourcing of first line maintenance (FLM), 2022
Stacked column chart by location:
- Branch: In-house 12,037; Outsourced 1,163
- Off-site: In-house 25,754; Outsourced 12,082
Table (FLM, ATMs outsourced / Share outsourced / ATMs in-house / Share in-house):
- Branch ATMs — 1,163 — 8.8% — 12,037 — 91.2%
- Off-site ATMs — 12,082 — 31.9% — 25,754 — 68.1%
- Total — 13,245 — 26.0% — 37,791 — 74.0%
Narrative: many UK deployers outsource FLM to the same third party doing cash replenishment. Branch ATM FLM commonly done by branch staff; retail-located ATMs may have store staff. NatWest Group outsources FLM on ATMs without an automated deposit function; deposit-ATM FLM done by branch staff to minimise downtime. Santander and Nationwide BS outsource cash replenishment but branch staff do FLM of branch ATMs.

Stacked column chart by location:
