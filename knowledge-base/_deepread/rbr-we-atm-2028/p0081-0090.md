### p.81 — Figure 6.11c: Cash replenishment providers, 2022 (pie + table)
- Pie shares: GSA 53%, In-house 42%, Loomis 4%.
- Table (Provider / ATMs / Share): GSA 4,779 (53.5%); In-house 3,792 (42.4%); Loomis 362 (4.1%); Total 8,933 (100.0%).
- GSA is the cash-replenishment/outsourcing partner for Fiserv, Bank Austria, the Raiffeisen banks, and numerous savings banks including Erste Bank. GSA is ultimately owned by the Austrian state via the central bank Österreichische Nationalbank.
- Loomis also handles cash replenishment for several savings banks, including Steiermärkische Bank und Sparkassen.

### p.82 — Figure 6.12a: Outsourcing of first line maintenance, 2022 (stacked bar Branch vs Off-site + table)
- Chart bar labels (In-house / Outsourced): Branch — In-house 5,122, Outsourced 1,114; Off-site — In-house 312, Outsourced 2,385.
- Table (ATMs outsourced / Share outsourced / ATMs in-house / Share in-house): Branch ATMs 1,114 / 17.9% / 5,122 / 82.1%; Off-site ATMs 2,385 / 88.4% / 312 / 11.6%; Total 3,499 / 39.2% / 5,434 / 60.8%.
- First line maintenance is done in-house for most branch ATMs but only ~10% of non-branch ATMs. In the co-operative sector, Raiffeisen banks and Volksbank Wien do first line maintenance in-house on almost all branch ATMs; same practice common at savings banks. A small number of individual Raiffeisen and savings banks do first line maintenance of some non-branch ATMs in-house.

### p.84 — Figure 6.12c: First line maintenance providers, 2022 (pie + table)
- Pie shares: In-house 61%, GSA 29%, Atos 9%, Diebold Nixdorf 1%.
- Table (Provider / ATMs / Share): In-house 5,434 (60.8%); GSA 2,595 (29.0%); Atos 845 (9.5%); Diebold Nixdorf 59 (0.7%); Total 8,933 (100.0%).
- Fiserv outsources first line maintenance of its ATMs to GSA, which also maintains off-site terminals of Volksbank Wien and the 3-Banken group. TSG, a subsidiary of Atos, carries out first line maintenance for Erste Bank.

### p.85 — Figure 6.13a: Outsourcing of second line maintenance, 2022 (stacked bar Branch vs Off-site + table)
- Chart bar labels (In-house / Outsourced): Branch — In-house 3,421, Outsourced 2,815; Off-site — Outsourced 2,585, In-house 112.
- Table (ATMs outsourced / Share outsourced / ATMs in-house / Share in-house): Branch ATMs 2,815 / 45.1% / 3,421 / 54.9%; Off-site ATMs 2,585 / 95.8% / 112 / 4.2%; Total 5,400 / 60.5% / 3,533 / 39.5%.
- Unlike many other Western European markets, a significant share of Austrian ATMs have second line maintenance done in-house, mainly because the Raiffeisen group has a dedicated ATM maintenance subsidiary repairing the large majority of its terminals; some savings banks also do second line maintenance in-house, including for a small number of non-branch terminals. Other deployers (Fiserv, Erste Bank, Bank Austria) outsource second line maintenance for all their ATMs.

### p.87 — Figure 6.13c: Second line maintenance providers, 2022 (pie + table)
- Pie shares: In-house 40%, NCR 22%, Atos 22%, Diebold Nixdorf 13%, KEBA 4%.
- Table (Provider / ATMs / Share): In-house 3,533 (39.5%); NCR 2,005 (22.4%); Atos 1,939 (21.7%); Diebold Nixdorf 1,134 (12.7%); KEBA 322 (3.6%); Total 8,933 (100.0%).
- Second line maintenance is in-house for the majority of Raiffeisen ATMs and at some savings banks. Atos subsidiary TSG is the outsourcing partner of Erste Bank, numerous other savings banks, and Bank Austria. ATM manufacturers NCR, Diebold Nixdorf and KEBA hold significant shares as many deployers contract the vendors for second line maintenance. Fiserv, which uses NCR-manufactured ATMs, accounts for most of NCR's share.

### p.88 — Figure 6.14a: Outsourcing of help desk, 2022 (stacked bar Branch vs Off-site + table)
- Chart bar labels (In-house / Outsourced): Branch — In-house 4,640, Outsourced 1,596; Off-site — In-house 2,260, Outsourced 437.
