### p.332 — Figure 4.5: ATMs and SATs with automated deposit functionality, 2022 + cardless commentary

Text: Cardholders can change PIN across all ATMs and ~80% of ATMs permit bill payment via credit transfer to pre-arranged payee, funds transfer to another account at the same bank, reloading prepaid cards, printing statements, and dynamic currency conversion. Share of ATMs offering automated deposit functionality grew to 44% this year (from ~1% in 2021); change is due to Euronet — who do not offer this facility in any of its terminals — accounting for a larger share of Greece's installed base end-of-2022. Cheque deposit niche feature for Greek ATMs (offered by Piraeus Bank and 2022 Piraeus Bank also has a small number of ATMs allowing envelope deposits, but these have been getting phased out). The four major bank deployers all had stand-alone deposit terminals, terminals permitting cash deposits but not allowing cash withdrawals, at end of 2022. There were a total of 2,019 such machines at end of 2022, a minor increase from end-2021 total of 2,017. Nearly half of installed-base features bank product information allowing customers to purchase financial products. Over a quarter of ATMs offer mobile phone top-up functionality, although this share is comprised entirely of Piraeus Bank's terminals and has fallen slightly this year from the bank reducing its fleet size. Piraeus Bank is the only major bank deployer to provide cardless cash withdrawals, for which customers must use a one-time PIN that is either generated through the bank's online banking platform or its mobile app.

### p.333 — Figure 4.6: Hardware and security features, 2022
Table (ATMs / share of 7,261):
- NFC reader: 3,488 (48%).
- QR code reader: 1,714 (24%).
- Touch screen: 2,261 (31%).
- Speech capability (for visually impaired): 1,353 (19%).
- Video camera for assisted self-service: 11 (0%).
- Built-in/integrated security camera: 1,311 (18%).
- Biometric identification: 0 (0%).
- Anti-skimming device: 6,726 (93%).
- Note degradation: 1,098 (15%).
Text: Almost half of Greek ATMs offer NFC reader hardware at end-2022, but contactless cash withdrawal via NFC is only enabled across 11% of Greece's ATMs. This service is also only provided by Eurobank, at ~3/4 of its fleet. QR code readers are becoming increasingly common and used to facilitate bill payments at the majority of Alpha Bank and Eurobank ATMs. Around 30% of Greek ATMs feature touch screens. Three of the four largest deployers have at least a small proportion of ATMs with touch screens, in addition to the majority of Alpha Bank's fleet. Only 16% of installed base was equipped with speech capability hardware for the visually impaired and audio guidance service. The share of ATMs incorporating speech capability hardware remains unchanged from the previous year, but the share offering the audio guidance service saw a noticeable increase, rising by three percentage points in 2022. While the majority of these are still deployed by Eurobank, Alpha Bank more than doubled the number of machines offering audio guidance at end-2022. Piraeus Bank has 11 Video Teller Station terminals located across its estate. These special terminals enable assisted self-service via video, where the seller is used by Eurobank and the National Bank of Greece while National Bank of Greece's ATMs do not feature this functionality. Anti-skimming devices have been fitted across the majority of Greek ATMs for several years. Other security measures are also becoming increasingly common; as of end-2022, 18% of ATMs are protected by integrated security cameras. Note degradation technology has also been incorporated at over half of Eurobank's ATMs and at around 40% of Alpha Bank's ATMs by end-2022. In the case of attack, ink staining renders banknotes invalid at these terminals.

### p.334 — Section 4.5 Number and value of cash withdrawals / Figure 4.7: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2022
Bar chart (annual volume, million; left axis 0-360) with overlaid line (average per ATM, thousands; right axis 0-3,600).
Table (2018 / 2019 / 2020 / 2021 / 2022 / CAGR):
- Annual number of cash withdrawals (million): 274 / 302 / 231 / 237 / 248 / -2.5%.
- Average cash withdrawals per ATM per month (thousands): 3,305 / 3,590 / 2,654 / 2,753 / 2,862 / -3.7%.
Text: Number of cash withdrawals in Greece totalled 248 million in 2022, rising by almost 5% from last year. Average cash withdrawals per ATM per month also increased, growing by just under 4% to reach 2,862. Greece's continued recovery from the COVID-19 lockdowns in 2020 and 2021 is the primary driver for this resurgence in cash usage, along with the boost in economic activity from returning inbound tourism. Despite this, the annual number of cash withdrawals is still far below the pre-pandemic total of 302 million as the economic recovery has not reversed the pandemic's lasting impact on accelerating the trend of consumer preference shifting away from cash usage and towards electronic payment methods. Average number of withdrawals per ATM rose by 4% in 2022 to reach 2,939. As with the number of cash withdrawals, however, this figure remains well below the pre-pandemic levels. While cash withdrawals are still the most frequent type of ATM transaction, its share fell at an average annual rate of 3% between 2018 and 2022, to stand at 54%. This is due to ATMs diversifying their functionality in order to mitigate the impact of reduced demand for cash; there has been an increased use of terminals for transactions previously done by tellers, such as bill payments and cash deposits.
