### p.717 — Turkey, help desk providers commentary (Turkey: Page 36)
Mostly body text (large NCR watermark). NCR and Diebold Nixdorf are by far the two largest third-party help desk providers, because Akbank outsources the service to its ATM manufacturers. Bantaş's share has fallen — partly from NCR/Diebold Nixdorf emerging as providers, and because DenizBank now provides in-house help desk for both its branch and off-site ATMs. Odea Bank is now the only deployer outsourcing help desk to Bantaş, required to unlock off-site ATMs before any first/second line maintenance; branch-based ATMs can be unlocked by branch staff themselves.

### p.718 — Turkey, Section 7 Market forecasts: forecast installed base (Turkey: Page 37)
Section 7: Market forecasts. **7.1 Forecast installed base.**
**Figure 7.1: Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028** (table):
- ATMs: 51,700 (2023), 51,400 (2024), 51,200 (2025), 51,000 (2026), 50,800 (2027), 50,700 (2028); CAGR -0.4%.
- Absolute growth: -353, -300, -200, -200, -200, -100; CAGR N/A.
- Percentage growth: -0.7%, -0.6%, -0.4%, -0.4%, -0.4%, -0.2%; N/A.
- ATMs per million people: 606, 602, 600, 598, 595, 594.
- Text: Turkish installed base forecast to contract ~3% between 2022 and 2028, to 50,700; 594 ATMs per million people in 2028 (vs 610 in 2022). Bank branches also expected to fall ~3%, so ATMs per 100 bank branches stays roughly flat to 2028 (474 vs 473 at end-2022). Turkey is a cash-heavy society; except for 2020 (COVID-19 movement restrictions), cash withdrawals have grown year-on-year. Growing banked population sustains cash/ATM demand; banks rationalising branch networks may install ATMs in areas affected by branch closures, offering banking/cash access. Some deployers see ATMs as an important part of digitalisation and branch transformation projects, indicating newer machines may also be installed in branches over the coming years.

### p.719 — Turkey, historical/forecast ATM count + forecast shipments (Turkey: Page 38)
Top text: COVID-19 led to restrictions; cash withdrawals increased year-on-year. Discusses TAM (Türkiye ATM pooling/sharing) agreement: TAM expected to expand, reaching ~20,000 ATMs by completion of members' ATM migration; main motivation was cost savings; smaller banks may join over the forecast period. Turkish ATMs are among the most sophisticated in Western Europe — automated note acceptance offered by majority at end-2022; audio guidance for visually impaired risen to 61%, expected at nearly all ATMs by 2028. ASSTs (assisted self-service terminals) becoming more common as banks reduce branches but optimise those remaining with remote tellers.

### p.720 — Turkey, forecast cash withdrawals (Turkey: Page 39)
Top text: TAM project not expected to increase ATM shipment numbers; TAM ATMs will be existing ATMs running common software, replaced with joint TAM branding; several banks continue expanding fleets.
Section **7.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value.**
**Figure 7.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo chart; left axis 0-2,500 = Annual number, million; right axis 0-4,000 = Average number per ATM; source: BKM, Turkish ATM deployers and RBR analysis). Bars rise to ~1,500m in 2018-2019, dip ~2020 (COVID), recover and climb steadily through forecast to ~2,100m by 2028. Average-per-ATM line (markers) trends upward across the period toward the upper right.
- Text: significant growth in cash withdrawals in 2022; growth sustained over forecast period. Total number of cash withdrawals in Turkey forecast to grow at +4% CAGR to reach 2.1 billion in 2028. Equates to a monthly average of 3,498 withdrawals per ATM in 2028, up from 2,620 in 2022. Despite expected growth in cardholding and digital payments, Turkey's increasingly banked population likely to sustain cash demand and ATM usage.
