### p.91 — Section 7: Market forecasts; 7.1 Forecast installed base
Country: Austria. Opens **Section 7: Market forecasts**, sub-section **7.1 Forecast installed base**.

**Figure 7.1: Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028** (Source: RBR analysis):
| Metric | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | CAGR |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ATMs | 8,975 | 9,000 | 9,025 | 9,050 | 9,050 | 9,025 | 0.1% |
| Absolute growth | 42 | 25 | 25 | 25 | 0 | -25 | N/A |
| Percentage growth | 0.5% | 0.3% | 0.3% | 0.3% | 0.0% | -0.3% | N/A |
| ATMs per million people | 1,004 | 1,007 | 1,010 | 1,012 | 1,012 | 1,010 | 0.1% |

Body text takeaways: the Austrian ATM installed base is forecast to remain broadly flat (CAGR 0.1%), peaking at 9,050 in 2026-2027 then edging down to 9,025 by 2028. IADs (independent ATM deployers) are expanding the base; much of any new growth will be off-site IAD ATMs, partly via the Euronet model. Banks continue to rationalise/consolidate on-site fleets and increasingly cooperate (shared/pooled ATM networks among bank ATMs), which some deployers have trialled. Bank ATMs are expected to feature more bullets/recyclers over the forecast period, with cash recycling and automated cheque/note deposit becoming more widespread.

### p.92 — 7.1 (chart) + 7.2 Forecast ATM shipments
Country: Austria.

**Figure 7.2: Number of ATMs, 1995-2028** (bar chart; Sources: Austrian ATM deployers, networks and data centres, and RBR analysis). Historical bars (dark blue) 1995-2022 and forecast bars (light blue) 2023-2028. Y-axis 0 to 10,000. Trend: rises steeply from ~3,800 in 1995 to ~6,000 around 2000, ~7,000 by ~2005, plateauing around 8,000-9,000 from ~2013 onward; peaks ~9,000-9,100 around 2020-2021, then flat-to-slightly-declining through the 2028 forecast (consistent with Fig 7.1's ~9,000 plateau).

| Shipments | 590 | 500 | 490 | 490 | 540 | 600 | 0.3% |

Body: ~**3,200 ATMs** forecast to ship to Austrian deployers 2023-2028, an annual average of ~530 machines. **92%** will be replacements; remainder new locations, particularly off-site ATMs deployed by IADs. New-installation shipments fall at ~23% annual average (slowdown of market growth); replacement share rises to **98% by 2028**.

### p.93 — 7.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value
Country: Austria. Sub-section **7.3**. Two charts (both Sources: Austrian ATM deployers, networks and data centres, and RBR analysis).

**Figure 7.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo chart). Left axis = Annual number, million (bars, 0-300); right axis = Average number per ATM (line, 0-3,000). Bars: 2018 ~270m and 2019 ~277m (peak), dropping to ~213m in 2020 (COVID), ~165m in 2021, ~170m 2022; then forecast bars ~178m (2023) easing to ~165m by 2028. Average-number line declines from ~2,600 (2018-2019) to ~1,500-1,550 across the forecast, ending ~1,520.

**Figure 7.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo chart). Left axis = Annual value EUR bn (bars, 0-50); right axis = Average value EUR (line, 0-300). Annual value bars: ~EUR 45bn (2018), ~46bn (2019), ~40bn (2020), ~39bn (2021), ~41bn (2022), rising across forecast to ~47bn (2023), ~48-49bn (2024-2028, ending ~49bn). Average value per withdrawal line rises steadily from ~EUR 170 (2018-2019) to ~EUR 295 by 2028.

Body takeaways: after a limited rebound in 2022, annual ATM cash withdrawals in Austria are expected to **decline further**, ~1%/yr (volume), with cash-withdrawal value broadly flat. Austrians' relatively strong preference for cash, plus the COVID-19 pandemic's accelerated migration toward cashless payment methods. ATM cash-withdrawal surcharging (trialled/introduced by some deployers) may incentivise fewer, higher-value withdrawals.
