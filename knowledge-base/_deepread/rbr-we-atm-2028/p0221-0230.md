Text: most cash replenishment performed in-house in France at end-2022. Of external service providers, Loomis and Brink's have the largest market shares. In addition to being an outsourcing partner to several deployers, Brink's also replenishes the cash of the ATMs it manages on behalf of the BPCE Group, with these ATMs falling under the "in-house" category in the chart above. CIT contracts in France can be awarded on a regional basis (rather than nationwide), so a single deployer may use several providers, each operating within a specific region — instead of refilling all of a deployer's ATMs across the country. In 2022, Worldline updated the ATM fleet of French retail group Groupe Les Mousquetaires (GLM), through the group's banking subsidiary Banque Chabrières. The migration of over 700 ATMs had been completed by February 2023, with Worldline managing GLM's entire fleet. Through this partnership, Worldline is understood to act as the cash replenishment provider for GLM's new ATMs.
