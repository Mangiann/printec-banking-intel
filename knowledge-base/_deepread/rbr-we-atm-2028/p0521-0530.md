Body text: Nearly 90% of Portuguese ATMs — including virtually all of those in the Multibanco network — offer speech capability, while touch screens are available at a third. On security: 89% of installed base (including the entire Multibanco network) is fitted with anti-skimming devices. All card readers installed on SIBS ATMs are fitted with a "jitter" mechanism (anti-skimming). As of end-2022, 63% of Portuguese ATMs have note degradation technology, up from 58% at end-2021. Physical attacks on ATMs in Portugal are now extremely rare. NFC readers became available for the first time in 2022 (837 ATMs). QR-code readers and biometric identification have yet to be implemented in Portugal.

Body text: A wide range of manufacturers present in Portuguese ATM market, with a number having entered the market in recent years. Diebold Nixdorf held a 52% market share at end-2022 — proportion that has risen year-on-year since 2018; the pattern is largely attributable to the demise of Glory, which has gradually replaced Glory with Diebold Nixdorf (the largest supplier to the entirety of the proprietary fleets of Crédito Agrícola, Banco BPI and Montepio, as well as 90% of CGD's). Glory is the largest supplier to all major deployers, with the exception of SIBS ATM Express, for which GRG is the main vendor, and Euronet, which has a fleet manufactured entirely by NCR. The most commonly reported Diebold Nixdorf model is the ProCash 2150xe.

### p.526 — Portugal, Section 5 manufacturer narrative cont. (Portugal: Page 20)
Body text only (zoomed page). Key points:
- Glory remains the second-largest manufacturer to the Multibanco network, with a number having entered the market in recent years from a 49% share in 2018, although its share of the installed base has fallen to 25% by 2022. Glory virtually all of these are in the Multibanco network. All cash recyclers used on SIBS ATMs are by Glory and fitted with a "jitter" mechanism, which is designed to prevent card cloning. For proprietary fleets and reduced anti-skimming devices. The average age of an installed ATM in Portugal is rising.
- NCR third-largest supplier, with an 8% installed-base CAGR. NCR's share of the installed base rose from 9% (2018) to 13% (2022). NCR is the sole supplier to Euronet (the country's largest independent/IAD deployer) and the second-largest supplier to Caixa Geral de Depósitos (CGD).
- Hyosung Innovue connected to RBR's Multibanco network, growing share, fast-growing manufacturer, present since 2019. GRG entered the market in 2019, deployed exclusively as part of the Multibanco network. Held a 4% share by 2022.
- Mention of Banco Montepio and the rapid entry of GRG. Sigma and OKI also referenced with small/declining presence.
- Hyosung Innovue entered the market in 2019; ATEC is a Portuguese manufacturer whose machines are deployed by SIBS ATM Express, with 130 machines by 2022. Most commonly reported Hyosung Innovue model unspecified.
- ARCA has been present in the Portuguese market since 2019. The manufacturer is reportedly exclusively by Millennium bcp as part of the bank's proprietary estate, all are the Pro Teller V2 model — a cash-recycling model with potential to enable note recycling. Around 30 ARCA terminals/units; this represents fewer than 1,500 machines from this vendor across the proprietary and Multibanco terminals deployed.
- Novo Banco began proprietary deployment of CPM terminals in 2021, while Millennium bcp installed its first proprietary Sigma machines that same year. While these banks increased their deployment of both vendors as they expanded their proprietary fleets, both Millennium bcp and Crédito Agrícola use proprietary CRM solutions for their proprietary estates.
