### p.33 — Location narrative continued (TTW, lobby, hall, drive-through)
- **TTW**: share unchanged in 2022 at 29%; number of TTW machines fell 4% in 2022, a net decrease of more than 4,000, driven by branch closures (TTW machines also typically older). TTW = 52% of Italy's total (up from 51% in 2021); also common in France (42%), Denmark (41%), Turkey (40%). TTW is the most common location type in Italy, Spain and France.
- **Lobby (extended-access)**: 25% of western Europe's ATMs (same as 2021); lobby total fell by more than 4,500 (-5%) in 2022 — the largest absolute decline at any location — mainly from branch closures in Spain, Germany, Belgium. Most prevalent in Belgium (77% of total), Germany (65%), Austria (53%); no other market above one third. Minimal presence in Greece, Turkey, UK, Finland, Ireland; now absent from the Netherlands.
- **Hall**: 7% of western European total, down from 8%. Hall ATM numbers fell in every market in 2022 except Turkey and Switzerland (Turkey actively uses the hall as well as the lobby for new self-service tech; some Swiss deployers installed new assisted self-service terminals in the hall). Branch closures removed more than 2,000 hall ATMs in France in 2022, over 600 in the UK, over 400 in Italy, more than 200 in Spain. Hall ATMs are still relatively common in Ireland (17%), the UK (12%), Italy (11%); under 5% in most of the region.
- **Drive-through**: a rarity — 65 at end-2022 (little change in recent years). Found in Germany, Switzerland, Austria, Denmark, Portugal, Italy, France, Spain, Turkey, UK. Below 1% of total in any western European market.

### p.35 — Figure 1.11: ATMs and SATs with automated deposit functionality, 2022
Table (ATMs | SATs; enhanced markets only; Source: RBR analysis):
- Automated note deposit: ATMs 153,531 | SATs 6,752
- of which note recycling: ATMs 98,378 | SATs N/A
- Automated cheque deposit: ATMs 37,647 | SATs 4,007

Narrative: ATMs with automated deposit fell 1% in 2022 to 153,542 = 43% of region's ATMs. Nearly all accept banknote deposits; almost a quarter accept cheques. 64% of automated-note-deposit ATMs also recycle notes (up from 62% a year earlier). Turkey has the highest automated-deposit penetration (96% of its ATMs). Penetration above 50% also in Belgium (58%) and Spain (54%); above 40% in Denmark, Greece, Italy, Germany. France relatively low at 29% but, given market size, has over 14,000 automated-deposit ATMs (9% of the western European total). Lowest penetration: UK at just 11% (growing IADs rarely accept note deposits). Cash-recycling deposit ATMs most common in Turkey (nearly 33,000 = 63% of country's ATMs); also high in Belgium (58%), Germany (44%), Italy (35%) — these four = 77% of western Europe's cash-recycling ATMs; lowest in Greece and UK (both 3%). Automated cheque deposit a major Italy feature: over 17,000 (39% of Italy's total); Spain over 10,000 (23%); also significant in Portugal and Ireland. SATs present in eight major markets, most numerous in France (2,817 units), UK (2,758), Greece (2,019).

### p.36 — Figure 1.12: Hardware and security features, 2022
Table (ATMs / Share; enhanced markets only; Sources: RBR analysis):
- NFC reader 68,381 (20%)
- QR code reader 52,958 (16%)
- Touch screen 146,879 (43%)
- Speech capability (for visually impaired) 186,282 (55%)

- Built-in/integrated security camera 125,824 (37%)
- Biometric identification 5,802 (2%)
- Anti-skimming device 272,036 (80%)
- Note degradation 79,838 (24%)
Narrative: across the twelve enhanced markets, cash can be withdrawn without a card at almost half of ATMs, though NFC cash withdrawal present at only 16% of terminals at end-2022. Portugal, Spain and the Netherlands have the highest cardless-cash-withdrawal proportions. Eight markets have NFC-reader ATMs offering contactless cash withdrawal — highest in Spain (62% of ATMs allow contactless withdrawal). Majority of region's ATMs allow PIN change, statement printing, mobile top-ups and funds transfers. Assisted self-service terminals present in eight of the twelve enhanced markets; 13% of Italy's ATMs offer in-person assistance. Just over half of enhanced-market ATMs had speech capability; over 40% had touch screens. NFC (20%) and QR (16%) readers still relatively uncommon but increasingly installed. Security: 80% of region's ATMs have anti-skimming devices; security cameras at 37%; note-degradation systems at almost a quarter (24%). Biometric ID rare — only Austria, Sweden, Spain, Turkey had ATMs offering it at end-2022.

Narrative: ~10 billion cash withdrawals at western European ATMs in 2022, +7% vs 9.3 billion in 2021, but still well below pre-pandemic >12 billion. Largest markets by withdrawals in 2022: Germany 2.1 billion, Turkey 1.6 billion, UK 1.3 billion, France 1.2 billion, Spain 1.1 billion — these five = 74% of region's withdrawals (up from 73% in 2021); all five rose in 2022. Fastest increase in 2022: Turkey +18% (soaring inflation + per-withdrawal cash limits drove more frequent withdrawals). Fastest decrease: Belgium -12%; Finland next at -6%; one other Nordic market declined ~ -2%. Most western European countries saw withdrawals rise as consumers returned to physical outlets post-COVID.

### p.39 — Figure 1.15: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2022
Table (primary markets only; Source: RBR analysis) + combined column/line chart (left axis USD bn 0–2,000; right axis avg USD 0–200):
- Annual value of cash withdrawals (USD bn): 1,919 / 1,825 / 1,515 / 1,644 / 1,542 — CAGR -5.3%
- Average value of a cash withdrawal (USD): 151 / 145 / 165 / 176 / 155 — CAGR +0.7%
- Average value withdrawn per ATM (USD mn): 4.8 / 4.6 / 3.9 / 4.4 / 4.3 — CAGR -2.9%
Narrative: USD 1.5 trillion withdrawn from western Europe's ATMs in 2022, down 6% from USD 1.6 trillion in 2021 — mainly due to depreciation of European currencies vs the US dollar (value often rose in local currency but fell when converted to dollars in all but one country, the Netherlands, which saw a marginal increase). Germany by far the largest market by value: German ATMs dispensed USD 363 billion in 2022; Italy USD 253 billion; Spain USD 213 billion. Average value of a cash withdrawal in 2022 = USD 155 (down from USD 176 in 2021), driven by inflation/currency depreciation and more frequent, smaller withdrawals post-pandemic. Highest average withdrawal value: Switzerland USD 386; six markets (Italy, Greece, Austria, Netherlands, Norway, Denmark) between USD 200–300; lowest: Turkey USD 72, Portugal USD 85. Average value dispensed per ATM in 2022 = USD 4.3 million; highest Switzerland USD 9.8m, Greece USD 8.1m, Germany USD 6.9m; lowest UK USD 2.8m, Portugal USD 2.7m, Turkey USD 2.3m.
