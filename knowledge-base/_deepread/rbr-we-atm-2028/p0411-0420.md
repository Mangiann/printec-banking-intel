### p.420 — Italy, Section 7: Market forecasts (Forecast installed base)

- ATMs: 43,400 / 43,000 / 42,600 / 42,200 / 41,900 / 41,600 / -0.8%

- Percentage growth: -1.0% / -0.9% / -0.9% / -0.9% / -0.7% / -0.7% / N/A
- ATMs per million people: 735 / 728 / 722 / 715 / 710 / 705 / -0.8%
Source: RBR analysis.
Body text: Italy's installed base of ATMs forecast to contract by end of 2028, at an average annual rate of ~1% (-0.8% CAGR), to reach ~41,700 (text reads ~41,700, table shows 41,600). Would imply a fall of ~25% from peak population level, down from 742 (2022). The market is forecast to continue throughout the five-year period owing to banks rationalising their branch footprints and reducing operational costs. In addition, online payments are also a big factor to consider in terms of the forecast due to the increasing importance and prevalence of this payment method. Number of branches expected to decline during the period, to ~18,900 in 2028, down from ~20,986 at end-2022, due to ongoing banking consolidation; ~3% of common ATMs found in Italy will also decrease. Deposit automation and note recycling expected to increase at ATMs during the forecast period. These functions can be seen as a way to reduce the high costs of outsourced cash handling; Cardless cash withdrawal a growing range of functionalities being requested to become more widespread. Another driver of growth in automated deposit ATMs is the growth of "cashless branches" which require advanced self-service machines to process more complex transactions. The IAD sector expected to grow at avg ~6%/yr over the forecast period, to reach ~2,400 IAD ATMs in 2028.
