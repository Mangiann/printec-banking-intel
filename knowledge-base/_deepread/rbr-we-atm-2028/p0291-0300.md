### p.291 — Germany, Section 7.2 Forecast ATM shipments + 7.3 cash-withdrawal chart
- **Section 7.2: Forecast ATM shipments.** Figure 7.3 "Forecast ATM shipments, 2023-2028" (units, source: RBR analysis):
  - Shipments total: 2023 = 4,800; 2024 = 4,700; 2025 = 4,600; 2026 = 4,500; 2027 = 4,400; 2028 = 4,300. CAGR = **-2.2%**.
  - of which new installations: 2023 = 160; 2024 = 150; 2025 = 150; 2026 = 140; 2027 = 130; 2028 = 120. CAGR ≈ -5%.
  - of which replacements: 2023 = 4,640; 2024 = 4,550; 2025 = 4,450; 2026 = 4,350; 2027 = 4,310 (approx); 2028 = 4,180. CAGR ≈ -2%.
  - Replacements as % of shipments: 97% in 2023, rising to 97% across the period (97% each year).
- Narrative: around 4,500 ATMs per year expected to ship to German deployers 2023-2028; the vast majority (~97%) are replacements of existing machines, not net new. Replacement rate ~9-year cycle. Deployers like Deutsche Bank and Commerzbank merging/refurbishing fleets reduces demand. Replacements at 97% of shipments depress the number of new ATMs.
- **Section 7.3: Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value.** Figure 7.4 "Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028" (bar = annual number [billion, left axis], line = average number per ATM [right axis]):
  - Annual number of withdrawals fell from a ~2018/19 peak (~3.2bn) to a 2020 low (~2.8bn), partial rebound 2021-2022, then declines on the forecast through 2028. Bars trend downward across 2023-2028.
  - Narrative: after falling sharply in 2020, rebounding in 2021/22, annual withdrawals forecast to decline 2023-2028; ATM cash withdrawals fell ~3,600 (avg per ATM) as deployers remove machines. Bank ATMs continue to be used more heavily than IADs.

### p.292 — Germany, Figure 7.5 Value of cash withdrawals 2018-2028
- Figure 7.5 "Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028" (bars = annual value EUR bn [left axis 0-400], line = average value EUR per withdrawal [right axis 0-200]). Source: German ATM deployers and data centres, Deutsche Bundesbank and RBR analysis.
  - Annual value (EUR bn): ~355 (2018), ~358 (2019), **287 (2020 low)**, ~315 (2021), ~342 (2022), ~352 (2023), then roughly flat-to-slightly-declining ~355-352 through 2024-2028.
  - Average value per withdrawal (EUR, line): ~158 (2018) rising gradually to ~189 by 2028.
- Narrative text: after recovering from the **2020 low of EUR 287 billion (USD 328 billion)**, the annual value of cash withdrawn from German ATMs is forecast to fall between 2023 and 2028, to **EUR 352 billion (USD 346 billion, at 2022 exchange rates) by 2028** — higher than it would have been before the rapid inflation of 2022/23. The **average value of an ATM withdrawal is forecast to fall to EUR 189 (USD 199, at 2022 exchange rates).**
