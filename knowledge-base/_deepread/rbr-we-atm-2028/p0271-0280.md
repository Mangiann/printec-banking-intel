### p.272 — Germany, average withdrawal values (Germany: Page 21)
Average value of an ATM cash withdrawal has changed little (entrenched cash habits). EUR 164 (USD 172) in 2022 — high vs many western European markets; German consumers carry considerably more cash than counterparts elsewhere.
- Co-operative banks: highest withdrawal values, averages around EUR 275 (USD 289) — customer base makes fewer but larger withdrawals.
- Most banks: average withdrawal in range EUR 150 (USD 161) to EUR 200 (USD 215). Commerzbank averaged EUR 223 (USD 234) in 2022, unchanged from 2021.
- Non-bank deployers: lower averages, typically EUR 100 (USD 107) to EUR 130 (USD 139).
- On average a German ATM dispensed EUR 6.6 mn (USD 6.9 mn) in 2022 — higher than recent years, consistent with less-heavily-used ATMs being removed from service.

- NCR: 14,500 / 14,620 / 14,113 / 13,069 / 13,089 (CAGR -2.5%)

- Hyosung Innovue: 265 / 285 / 285 / 265 / 266 (CAGR 0.1%)
- Total: 58,248 / 57,597 / 55,832 / 53,813 / 51,260 (CAGR -3.1%)
Narrative: Diebold Nixdorf supplied majority of Germany's ATMs — ~59% as of end-2022. Share fallen since 2018, primarily because Diebold Nixdorf is the main supplier to the savings and co-operative banks, which have undertaken major ATM-estate rationalisation over the last few years. Diebold Nixdorf has provided 70% of ATMs deployed by the IADs (which have removed fewer ATMs), so their strategic decisions have a significant impact on its market share. Some co-operative banks have also been replacing some older Diebold Nixdorf and NCR ATMs with cash-recycling units from Austrian manufacturer KEBA — a tendency that had already been seen for several years among commercial and savings banks. Commerzbank, for example, removed some Diebold Nixdorf ATMs in 2021 and 2022, while increasing the use of KEBA. Apart from Sparkasse (which is a co-recycle/KEBA client), all large and medium-sized deployers have at least some ATMs from Diebold Nixdorf. Its largest financial clients are Sparkasse banks (Commerzbank, HVB, ING-DiBa and Euronet, although together these account for just 11% of its installed base, which indicates the breadth of its clients in Germany). The savings banks account for 32% of the co-operative banks. Most commonly reported KEBA models are the ProCash 2000xe; CINEO models now account for the majority of the company's German installed base. The new DN Series 200 and DN Series 400 models have been deployed by co-operative and savings banks since 2020.

### p.274 — Germany, manufacturer detail (Germany: Page 23)
- NCR supplied 25% of Germany's ATMs — a share that has changed little over the past few years. With Diebold Nixdorf, some of its older bank-deployed NCR ATMs have been replaced by machines from other suppliers, but its market share has been maintained by the IADs, which have removed fewer ATMs than the bank counterparts.
- Excluding NCR's own ATM estate, its largest German customers are Deutsche Postbank, Euronet, ING-DiBa and Targobank, which together account for 35% of the company's German installed base. The co-operative banks account for 22% (savings banks for 22%).
- Most commonly reported NCR models are the SelfServ 26 (6626), SelfServ 34 (6634) and SelfServ 22 (6622). Nearly all NCR ATMs in Germany now come from the SelfServ range. A small number of SelfServ 91 (6691) Interactive Teller machines remain installed by some savings banks.
- Diebold Nixdorf is the largest supplier to Germany's banks, with 63% of this sector at end-2022, while NCR is the largest supplier to IADs, with a 68% share.
- Austrian manufacturer KEBA accounts for 14% of ATMs in Germany — a share that has increased since 2018. KEBA specialises in cash-recycling ATMs, and its main German clients are the savings and co-operative banks, which account for 43% and 22% of the company's German presence, respectively. Deutsche Bank and Commerzbank are its largest commercial bank customers, with 13% and 12% of KEBA's German installed base respectively. KEBA's clients in Germany are all banks.
- The KePlus Rilexa is the most commonly reported KEBA model in Germany, followed by the KePlus F10 and KePlus FT10.
- Two other vendors have supplied ATMs to German deployers. GRG of China has sold terminals mainly to co-operative banks, as well as a smaller number to commercial and savings banks; South Korea's Hyosung Innovue has sold ATMs to the IAD InterCard.

NDC, the protocol developed by NCR, is used by the company for its own machines, and by Deutsche Postbank, while DDC is used by some savings banks.

Narrative: ATM cash replenishment is outsourced for just over half of Germany's branch ATMs, and for virtually all non-branch ATMs. Banks that use their own staff to refill branch ATMs include Deutsche Postbank, and some of the larger savings and co-operative banks, such as Kreissparkasse Köln, Stadtsparkasse München and BBBank.
