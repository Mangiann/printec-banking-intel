### p.642 — Switzerland, Sec 4.5: Number and value of cash withdrawals
**Figure 4.7: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018–2022** (table; CAGR is 2018–22):
- Annual number of cash withdrawals (million): 289 / 280 / 185 / 169 / 170; CAGR −12.4%
- Average cash withdrawals per ATM per month: 3,355 / 3,221 / 2,152 / 2,015 / 2,123; CAGR −10.8%

Chart: bars (annual number, left axis to ~400m) decline; line (average number, right axis to 4,000) drops then flattens. Text: withdrawals fell for the 4th consecutive year in 2022 to 164 million — lowest annual total since 1997. (Note: chart table shows 170m for 2022; body text cites 164m.)
