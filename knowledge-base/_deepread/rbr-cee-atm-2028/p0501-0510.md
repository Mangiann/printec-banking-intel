### p.504 (page 2) — Banking structure narrative (text only)
- COVID-19 in 2020: GDP contracted by almost 6% in H1 2020; gold-export-dependent economy saw further GDP decline in 2021.
- 2022 economy resilient despite Russia/Ukraine war and Eurasian Union membership; real GDP grew ~7% in first months of 2022; imports grew strongly (higher oil prices + transit trade); high inflation from Russian-migrant-driven demand; strong wage growth.
- Banking sector assets: KGS 482 billion (USD 5.6 billion) at end-2022, up 34% from KGS 361 billion (USD 4.3 billion) end-2021.
- Bank branches +4 to 316 by end-2022; only KICB (+3) and Optima Bank (+1) opened new branches.

### p.506 (page 4) — Section 2: ATM networks, sharing & services + Figure 2.1
2.1 ATM networks and sharing. Interbank Processing Centre (IPC) founded 2004 by NBKR and 11 commercial banks; operates the market. Elcart is Kyrgyzstan's domestic ATM/card scheme used to promote payments and extend access to financial services, esp. low-income population. All banks issue Elcart cards (salaries, pensions, state benefits).
Figure 2.1 Domestic sharing agreements 2018-2022 (Elcart): 2018 = 1,583; 2019 = 1,712; 2020 = 1,856; 2021 = 1,919; 2022 = 2,061; CAGR = 6.8%. (Counts of ATMs in the Elcart sharing agreement.)
Text: Elcart participants began opening card-acceptance networks in 2019 (accepting Russian Mir cards); from 2020 most/all ATMs accepted Mir; Elcart cards accepted outside Kyrgyzstan for the first time in Russia's Mir network. In 2022 acceptance grew; Russia-Ukraine war pressures in 2022; deployers Dos-Kredobank, Kompanion Bank, Bakai Bank, KICB, Capital Bank, FinanceCreditBank, DemirBank, Kompanion Bank operated "internal friendly" ATM networks as of mid-2022.

### p.509 (page 7) — Section 3: Current ATM market + Figure 3.1 Number and growth of ATMs, 2018-2022
Figure 3.1 — combined column (ATMs, left axis 0-2,500) + line (growth %, right axis 0-15%) chart. Underlying data table:
- ATMs: 2018 = 1,583; 2019 = 1,712; 2020 = 1,856; 2021 = 1,919; 2022 = 2,061; CAGR = 6.8%
- Absolute growth: 2018 = 170; 2019 = 129; 2020 = 144; 2021 = 63; 2022 = 142; CAGR = N/A
- Percentage growth: 2018 = 12.0%; 2019 = 8.1%; 2020 = 8.4%; 2021 = 3.4%; 2022 = 7.4%; CAGR = N/A
Chart takeaway: installed base rose steadily 1,583→2,061 over 2018-2022; growth-rate line peaked ~12% in 2018, dipped to ~8% in 2019-2020, fell to a low ~3.4% in 2021, then rebounded to 7.4% in 2022. Average ~7% p.a. over the period.
Text: expansion led by cash-handling banks; growth via shared/agreed networks; cards mainly for salary/pension/benefit disbursement, plus growing promotion of cards. Recovery 2022 from COVID-19 lull. Continued political instability and reliability concerns; cash demand dampened by introduction/expansion of contactless cards and growth of mobile/QR payments. Mentions Apple Pay, Google Pay (Nov 2022), Commercial Bank rolled out contactless cash withdrawals at ATMs (2022); growing P2P/mobile-wallet interest slowly undermining cash demand.
