### p.155 — 6.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value (Figures 6.4 & 6.5)
**6.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value.**
**Figure 6.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo chart). Bars = Annual number, million (left axis 0-200); line = Average number per ATM (right axis 0-3,000). Bars: 2018 ~183, 2019 ~177, 2020 ~142, 2021 ~140, 2022 ~148, then forecast declining gently to ~128 by 2028. Average-number line declines from ~2,800 (2018) to ~1,800 (2028). Sources: Czech ATM deployers, SBK and RBR analysis.
Text: Number of cash withdrawals expected to decline slowly but steadily, average rate ~3% per year between 2023 and 2028; withdrawal numbers forecast to fall to **128 million by 2028, compared to 148 million in 2022.** Average number of withdrawals per ATM per month also expected to drop, declining from **2,187 in 2022 to 1,787 by 2028.** Number of ATMs set to increase, so withdrawal levels spread over a greater number of ATMs (fewer transactions per machine).
**Figure 6.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo chart). Bars = Annual value CZK bn (left axis 0-1,000); line = Average value CZK per withdrawal (right axis 0-7,500). Annual value bars ~700 (2018) rising through forecast to ~900+ by 2028; average-value line rises steadily from ~3,000 (2018) to ~6,500-7,000 (2028).
Text: Total annual value of cash withdrawals expected to rise in early forecast period, primarily the result of high inflation in the Czech Republic driving up the average withdrawal sum. Growth set to slow mid-forecast as the decline in withdrawal volumes offsets the increase.
