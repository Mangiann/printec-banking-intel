### p.591 — Uzbekistan, Section 2: ATM networks, sharing and services (body text, no figures)
- ATM fleet first installed in early 1990s; slow growth until ~2016, then a steep increase from ~2016. Network did not occur until 2016.
- Two domestic payment systems: Uzcard and Humo, both state-owned (Uzcard under Unified Nationwide Processing Centre / UZPC; Humo a more recent system from CBU). Cards interoperable / interchangeable; both used for cash withdrawals/transfers. Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay also present.
- Self-service ATMs and full-function (deposit-taking) ATMs both in market. Uzcard and Humo work with ATMs of all banks independently.
- International schemes (Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay) cards can be used at most ATMs. Cards issued in Uzbekistan now mostly co-badged.
- Independent ATM deployers (IADs): payments via exchange with an operator, one-time deposit per ATM, fee per cash withdrawal. ATM-as-a-Service exists. Sber/Russian banks exited after sanctions following Russia's invasion of Ukraine in 2022.
- Cash withdrawals abroad with Russian-issued cards restricted/banned (2022). Sberbank Uzbekistan rebranded; Yangiumamabank and Mavaqdaxonbank changed names; name to Garant Bank in June 2022.

### p.596 — Uzbekistan (text continuation of cash-withdrawal value)
- Growth in cash-withdrawal value continues to surpass that of installed base: average **UZS 14 billion (USD 1.2 million) withdrawn per ATM in 2022**, vs UZS 10 billion (USD 0.9 million) in 2021.
- Cash withdrawals an ever-higher share of GDP: **accounting for 13% of USD GDP in 2022.**

### p.597 — Uzbekistan, Section 4: Manufacturer market shares (body text, no figures)
- As of end-2022, three main ATM suppliers in Uzbekistan. **NCR is the largest supplier**, machines used by most deployers; largest clients include Agrobank, Xalq Bank, Kapitalbank.
- Multi-vendor procurement common; several deployers buy from another vendor in addition to NCR.
- **Diebold Nixdorf** frequently installed; clients include Ipak Yuli Bank, Asaka Bank, Aloqabank.
- **GRG** machines also in use; clients include Ipoteka-Bank, InFinBank, Mikrokreditbank.
