### p.232 — Poland: management/monitoring (cont.) & remote software, 2022 (internal Poland p.20)
Body: Euronet's Aravo solution was present on one third of Polish ATMs, corresponding to Euronet's terminals. The second-most common third-party provider of management/monitoring software was Diebold Nixdorf, with its ProView solution installed on ~19% of Polish terminals, including those deployed by Planet Cash and Bank Pekao. Fiserv's Device Manager exclusively installed on Bank Millennium's ATMs, while FIS's Core was used and manage and monitor terminals deployed by Santander Bank Polska. Visiona represented a negligible share of the installed base. 39% of Polish ATMs used proprietary management/monitoring solutions on their terminals, including those deployed by Planet Cash, PKO BP and SGB IPS.

Body: The Polish ATM market is supplied by two third-party providers of remote software solutions: Diebold Nixdorf and SBIS. Most ATMs in Poland use proprietary software, including Euronet's, PKO BP's and Bank Pekao's terminals. Additionally, 5% of ATMs in Poland do not use such software at all, as is the case with the majority of ATMs deployed by SGB IPS.

### p.235 — Poland: outsourcing of cash replenishment 2018-2022 (internal Poland p.23)
Body: vast majority of Polish ATMs outsource cash replenishment, covering 88% of off-site terminals and 67% of branch-based ATMs. Cash replenishment performed in-house for all PKO BP and KBS ATMs, as well as Bank Millennium's branch-based machines.

**Figure 6.11b: Outsourcing of cash replenishment, 2018-2022** (100% stacked bar + table)
Stacked-bar share labels (In-house top / Outsourced bottom): 2018 = 30%/70%; 2019 = 27%/73%; 2020 = 21%/79%; 2021 = 21%/79%; 2022 = 21%/79%.
Table (ATM counts; CAGR is 2018-2022):
- In-house — 2018: 6,823 — 2019: 6,104 — 2020: 4,666 — 2021: 4,550 — 2022: 4,496 — CAGR -9.9%
- Outsourced — 2018: 16,056 — 2019: 16,598 — 2020: 17,163 — 2021: 16,846 — 2022: 16,784 — CAGR 1.1%
- Total — 2018: 22,879 — 2019: 22,704 — 2020: 21,829 — 2021: 21,396 — 2022: 21,280 — CAGR -1.8%
Body: proportion of terminals that outsource cash replenishment has grown steadily in recent years, with around 16,800 outsourcing this service to third parties at end-2022, representing 79% of all ATMs. Many deployers find it convenient to outsource cash replenishment, as well as Bank Millennium's branch-based employees to focus on customer-facing services instead. Branch decline (which has been taking place in the market since 2011) has also contributed to the rise in the share of outsourced terminals. A particularly high number of branches (1,200) were closed in 2020 alone. As a result, the share of off-site terminals has been gaining share, and such ATMs are typically outsourced to third parties for cash replenishment. Between 2018 and 2022, the share of ATMs that had their cash replenished in-house declined from 30% to 21%.

### p.238 — Poland: first line maintenance providers, 2022 (internal Poland p.26)
Body: share of ATMs which have FLM outsourced to a third party has been on the rise in recent years. There was a spike in 2019, when the share of outsourced ATMs for FLM went up by eight percentage points. This was largely due to ATM migration, as some deployers which previously performed FLM in-house sold their terminals to IADs, which typically outsource this function. In 2020, the share of outsourced ATMs for FLM saw a significant jump of five percentage points, to stand at 83%. This can largely be attributed to widescale branch closures and the contraction of the installed base in that year. In 2021, ATMs which outsourced FLM increased by a further one percentage point to 84%, with this share remaining unchanged in 2022.
