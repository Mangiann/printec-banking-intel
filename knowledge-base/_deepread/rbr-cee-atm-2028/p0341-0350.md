### p.341 — Serbia, Section 4.4 Functionality and hardware features (footer: Serbia Page 9)
Body text on Serbian ATM features:
- The exterior wall of the bank branch (TTW = through-the-wall) is the most common location type for Serbian ATMs, accounting for **half** of the installed base at end-2022. Among Serbia's largest deployers, UniCredit Bank and NLB Komercijalna banka have the largest fleets of TTW ATMs, with **80%** and **73%** of their fleets respectively deployed in this location. Almost all (**93%**) of Erste Bank's ATMs are installed through-the-wall.
- The most common location for ATMs is off-site, at offers convenient access to cash across the country. With a relatively consolidated branch network, AIK banka has most/half of its ATMs installed remotely; in 2022, around 64% of the CEE region ATMs were deployed remotely. The IAD sector will show some of the growth in offsite deployment, with the number of IAD ATMs expected to increase by **75%** between 2022 and 2028.
- Second most common location for ATMs is off-site, as it offers convenient access to cash across the country. With a relatively consolidated branch network, AIK banka has half of its ATMs installed remotely. Overall, the share of off-site ATMs has increased since 2018 as a result of branch closures/de-site installations.
- The number of lobby ATMs has increased year-on-year since 2018 as more banks have added 24-hour self-service areas where customers can make a range of transactions of TTW ATMs with 80% and 73% of their fleets respectively deployed in this location. Almost all (93%) of Erste Bank's ATMs are installed through the wall and self-service areas (lobby). The number of IAD ATMs is expected to increase by 75% between 2022 and 2028.
- **Figure 4.4: ATMs and SATs with automated deposit functionality, 2022** (table). Type of deposit | ATMs | SATs. Automated note deposit = 963 ATMs / 88 SATs; of which note recycling = 40 / n/a; Automated cheque deposit = 0 / 0; Total = 963 / 88. (Sources: Serbian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.)
- Around three-quarters of Serbian ATMs offer PIN change, while around half offer the ability to make deposits. The largest specific deployers only: Banca Intesa and AIK banka deploy stand-alone deposit terminals (SATs) which can be used for larger cash deposits by business customers.
- **60%** of Serbian ATMs have built-in security cameras while **50%** have anti-skimming devices.
- Dynamic currency conversion (DCC) is available at around a quarter of Serbian ATMs.

### p.342 — Serbia, Section 4.5 Number and value of cash withdrawals (footer: Serbia Page 10)
- The availability of ATMs with automated note deposit functionality has increased significantly in recent years, with this facility offered at 13% of ATMs at end-2022, up from 8% in 2021. NLB Komercijalna banka is now the largest deployer of automated-deposit ATMs and is currently rolling out the facility at all its installations, as part of a full estate upgrade. Banca Intesa, Eurobank Direktna and AIK banka all increased deployment of deposit ATMs in 2022 with many such units installed in 24/7 self-service zones.
- Banca Intesa and AIK banka also deploy stand-alone deposit terminals (SATs) which can be used for larger cash deposits by business customers.
- NFC and/or QR code readers are installed on 16% and 13% of ATMs respectively.
- 60% of Serbian ATMs have built-in security cameras while 50% have anti-skimming devices.

- The average number of withdrawals per ATM per month also increased to 4% in 2022, surpassing 2,734 for the first time. Deployers are increasing transaction volumes as the mean share of growth in ATM deployment. Banca Intesa was one of the largest in the installed base shows. Overall, 87% of deposits will be for replacement units between 2023 and 2028.
- The share of ATM transactions made up of cash withdrawals declined in 2022 as customers increasingly take advantage of additional ATM features. The number of automated cash deposits at ATMs in Serbia is expanding rapidly and banks have sought to migrate more deposits from the teller to terminals.

### p.345 — Serbia, Section 6 Market forecasts / 6.1 Forecast installed base (footer: Serbia Page 13)
- **Figure 6.1: Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028** (table):
  - ATMs: 2023 = 3,150; 2024 = 3,200; 2025 = 3,250; 2026 = 3,300; 2027 = 3,325; 2028 = 3,350; CAGR 1.2%.
  - Absolute growth: 79 / 50 / 50 / 50 / 25 / 25; N/A.
  - Percentage growth: 2.6% / 1.6% / 1.6% / 1.5% / 0.8% / 0.8%; N/A.
  - ATMs per million people: 436 / 443 / 450 / 457 / 460 / 464; CAGR 1.2%.
  (Source: RBR analysis.)
- After several years of growth, the number of ATMs in Serbia declined in 2022 as a result of estate consolidation following numerous bank mergers in 2021 and 2022. Growth in deployment is, however, expected to resume in 2023 with the conclusion of rationalisation programmes and estate expansion planned by many of Serbia's deployers. Rising demand for ATM services is considered a key market driver, with the number of withdrawals and other transactions forecast to continue increasing between 2023 and 2028 as Serbia remains a cash-heavy economy.
- The installed base is forecast to grow to **3,350 units by 2028**, representing an overall increase of **9%** from 2022. This would assume a density of **464 ATMs per million people**, up from 425 in 2022, based on the 2022 population figures. Despite the increase in density, Serbia will continue to rank below the CEE average for ATMs per million people.
- Deployment of ATMs relative to branches will also increase, reaching **264 ATMs per 100 outlets in 2028**, compared with 219 in 2022, as many Serbian banks continue to rationalise their branch networks while expanding ATM access as they seek to boost usage of self-service technology. Through-the-wall (TTW) is expected to remain the most common location type; however, the number of ATMs installed in such sites will decrease in line with branch closures. By contrast, lobby installations are expected to increase as banks invest in 24/7 self-service areas to meet rising demand for cash withdrawals and deposits outside of typical branch working hours.

### p.346 — Serbia, Section 6.2 Forecast ATM shipments (footer: Serbia Page 14)
- Body text: There is also ample potential for off-site ATM deployment to grow in Serbia — just 37% of ATMs in the country were deployed remotely in 2022 versus a share of 64% for the CEE region as a whole. The IAD sector will show some of the growth in offsite deployment, with the number of IAD ATMs expected to increase by 75% between 2022 and 2028.
- The functionality of ATMs is predicted to broaden significantly over the forecast period, with many large and mid-sized deployers investing in automated deposit ATMs. The number and value of automated cash deposits at Serbian ATMs increased by 41% and 67% respectively in 2022 and local banks recognise the benefits of the functionality for both the customer and the organisation as cash remains widely used. NLB Komercijalna banka became the first major Serbian bank to commit to offering automated deposits at all its ATM installations in 2022.
- Hardware features such as QR code and NFC readers which enable contactless and cardless transactions are also expected to become more widely installed as ATM functionalities are adapted to increasing digitalisation and adoption of mobile banking.

  - of which new installations: 79 / 50 / 50 / 50 / 25 / 25; CAGR -20.6%.

  - Replacements as % of shipments: 80% / 88% / 86% / 86% / 92% / 92%; CAGR 2.7%.

- A total of 2,100 ATMs are forecast to be shipped to Serbia between 2023 and 2028, equating to an average of 350 units per year. The share of shipments for replacements relative to new installations is projected to rise over the forecast period as growth in the installed base slows. Overall, 87% of shipments will be for replacement units between 2023 and 2028.

### p.347 — Serbia, Section 6.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value (footer: Serbia Page 15)
- **Figure 6.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (bar = annual number million [left axis 0-120], line = average number per ATM per month [right axis 0-3,000]). Annual number bars rise gradually to ~112 million by 2028; per-ATM line roughly flat/slightly rising near 2,800. (Sources: Serbian ATM deployers, NBS and RBR analysis.)
- The number of ATM cash withdrawals is forecast to grow by an average of **1% annually between 2023 and 2028, reaching 112 million transactions by 2028**. Despite projected growth in card and digital payments, Serbian consumers will continue to rely heavily on cash for everyday payments, necessitating regular ATM usage. With ATM deployment set to increase to meet additional demand, the average number of withdrawals per ATM per month is expected to **stabilise at around 2,800 by 2028**.
- **Figure 6.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (bar = annual value RSD bn [left axis 0-2,500], line = average value RSD [right axis 0-25,000]; both rising). Annual value rises to RSD ~2,100 bn area by 2028; average value line rises toward ~18,900. (Sources: Serbian ATM deployers, NBS and RBR analysis.)
- As the number of cash withdrawals rises, the total value of cash withdrawn per year is also expected to increase, at an average of **6% annually between 2022 and 2027, to reach RSD 2.1 trillion (USD 19 billion)** at the 2022 exchange rate. The average value of a cash withdrawal is also projected to rise to **RSD 18,900 (USD 170) in 2028**.
