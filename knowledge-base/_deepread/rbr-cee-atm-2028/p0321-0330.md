### p.321 — Russia: ATM/branch network outlook + Figure 7.2 (Number of ATMs, 1995-2028)
Body text: card-funded mobile payments have been heavily influenced by the continued rise of cashless payments, driven by Faster Payments System (FPS) launched 2019 and the introduction of the FPS itself; cashless transfers expanded. Notes that domestic manufacturers may take time to fill the market gap left by Western vendors that exited; ATM upgrade/replacement plans continue. Discusses deposit automation, cashless functionality, NFC/contactless adoption, and branch rationalisation: largest banks are reducing branch numbers; banks expected to operate ~**26,000 branches by end of 2027, 3,000 fewer than end of 2022**. ATM market expected to fall to ~**26,000 branches by end of 2027** (network rationalisation theme).

### p.322 — Russia §7.2 Forecast ATM shipments + Figure 7.3 (table) + Figure 7.4 (cash withdrawals chart)
Body: domestically manufactured terminals expected to gradually fill the market gap; from 2023 several major Russian technology firms plan to produce their own ATMs. **In total, 48,200 ATMs are expected to be shipped to Russia between 2023 and 2028**, an average of ~**8,000 each year**; the vast majority will be replacements (~**99% by end of the forecast period**). Russian installed base is shrinking; ATM shipments will primarily replace old ATMs. Deployers expected to make new installations of between **150 and 200 ATMs each year**; absolute installed-base growth will be negative as rationalisation by the largest deployers offsets expansion by others.

- **Figure 7.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo bar+line; sources CBRF, Russian ATM deployers and RBR analysis). ### p.323 — Russia §7.3 Forecast ATM cash-withdrawal number and value + Figure 7.5 (Value of cash withdrawals)
Body: number of cash withdrawals forecast to continue declining, falling at an average annual rate of **2% between 2023 and 2028**. By end of period, annual total of cash withdrawals expected to be ~**1.8 billion, down from 2.1 billion in 2022**. Average number of cash withdrawals per ATM per month also expected to decline, standing at **1,481 by 2028 vs 1,518 in 2022**. Weakening cash-withdrawal levels driven primarily by generally declining cash usage in Russia as consumers shift to digital and mobile payments at a particularly fast rate.
- **Figure 7.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (combo bar+line; sources CBRF, Russian ATM deployers and RBR analysis). Left axis "Annual value RUB [left axis]" 0-45,000 (gridlines 9,000); right axis "Average value RUB [right axis]" 0-25,000 (gridlines 5,000). Annual-value bars (dark historic, light forecast) roughly flat-to-slightly-rising around ~28,000-33,000 RUB bn across 2018-2028. Average-value line (diamonds, right axis) rises steadily from ~9,000 (2018) to ~20,000 (2028). Body note (continues onto p.323): unlike the number of withdrawals, the value is expected to rise over the forecast period (~2% each year), as inflation and changing customer habits drive up individual withdrawal sizes; value of a withdrawal forecast to reach ~RUB 19,600 (USD 322) by the end of the period (2028, vs ~RUB 13,600/USD 523 in 2022). Customers are, to some extent, off-setting reduced ATM usage by taking out larger sums each visit, partly motivated by the desire to maintain a cash contingency during turbulent economic times.
