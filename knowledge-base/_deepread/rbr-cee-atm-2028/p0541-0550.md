### p.547 — Figure 3.3 ATMs by location, 2018-2022 (Moldova page 5)
Stacked bar chart (off-site % shown on top of branch-based %): branch-based share 40% (2018) / 39% (2019) / 37% (2020) / 35% (2021) / 33% (2022); off-site share 60% / 61% / 63% / 65% / 67%.
Table (Location | 2018 | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | CAGR):
- Branch-based: 456 | 448 | 420 | 415 | 408 | -2.7%
- Off-site: 682 | 689 | 714 | 786 | 827 | +4.9%
- Total: 1,138 | 1,137 | 1,134 | 1,201 | 1,235 | +2.1%
Sources: Moldovan ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Text: Off-site ATMs account for a growing majority (67%) of Moldovan ATMs, with most common locations including high-footfall areas such as shopping centres, medical centres and supermarkets. It is common in Moldova, as in many other ex-Soviet markets, for businesses to make agreements with banks to issue salary cards to their employees, with larger businesses often requesting that an ATM be deployed at their premises as part of the contract. Some, but not all, such ATMs are accessible to the public. 33% of ATMs are located in bank branches, with terminals including those installed through the exterior wall of the branch (TTW) and those installed in lobbies and halls. Some Moldovan banks operate a number of fully automated branches, where most transactions are only possible via self-service channels; customers can carry out routine activities such as cash withdrawals and deposits, credit transfers and bill payments without the involvement of staff. The number of off-site ATMs increased during 2022 due to expansion of BPay's network and the replacement of some closed bank branches with ATMs. Off-site ATMs are not evenly spread across the country, with most being installed in urban areas. The capital city of Chisinau is the most well-served locality in terms of access to ATMs, accounting for almost half of Moldovan installations, although the city is home to only 20% of the population.

Table (| 2018 | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | CAGR):
- Annual value of cash withdrawals — MDL bn: 46 | 52 | 54 | 68 | 81 | 15.4%

- Average value of a cash withdrawal — MDL: 1,706 | 1,848 | 2,091 | 2,377 | 2,654 | 11.7%
- Average value of a cash withdrawal — USD: 102 | 105 | 121 | 134 | 140 | 8.4%
- Average value withdrawn per ATM — MDL mn: 41 | 46 | 47 | 58 | 66 | 13.0%
- Average value withdrawn per ATM — USD mn: 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 9.7%
- Cash withdrawal value as a proportion of GDP: 24.2% | 25.3% | 27.0% | 28.1% | 29.7% | 5.3%
Sources: BNM, Moldovan ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Text: Total value of cash withdrawals at Moldovan ATMs rose significantly during 2022, to MDL 81 billion (USD 4 billion) up from MDL 68 billion (USD 4 billion) in 2021. This is largely attributed to high rates of inflation affecting consumer prices during 2022, with the annual value of withdrawals increasing by ~19% in 2022 (compared to ~7% the prior). Demand for cash also increased due to the issuing of prepaid cards allowing free ATM withdrawals to Ukrainian refugees in Moldova. These were issued as part of UNHCR cash assistance to refugees following the Russian invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. The average value of a withdrawal increased from MDL 2,377 (USD 134) in 2021 to MDL 2,654 (USD 140) in 2022. The average value withdrawn per ATM rose by 14% during the year.
