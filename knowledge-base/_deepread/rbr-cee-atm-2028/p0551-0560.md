### p.559 — North Macedonia, Section 3.3: Number and value of cash withdrawals
Text: ATMs per million people sat at 484 end-2022, rising from 466 end-2021. ATMs per 100 bank branches was 259, up from 250 end-2021, as number of branches declined while number of ATMs increased.
Figure 3.3 (Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2022) — bars (annual number, million, left axis) + line (average number, right axis):
- Annual number of cash withdrawals (million): 2018 28 / 2019 28 / 2020 25 / 2021 28 / 2022 29; CAGR 1.0%
- Average cash withdrawals per ATM per month: 2018 2,249 / 2019 2,265 / 2020 2,065 / 2021 2,344 / 2022 2,426; CAGR 1.9% (Source: NBRM and RBR analysis)
Text: Annual number of cash withdrawals fluctuated over the five years; between 2018-2019 stayed roughly constant; decreased 2020 due to COVID-19 pandemic; in 2021 recovered to pre-pandemic numbers and increased further in 2022. Average number of withdrawals per ATM per month increased for most of the period; 2020 the only year with a fall. Average growth 2018-2022 = 2%; per-ATM monthly average reached 2,426 end-2022.
