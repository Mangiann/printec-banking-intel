### p.12 — Methodology (Introduction: Page viii)
Describes how data were gathered: statistical information obtained via the RBR survey carried out **between January and May 2023**, sent to major banks and independent ATM deployers. RBR complemented survey responses with other sources incl. previous ATM surveys, network operators, central banks and existing market knowledge. Country populations sourced from the UN; country area from the CIA World Factbook; GDP from the IMF. Notes the right to round figures. "Analysis and forecasting": variety of sources used for analysis incl. forecasting; analysis of each country market drawn on questionnaires, RBR market knowledge, macroeconomics and broader market landscape, and local media. ATM density and usage levels calculated on a cross-checks-for-forecasts basis. No numeric tables.

### p.13 — Terminology (Introduction: Page ix)
Definitions only, no data. ATM = automated teller machine (self-service, dispenses cash; may also offer deposit, transaction enquiry, printed statement, account-to-account transfer, bill payment). Independent ATM deployer (IAD) = non-bank operator. Sharing arrangements: most ATMs shared either via a network managed by an interbank organisation or bilateral/multilateral agreements; proprietary ATMs are only those of the deployer's own cardholders, not shared. Branches: total number of branches excludes post offices and other banking offices unless otherwise stated. Usage patterns: average monthly number of transactions per machine; average value of a cash withdrawal. ATM location types defined: Hall (within bank branch, accessible outside hours), Lobby (within branch, accessible normal hours), Through-the-wall (TTW, exterior of branch wall), Off-site (away from branch, e.g. shopping centre/railway station), Drive-through (accessible from a vehicle).

- Population (million): 413 | rank 5 | World 7,964

- Cash withdrawals per ATM per month: 1,795 | rank 5 | World 1,955
(Source: RBR analysis)
**Body text:** At end-2022 there were 236,507 ATMs deployed in CEE, a decrease of 4% from the prior year. ATMs per million people fell to 573 (from 592) but remained well above the global average of 380. CEE has a higher ATMs-per-100-bank-branches ratio (341 vs global 305) and a larger share of off-site ATMs — 64% of CEE ATMs are off-site vs the global 53%. CEE ATM deployment began relatively late, with significant growth not seen until the 1990s; the regional installed base grew by >1,000 for the first time in 1995, peaking in 2008 with an increase of ~36,000 ATMs (+28%); subsequent growth slower, partly due to the global financial crisis. In 2014 the regional installed base declined for the first time, followed by two further years of contraction largely due to political/economic instability in Russia and Ukraine (the two largest markets). Returned to growth in 2018 and 2019, then declined again in 2020 and 2021 (largely COVID-19 impact). Since 2022 the region has been significantly affected by the war in Ukraine, contributing to large-scale ATM removals in Russia and Ukraine and disruption to neighbouring banking/financial sectors.
