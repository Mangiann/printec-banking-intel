### p.71 — Belarus 4.5 Number and value of cash withdrawals (numbers)
Figure 4.5 "Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2022" (bar = annual number million [left axis 0-250]; line = average number [right axis 0-5,000]). Both bars and the average-per-ATM line trend down over 2018-2022.
Table (2018 / 2019 / 2020 / 2021 / 2022 / CAGR):
- Annual number of cash withdrawals (million): 213 / 205 / 179 / 178 / 153 / **-7.9%**
- Average cash withdrawals per ATM per month: 4,118 / 4,001 / 3,439 / 3,366 / 2,948 / **-8.0%**
- Cash withdrawals as share of ATM transactions: 85% / 81% / 81% / 81% / 88% / +1.0%
Sources: NBRB, Belarusian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Body text: cash withdrawals fell at avg -8% pa 2018-2022; sharpest drop ~13% due to COVID-19 (2020); a slight rise in 2022 (the annual number rose ~1% as sanctions limited spending). Cash withdrawals remained ~81% of ATM transactions and rose to 88% in 2022 — cash still the most-used payment method, ATM reliance increasing.

### p.76 — Belarus 6.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value
Figure 6.4 "Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028" (bar = annual number million [left axis 0-250]; line = average number [right axis ~0-4,500]). Both decline across forecast — annual number falls and average per ATM per month declines.
Body (text only, no new table): pre-pandemic cash-withdrawal levels not expected to return as customers/businesses move to cashless. Annual number of cash withdrawals forecast to decline reaching 141 million in 2028 (per Figure 6.6). International sanctions targeting Belarusian banking sector cut access to foreign currencies; with cards reintroduced (e.g. via foreign card schemes leaving), Mir/Mastercard reintroduced cards in 2022; sanctions, Visa & Mastercard exit (March 2022) cited. NBRB regulation enforcing cash-withdrawal facilities in retail outlets in Belarus will play a significant role in reducing the fall in ATM-based withdrawals.

### p.77 — Belarus Figure 6.5 Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028 (forecast chart)
Figure 6.5 (bar = annual value BYN bn [left axis 0-50]; line = average value BYN [right axis 0-350]). Annual value bars rise from ~30 (2018) to ~47 (2028); forecast bars (2023-2028, light blue) ~39→47. Average-value line climbs steadily from ~140 (2018) to ~330 (2028). Sources: Belarusian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Body: annual value of cash withdrawals forecast to increase from BYN 36 billion (USD 13 billion) in 2022 to BYN 44 billion (USD 16 billion, at 2022 exchange rate) in 2028, despite the falling number. First half of forecast: faster growth in average value, boosted by high inflation (global pressures + international sanctions); latter half slower as instability settles. Annual value grows at avg **+4% pa 2023-2028**. Average value of a cash withdrawal forecast to rise from BYN 237 (USD 84) in 2022 to **BYN 315 (USD 111, at 2022 exchange rate) in 2028**.
