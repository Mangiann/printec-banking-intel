Source: RBR analysis.
Narrative: there were 25 banks operating in Azerbaijan at end-2022, 23 of which are private and one state-owned (+1 foreign-capital). Central Bank of Azerbaijan (CBAR) is the financial-sector supervisor, although this role temporarily belonged to the Financial Markets Supervisory Authority (FIMSA) between 2016 and 2019 before its dissolution. Bank consolidation has reduced the number of banks; some banks had licences revoked; deposit insurance (Azerbaijan Deposit Insurance Fund) referenced. GDP context: GDP contracted ~12% in 2020 due to COVID-19's effect on unemployment and oil prices, then a particularly significant impact on the Azerbaijani economy because of its reliance on energy/oil revenue; recovery of oil prices following year (Russia/Ukraine war / higher energy prices). GDP rebounded; elevated energy prices from Russia's invasion of Ukraine kept the Azerbaijani economy robust. Banking-sector total assets ~AZN 47 billion (USD 28 billion), up ~20% from 2021 levels (AZN 38 billion / USD 23 billion at end-2021).
