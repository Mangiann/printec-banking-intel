- of which new installations: 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 10 | 0.0%
- of which replacements: 34 | 45 | 65 | 105 | 135 | 145 | 33.7%
- Replacements as % of shipments: 77% | 82% | 87% | 91% | 93% | 94% | 3.9%
Source: RBR analysis.
Text: Approx 590 ATMs expected to be shipped to Latvia over the forecast period, average 98 per year. Replacements make up the majority of shipments; small number of new installations expected each year as banks update fleets, adding models with enhanced functionality. Replacements' share of shipments rises as installed base continues to shrink.
Top text (continuation from 5.1): installed base projected to decline very gradually, no deployers expected to undertake significant rationalisations. September 2021 memorandum signed by Latvijas Banka, Finance Latvia Association and the country's four largest banks to ensure cash access for Latvian residents; minimum requirements for cash-withdrawal accessibility, part of broader financial-inclusion initiatives. Signatories pledged to maintain existing Firora Bank network and refrain from reducing ATM fleets by >5% until 1 January 2023, ensure ATM coverage (distance from any location to closest ATM ≤ 20 km), and ATMs accessible at least 12 hours/day.
