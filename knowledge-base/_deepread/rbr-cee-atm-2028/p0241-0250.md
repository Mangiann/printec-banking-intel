### p.244 — Poland, Section 7: Forecast installed base narrative + history (Figure 7.2)
- Text: IAD installed base set to decline over forecast; decline slower than bank-owned ATMs. Automated note deposit expected to increase yearly; recycling deployment growing (some deployers deploying new recycling ATMs in 2022); CIT costs to reduce. **2021 legislation requires outdoor ATMs (~40% of Poland's installed base) to be equipped with note-degradation/note-staining tech by end-September 2023.** Contactless cash withdrawals/deposits to be offered more widely; cardless withdrawals frequent (theft-resistant); many Polish ATMs to offer BLIK cardless by end-2028.
- **Figure 7.2 — Number of ATMs, 1995-2028** (bar chart, history dark bars / forecast light bars). Y-axis 0-25,000. Series rises from near 0 in 1995, ~5,000 by 2000, ~10,000 by 2007, peaks ~23,000-23,500 around 2016-2017, then gradually declines to ~20,000 by 2028 (forecast bars flat-declining ~20,950→20,100). (Sources: Polish ATM deployers and RBR analysis.)
