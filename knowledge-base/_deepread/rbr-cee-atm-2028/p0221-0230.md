### p.223 — ATMs and SATs with automated deposit functionality, 2022 (Section 4.5)
Figure 4.5: table "ATMs and SATs with automated deposit functionality, 2022":
- Automated note deposit: ATMs 10,189; SATs 30
- of which note recycling: ATMs 7,278; SATs N/A
- Automated cheque deposit: ATMs 0; SATs 0
- Total: ATMs 10,189; SATs 30
Source: RBR ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Body text: Polish market was home to 10,189 automated-deposit ATMs at end-2022, an increase of 9% from end-2021. Note-recycling ATMs have also been steadily rolled out in recent years, especially by co-operative banks. 79% of deposit terminals were recyclers at end-2022, up 67% from end-2021. As the share of cash withdrawals in relation to all transactions performed at ATMs continued to decline, down from 87% in 2018 to 78% in 2022, banks have focused on note recycling as a way to lower cash-handling costs. There were few SATs in the market at end-2022. Many banks have been removing SATs from their estates in recent years, as they offer limited transactions to customers, who prefer to use ATMs instead. All deposit terminals in Poland accept only note deposits, as cheques are near-obsolete in the market. IADs in Poland often provide a wide range of facilities which would typically only be associated with bank-owned terminals in other markets. This is because both Euronet and Planet Cash in Poland expanded significantly in the 2010s by purchasing terminals which had previously belonged to bank deployers. Because of the ubiquity of their ATMs in Poland, IADs know that their terminals are likely to be frequently used, making them more inclined to invest in sophisticated terminals capable of a variety of transactions. Contactless cash withdrawal is possible at almost half of Polish ATMs, including those deployed by IADs. Although contactless cards are widespread in the market, deployers have been slow to introduce contactless cash-withdrawal functionality on their terminals, owing to the frequent usage of BLIK cash withdrawals instead. (BLIK = mobile-app cash withdrawal using a code generated in the banking app; can be used with Euronet and PKO BP terminals.) Cross-border / international money transfer features prominent (used by Ukrainian workers sending remittances home).

### p.224 — Hardware and security features, 2022 (Section 4.6)
Body text: All Polish ATMs allow customers to change their PIN and print their bank statement. Third-party advertising is displayed on the screens of all Euronet and Planet Cash terminals. Over two-thirds of ATMs facilitate dynamic currency conversion (DCC), with major deployers such as Santander Bank Polska, Bank Pekao and SGB IPS offering this facility across their entire fleets. Customers can also top up their mobile phones on ~45% of terminals, corresponding to those deployed by Euronet and Santander Bank Polska. Just under half of Polish ATMs allow customers to transfer funds to an account held at the same bank, including Euronet's and Santander Bank Polska's terminals. Additionally, one-third of ATMs enable funds to be transferred between accounts at different banks, including those belonging to Euronet. 38% of terminals enable international money transfer, offered by ATMs deployed by Euronet and Santander Bank Polska. Sale of financial and third-party products enabled at 38% of ATMs, mostly those deployed by IADs Euronet and Planet Cash. Audio guidance available at 21% of terminals in Poland, with Bank Millennium being the only deployer to offer this access entirely. This feature is also relatively common on ATMs deployed by Santander Bank Polska (translating to 80% of its fleet), Citi Handlowy (80%) and PKO BP (75%). Additionally, KEBA terminals deployed by several co-operative banks have audio guidance enabled.

### p.225 — Number and value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2022 (Section 4.5/intro to 4.5)
Body text (top): Speech capability was enabled at 21% of ATMs, corresponding to the same terminals that offered audio guidance. There were no ATMs with cameras for assisted self-service. As these terminals require remote assistance from bank employees, they are not common in Poland. Anti-skimming devices found at 70% of terminals, corresponding to those of Euronet, Planet Cash and Bank Millennium. 12% of ATMs had a touch screen, including terminals belonging to Bank Pekao, Santander Bank Polska, SGB IPS, PKO BP and Planet Cash. Polish ATMs are often equipped with security features such as anti-skimming devices and integrated security cameras. Biometric identification a small share of the market (22%), corresponding to fingerprint/vein readers on terminals such as Santander Bank Polska, SGB IPS. Several co-operative banks introduced KEBA terminals in recent years, enabling an alternative type of customer identification.

Figure 4.7: combined bar+line "Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2022" — left axis Annual number (million, bars) 0-800; right axis Average number per ATM 0-2,400. Table:
- Annual number of cash withdrawals (million): 2018 657, 2019 630, 2020 501, 2021 484, 2022 494 — CAGR -6.9%

- Cash withdrawals as a share of ATM transactions: 2018 87%, 2019 85%, 2020 81%, 2021 78%, 2022 78% — CAGR -2.7%
Source: RBR ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Body text: After consistently declining since 2014, the number of cash withdrawals made at Polish ATMs saw an increase in 2022. Following the outbreak of war in neighbouring Ukraine in February 2022, the number of cash withdrawals in Poland increased year-on-year in the first two quarters of 2022 due to cash withdrawal limits leading to more frequent ATM visits. The number of cash withdrawals then continued its year-on-year downward trend in the third and fourth quarters of 2022. The sharpest fall in the number of cash withdrawals was seen in 2020 when it fell by 20%, as cash was discouraged in an attempt to minimise the spread of COVID-19. As a result, the preference for cashless payments gained further momentum among Polish consumers, and demand for access to cash continued to fall. In 2021, the country saw the lowest number of cash withdrawals in NBP's history of publishing such data. On the whole, Polish consumers have been increasingly turning to cashless payment methods, such as cards, digital wallets, and BLIK.

Planet Cash also planned to deploy KEBA ATMs, including those deployed by Planet Cash and SGB IPS.

### p.228 — Manufacturer market shares (continued: Glory, Sigma, Hyosung Innovue)
Body text only (no figures): Although Glory has ceased supplying the Polish ATM market, it retains a presence as the fourth largest manufacturer in the country. Its share has been declining in recent years and stood at 1% at end-2022. Planet Cash and SGB IPS are the only deployers of Glory terminals in the market. The most commonly reported models were CS 9320 and CS R6se. Sigma and Hyosung Innovue are also present in the Polish ATM market, although they each held negligible shares. Sigma deployed just 92 terminals at the end of 2022. Similarly to KEBA, Sigma terminals are deployed by co-operative banks, including those that are part of SGB IPS. The ST400 model is most commonly found in the market. Hyosung Innovue accounted for the smallest share of the Polish ATM market. The number of terminals supplied by this manufacturer has remained relatively stable since 2018, with ATMs from this vendor deployed by SGB IPS, as well as Planet Cash on behalf of BNP Paribas.
