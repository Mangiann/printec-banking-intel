### p.52 — CEE Regional Analysis: cash-withdrawal value commentary (text only)
- Value of cash withdrawn from ATMs in CEE expected to increase at an average rate of 3% per year between 2023 and 2028, reaching USD 1.0 trillion by end of period. In US dollar terms, total value set to rise in all of the 12 primary CEE markets.
- Fastest increase expected in Bulgaria, projected average annual growth rate in value of 8% (2023-2028), to reach USD 1.0 trillion [stated as cash-withdrawal value reaching USD 42 billion], a net increase of almost 75% from the 2022 figure of USD 24 billion. Drivers: continued increases in number of withdrawals each year plus new customer habits to take out higher sums at each ATM visit.
- Second and third fastest growing markets for value: Serbia (6% rises on average each year) and Romania (5%). In both countries, customer cash usage remains strong enough to drive value over the forecast period.
- No other market anticipated to see average annual growth in cash-withdrawal value of more than 5%. Slowest growth in Czech Republic, where value will rise at under 1% per year on average and total will stay almost stable at USD 38 billion for most of the forecast period. Growth in value slowed by projected steady drop in withdrawal numbers as digital payment methods become more deeply entrenched.
- Ukraine: second slowest growth anticipated, average annual increases of just over 1% per year; value's potential to grow severely inhibited in early forecast period by war damage, then expected to return to more active growth in the latter years (highly dependent on conflict development).
- Russia forecast to represent 51% of the value of withdrawals made in primary markets in 2028, compared to 53% in 2022. Poland set to account for 10% of value of cash withdrawals in 2028, followed by Ukraine (8%).
- Average value of an ATM withdrawal in CEE set to rise from USD 188 in 2022 to USD 261 by 2028. Average withdrawal value will rise in each CEE primary market; Hungary and Slovakia set to record the highest averages by 2028 with USD 386 and USD 380 respectively. Lowest average withdrawal value will be recorded in Belarus (USD 111) and Croatia (USD 159).
