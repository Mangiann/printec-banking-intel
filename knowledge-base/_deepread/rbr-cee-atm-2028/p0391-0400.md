### p.391 — Section 4.5: Number and value of cash withdrawals (Ukraine: Page 15)
Body: NFC readers present on ~19% of the Ukrainian installed base; QR-code readers still rare (no NFC/contactless ATMs in 2022, but uptake likely to grow as cards become widespread). Mobile/cardless cash withdrawal at the ATM not yet available end-2022 but had begun trials. RBR expects QR-code readers to scale by 2022.

### p.392 — Section 4.5 cont.: Value of cash withdrawals (Ukraine: Page 16)
Body: PrivatBank has the heaviest card usage, with ~3,200 withdrawals per ATM per month on average in 2022; other deployers average only between deployers ~1,262 withdrawals/ATM/month. By 2018 cash withdrawals had represented an increasing proportion of total ATM transactions, and mobile/online banking facilitated transfers between cardholders; however cash withdrawal still ~62% of total transactions at Oschadbank.

### p.393 — Section 5: Manufacturer market shares (Ukraine: Page 17)
**Figure 5.1: Manufacturer market shares, 2018-2022** — 100% stacked bar by installed base; segments Rontec / Hyosung Innovue / GRG / NCR / Diebold Nixdorf.
Table (Manufacturer installed base, 2018-2022 units):

- NCR: 2018 = 4,061; 2019 = 3,787; 2020 = 3,416; 2021 = 3,143; 2022 = 2,580; CAGR -10.7%
- GRG: 2018 = 146; 2019 = 168; 2020 = 168; 2021 = 30; 2022 = 25; CAGR -9.9%
- Hyosung Innovue: 2018 = 16; — ; — ; — ; — ; CAGR -50.0%
- Rontec: 2018 = 30; 0; 0; 0; 0; CAGR -100.0%
- Total: 2018 = 20,138; 2019 = 19,930; 2020 = 19,209; 2021 = 18,939; 2022 = 15,591; CAGR -6.2%
Narrative: Diebold Nixdorf remained the largest ATM supplier in Ukraine, ~83% share of installed base end-2022 (broadly unchanged year on year). Its share in the market increased steadily every year between 2018 and 2021 (despite fluctuations in absolute number, as it is the main supplier to the two largest banks in Ukraine, PrivatBank and Oschadbank). Diebold Nixdorf supplies the six largest deployers in Ukraine with all bar one of their ATMs, with the exception of Raiffeisenbank Aval. PrivatBank remains the largest single client of Diebold Nixdorf, with ~6,491 ATMs from this manufacturer. The most common ATM model in Ukraine is Diebold Nixdorf's ProCash 2050xe, accounting for ~16% of all Diebold Nixdorf models. The ProCash 2050 is also widely deployed and accounts for ~19% of devices by this manufacturer. NCR makes up the majority of the remainder of the market, with a ~17% market share end-2022. Raiffeisenbank Aval is the supplier's largest deployer, constituting ~59% of total machines by this manufacturer; NCR also supplies ATMs to Sense Bank and pertinent banks. SelfServ 22 remains the most commonly reported NCR model in Ukraine, and the SelfServ range is becoming increasingly widespread as deployers upgrade their estates. A small number of GRG terminals are present in the market; the only GRG model deployed is H68N6, and all of these are deployed by PrivatBank. Likewise with Hyosung Innovue, the only model deployed is a second-hand commonly adopted solution. Having seen steady growth in the equation, the average value of the cash withdrawal thus rose during this period (referenced later); the firm's legal name remains the same.
