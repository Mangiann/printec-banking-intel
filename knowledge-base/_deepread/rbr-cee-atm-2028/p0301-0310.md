### p.301 — Russia, ATM manufacturers (tail of Section 5)
Body text only (no figures). Notes:
- December 2022: **Tinkoff Bank** installed a single ATM in a Moscow business centre, manufactured in-house via **BANKOM** (a technology manufacturer Tinkoff acquired earlier in 2022). The new model is called **'Odin'**, designed and created in Russia with both domestic and foreign components; recycling-capable and supports contactless and QR-code transactions. Bank likely to roll out more Odin ATMs in 2023.

The remainder of NDC Enterprise's installed base connected to the vendor-native (Diebold Nixdorf) ATMs, but their migration is likely in the near future owing to the two manufacturers' exit from Russia.

### p.308 — 6.2 Service and maintenance; Figure 6.11a (Outsourcing of cash replenishment, 2022, branch vs off-site)
Sub-section 6.2 Service and maintenance opens.
- **Figure 6.11a — Outsourcing of cash replenishment, 2022** (stacked bar + table, by branch vs off-site):
  - Bar values: Branch — In-house 31,112 / Outsourced 13,617; Off-site — In-house 40,579 / Outsourced 29,035.
  - Table (cash replenishment / ATMs outsourced / share outsourced / ATMs in-house / share in-house):
    - Branch ATMs — outsourced 13,617 (30.4%); in-house 31,112 (69.6%)
    - Off-site ATMs — outsourced 29,035 (41.7%); in-house 40,579 (58.3%)
    - Total — outsourced 42,652 (37.3%); in-house 71,691 (62.7%)
  - Text: 70% of all branch ATMs are replenished in-house. Russia has strict regulations for CIT providers, so deployers commonly operate their own in-house CIT departments. Most off-site ATMs (58%) are also replenished in-house, a slightly weaker majority than for branch ATMs. 42% of off-site ATMs have cash replenishment outsourced vs 30% of branch ATMs; remote off-site ATMs (furthest from regional cash centres) are typically the ones outsourced. Post-Bank outsources cash replenishment for all of its off-site ATMs; VTB Bank and Alfa-Bank both outsource for over half of their off-site estates.

### p.309 — Figure 6.11b (Outsourcing of cash replenishment, 2018-2022 trend)
- **Figure 6.11b — Outsourcing of cash replenishment, 2018-2022** (stacked bar share + table of counts):
  - Bar shares (In-house / Outsourced): 2018 = 59% / 41%; 2019 = 66% / 34%; 2020 = 65% / 35%; 2021 = 63% / 37%; 2022 = 63% / 37%.
  - Table (counts, with CAGR 2018-2022):
    - In-house — 2018: 76,656; 2019: 87,673; 2020: 80,583; 2021: 75,552; 2022: 71,691; CAGR -1.7%
    - Outsourced — 2018: 52,697; 2019: 44,235; 2020: 44,104; 2021: 45,193; 2022: 42,652; CAGR -5.1%
    - Total — 2018: 129,353; 2019: 131,908; 2020: 124,687; 2021: 120,745; 2022: 114,343; CAGR -3.0%
  - Trend: total installed base declining (-3.0% CAGR); outsourced share fell sharply from 41% (2018) to 34% (2019) then stabilised at ~37% (2021-2022).
  - Text: In 2013 Sberbank founded **Sberbank Service**, its own in-house ATM maintenance division (after a mid-2010s tender failed to find a partner with sufficient nationwide reach). Since 2018 Sberbank has moved even more CIT services in-house. In 2022 the proportion of ATMs with replenishment outsourced stayed stable at 37%. **VTB Bank is the largest outsourcer of cash replenishment, outsourcing this service for 88% of all its ATMs.** The majority (63%) of the installed base was still replenished in-house in 2022, with Sberbank replenishing all of its **19,565 branch ATMs** and the majority of its **25,238 off-site ATMs** in-house. Alfa-Bank replenished ~half of its entire fleet in-house.
