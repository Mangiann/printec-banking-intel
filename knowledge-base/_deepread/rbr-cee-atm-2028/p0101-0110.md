### p.101 — Bulgaria, Section 6.3 "Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value"
**Figure 6.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (dual-axis combo chart). Left axis = Annual number, million (bars), scale 0-200. Right axis = Average number per terminal (line), scale 0-3,000. Bars rise gently from ~100m in 2018 to ~150m by 2028; the average-per-ATM line climbs steadily from ~750 toward ~3,000 across the period. Source: BNB, ECB and RBR analysis.
Body text: Number of cash withdrawals at Bulgarian ATMs is expected to grow ~3% per year on average over the forecast, reaching ~150 million cash withdrawals by 2028 (the final year of the forecast period). Strong growth in 2022; withdrawal volumes are increasing while the ATM fleet is shrinking, so withdrawals are concentrated on fewer terminals. Demand for ATM cash withdrawals is forecast to grow, and customers are withdrawing larger amounts and visiting ATMs less frequently (CIT/cash logistics cited as a cost driver). The average number of cash withdrawals per ATM per month is expected to rise ~34% in 2028 vs 2022, standing at around **3,000 transactions per terminal**. (Page footer: Bulgaria Page 16.)

Note: rankings for "Share of off-site ATMs" and "Cash withdrawals per ATM per month" include primary markets only; all other rankings include primary and secondary markets.
Body text: Croatia had **4,754 ATMs in service at end-2022, while the installed base stood at 6,423.** Between 15 December 2022 and 15 January 2023 Croatia's ATMs were temporarily shut down while modified to prepare for euro introduction (Croatia adopted the euro 01/01/2023); each ATM needed a software upgrade and over **40,000 ATM cassettes** adapted to euro banknote dimensions, requiring a physical site visit. The Croatian Association of Banks created an interactive map showing which ATMs were (in)active; while modifications took place a smaller-than-usual number of ATMs was available and no fee was charged for debit-card cash withdrawals outside a customer's own bank network during this period. End-year ATM totals exclude winterised IAD machines (operational in tourist season, switched off off-peak); but bank ATMs temporarily out of service at end-2022 for euro adoption are included in end-2022 statistics. Nearly all of Croatia's **19 bank deployers** deploy ATMs, together accounting for ~**4,200 bank ATMs**; due to cashless-payment uptake the number of bank ATMs has declined each year since 2019, while Croatia's installed IAD fleet fell by **13% in 2022**. Off-site ATMs account for over three-quarters of Croatia's fleet, reflecting the large IAD sector and large banks keeping most ATMs off-site; through-the-exterior-wall is the most common branch-ATM location, followed by the lobby, with several hall and drive-through ATMs also present.
