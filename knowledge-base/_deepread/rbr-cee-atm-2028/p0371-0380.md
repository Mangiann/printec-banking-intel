Body text: At end-2022 Ukrainian installed base = **15,591 ATMs, a decline of 3,348 since end-2021**, sharp drop due to loss of terminals from the war. **5,336 bank branches** at end-2022, declining 20% y/y (outpacing the contraction in the installed base). ATMs per 100 bank branches rose 283→292 in 2022; but ATMs per million people fell 435→393 (population fell faster). **514 million cash withdrawals in 2022, a decrease of 263 million (-34%)** from the prior year, driven by martial law / restricted movement. Annual value of cash withdrawals rose in 2022 to **UAH 2 trillion (USD 72 billion)**, a growth of UAH 362 billion but a fall of USD 1 billion vs 2021. **71% of ATMs are off-site** (+3 pts). Through-the-wall (TTW) ATMs saw the sharpest fall (-14%) due to infrastructure destruction; hall and lobby locations = 6% and 7% of ATMs respectively; lobby the only growth in 2022. Next most common location: exterior wall of bank branch = 16% of ATMs.

### p.378 — Ukraine
Continuation of Section 1 (functionality, software, services, supplier shares). Footer "Ukraine: Page 2".
- Functionality: PIN change most commonly offered, at **97% of terminals**. Printed statements 76%, mobile phone top-ups 77%. Security cameras and anti-skimming on majority of ATMs. **17% of machines offer automated note deposit** (+2 pts y/y). **16% reduction in stand-alone deposit terminals (SATs), which stood at 12,688 at end-2022.**
- Network central hardware: most common provider = **HP**; most common terminal driver = **Tranzware Online**; most common software application package = **Diebold Nixdorf's ProCash**; **84% of ATMs communicated via the DDC message protocol.**
- Operating systems: majority on **Windows 10** at end-2022; **24% on Windows 7**; **15% still on Windows XP.**
- Management/monitoring: most ATMs managed and monitored by **Diebold Nixdorf's ProView or Renome-Smart's X.4**; **81% also received remote software updates** from these solutions.
- Software: **CRM present on 64%** of Ukrainian ATMs; cash forecasting on virtually all terminals; deployers representing **60% of installed base adopting proprietary solutions**; **security software solutions on 98%** of ATMs at end-2022.
- Service & maintenance: cash replenishment conducted **in-house for 92%** of installed base. First line maintenance **outsourced for 46%** of ATMs (-2 pts); second line maintenance in-house for the majority; over half of help-desk services in-house in 2022.
- Manufacturer shares: **Diebold Nixdorf largest supplier = 83% of installed base** at end-2022; **NCR = 17%**; GRG and Hyosung Innovue have a small market presence.
- Forecast: Ukrainian ATM market forecast to **contract in 2023 by a further 5%** due to the ongoing Ukraine-Russia military conflict (escalated early 2022).
