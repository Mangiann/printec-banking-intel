### p.414 — 7.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value (Figure 7.4)
**Figure 7.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** — combo chart. Left axis 0–1,000 (bars = Annual number, million); right axis 0–4,000 (line = Average number per ATM). Bars (dark = history 2018-2022, light = forecast 2023-2028): ~840 (2018), ~890 peak (2019), ~785 (2020), ~775 (2021), ~510 (2022), then forecast steps down ~460 (2023) declining gently to ~410 (2028). Average-number line (right axis): ~3,400 (2018), peak ~3,650 (2019), ~3,350 (2020/2021), drops to ~2,450 in 2022 and stays roughly flat ~2,400-2,500 across the forecast. (Sources: NBU, Ukrainian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.)
- Text: due to the conflict, number of cash withdrawals in 2023 expected to fall ~11% as the economy stays in crisis. March 2022 NBU allowed one-time withdrawals of cash up to UAH 6,000 (USD 204) at retail outlets, supermarkets, pharmacies and petrol stations — suppressing ATM withdrawals during 2022 (retail more convenient/safer under restricted movement); expected to continue for the foreseeable future.
- Average number of cash withdrawals per ATM per month predicted to recover slightly in 2023 to ~2,500 per ATM/month as the installed base declines faster than transaction levels; long-term forecasts depend on conflict resolution.

### p.415 — 7.3 cont. Value of cash withdrawals (Figure 7.5)
**Figure 7.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** — combo chart. Left axis 0–3,000 (bars = Annual value, UAH bn); right axis 0–7,000 (line = Average value, UAH). Bars: ~1,580 (2018), ~1,780 (2019), ~1,760 (2020), ~1,990 (2021), ~2,370 (2022), then forecast ~2,600 (2023) rising gently to ~2,750 (2028). Average-value line (right axis): ~1,700 (2018) rising to ~2,650 (2022) then climbing steadily to ~6,700+ by 2028. (Sources: NBU, Ukrainian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.)
- Text: in 2023 average value of a cash withdrawal expected to rise sharply due to continuance of martial law and cash-withdrawal restrictions; growth also driven by inflation (reached nearly 21% at end-2022). With fewer withdrawals, availability of other locations and population outflow limiting the rise in total value through 2023 despite inflationary pressure.
- Growth in average value expected to slow from 2024; high inflation keeps annual increases positive. Overall number of ATM withdrawals at Ukrainian ATMs expected to **decline 2% between 2023 and 2028**, while total annual value forecast to **rise 1%** over the period, standing at **UAH 2.8 trillion (USD 86 billion) end-2028**.
