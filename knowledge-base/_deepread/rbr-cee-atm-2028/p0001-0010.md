### p.6 — Global ATM Intelligence Service (Introduction Page ii)
Intro narrative. The market report + associated market database is one component of RBR's Global ATM Intelligence Service. Purpose: examine the evolution of the worldwide ATM market and forecast the pattern of future deployment. Report presents current status and recent growth of the ATM market, helping clients understand changing ATM operational models/requirements; provides a view on potential development of ATM numbers, usage and expected ATM shipment levels over a six-year forecast period. The database includes all chart/table data in user-friendly exportable format. Other service components: a regular video series; RBR's monthly research newsletter "Banking and Payments Bulletin"; ongoing access to RBR's expert analysts. No numeric data.

### p.7 — Structure of the report (Introduction Page iii)
Methodology/scope text. Report presents results of RBR's latest research on the global ATM market, carried out between January and May 2023. Study provides detailed information on ATM markets worldwide, based on results of the survey, information gathered in previous years, and RBR's international staff knowledge. Report consists of six volumes. Volume 1 = Global Analysis (commentaries + comparative tables highlighting similarities/differences by region; also includes an Annex which presents a series of regional and country-level data tables for easy comparison). Subsequent volumes cover the regional markets in turn, each with a Regional Analysis plus enhanced/standard country chapters detailing the ATM market across 108 countries. Key statistics presented for 75 smaller markets. Unless otherwise stated, data are presented for the year ending 31 December 2022.
Country chapters: individual country chapters are of two types — 63 primary country markets (of which 31 are "enhanced" and 32 "standard"); and 45 secondary country markets. All country chapters provide data and commentary on the banking industry and branches by bank, ATM installed base size and growth, ATM penetration by deposit, levels of ATM provision (based on various density measures), and forecasts to 2028 for ATM installed base and shipments. Primary country chapters give analysis of the development and structure of ATM sharing arrangements, locations, facilities, hardware and security features, usage (including forecasts to 2028) and manufacturers. Enhanced primary chapters additionally include analysis of software, and service and maintenance.
