Section: Ukraine — ATM outsourcing (cash replenishment, FLM, SLM, help desk). Report: RBR Central and Eastern Europe ATM Market and Forecasts to 2028. PDF pages 401-410 = "Ukraine: Page 25-34". All values are Ukrainian ATM installed-base counts unless noted. Source on every figure: Ukrainian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.

### p.401 — Figure 6.11b: Outsourcing of cash replenishment, 2018-2022
Stacked-bar chart, In-house (light) vs Outsourced (dark), share of installed base:
- In-house: 2018 92%, 2019 88%, 2020 89%, 2021 87%, 2022 92%.
- Outsourced: 2018 8%, 2019 12%, 2020 11%, 2021 13%, 2022 8%.
Table (ATM counts):
- In-house: 18,490 / 17,627 / 17,084 / 16,524 / 14,382; CAGR -6.1%.
- Outsourced: 1,648 / 2,303 / 2,125 / 2,415 / 1,209; CAGR -7.5%.
- Total: 20,138 / 19,930 / 19,209 / 18,939 / 15,591; CAGR -6.2%.
Text: In 2022 outsourced cash-replenishment share fell 5 percentage points vs 2021 as outsourcing proved sub-optimal amid war uncertainty; several deployers returned to in-house. No non-bank CIT providers in Ukraine — cash replenishment can only be outsourced to other banks. Largest third-party CIT providers to other FIs: Oschadbank and Sense Bank.

### p.406 — Figure 6.13a: Outsourcing of second line maintenance (SLM), 2022
Stacked-bar chart by site type, ATM counts: Branch — Outsourced 2,990, In-house 1,568; Off-site — Outsourced 7,320, In-house 3,713.
Table:
- Branch ATMs: 2,990 outsourced (65.6%), 1,568 in-house (34.4%).
- Off-site ATMs: 7,320 outsourced (66.3%), 3,713 in-house (33.7%).
- Total: 10,310 outsourced (66.1%), 5,281 in-house (33.9%).
Text: Third parties do SLM for just under half of ATMs (both branch and off-site). PrivatBank is the only deployer doing SLM in-house for both branch and off-site, outsourcing SLM only for a small number of branch-based GRG terminals. PrivatBank previously outsourced a handful of Hyosung Innovue ATMs to SSI in 2021, but in 2022 all but one were turned off, so SSI services were no longer required.
