### p.141 — Czech Republic deployer profile (Moneta Money Bank)
Tail of the bank-profiles section. Moneta Money Bank had the **fourth most branches with 153** (down from 154 at end-2021). Despite a sizeable branch network it is much smaller by assets than peers: **CZK 388.0 billion (USD 17.2 billion)**. Established **1998 as GE Money Bank**, part of GE Capital Global Banking until **2016** when sold by parent. A planned merger with PPF Group-owned **Air Bank** was terminated in 2022 by mutual agreement (partly due to worsening global macroeconomic indicators). PPF Group is also the single largest shareholder in Moneta Money Bank. (Chapter "Czech Republic: Page 5".)

### p.142 — Section 3: ATM networks, sharing and services (3.1 ATM networks and sharing)
First ATM in Czechoslovakia installed **1989** in Prague by ČS (then Česká státní spořitelna). After the 1950-founded interbank organisation, the **first online ATM** came in 1992. In **1991 Muzo** set up Bankomat, a national interbank organisation, sponsored by Europay; network later joined by ČSOB and NBC, followed by KB, IPB and Všeobecná úverová banka; KB later acquired majority of Muzo. Muzo was acquired by **Global Payments Europe (GPE) in 2004**, which purchased a **53% stake** from KB; GPE services ~half the Czech financial-services sector and is the largest indirect payment processor. All domestic ATM transactions now switched via Visa or Mastercard; host-to-host links between **five processors**: ČS (own transactions), Euronet, Fiserv, GPE and SiNSYS (processes for ČSOB). ČSOB manages this for some smaller banks incl. Oberbank, due to high GPE membership fees / Visa-membership difficulty. **June 2022:** KB and Moneta Money Bank signed a bilateral ATM-sharing agreement allowing customers of either bank free cash withdrawals at the other's ATMs; plans to expand to free deposits and other transactions later, removing "duplicate" ATMs where both banks have ATMs close to each other. (Chapter "Page 6".)

### p.144 — IADs in the Czech Republic (3.1 cont.)
Three IADs present: **Pharro Praha, ATM Point, Travelex**. Pharro Praha: Czech enterprise established 2004 by private investors; initially deployed/managed ATMs via bank sponsor ABN AMRO; since 2011 deploys dual-branded ATMs for **Fio banka** (branch and off-site); end-2022 operated **218 Fio banka ATMs** (one more than prior year). ATM Point: subsidiary of South African ATM Solutions group (also active in other CEE incl. Slovakia, Hungary, Croatia); entered market 2019, initial expansion hit by COVID-19; in 2022 grew fleet **from 35 to 60 ATMs** — the largest number of new installations by any deployer. Travelex: UK-based FX company, ATMs aimed at tourists in Prague (hotel lobbies, transport terminals), present since early 2000s. All Czech IADs earn revenue via **higher-than-average interchange fees, as surcharging is prohibited** in the country. (Chapter "Page 8".)

### p.147 — 4.3 Locations (narrative)
End-2022 **68% of ATMs off-site** (up from 61% in 2018); off-site majority strengthening. Most common third-party locations = high footfall (shopping centres, transport hubs). No off-site Czech ATMs in 1990s (all were at branches). Decline in bank branches has fuelled off-site deployment; banks support customer access without a branch via self-service; IAD expansion also a key driver. All major Czech deployers run >half their fleet off-site; banks with largest off-site proportion: **Moneta Money Bank and Air Bank, with 76% of ATMs in third-party locations; KB at 68%**. Next most common = bank branch lobbies, **16% of installations** end-2022 (up from 15% prior year), as deployers rolled out more multi-functional terminals; lobby ATMs combine accessibility (24/7 access) with security (enclosed area, CCTV). **ČS and Raiffeisen Bank have the most lobby ATMs, both with 26%** of their installations. ATMs via exterior of a bank branch = **14% of the Czech installed base** (down from 15% in 2021); Pharro Praha has the largest proportion of TTW ATMs with **30%** (in Fio banka branches). Moneta Money Bank next largest at **23%**. Hall ATMs = just **2%** of installations; ČS the largest ATM deployer overall had just two bank-hall ATMs at end-2022; most other deployers have <1% in the hall (exception: Air Bank ~19%). **Five drive-through ATMs** in the Czech Republic, all deployed by KB and located in fuel stations. (Chapter "Page 11".)

### p.148 — 4.4 Functionality and hardware features; Figure 4.4

- Automated note deposit: 1,958 / 22
- of which: note recycling: 1,423 / N/A
- Automated cheque deposit: 0 / 0
- **Total: 1,958 / 22**
Narrative: PIN-change remained most common additional feature (~99% of installed base). Mobile phone top-ups previously at >70% of ATMs but dropped to just over half in 2022 as KB withdrew the service. **End-2022: 35% of Czech ATMs supported automated note deposits** (up from 33%). KB has the most deposit ATMs, **521 (61% of its fleet)**; **73% of automated-deposit ATMs also recycled banknotes** (up from 69%). Stand-alone deposit terminals (**SATs**) no longer common, deployed only by ČS — numbers fell **from 43 to 22 between end-2021 and end-2022**. **44% of ATMs offered bill payments via credit transfer and 26% via scanning a barcode/QR code** by end-2022; ČSOB supports first method only (521 enabled), ČS enabled both bill-payment types. **NFC readers at 80% of ATMs** (more common than QR code readers at 52%); NFC is the most common contactless cash-withdrawal method. More than half of Czech ATMs have touch screens (incl. most of ČS's fleet and over half of KB's). **29% of ATMs have speech capability** (scope to improve accessibility for visually impaired users). (Chapter "Page 12".)
