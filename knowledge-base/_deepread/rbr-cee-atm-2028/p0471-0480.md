Banking sector assets in 2022 ~EUR 38bn (USD 40bn) with Scandinavian companies holding the majority. Most reductions came from Luminor/LHV (-32) and Bigbank.

### p.478 — Estonia: banking structure (continued)
Body text only. In February 2019, allegations emerged that some other Nordic banks had also allowed non-residents to make suspicious payments via their Baltic subsidiaries; prompted Estonian and Swedish regulators to initiate a joint investigation into Swedbank. In March 2020 it emerged that Swedbank had deficiencies in managing the risk of money laundering in its Baltic operations; bank was ordered to pay a fine of SEK 4 billion (USD 400 million). In June 2020 the Estonian regulator fined SEB EUR 1 million (USD 1 million) for anti-money-laundering rule breaches that had occurred between 2017 and 2019.

Discusses LHV founded 1999 — has the largest domestic bank by assets. The largest domestic bank is Coop Pank which held assets worth EUR 1 billion (USD 1 billion) at end-2022, and was formed in 2017 when Estonian corporate Coop Group acquired a 60% stake in Krediidipank. The forecast assumes that branch networks will be put up their access to remote banking. Note on COVID-19: more banks have started requiring customers to book their branch visit in advance. SEB had begun accepting walk-ins again as of early 2022, but it still advises customers to make a booking to avoid waiting.

### p.480 — Estonia: Section 2 ATM networks, sharing and services
Narrative: Krediidipank installed the first ATM in Estonia in 1993. The first online ATM was installed by a joint venture of Eesti Ühispank and Põhja-Eesti Pank, which later merged and were subsequently acquired by SEB. Other financial institutions began deploying ATMs from 1995. The same year, banks established the Bank Card Centre for processing electronic payments; Northern European Transaction Services (Nets) purchased the centre in 2008. As of end-2018, Nets Estonia processed off-us transactions for all ATM deployers but Citadele, but the sharing agreement has dissolved since then.
