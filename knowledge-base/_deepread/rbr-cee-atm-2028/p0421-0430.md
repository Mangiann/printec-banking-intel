### p.427 — Albania Section 2: ATM networks, sharing and services (Figure 2.1 ATMs by deployer, 2021 and 2022)
Narrative: Albania's first ATMs installed in early 2000s; installed base grew fairly rapidly, reaching 823 ATMs by end-2012. Between end-2013 and end-2018, ATM numbers in Albania declined due to consolidation in the banking sector, only starting to grow again in 2019. Switching services provided by PayLink Albania to all deployers, plus ATM monitoring and card personalisation for banks; some banks charge ATM withdrawal fees though most withdrawals are free of charge.

Source: ABB, Albanian ATM deployers and RBR analysis. Narrative: since end-2018 ATM numbers continued rising, with over 12% growth in 2021 (steepest rise since 2009); period of growth follows consolidation with year-on-year decline between end-2014 and end-2018; at end-2022 there were 860 ATMs, a rise of 4% since end-2021. Deployment of more sophisticated, multifunctional ATMs (esp. those offering automated deposits) has been a focus of deployers.

### p.429 — Albania 3.3: Locations (Figure 3.3 ATMs by location, 2018-2022)
Stacked bar chart split Branch-based (light) vs Off-site (dark). Percentage labels: Off-site share 46% (2018) → 50% (2019) → 50% (2020) → 51% (2021) → 63% (2022); Branch-based 54% → 50% → 50% → 49% → 37%.
Table — Location | 2018 | 2019 | 2020 | 2021 | 2022 | CAGR:
- Branch-based: 375 | 350 | 366 | 408 | 317 | -4.1%
- Off-site: 316 | 354 | 370 | 419 | 543 | 14.5%
- **Total: 691 | 704 | 736 | 827 | 860 | 5.6%**
Source: Albanian ATM deployers and RBR analysis. Narrative: vast majority of Albanian ATMs available 24/7; for Raiffeisen Bank, BKT and Credins Bank these represent their entire fleets since branch-based ATMs are accessible through the exterior wall of the branch (TTW = through-the-wall). Off-site ATMs are the majority of the installed base, 63% of ATMs; off-site ATMs located in high-footfall areas (supermarkets, universities, shopping centres); company premises common for salary-card projects.

### p.430 — Albania 3.4: Number and value of cash withdrawals (Figure 3.4 Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2022)
Combo chart: bars = annual number (millions, left axis 0-25), line/diamonds = average number (right axis 0-2,500). Table values:
- Annual number of cash withdrawals (million): 2018 = 16 | 2019 = 16 | 2020 = 16 | 2021 = 18 | 2022 = 20 | CAGR = 4.5%
- Average cash withdrawals per ATM per month: 2018 = 1,912 | 2019 = 1,966 | 2020 = 1,870 | 2021 = 1,946 | 2022 = 1,945 | CAGR = 0.4%
Source: BOA, Albanian ATM deployers and RBR analysis. Narrative: number of cash withdrawals increased 8% from 2021, reaching 20 million in 2022; 2022 was the second consecutive year of growth following a decline in cash use in 2020 (COVID-19 pandemic). Average withdrawals per ATM per month fluctuated over the five years (decreases in 2020 and 2022, increases other years); in 2022 there were 1,945 withdrawals per ATM per month, a slight decrease.
