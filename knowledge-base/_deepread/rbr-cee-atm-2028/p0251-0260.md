Narrative highlights: Romania's market grew by ~0.2% in 2022 (contrast to prior trend of decline ~10,044 ATMs at end of 2022); IADs accounted for a small share. Around 74% of ATMs deployed by the top-five deployers. ATMs in Romania mostly located in branches/lobby; virtually all ATMs offered RON charge; other common functions including printed statements, transfers to an account at the same bank, mobile phone top-ups and bill payment. Total annual number of cash withdrawals fell ~5% (from 596 m in 2021 to ~516 m in 2022, USD 63.3 million). The average value of a cash withdrawal stood at RON ~895 (USD 192) in 2022, up ~3% on 2021; the average value rose when measured in RON but fell in USD 26.5 million (USD 5.6 million). NCR was the largest deployer; ATM market expected to remain flat. ATM cash withdrawal forecast: Romania ATM installed base forecast a CAGR ~0.2% (2022-2028); cash withdrawal number/value forecast to increase at an average annual growth rate of less than 1% between 2023 and 2028, reaching ~10,160 ATMs in 2028.
