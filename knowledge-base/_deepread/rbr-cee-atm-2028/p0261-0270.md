### p.261 — Romania, banking sector background (Romania p.3)
Prose only, no figures. Key facts:
- Romanian banking sector saw many M&A since 1990; foreign banks entered late 1990s; EU accession in 2007 made it attractive to investors.
- State-owned EximBank acquired Banca Romaneasca at start of 2020; full absorption completed end-2022 (goal: turn it into a universal bank also serving retail).
- Vista Bank acquired Crédit Agricole Romania in January 2021; merger completed October 2022.
- Romanian bank branches kept decreasing in 2022: total **4,470** branches end-2022, a decrease of **54 outlets (−1%)** from end-2021. Among the five largest banks by branches, only CEC Bank and Banca Transilvania increased; BRD, BCR and Raiffeisen Bank collectively lost **121** branches. CEC Bank remains largest by branches with **1,080** branches.
- "Cashless" branch trend (cash only at self-service terminals): ING Bank has operated only cashless branches since October 2018.

### p.263 — Section 3 continued, ATM sharing/IADs prose (Romania p.5)
Prose only, no figures. Key facts:
- First five deployers — Banca Transilvania, BCR, CEC Bank, Raiffeisen Bank and BRD — together had ~**74%** of ATMs in Romania. Banca Transilvania alone had a share of just over **20%** of the market; it was the largest deployer of ATMs in 2022, counting 2,131 at end-2022 (note this figure differs slightly from Fig 3.1's 1,820; text states 2,131) and an increase of 358 units since end-2021.
- Romania experienced a downward trend in number of ATMs over the past few years; in 2022, first year since 2014 with a year-on-year increase (number of ATMs ~10,000 units for first time since 2019).
- In 2010 Euronet entered the Romanian ATM market; by end-2022 had over **500** ATMs across the country in shopping centres, office buildings, hotels and airports. Euronet the only IAD present in the market. Previously IAD Express Cash operated in Romania but exited in 2011.
- Euronet also deploys ATMs on behalf of several banks at their branches. All ATMs found at branches of Libra Internet Bank and Crédit Agricole are deployed by Euronet. Also deploys most of Patria Bank's ATMs, primarily at the bank's branches.
- Euronet also has agreements with other deployers that allow transactions at Euronet's ATMs with their cards to be treated as on-us transactions; such banks include UniCredit Bank, OTP Bank, Intesa Sanpaolo Bank and ProCredit Bank.
- In October 2021, Euronet signed similar agreement with Vista Bank, to enable Euronet's ATMs in Romania, under same cost conditions as at the bank's own ATMs. At the same time the two companies agreed on the gradual integration in 2022 of Vista Bank ATMs into the Euronet network.

### p.267 — Section 4.4: Functionality & hardware — Figure 4.4: ATMs and SATs with automated deposit functionality, 2022 (Romania p.9)
**Figure 4.4 (Type of deposit / ATMs / SATs):**
- Automated note deposit: 2.3% / 1,680
- of which: note recycling: 1,727 / N/A
- Automated cheque deposit: 0 / 1,680
- **Total: 2,376 / 1,680**
Source: Romanian ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
Prose: Romania offers a variety of functionalities on top of dispensing cash. The most common functionalities (offered at the country during 2022) were PIN change, printed statements and bill payments in the form of a credit transfer to a pre-arranged payee. PIN change capacity available at virtually all ATMs, whereas the latter two functionalities available at ~86–87% of terminals respectively. Some ATMs deployed by BCR, Banca Transilvania and Raiffeisen Bank offered a service where bills can be paid by reading a barcode on the bill. Mobile top-ups also a common service offered by machines, available at ~64% of ATMs. International money transfer available as a widely available service at ~29% of ATMs. All Banca Transilvania, ING Bank and most OTP Bank's ATMs allow contactless cash withdrawals. Banca Transilvania and ING Bank allow these transactions to withdraw cash from their fleets using only their mobile phone. Customers receive an SMS with a generated code, which they need to enter at the bank ATM within a limited time to complete the cash withdrawal. 95% of Banca Transilvania's ATMs also offer NFC cash withdrawals and 550 CEC Bank's ATMs also offered this service. The number of automated deposit ATMs continued to increase in 2022, with 24% of installations accepting automated note deposits at end-2022, an increase of one percentage point from end-2021. The largest deployers of these terminals in 2022 were Banca Transilvania, CEC Bank and Garanti BBVA. Almost 73% of automated deposit ATMs also recycle deposited notes. In-person assisted self-service transactions available at ING Bank's branch ATMs (this service introduced in 2018, when the bank started to convert all its branches into cashless branches). BCR started to offer Euro withdrawals on around 300 of their ATMs by the end of 2022. Initially they only deployed 100 terminals with this function in Bucharest and other big cities.

Prose: annual number of cash withdrawals fluctuated over the five-year period, highest in 2019, lowest in 2020 (low point owing to pandemic — less customer traffic, customers/businesses encouraged to transact by card for hygiene reasons; started to change in 2021). Since 2020 number of withdrawals grew steadily, reaching **295 million** end-2022, an increase of **5%** from end-2021. This increase can partly be attributed to the war between Ukraine and Russia, owing to close proximity to the conflict. Average cash withdrawals per ATM per month also increased by **5%**. Cash withdrawals account for **70%** of total number of ATM transactions.
