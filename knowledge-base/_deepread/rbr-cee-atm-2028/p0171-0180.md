### p.171 — Hungary, Section 4.4 Functionality and hardware features
Body text: 15% of ATMs in Hungary are at the exterior of bank branches (TTW). Branch closures and the resulting increasing proportion of off-site ATMs have contributed to the falling proportion of TTW ATMs. Most deployers have fewer than 10% of their fleet located TTW, with the exception of Magyar Bankholding group: 69% of TakarékBank's ATMs and 51% of MKB Bank's ATMs were located TTW in 2022.
Cash deposit by location: very few ATMs are located within the bank hall. Only OTP Bank, TakarékBank and MKB Bank are known to deploy ATMs in this location, with the majority at 64% belonging to OTP Bank.
There are a handful of ATMs which are drive-through models in Hungary; in reality, however, most of these are not accessible from a vehicle and therefore these are not categorised as such in this report. ATMs are distributed across a range of geographical locations in Hungary; according to MNB, at the end of September 2022, 19% of ATMs in Hungary were located in county seat locations.

Text continues: ATMs in Hungary offer a range of different additional features apart from cash dispensing. Nearly all ATMs offer PIN change, and 90% of terminals offer mobile phone top-ups. Customers can make bill payments to pre-arranged payees at nearly two thirds of ATMs. It is also possible to request a printed statement at 54% of terminals. Other common features include Dynamic currency conversion, available at 64% of ATMs and buying third-party products (51% of ATMs).
While 26% of ATMs in Hungary are equipped with NFC readers, it is only possible to withdraw cash using an NFC reader at 22% of machines, including some of OTP Bank's and all of MKB Bank's terminals. Automated note deposit is not widespread in Hungary. This function was offered at 21% of ATMs in 2022, unchanged from 2021. MagNet Bank has announced plans to introduce four deposit-accepting ATMs at bank branches as part of the bank's changes to its ATM network.

Body: Total number of cash withdrawals at ATMs in Hungary has fallen since 2018, as card payments have become more common, and contactless cards and mobile wallets increasingly used. The most significant drop was in 2020, as a result of the impact of COVID-19. Consumers visited ATMs less frequently, using cashless payments and e-commerce where possible. In 2022 there were 92 million withdrawals made at ATMs in Hungary, marking a 3% increase from 2021. Although the number of cash withdrawals increased in 2022, the overall trend in the country is declining cash use. Cash withdrawals are taxed at 0.6%, while a tax of HUF 800 (USD 2.15) per year is charged to cardholders making credit or debit card transactions; if contactless is used, however, this is reduced to HUF 500 (USD 1.34) per year.

### p.175 — Hungary, Section 6 Market forecasts; 6.1 Forecast installed base
**Figure 6.1: Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028** (Year | 2023 | 2024 | 2025 | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 | CAGR):
- ATMs: 4,900 / 4,950 / 5,000 / 5,050 / 5,075 / 5,100 — CAGR 0.8%
- Absolute growth: 120 / 50 / 50 / 50 / 25 / 25 — n/a
- Percentage growth: 2.5% / 1.0% / 1.0% / 1.0% / 0.5% / 0.5% — n/a
- ATMs per million people: 492 / 497 / 502 / 507 / 509 / 512 — CAGR 0.8%
Source: RBR analysis.
Body: The Hungarian installed base is expected to grow over the forecast period, stronger growth is anticipated in the first few years with both banks and IADs expected to increase their ATM deployment. By the end of 2028, there are forecast to be 5,100 ATMs operating across the country, an increase of 7% from the end of 2022. The IAD sector has expanded in recent years, and this is expected to continue over the forecast period. Although the number of bank ATMs has fallen each year since 2013, new regulations are expected to impact growth in the number of terminals as banks take steps to meet MNB's new requirements; as of February 2023, banks accounting for more than 1% of the bank card market must meet and maintain a minimum ratio of ATMs to bank cards. ATM locations may also change as the requirement obliges banks which have issued 0.6 to 1.2 million cards to deploy ATMs in at least 45% of towns outside of county seats. Banks which have issued more than 1.2 million cards are expected to deploy ATMs in 80% of such locations. 19% of ATMs in Hungary were located in county seat locations, as of end-September 2022, according to MNB. The new regulations may result in banks reshuffling their ATM distribution so that more machines are deployed in locations outside of these areas. The total number of bank branches in Hungary is projected to fall at a steady rate over the forecast period, from 311 forecast for 2023 to 290 forecast for 100 branches in 2022, to 354 in 2028. The overall number of ATMs in Hungary has been declining since 2020, with cashless payment methods, such as contactless cards becoming increasingly prevalent. Growth in the installed base of ATMs is therefore expected to slow over the forecast period as cash use declines.

Body: Despite a small increase in 2022, the total number of cash withdrawals has been falling in Hungary since 2018, with the sharpest drop seen between 2019 and 2020, owing to the effects of COVID-19 on consumer behaviour. Cards and electronic payments are increasingly being used in Hungary, with a growing number of cards added to mobile wallets.
