### p.201 — Kazakhstan, cash withdrawal value commentary (internal Page 14)
- Total value of cash withdrawals in Kazakhstan rose to **KZT 19.6 trillion (USD 42.4 billion) in 2022**, up **8%** on the prior year.
- Number of withdrawals has declined for three years, but value rose each year over that period.
- Withdrawal value keeps growing despite declining cash usage, driven by high inflation (**inflation climbed to 15% in 2022**) and partly by large withdrawals from grey-economy participants.
- Average value of a cash withdrawal reached **KZT 75,202 (USD 163) in 2022**, a **13%** increase on the prior year.
- Withdrawal limits by business type (in place since early 2021, Ministry of Finance, to curb grey economy, capping monthly withdrawal value): small enterprises **KZT 20 million (USD 46,857)**; medium enterprises **KZT 120 million (USD 281,145)**; large entities **KZT 150 million (USD 351,431)**. Exemptions: agricultural, food and medical sectors, and banks withdrawing from correspondent accounts at the NBRK.

### p.203 — Section 6: Market forecasts; 6.1 Forecast installed base (internal Page 16)
- **Figure 6.1: Forecast ATM installed base, 2023-2028:**
  - ATMs: 12,550 (2023), 12,700 (2024), 12,800 (2025), 12,900 (2026), 12,950 (2027), **13,000 (2028)**; CAGR **0.7%**.
  - Absolute growth: 159, 150, 100, 100, 50, **50**.
  - Percentage growth: 1.3%, 1.2%, 0.8%, 0.8%, 0.4%, **0.4%**.
  - ATMs per million people: 647, 655, 660, 665, 668, **670**; CAGR **0.7%**.
- Total ATMs forecast to reach **13,000 by end-2028**, a net increase of **5%** on the 2022 figure of 12,391; density **670 ATMs per million people**, up from 639 at end-2022.
- Installed base to grow at ~**1% per year** 2023-2028, with banks adding **between 50 and 160 new ATMs each year**.
- Growth driven by banks expanding into rural/underbanked regions; financial inclusion a key aim of Kazakhstan 2030 and 2050 government roadmaps.
- Bank branch numbers forecast to fall from **1,697 at end-2022 to 1,440 by end-2028** as branches are rationalised; shift to remote/digital channels.

### p.205 — 6.3 Forecast ATM cash withdrawal number and value: Figure 6.4 (internal Page 18)
- **Figure 6.4: Number of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (bars = annual number in millions, left axis 0-400; line = average number per ATM per month, right axis 0-2,500). Annual number: ~255 (2018), peak ~322 (2019), ~300 (2020), ~270 (2021), ~260 (2022), then declining gently through forecast to ~230 (2028). Average per ATM per month line peaks ~2,400 in 2019 then declines steadily to ~1,477 in 2028.
- Cash withdrawals to see very slow decline: **230 million withdrawals in 2028**, a **12%** reduction on the 2022 figure of **260 million**.
- New users in remote areas offset by intensifying cashless habits of urban dwellers; QR-code payments replacing cash for everyday payments.
- Average withdrawals **per ATM per month** falls to **1,477 in 2028** vs **1,746 in 2022**. Source: NBRK, Kazakhstani ATM deployers and RBR analysis.

### p.206 — Figure 6.5 Value of cash withdrawals (internal Page 19)
- **Figure 6.5: Value of cash withdrawals, 2018-2028** (bars = annual value KZT bn, left axis 0-24,000; line = average value KZT per withdrawal, right axis 0-120,000). Annual value rises steadily from ~11,500 KZT bn (2018) to ~19,600 (2022, dark) and onward to ~23,300 KZT bn (2028, lighter forecast bars). Average value line dips slightly 2018-2019 then climbs strongly, leveling near ~101,000 KZT by 2028.
- Total value to grow at ~**2% per year**, reaching **KZT 23.3 trillion (USD 50.6 billion at 2022 rate) by 2028** — a **19%** increase on 2022. Inflation is the key driver.
- Average value of a cash withdrawal forecast to rise to **KZT 101,431 (USD 220 at 2022 rate) by 2028**, vs **KZT 75,202 (USD 163) in 2022**. Behavior partly reflects COVID-era habit of taking larger amounts per transaction. Source: NBRK, Kazakhstani ATM deployers and RBR analysis.
