## What this is
The official Printec Group company presentation, dated January 2026 (114 slides). This is the single canonical internal reference for **what Printec sells, who its vendor partners are, and its headline company facts**. Agents should use it to map external market signals (e.g. a bank tendering for cash recyclers, a regulator mandating AML) to the specific Printec solution and the underlying vendor that fulfils it.

Structure: 01 Who we are (slides 4-15), 02 Financials (16-17), 03 Business solutions (18-69), 04 Our services (71-74), 05 Working with the best / partners & customers (76-78), 06 Best practices / case studies (80-85), 07 Our vendors (86-113).

## Company facts (Slide 5 "At a glance")
- **17 countries** across CEE/SEE; **38 years** in business (Greece est. 1988; group re-domiciled to Austria 2019, earlier in Cyprus 2008) (Slide 6).
- **1,000+ employees**; **21,500 ATMs**; **500+ POSs** [as printed]; **450,000 clients**; EcoVadis Silver Medal, top 15% globally 2025 (Slides 5, 12).
- Recent M&A: majority stakes in **NeoInfo (2023)** and **KEA (2025)**; CardPay Systems acquired in Ukraine 2011; NCR SEE subsidiaries acquired 1999 (Slide 6).
- Service footprint: **500+ repair/maintenance engineers across 100+ service points** (Slide 72); **120+ professional-services experts** (Slide 74).

## Key points
- Use the product table to translate a market signal into Printec's offer + the vendor behind it (e.g. cash recycling → NCR/Glory/Sesami; AML/KYC → IMTF Siron / FICO; HSM/PCI → Thales).
- Printec is positioned as an integrator/distributor: most flagship products are vendor HW (NCR, Verifone, Castles, Glory, Thales) wrapped with Printec in-house SW (x-core, x-pos, x-visio, POSight, Bizwire, telemetry) and managed services.
- Compliance posture is a selling point: ISO certifications, GDPR, eIDAS, PCI PIN/PTS, EcoVadis Silver (Slides 14, 43, 52, 67).
- All figures above are as printed on the cited slides (some labels, e.g. "500+ POSs" and "38 years" vs "35+ years", appear inconsistently in the source); treat as the deck's own claims, not independently verified.
