Internal Printec reference workbook of strategic **partners** and **competitors** in banking payments and compliance. Two sheets: **Partners** (Namirial, IMTF) and **Competitors** (Payten / Asseco SEE). Updated version superseding the 2026-06-23 digest — IMTF newly added as a partner and entries expanded.

## Key points
- **Partners** (capabilities Printec can position alongside): **Namirial** — digital trust / e-signature / identity stack (eIDAS, Qualified Trust Service Provider). **IMTF** — Swiss RegTech anti-financial-crime suite (Siron®One: AML monitoring, KYC/CDD/EDD, sanctions screening, fraud detection, regulatory reporting, digital onboarding); 37 yrs, 300 experts, 1,000+ customers, 90+ countries, acquired Siron from FICO in 2022.
- **Competitor**: **Payten / Asseco SEE** — Asseco SEE's payments brand; end-to-end card/cardless payments, acquiring/issuing, ATM & POS, SoftPOS/mPOS, processing outsourcing; most relevant on payment-gateway / merchant-acceptance / processing deals. Trust signals: PCI DSS Level 1, ISO 27001:2022, ISO 22301:2019 (Türkiye), PCI 3DS Core Security Standard.
