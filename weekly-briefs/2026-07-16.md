## The fuller opportunity list (plain language)

- **Hungary — the legally forced ATM programme just gained teeth.** On top of the ~1,039 net-new rural machines banks must install (Phase 2 due 31 December, with published bank-by-bank quotas), the central bank now requires two hard things: from 1 July, forint may only be recirculated through ATMs fitted with an **approved banknote validator** (a certify-and-retrofit requirement), and from 1 August the largest bank, MBH, must keep **≥98% ATM availability** or face the regulator. Certified recyclers + validators + a managed uptime contract is the strongest combined hardware-and-services signal in the footprint. Pursue OTP, MBH, K&H and Erste through the NCR Atleos channel. *(High, XL)*
- **Romania — two funded windows, best worked before the Brink's-Atleos merger closes.** CEC Bank (a ~1,484-machine state bank) launched five self-service "Digital Agencies" with video-banking on 5 June and is deciding on national scale-up — a live chance to shape the vendor choice — while replacing its core system. Banca Transilvania approved a **RON 1.02bn (+20.6%) 2026 budget** with named cards, cash-processing and hardware lines. The point-of-sale estate is growing (the ATM count is roughly flat on the primary statistics), and the instant-payment receive deadline lands in early 2027. *(High, XL / L)* *(Note: the master table records "dual ATM+POS growth," but the central-bank statistics show Romanian ATMs roughly flat/slightly down — treat the ATM half as flat, not growing.)*
- **Albania — the statutory card-acceptance rollout.** Card acceptance becomes compulsory for **all businesses by 31 December 2026** (phase 1 already passed), deposit-capable ATMs more than doubled, and Printec is effectively the market-wide NCR software/maintenance partner (NCR runs ~95% of Albanian ATMs). A large terminal/terminal-management opportunity plus recycler upside. *(High, XL)*
- **Bulgaria — sell cash optimisation now that the euro surge is over.** The free lev-to-euro exchange window closed 30 June and dual price-display ends 8 August, so the extra cash workload the banks built for the changeover collapses and they will right-size over-built fleets — a good moment for OptiCash recalibration and a Verification-of-Payee/fraud bundle ahead of the early-2027 instant-payment deadline. *(High, M — the biggest gap is that DSK's ATM vendor is still unknown; note the exact Bulgarian IPR date is being reconciled — 1 Jan 2027 as a euro-area member vs the 9 Jan 2027 non-euro track — so quote "early 2027" until confirmed.)*
- **Cyprus — defend and upsell, with a digital-euro hook.** Bank of Cyprus is renewing its branch network where Printec is the confirmed cash-recycler/POS incumbent, and BoC + JCC were just named to the **ECB digital-euro pilot** (14 July) — an early acceptance/HSM angle. H1 results land 4 August. *(High, L)*
- **Slovakia — a live acceptance mandate plus a recycler swap.** Cashless acceptance became mandatory on 1 May, and banks are swapping withdrawal-only ATMs for recyclers (Tatra's "Bankomat 2.0", SLSP card-less deposit). Sell POS/terminal-management and recyclers directly — but watch that SLSP moved its ATM software to a competitor (Asseco). *(High, L)*
- **North Macedonia — ride the NBG/Stopanska programme.** Stopanska Banka (owned by NBG) is running an NBG-funded transformation — a new flagship branch with a 24/7 self-service zone opened 26 May — while deposit-capable ATMs grow +16.6% a year. Lead with managed services, OptiCash and AML. *(High, XL)*
- **The EU compliance wave — H2 2026 is the buying window.** A stack of rules with 2026 deadlines (the **Cyber Resilience Act**'s incident-reporting duty from 11 September, which puts ATMs/POS/HSMs in scope; the first DORA fines; AMLA's provisional ~40-bank supervised list by end-September) turns the rest of the year into a concentrated market for identity, monitoring and resilience tooling — core Printec territory, with Alpha and Eurobank (where Printec runs the FICO/Siron platform) as repeatable references. *(High, XL)*
- **Ukraine — the biggest hardware prize, now a contested defence not a clean renewal.** PrivatBank has reconfirmed a **~800-unit deposit/recycler ATM buy for 2026** and Printec is the incumbent (1,140 NCR recyclers signed Jan 2025) — but as of 14 July **no bulk lot has appeared on the Prozorro procurement portal**, and rivals Diebold Nixdorf Ukraine and Servus (SSI) have been winning the spare-parts lots, so the fleet is now mixed. Monitor Prozorro weekly and brief NCR Atleos on an incumbent-defence bid. *(High, XL — installed base to defend.)*
