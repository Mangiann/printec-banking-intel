- **Western Balkans instant rail.** A shared instant-payment system (built by Italy's central bank) goes live across Albania, Bosnia, Kosovo, Montenegro and North Macedonia around July 2026, on top of genuinely growing ATM/POS markets (Montenegro fleet +23%).
