# Glossary

Plain-language definitions, maintained by the Orchestrator. Seed terms:

- **ATM pooling** — several banks sharing one jointly-operated ATM network instead of each running its own, to cut costs.
- **ATM-as-a-Service / managed services** — a vendor (like Printec) owns/runs the bank's ATMs for a fee; bank turns capex into opex.
- **Acquiring (card acquiring)** — the business of enabling merchants to accept card payments; the "acquirer" processes the merchant's side of the transaction.
- **Cash recycling** — ATMs/devices that accept deposited cash, validate it, and re-dispense it to the next customer — fewer cash-in-transit trips, lower cost.
- **CIT (cash-in-transit)** — armored transport companies moving cash between banks, branches, and ATMs.
- **Cost-to-income ratio** — bank efficiency measure; pressure to lower it drives branch closures, self-service, and outsourcing.
- **DORA** — EU Digital Operational Resilience Act; forces banks to manage IT risk and vendor (third-party) risk formally. In force Jan 2025.
- **eKYC / digital onboarding** — opening a bank account / verifying identity remotely, without a branch visit.
- **IAD (independent ATM deployer)** — non-bank company operating its own ATM fleet (e.g., Euronet).
- **Instant payments** — credit transfers that settle in seconds, 24/7 (SEPA Instant in the EU). EU regulation forces banks to offer them.
- **ISO 20022** — the new global standard message format for payments; banks must migrate their systems to it.
- **POS terminal** — the card payment device at a merchant's checkout.
- **PSD3 / PSR** — upcoming EU payment services rules (successor to PSD2); affects fraud liability, open banking, acquiring.
- **RFP / tender** — formal request for vendors to bid on a contract; public institutions must publish them.
- **Self-service** — customer-operated devices (ATMs, kiosks) replacing teller transactions.
- **CPV code** — the EU's standard numbering for what a tender is buying (e.g., 30123200-9 = ATMs/cash dispensers); lets you search tenders across languages. *(added by Agent 2, 2026-06-12)*
- **Prozorro / SEAP / ΚΗΜΔΗΣ / CAIS EOP** — the national electronic public-procurement portals of Ukraine, Romania, Greece and Bulgaria respectively. *(added by Agent 2, 2026-06-12)*
- **White-label / multi-bank ATM** — an ATM not branded to one bank, serving customers of all banks (e.g., the planned ELTA post-office ATMs in Greece). *(added by Agent 2, 2026-06-12)*
- **VoP (Verification of Payee)** — mandatory pre-transfer check that the recipient name matches the IBAN; anti-fraud requirement of the EU Instant Payments Regulation. *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-12)*
- **CTPP (Critical ICT Third-Party Provider)** — tech vendor designated under DORA as critical to EU finance and placed under direct EU oversight (first list Nov 2025: AWS, Microsoft, Google, etc.). *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-12)*
- **TLPT (Threat-Led Penetration Testing)** — DORA-mandated "ethical hacker" attack simulations on major banks' live systems, at least every 3 years. *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-12)*
- **AMLA / AMLR** — the EU's new anti-money-laundering authority (Frankfurt, operational since Jul 2025) and the directly-applicable AML Regulation replacing national rules from 10 Jul 2027. *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-12)*
- **EAA (European Accessibility Act)** — EU law (applies since 28 Jun 2025) requiring new ATMs/self-service terminals and banking services to be accessible to people with disabilities; old terminals tolerated until end of economic life, service transition ends 2030. *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-12)*
- **Structured addresses (CBPR+)** — ISO 20022 rule that payment-message addresses use defined fields, not free text; free-text addresses rejected after Nov 2026. *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-12)*
- **ORD (Operational Readiness Date)** — date from which a newly admitted SEPA country's payment providers can go live in the schemes (Serbia: earliest May 2026). *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-12)*
- **Core banking system** — the bank's central software holding all accounts and transactions; replacing it is the biggest IT project a bank does, and channel/self-service renewal typically follows go-live. *(added by Agent 1, 2026-06-12)*
- **Phygital** — branch model mixing digital self-service (ATMs, recyclers, kiosks) with human advisors (e.g., Eurobank's next-gen branches). *(added by Agent 1, 2026-06-12)*
- **Voluntary separation scheme (VSS)** — paid program for bank staff to leave voluntarily; usually precedes branch network or operating-model change. *(added by Agent 1, 2026-06-12)*
- **ROTE (return on tangible equity)** — profitability measure; a high ROTE signals the bank has budget capacity for investment. *(added by Agent 1, 2026-06-12)*
- **Multi-acquiring double counting** — when several payment firms serve the same POS/ATM, ECB statistics may count the terminal more than once; treat terminal counts as trends, not exact estate sizes. *(added by Agent 4, 2026-06-12)*
- **Dual circulation period** — short window after a euro changeover when both old and new currency are accepted in shops (Bulgaria: 1–15 Jan 2026). *(added by Agent 4, 2026-06-12)*
- **SSI / PTN (ECB datasets)** — ECB Data Portal series for bank structural indicators (branches, employees) and payment terminal counts (ATMs, POS) per country; updated annually/half-yearly. *(added by Agent 4, 2026-06-12)*
- **IRIS / RoPay / qvik / IPS** — the national instant-payment schemes of Greece, Romania, Hungary and Serbia respectively. *(added by Agent 4, 2026-06-12)*
- **Vault cash** — the banknotes loaded inside ATMs; the operator pays interest to borrow this cash, so rising rates raise ATM operating costs (added by Agent 6, 2026-06-12).
- **TCR (teller cash recycler)** — branch-counter machine that accepts, validates, stores and re-dispenses cash for tellers; core of branch cash automation (added by Agent 6, 2026-06-12).
- **FLM / SLM (first-/second-line maintenance)** — FLM is routine ATM care (paper, jams, cleaning, cash loading); SLM is technical repair of modules. Often outsourced separately (Serbian: "опслуга банкомата"). *(added by Agent 2, 2026-06-13)*
- **NDR (network detection & response)** — security software watching internal network traffic for intrusions (e.g., Cisco Stealthwatch); a common DORA-driven bank purchase. *(added by Agent 2, 2026-06-13)*
- **SCT / SCT Inst** — SEPA Credit Transfer (standard euro bank transfer) and its instant version (settles in seconds, 24/7). Serbia went live on SCT in May 2026; SCT Inst is the next phase. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **A2A (account-to-account) payment** — paying straight from one bank account to another (e.g. by phone number or QR) instead of via a card; the model behind IRIS, RoPay, IPS, BLIK. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **SoftPOS** — turning an ordinary smartphone into a card-acceptance device (tap-to-pay) with no separate terminal; a low-cost POS alternative Mellon and others now sell. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **DCC (dynamic currency conversion)** — at an ATM/POS, offering a foreign cardholder the choice to be charged in their home currency; a tourist-driven revenue line, large for Euronet in Greece. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **BPO (business process outsourcing)** — paying an outside firm to run a whole bank function (call centre, back office, fraud checks); Greek banks increasingly route this to Mellon, ICAP and others. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **PMO (project management office)** — the in-house team that coordinates a bank's portfolio of change projects; PMO hiring usually comes just before a wave of new projects (and vendor selection). *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **BAS (Branch Automation Solutions)** — Diebold Nixdorf's brand for end-to-end ATM/branch automation and network management; its move from selling hardware into managed services, competing with Printec. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **AMS / DRS (Brink's segments)** — ATM Managed Services and Digital Retail Solutions: Brink's higher-margin businesses that run banks' ATM fleets end-to-end, not just transport cash. Europe is Brink's highest-AMS region (44%). *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **RTGS (real-time gross settlement)** — the central-bank system that settles large interbank payments one-by-one in real time (e.g. the NBS system upgraded in Serbia). *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **Order backlog** — the value of signed-but-not-yet-delivered contracts a vendor holds; a forward indicator of revenue (tracked for Asseco SEE/Payten). *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-13)*
- **TIPS clone / Western Balkans IPS** — a copy of the Eurosystem's TIPS instant-settlement platform, built and run by Banca d'Italia for Western-Balkans central banks (AL, BA, XK, ME; MK later) so their banks can settle instant payments 24/7 in local currencies; operational expected mid-2026. *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-15)*
- **EuroPA / EPI** — two pan-European account-to-account payment networks. EuroPA links national instant schemes (Greece's IRIS, Italy's Bancomat Pay, Spain's Bizum, Portugal's MB WAY) for cross-border instant payments; EPI runs the "Wero" wallet. *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-15)*
- **SREP / Pillar 2** — the ECB's annual Supervisory Review and Evaluation Process; a weak score forces a bank to hold extra capital ("Pillar 2"). From 2026, DORA digital-resilience performance feeds the score — weak ICT resilience now costs capital. *(added by Agent 3, 2026-06-15)*
- **x-core** — Printec's vendor-agnostic ("multivendor") ATM software that runs across mixed-brand fleets (NCR, Diebold, Hyosung, OKI), letting one bank manage all its machines from one platform. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **HSM (Hardware Security Module)** — a tamper-proof device that stores cryptographic keys and processes PINs/card crypto; mandatory for card issuing, instant payments and (future) digital-euro security (Printec resells Thales payShield). *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **MFM / CDM / IDM** — Multifunction Machine, Cash-Deposit Machine, Intelligent-Deposit Module: ATM variants that accept cash. An MFM/recycler both takes and re-dispenses notes; a CDM only accepts. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **eKasa** — Slovakia's electronic cash-register/fiscalisation system; under Act 384/2025 every eKasa merchant must accept a cashless payment (card or QR) for any sale over €1 from 1 May 2026. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **FATF grey list** — the Financial Action Task Force's "jurisdictions under increased monitoring"; banks dealing with a grey-listed country (Bosnia & Herzegovina, added 19 Jun 2026) must apply Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) — extra identity/source-of-funds checks. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **EDD (Enhanced Due Diligence)** — deeper customer/transaction checks applied to higher-risk clients, countries or products; a driver of AML/screening software demand. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **IBNS / banknote neutralisation** — anti-theft systems that stain or destroy banknotes inside an ATM if it is attacked (e.g. an explosive attack), making the stolen cash worthless; demand rising as attacks shift east into the footprint. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **ATMaaS (ATM-as-a-Service)** — full outsourcing where a provider (Euronet, NCR Atleos, Brink's) owns and runs a bank's entire ATM fleet for a recurring fee; the model that is locking up estates and narrowing hardware-sale opportunities. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **MiCA / CASP / TFR** — the EU's Markets-in-Crypto-Assets regulation, the Crypto-Asset Service Provider licence it requires (hard backstop 1 Jul 2026), and the Transfer-of-Funds "Travel Rule" requiring originator/beneficiary data on every crypto transfer. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **EUDI Wallet** — the EU Digital Identity Wallet each member state must offer by ~6 Dec 2026; banks become "relying parties" that accept it for remote account opening and strong authentication. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-06-25)*
- **CRA (Cyber Resilience Act)** — EU law setting cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements — which explicitly includes ATMs, POS terminals, kiosks and HSMs. Vulnerability/incident-reporting duties apply from 11 Sep 2026 and the CE-marking (conformity) requirement from 11 Dec 2027, so Printec is in scope both as a maker of devices and as the managed-service operator of them. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-07-16)*
- **IPR (Instant Payments Regulation)** — the EU rule that forces banks to send and receive instant euro credit transfers at no extra cost, with Verification-of-Payee built in. Key footprint dates: Bulgaria and Romania must be able to *receive* instant payments by 9 Jan 2027; non-euro countries' banks must fully comply (send + name check) by 9 Jul 2027. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-07-16)*
- **NIS2** — the EU's updated network-and-information-security directive; widens mandatory cyber-risk management and incident reporting to more sectors (banks included) and is being written into each country's law (in force in Bulgaria from ~Feb 2026). Sits alongside DORA as a driver of security spend. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-07-16)*
- **PQC / CBOM (post-quantum cryptography / cryptographic bill of materials)** — PQC is the move to encryption that a future quantum computer could not break; the EU wants banks to inventory all their cryptography (the "CBOM") by end-2026 and migrate the highest-risk systems by 2030. This forces a fleet-wide refresh of HSMs and keys — a Printec/Thales opportunity. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-07-16)*
- **MNB banknote validator** — a machine on the Hungarian central bank's (MNB) approved list that checks a banknote is genuine and fit before an ATM re-dispenses it. From 1 Jul 2026 forint may only be recirculated through ATMs fitted with such a validator — a certified-hardware retrofit requirement. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-07-16)*
- **pKYC (perpetual KYC)** — continuously re-checking a customer's identity/risk in the background instead of only at onboarding and periodic reviews; moving from pilots to rollout and cutting manual review effort sharply. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-07-16)*
- **Joint Supervisory Team (JST)** — the mixed ECB-and-national-supervisor team assigned to each large euro-area bank. It is the body a bank files its supervisory homework with — including, by 31 Oct 2026, the action plan on AI-enabled cyber threats and end-of-life technology that the ECB demanded in July 2026. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **End-of-life technology** — hardware or software the maker no longer supports with security patches. Supervisors now treat it as a named risk a bank must inventory and commit to replacing, which is what turns an ageing ATM fleet from an operations question into a board one. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Cash-in / cash-out ATM** — plain shorthand for whether a machine can take money in as well as pay it out. Most of the footprint's fleets are still cash-out only: roughly 1,840 of the sites run by Bulgaria's four largest banks (UBB 492, Postbank 273, DSK 514, UniCredit Bulbank 564 — an earlier 'roughly 1,500' in this entry was wrong), 957 of 1,917 in the Czech shared network, 457 of 568 at Serbia's AikBank. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Locator API** — the public "find a branch or ATM" feed on a bank's own website. Because it lists each machine and what it can do, it is the most reliable public way to count a bank's deposit-capable devices, and this cycle it replaced guesswork in eleven markets. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Two-stage tender (qualification then auction)** — a procurement run in two halves: bidders first prove they meet the technical requirements, sometimes by handing over sample machines, and only those who pass go into a price auction. PrivatBank's 1,000-unit recycler tender works this way, which is why price is not yet visible. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Value-unscoped opportunity** — a real, winnable contest whose buyer has published no euro figure. Most tenders in this footprint never publish a value, so a missing number is a gap in disclosure, not evidence there is no deal. These are counted in every opportunity list and contribute zero to the value bars. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Presumption of conformity** — when a product built to an officially-cited standard is legally assumed to meet the law. For accessibility there is currently **no** such standard cited under the European Accessibility Act, so a manufacturer must self-declare against the law's own requirements and hold the test evidence — which makes proven accessibility a genuine tender advantage. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Relying party** — an organisation that accepts someone else's digital identity credential instead of checking documents itself. From 24 Dec 2027 EU banks must accept the EU digital identity wallet on request, which makes every bank a relying party and creates a verifier to build or buy. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Shared / pooled ATM network** — an arrangement where several banks' customers use one common set of machines on equal terms. Croatia is legislating one for 1 Jan 2027, Czechia already runs two competing versions, and Slovakia's banks are openly discussing a third. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **goAML** — the United Nations-built reporting system many financial intelligence units use to receive suspicious-transaction reports. Slovak obliged entities must be registered in it by 30 Nov 2026, which forces every bank's reporting interface to be rebuilt to its schema. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Interchange fee** — the slice of a card payment that the merchant's bank pays the cardholder's bank. Capping it (Albania is drafting caps of 0.2% and 0.3%) squeezes acquiring margin and pushes banks to outsource terminal estates rather than own them. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Unattended (indirect) video identification** — remote identity checking with no bank employee on the call, the software instead proving the document is genuine, that a live person is present, and stamping each capture with a trusted timestamp. Serbia legalised it in May 2026 and gave banks twelve months to make their technology comply. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-03)*
- **Takeover threshold** — the minimum share acceptance a bidder sets for its offer to succeed. Raiffeisen lowered its Addiko threshold to just above 55%; the official result published on 3 Aug 2026 records acceptances for 10,831,435 shares, which is **56.16% of the shares subject to the offer and 55.55% of total share capital** — the same result measured two ways, not two conflicting figures. NLB, needing 50% plus one share, reached only 31.22%. *(added 2026-08-03; corrected and expanded 2026-08-31)*

- **Carve-out** — selling part of a group to a different buyer as a condition of, or alongside, a takeover. When Raiffeisen bought Addiko it kept Croatia, Slovenia and Austria and agreed to sell the Serbian, two Bosnian and Montenegrin banks onward to Alta Group — so one takeover produced two different owners, and two different buying centres. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Transitional Services Agreement (TSA)** — a temporary contract under which the seller keeps running IT and back-office services for a business it has sold, until the buyer can stand up its own. It matters commercially because it carries a hard expiry: the Addiko carve-out banks get 12 months plus one 12-month extension, so every shared service must be replaced by roughly 2028–29. The expiry date, not the deal-closing date, is what sets the procurement calendar. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Preliminary market consultation** — a formal stage before a tender in which a public buyer publishes a draft requirement and invites suppliers to comment. It is the only point at which the specification can still be shaped, which usually makes it worth more than the tender that follows. Greece's ELTA opened one on 18 Aug 2026 with comments due 10 Sep. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Quiet period** — the weeks before a listed company reports results, during which it stops discussing commercially sensitive plans. It acts as a deadline in reverse: scope has to be shaped before the shutter comes down, not after. Raiffeisen's began 23 Oct 2026. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Recital** — the numbered explanatory paragraphs at the front of an EU regulation. They are not the binding rule but courts and regulators use them to interpret it, so a recital naming ATMs and self-service terminals — as recital 11 of the instant-payments amendment does — is what turns a payments rule into a device-estate obligation. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Non-responsive bid** — a tender submission rejected on process grounds rather than on price or merit, for example for omitting a required form. It can decide an award outright: in Kosovo, SWIFT bid 56% below Nexi and was ruled non-responsive, so the more expensive bid won a two-horse race by default. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Sole-bidder (single-bid) procurement** — a tender that attracts only one offer. It is a warning sign about a captured account rather than a bargain: Banka Slovenije re-ran its banknote-sorter maintenance contract after cancelling a first attempt, received the same single bidder at exactly the same €1,890,000, and accepted it — 96% above the previous six-year contract. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Phase 1 / Phase 2 merger inquiry** — the two stages of a competition review. Phase 1 is a first-look screen with a statutory deadline (the UK CMA's on Brink's/NCR Atleos is 22 Oct 2026); if concerns remain it escalates to a longer, deeper Phase 2. Which way it goes decides how quickly a supplier changes hands. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **ATM hypervisor / virtualisation layer** — software that lets a newer operating system run on ATM hardware the manufacturer no longer supports. It is being sold to banks specifically as a way to avoid buying new machines, which makes it a direct competitor to a hardware refresh rather than an add-on to one. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **TIPS Clone** — a copy of the Eurosystem's instant-payment settlement platform, built for a non-euro central bank with Banca d'Italia's support, so a country can run domestic instant payments on the same technical model as the euro area. Montenegro went live on 20 Jul 2026; Albania, Kosovo and North Macedonia are in the November 2026 onboarding window. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Request to Pay / alias (proxy) lookup** — two things layered on top of an instant-payment rail once it works. Request to Pay lets a payee ask for a specific payment rather than waiting for one; an alias or proxy lookup lets a customer pay to a phone number instead of an IBAN, which needs a directory that maps one to the other. Both are the second wave of work after go-live — Montenegro dates its phase to March 2027. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Fitness certification (banknotes)** — official approval that a machine can judge whether a used banknote is clean and intact enough to be handed back out. Without it a machine may accept notes but not recycle them, which is why no customer-operated machine in Albania can currently re-dispense lek even though certified machines exist there. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Economic-operator account** — a supplier login some national procurement portals require before notices can even be read. Where one is needed, the market is invisible from outside — though it is worth testing the assumption: Kosovo turned out to publish awards, prices and losing bids as open PDFs on the central bank's own site. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **eKasa** — Slovakia's online cash-register system, through which sales are reported to the tax authority in real time. Rules attached to it — such as a duty to accept a cashless payment — reach every small merchant at once, which is what turns a tax measure into terminal demand. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
- **Cloudflare gate / bot protection** — an automated challenge some official websites use to block software agents. It is a research hazard rather than a market fact: North Macedonia's central bank looked like an empty market for a whole cycle purely because one agent could not get past the gate while another, using a real browser session, pulled the data out cell by cell. *(added by Orchestrator, 2026-08-31)*
