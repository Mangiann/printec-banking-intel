## What changed since last run

Four new items lead this run. Mellon Serbia won a POS-acquiring cooperation with Raiffeisen banka a.d. Beograd (supplying Ingenico EFTPOS terminals), extending its Serbian POS momentum and its best fresh opening (Medium win). COSMOTE Payments' payzy/payzy pro softPOS is now confirmed as an OTE Group EMI whose Android terminal app was built by Mellon Group, tightening the Mellon-telco payments link in Greece. Mellon Bulgaria surfaced as a headline partner/sponsor of Postbank's 35th-anniversary gala in Sofia, signalling a warm Postbank relationship worth pressing. In Cyprus, the Central Bank's centralised digital-onboarding/CLM tender was confirmed awarded to the Aqubix + Infocredit Group JV. On corrections: the Euronet Q2 2026 earnings date was contradicted and corrected to Thursday 30/07/2026 (call 9:00 a.m. ET), not a Wednesday 29/07 report; the Greek Tap-to-Pay launch-provider list (myPOS, Viva, Worldline, NBG Pay "coming soon") and several Serbian/JV deal counts and values remain only partially confirmed, with terminal counts undisclosed.
