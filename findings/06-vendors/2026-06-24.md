**A quiet fortnight for new contract wins, but three structural moves matter:** (1) the **Brink's–NCR Atleos $6.6bn merger cleared US antitrust (HSR, ~12 May) and now faces its first European gate — a UK CMA Phase 1 inquiry with a 14 Aug 2026 decision deadline; both shareholder votes are 30 Jun 2026.** (2) **Euronet's CrediaBank ATM takeover went operational** (14 May) — 2,500+ Euronet ATMs now serve CrediaBank customers and its 141 ATMs are folding in, cementing Euronet as Greece's dominant ATM operator even before the merchant-acquiring leg closes (Q3 2026).
