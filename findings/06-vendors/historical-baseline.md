## Headline takeaways (2024 → mid-2026)

1. **The ATM business model shifted from selling boxes to selling outcomes.** NCR Atleos grew ATM-as-a-Service ~33% in 2025 (ARR $288m, record Q4 bookings >$200m) and Diebold's banking growth was led by DN Series cash recyclers, with Europe explicitly strong. Banks in the footprint will increasingly buy *operated* ATM channels, not hardware — the Printec managed-services pitch is with, not against, the market.
2. **Greece became a live battleground in 2025–26.** Atleos built an owned Cashzone IAD presence (DirectPay/iXchange, Jul 2025), then sold ATMaaS direct to Epirus Bank (Jan 2026); Euronet agreed to buy CrediaBank's merchant acquiring AND take over full management of its ATM fleet (Jan 2026, close Q3 2026); Mellon completed the NBG 700-recycler rollout and then co-hosted the **ATM Innovation & Strategy Forum** (Athens, April 2026) with KAL + Brink's AMS — where NBG and Eurobank executives heard an ATM pooling/outsourcing pitch using a Lloyds/Brink's UK model as the live blueprint. **The competitive landscape for ATM outsourcing in Greece is now fully formed and transitioning from pitch to deals.** Three global players + one local rival all monetizing the Greek ATM/POS estate.
3. **Consolidation accelerated sharply in late 2025 – early 2026**: Euronet–CoreCard (Q4 2025), Global Payments–Worldpay close (Jan 2026), **Brink's–NCR Atleos $6.6bn (Feb 2026, close Q1 2027)**. Hardware, cash logistics and processing are merging into end-to-end "cash ecosystem" players — squeezing pure integrators from both sides.
4. **Worldline collapsed as a competitive force** over the window: three profit warnings and CEO exit (Sep 2024), fraud cover-up scandal (Jun 2025, −38% in a day), BB downgrade (Aug 2025), €4.7bn impairments and forced divestments + €500m capital raise (FY2025, reported Feb 2026). Yet in Greece it **bought out Eurobank's remaining 20% of Worldline Greece for €72m (May 2026)** — doubling down rather than exiting; Cardlink stays a committed POS/acquiring competitor there.
5. **Mellon expanded the Hyosung/KAL playbook regionally**: NBG Greece (Jan 2024) → Hyosung showcase Sofia (Jun 2024) → Hyosung+KAL launch in Romania (Oct 2025) → IRIS instant payments on POS/SoftPOS (Dec 2025). A deliberate multi-country challenge to incumbent ATM vendors/integrators.
