## What changed since last run

The biggest new development is regulatory, not commercial: on 30/06/2026 both Brink's and NCR Atleos shareholders overwhelmingly approved the ~$6.6bn Brink's acquisition of NCR Atleos (Brink's meeting >91% of eligible votes, NATL merger proposal 59.4M FOR vs 92k AGAINST), though the UK CMA inquiry remains pre-Phase-1, so close timing and any impact on Printec's NCR-aligned partner channel are still open. On the incumbency side, verification confirmed Printec's anchor position at PrivatBank Ukraine (16/01/2025 contract for 1,140 NCR recyclers, 838.64M UAH); however, new-but-unconfirmed Prozorro traces suggest Diebold Nixdorf and third parties have won PrivatBank recycler spare-parts lots, implying the fleet may be mixed rather than solely Printec/NCR — worth watching but not yet verified. Among fresh tender openings, Geldservice Austria's EUR 9.1M combined recycler framework (TED 305531-2026) is the most actionable NEW procurement in-footprint. Note two baseline corrections: the Mellon Group scale figures (revenue/headcount) remain unverifiable and conflicting, and several Bulgarian TED-sweep claims (Enigma Guard CCTV, ET Hristo Tsonkovski) could not be independently confirmed on re-check.
