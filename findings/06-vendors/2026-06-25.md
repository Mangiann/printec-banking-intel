## What changed since last run

The most consequential development is the Brink's–NCR Atleos $6.6bn merger reaching its decision point: special shareholder meetings for both companies are now confirmed for 30/06/2026 (terms unchanged at $30 cash + 0.1574 BCO per NATL share), while the UK CMA remains pre-Phase-1 with its invitation-to-comment closed — meaning Printec's key ATM partner is about to change ownership. Second, NCR Atleos formally crowned Printec its "Global Partner of the Year - Europe" plus Regional and (for Printec Romania) Software Partner of the Year, a strong relationship signal to leverage now. Third, Bulgaria's 01/01/2026 euro adoption executed cleanly (~96% of ATMs dispensing euro from day one per BORICA), opening the cash-hardware refresh window that Mellon/Hyosung and others are already chasing. On the competitor side, Brink's Q1 2026 AMS+DRS grew 15% organic for a 13th straight quarter and Loomis Pay is newly entering POS with Castles hardware in the Nordics/Spain. On corrections: the myPOS/Advent close and the Albacon/Glory Czech Post and Diebold Nixdorf CIO-date items were flagged contradicted by verification; Mellon's EFTPOS base was revised up from the prior 250,000+ baseline to a vendor-stated 350,000+ deployed / 500,000+ supported; and several vendor-stated figures (Brink's Hellas stack, Datecs growth claim) remain unconfirmed.
