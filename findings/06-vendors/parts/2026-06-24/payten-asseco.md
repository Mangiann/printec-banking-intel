# Payten / Asseco SEE (ASEE) — competitor findings (2026-06-24)

DIRECT competitor. Tier: regional (SEE/CEE payments + ATM-outsourcing + POS/SoftPOS + digital channels). Group: Asseco South Eastern Europe (ASEE), Warsaw-listed; Payten is the payments arm; MoneyGet is the owned ATM network (Serbia + Montenegro + EU brands).

## NEW / CHANGED this run
- **Halkbank Macedonia — "Halk Mobi POS" (Payten SoftPOS) — 17 Mar 2026 (NEW).** Halkbank AD Skopje launched Halk Mobi POS, letting merchants accept card payments on a phone/tablet with no physical terminal. Payten SoftPOS. "Once again found its way to the Macedonian market" = repeat SoftPOS sale in MK. Threatens Printec Verifone/Castles terminal + TMS revenue in North Macedonia; software-only acceptance erodes hardware POS. Source: payten.com news 17/03/2026.
- **Payten Payment Gateway AI push — 16 Jun 2026 (NEW, group-level product).** AI-Driven Integration Assistant for merchant onboarding; "Revenue Engineer" payment-orchestration framework; Paratika "Unlimited Payment Link"; Payten/Paratika won Golden+Bronze 2025 WSpark Awards. Signals e-commerce/orchestration buildout (TR-anchored Paratika). Adjacent to Printec acquiring but outside ATM core.
