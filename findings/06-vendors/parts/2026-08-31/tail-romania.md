# Agent 6 — Long tail & NEW competitors, ROMANIA (run 31/08/2026)

Delta window 24/07/2026 → 31/08/2026. Global names (NCR Atleos, Diebold, Glory, Worldline, Euronet, Mellon, Payten/Asseco, Brink's, Hyosung/KAL) excluded — separate units.

**Headline reads**
1. **Channel block on Glory.** ROPECO București (Romanian Business Consult group) is Glory's authorised Romanian distributor and was publicly honoured at a **Glory EMEA Distributor event on 28/04/2026** for a 30-year partnership. ROPECO also carries Ingenico EFT-POS, HSM and safes. Printec cannot lead with Glory TCRs in Romania — the Glory channel is taken.
2. **EUTRON INVEST ROMANIA** is the strongest genuinely-new local cash-automation rival found: nationwide service (18 centres), RIBAO counters/sorters, Glory-class TCR/recycling systems, Gunnebo safes, Oberthur CIT, plus a delivered **Alpha Bank România digital self check-in kiosk** project. It swept **14 ANAF/DGRFP Cluj direct awards 04–25/08/2026**.
3. **MONTRAN** owns Romania's national instant-payments rail (RoPay, with TRANSFOND), sits sole-sourced inside **BNR (RON 477,840, 04/05/2026)** and went live at **UniCredit on 02/02/2026**; 20-year Banca Transilvania relationship.
4. **Self-service payment estate is consolidating away from bank ATMs**: SelfPay 13,000+ terminals / 3,000+ localities, PayPoint 35,000 assisted POS heading to 50,000 by 2028 plus Mastercard cash-withdrawal-at-POS scaling 14,000→26,000 sites, Mobile Distribution (Un-Doi) newly BNR-licensed with 2,500 terminals, EasyPay building its own kiosks.
5. **softPOS has removed the terminal from the small-merchant deal** under the 01/01/2026 card-acceptance mandate — Global Payments/BCR GP tom (incl. Tap to Pay on iPhone), ING SoftPOS, OTP POSibil, and now SIBS Romania's POS Sharing on SoftPOS (16/07/2026).
6. **certSIGN** is the Romanian eIDAS/eKYC incumbent moving hard on EUDI Wallet — a direct alternative to Printec's Namirial onboarding line.
7. **EAE Solutions B.V.** took a EUR 6.77m postal-automation award covering **both Pošta Slovenije and C.N. Poșta Română (11/06/2026)** — an adjacency threat to Printec S.I.'s Slovenian parcel-locker incumbency.

Negative finding carried from Agent 2 and re-used: SEAP/SICAP contains **no** ATM, recycler, sorter, POS or card-personalisation tender at all in 01/05–27/08/2026 — the Romanian bank-technology market is fought bank-by-bank in private, not on the portal.
