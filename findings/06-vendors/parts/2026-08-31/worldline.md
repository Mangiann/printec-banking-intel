## Worldline Financial Services division - Regional (all 17) (30/07/2026, primary, new=True)
Worldline's bank-facing Financial Services division (card issuing processing, acquiring processing, ATM management, account & instant payments) shrank again: Q2'26 revenue EUR 183m, -6.9% organic fully pruned; H1'26 EUR 367m, -7.1% organic. Management quantified 'EUR 60 million impact coming from contract terminations - EUR 15 million in Q1, EUR 30 million in Q2' and said Financial Services 'should return to growth no earlier than the second half of 2027'. No client names or geographies were disclosed. The same division's Q2 wins were all Western European (ICS core-operations outsourcing, an acquiring-processing back-up service, a domestic Verification of Payee scheme, Wero migration in the Netherlands) - nothing in CEE/SEE.
**Implies:** Banks are terminating legacy Worldline processing and ATM-management contracts at a EUR 60m-a-half run rate, which means outsourced bank estates are coming back to market across Europe. This is the single best Worldline-created opening for Printec: pitch x-core multivendor ATM software, ATM/device telemetry and monitoring, APTRA OptiCash cash optimisation and managed/field services to any footprint bank whose Worldline processing or ATM-management contract is up for renewal - and use INETCO transaction monitoring as the migration-risk answer that a bank leaving an outsourcer needs.
