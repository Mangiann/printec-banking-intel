## Jargon used (added to glossary)

- **CPV code** — the EU's standard numbering for what a tender is buying (e.g., 30123200-9 = ATMs/cash dispensers); lets you search tenders across languages.
- **Prozorro / SEAP / ΚΗΜΔΗΣ / CAIS EOP** — the national electronic public-procurement portals of Ukraine, Romania, Greece and Bulgaria respectively.
- **White-label / multi-bank ATM** — an ATM not branded to one bank, serving customers of all banks (as planned in the ELTA model).
