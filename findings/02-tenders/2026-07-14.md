## What changed since last run

The biggest development is that PrivatBank's anticipated ~800-unit recycler/multifunction ATM programme remains pre-tender: verification found no bulk replacement lot on Prozorro or tender.privatbank.ua (an unretrievable Angular shell), and all live 2026 PrivatBank ATM notices are small spare-parts/module awards (Servus, Diebold Nixdorf, Zabolotnyi, Tecpro) — so the XL prize is real but still unconfirmed. Several new re-tender openings surfaced where incumbents failed: Poczta Polska's 413-unit banknote-counter tender and Fina's EUR 400k coin-counting tender both closed with no winner, and Croatia's HP-Hrvatska pošta payment-software tender (est. EUR 2.55m) was cancelled outright. TEB Kosovo opened a cluster of live own-portal tenders (cash-handling machines 292-25, ATM kiosk 375-25, digital onboarding). On baselines, verification corrected the OPSEA eID deadline to 25/07/2026 (not the previously stated 27/07) and could not confirm the Bank of Albania banknote-packaging tender (site returns HTTP 403); the Česká pošta Albacon coin/banknote framework values were flagged as contradicted.
