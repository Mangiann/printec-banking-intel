## Key structural finding (drives everything below)
Slovenia adopted the euro in 2007, and its commercial banks are privatised (NLB privatised 2018–19; OTP banka Slovenija = Hungarian OTP; Intesa Sanpaolo Bank = Italian; Gorenjska banka = Serbian AIK; Addiko; Delavska hranilnica). None are contracting authorities under ZJN-3, so **commercial-bank ATM / recycler / POS purchases do NOT appear on e-narocanje** — they are procured privately. The portal therefore yields almost no Printec-core banking-hardware tenders; the Printec-relevant public buyers are (a) Banka Slovenije (central bank — cash-processing, banknote-destruction, security, IDM) and (b) public transport / municipal self-service payment & ticketing kiosks.
