### 6. Slovak Republic — Act 384/2025 Z. z. (Zákon o evidencii tržieb) — Slovakia
- **Signal:** Verified in the consolidated statute: § 15 'Bezhotovostná platba' obliges every seller subject to eKasa sales-recording to let the buyer pay CASHLESSLY on any transaction ABOVE EUR 1. § 25(5) sets the start date at 1 May 2026 — POSTPONED from the original 1 March 2026 by amending Act 30/2026 Z. z., which took effect 28/02/2026, i.e. two days before the original date. Compliance is met by ANY 'platobný prostriedok', explicitly including a QR-code payment order (§ 15(3)), for which the Financial Directorate (or a third party) supplies the technical means for the eKasa till to confirm the payment. Penalty for breaching § 15: nothing on first detection (instruction only), EUR 500–15,000 on the second detection (§ 17(1)(h)), EUR 3,000–40,000 on every further detection (§ 17(2)(a)). Exemption where there is no internet signal or the confirmation service is down.
- **Source:** Slov-Lex, Zbierka zákonov SR — Act 384/2025 Z. z., consolidated text effective 28/02/2026, 28/02/2026 — https://www.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2025/384/20260228.html (regulator)
- **Implies:** A nationwide, penalty-backed card-acceptance mandate — the single biggest structural driver of Slovak POS/SoftPOS demand. BUT the law deliberately allows a free QR/Pay-by-square route, so it does NOT force a terminal into every shop; the winnable segment is merchants who want a real terminal or an eKasa-integrated SoftPOS, and the eKasa-confirmation interface itself is a new integration surface.
- **Likelihood:** In force now; enforcement ramp-up through 2026-2027 as second detections start attracting fines | **Confidence:** Medium | **Size:** XL | **Win:** Medium
- **Follow-up:** Confirm whether the Financial Directorate has published the § 15(3) technical specification for eKasa payment confirmation and whether third parties can supply it — that spec is the gateway for a Printec SoftPOS/acceptance product in Slovakia.
