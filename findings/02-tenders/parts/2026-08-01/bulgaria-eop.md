# Bulgaria — CAIS EOP (app.eop.bg) — 01/08/2026

**Headline: the post-euro ATM / CDM / recycler / self-service tender pipeline on EOP is genuinely EMPTY — third nil return, now proved and explained.** Bulgarian commercial banks and the interbank cash-services company ДКУ АД are private and sit outside the Public Procurement Act, so ATM and recycler buying never reaches EOP or TED. What the euro changeover *did* put into public procurement is the surrounding cash perimeter: BNB cash-equipment adaptation, Bulgarian Posts counting machines and cash-in-transit, municipal counting-machine maintenance, and BORICA's sweep of public-sector POS acquiring.

EOP itself is unreadable programmatically (Angular shell on every path). All findings below come from the TED mirror + notice PDFs.

## Coverage
Fresh run (no checkpoint existed). Target was Bulgaria CAIS EOP (app.eop.bg) with focus on post-euro (01/01/2026) ATM/CDM/recycler/self-service/POS tenders plus BNB own procurement and Borica. EOP itself is unreadable programmatically (see access_issues), so the portal was covered through its authoritative EU mirror: TED Search API v3 with expert queries, then per-notice PDFs extracted locally with pdfminer.six. Search angles used, English + Bulgarian: (1) CPV sweeps for BGR on 30123200 ATMs, 30123600/610/620/630 coin handling, 30132200 banknote counting machines, 30142000, 66172000 card processing, 50310000/50530000/42670000 machine maintenance, 35125100 sensors, 32323500 CCTV, 79713000 guard services; (2) Bulgarian full-text queries: банкомат, банкнотоброячни, монетосортираща, монетоброячна, детектор за фалшиви банкноти, касова техника, ПОС терминал, устройство за самообслужване, АТМ, ресайклър, cash recycler, евро+банкноти, БОРИКА, Дружество за касови услуги/ДКУ; (3) buyer-name sweeps for БЪЛГАРСКА НАРОДНА БАНКА (79 notices from 01/07/2025, 35 from 01/01/2026), БЪЛГАРСКИ ПОЩИ (29 from 01/10/2025), СТОЛИЧНА ОБЩИНА. Twelve notice PDFs were opened and read in full. The headline requirement — post-euro ATM/CDM/recycler/self-service tenders on EOP — returned genuinely ZERO, and that nil result is proved and explained in the two CAIS EOP rows above (exact queries and their TOTAL counts are recorded there). What DID surface instead is the public-sector cash perimeter of the euro changeover: BNB cash-equipment adaptation and sensors, Bulgarian Posts counting machines and CIT, Sofia Municipality counting-machine maintenance, and BORICA's sweep of public-sector POS acquiring. Genuine gaps: below-EU-threshold Bulgarian notices never reach TED and could not be read on EOP without a browser; Borica AD and the commercial banks are private and publish no tenders, so no procurement signal exists for them by construction; ДКУ АД is likewise private and was identified only indirectly, through the justification text of a BNB notice. Baseline items A/B/C/D relate to other countries and were out of scope for this unit.

## Access issues
app.eop.bg (CAIS EOP): Angular SPA — /today, /today/<id>, /buyer/<id> and /api/* all return the same 7,500-byte JS shell (HTTP 200); POST /api/* returns 405 for real and invented paths alike, so no backend endpoint could be discovered from main.b1e95fb35f8a74c3.js either. Route used instead: TED Search API v3 (POST api.ted.europa.eu/v3/notices/search, no auth) plus GET https://ted.europa.eu/en/notice/<id>/pdf extracted locally with pdfminer.six — worked for all 12 PDFs (system pdftotext still absent on this host). www2.aop.bg/e-uslugi/otvoreni-danni-ot-rop/ — 403 to the fetch tool, 200 to curl with a desktop User-Agent; its only open-data links are two XLSX field-mapping annexes, not the notice data itself. data.egov.bg AOP organisation profile — 403 to both the fetch tool and curl with a desktop UA; logged, not silently dropped. bnb.bg/AboutUs/AUPublicProcurement/index.htm — 200 but a Bulgarian error page ('Несъществуващ адрес'); correct path is /AUPublicProcurements/ (plural), which loads but archives only up to 2020. TED API returns HTTP 429 under rapid querying — pacing of 8-20s between calls was required. Claude-in-Chrome not connected this run.
