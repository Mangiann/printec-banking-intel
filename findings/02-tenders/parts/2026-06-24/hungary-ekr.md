# Hungary — EKR (kozbeszerzes.hu) — Act XVIII ATM rollout

Run date: 2026-06-24 (5th pass). Languages: EN + HU.

## Headline
The Act XVIII/2025 mandated-ATM rollout does NOT flow through EKR/kozbeszerzes.hu. All nine
obligated institutions (OTP, MBH, K&H, Erste, Raiffeisen, UniCredit, CIB, GRANIT, MagNet) are
PRIVATE banks; they buy ATMs commercially, so EKR legitimately returns zero rollout tenders.
That is the finding, not a gap. Printec must pursue these banks directly (or via NCR Atleos).

## Refreshed facts this run
- PHASE 1 (>1,000 pop, deadline 31/12/2025) effectively COMPLETE. Per MNB-sourced press, 1,039
  new mandated ATMs installed, ~HUF 4.156bn (~EUR 10.4m) at ~HUF 4m/unit. Bank split:
  MBH 316, OTP 128, K&H 92, CIB 38, Erste 36, UniCredit 17 (others making up balance).
  Total national fleet ~5,378 ATMs (MNB, end of period). OTP ~1,800, MBH ~1,200 units operated.
- PHASE 2 (500–1,000 pop, deadline 31/12/2026) live: 673 settlements in band, 631 had no ATM
  in 2022 -> ~631 new units, ~HUF 2.5bn (~EUR 6.3m) cost to sector. Inter-bank handover/transfer
  contracts deadline 28/02/2026 (passed). This is the live 2026 deployment wave.
- LEGAL: implementing decrees 16/2025 NGM, 19/2025 + 20/2025 MNB in force. OTP (joined by other
  credit institutions, reportedly incl. Erste/Raiffeisen) constitutional complaint Nov 2025 still
  PENDING at AB — no ruling. Govt (Nagy Marton) refused to amend; rollout proceeds. A May 2026 OTP
  AB complaint exists but concerns the kamatstop (rate cap), NOT the ATM law — do not conflate.
