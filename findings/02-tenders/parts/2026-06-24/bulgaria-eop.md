## Structural finding (the answer for core ATM/POS)
Bulgaria's ATM, recycler, POS and self-service demand sits with PRIVATE banks (DSK/OTP, UniCredit Bulbank, Postbank/Eurobank, UBB/KBC, Fibank, ProCredit) and with BORICA AD (bank-owned national card/ATM switch). NONE of these procure through EOP — so EOP legitimately yields ~zero on core Printec ATM/POS hardware. Pursue those banks directly, not the portal.
The 01/01/2026 euro cutover on ATM/POS was a SOFTWARE/parameter event (BORICA + OpenWay SmartVista), already completed with zero downtime — not a hardware tender. No ATM-fleet replacement tender surfaced.
