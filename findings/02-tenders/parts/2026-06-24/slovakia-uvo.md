# Slovakia — UVO / eKasa procurement (ATM/deposit/POS tenders)

Run: 2026-06-24 (5th pass). Portal: UVO (Urad pre verejne obstaravanie) https://www.uvo.gov.sk + uvostat.sk aggregator + posta.eranet.sk.
eKasa = Slovak Financial Administration online cash-register/fiscalisation system (POS/receipt fiscal devices), run by Financne riaditelstvo SR.

## Key findings
1. **Slovenska posta self-service cash kiosks** — pilot LAUNCHED 15/08/2025 at 5 branches (Bratislava x2, Kosice, Sturovo, Zilina). Does cash deposit + withdrawal, e-voucher (ePoukaz), bill pay via Pay-by-square QR, cash OR card. Evaluation to decide scale-up. NO scale-up tender published yet. Direct Printec fit: self-service kiosks + cash recyclers (Glory/Sesami/NCR) + field service. Vendor of pilot NOT disclosed. STILL PRE-TENDER (was in baseline).
2. **NBS (Narodna banka Slovenska) currency tech** — confirms baseline: Giesecke+Devrient Currency Technology GmbH banknote-production/currency-tech contract EUR 6,519,820 (2023). Coin minting dominated by Mincovna Kremnica (~EUR 25.9M, 2 contracts). G+D-locked on banknote processing. No new cash-handling ATM tender found.
3. **Mandatory cashless acceptance from 01/05/2026** (Act 384/2025 on revenue recording) — every merchant taking cash >EUR 1 must offer cashless (card/QR/SoftPOS). Subsidised terminal scheme to 30/06/2026. NATIONWIDE POS/SoftPOS demand driver. Macro/channel signal for Printec POS (Verifone/Castles) + TMS.
4. **eKasa SWERP** — Financial Admin procuring new SWERP info system to replace eKasa, live 2nd half 2026; current eKasa support EUR 91,670/mo (extended Apr-2025→Mar-2026). IT system, adjacent not core. No vendor named.

Slovak COMMERCIAL banks (SLSP, VUB, Tatra) are PRIVATE — no UVO tenders. That is the finding. Fleet: SLSP 280 deposit ATMs, Tatra 294 (6 recyclers), VUB 146 (expanding). Pursue banks directly, not via portal.
