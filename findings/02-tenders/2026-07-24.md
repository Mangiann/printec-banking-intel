## What changed since last run

Most of this run is fresh signal: 17 of the 19 items are new. The highest-value new lead is Greece's Ministry of Citizen Protection open tender for portable thermal-imaging cameras (three lots, EUR 2.92m excl. VAT, confirmed) — Printec's largest new addressable opportunity this cycle, though win odds are low. Two sizeable new Greek public-sector IT modernizations also opened: the Amarousi/Maroussi "Digital Transformation" subproject (EUR 1.18m, but note this is only a de minimis contract modification with NOVA ICT already incumbent, not a fresh award) and the Ministry of Digital Governance's G-Cloud infrastructure maintenance tender (EUR 700k, Lot 5). On the cash-handling side, the National Bank of Serbia's 2026 plan surfaced a cluster of new supply lines — desktop banknote counters/sorters (item 0003), coin counting/packaging machines (items 0131/0132) — that fit Printec's product range at Medium win odds, plus useful incumbent intelligence (Glory is the entrenched banknote-processing vendor). On baseline corrections: PrivatBank's ATM program is only partially confirmed — the ~800-ATM 2026 plan and the 7,401-ATM / 9,867-terminal fleet figures could not be fully verified — and the NBS cash-machine and ELTA maintenance lines are likewise partially confirmed pending tender publication.
