# Agent 2 — Tenders & Procurement — Historical Baseline (2024 → June 2026)

**Created:** 2026-06-12 | **Scope:** all 17 footprint countries, Jan 2024 – Jun 2026 (pre-2024 items included only where a program started earlier and is still running).
**Purpose:** one-time backfill of tender/award history so weekly runs (`YYYY-MM-DD.md` files) only need to track what is NEW or CHANGED against this baseline.
**Method & limits:** web search + direct fetch of buyer pages, press, and official notices. TED and most national portal search UIs are JavaScript-only and were not directly queryable; this baseline is therefore strongest where buyers publish on their own websites or where press covered awards. Absence of a country below ≠ absence of tenders. All confidence capped at Medium (single-agent rule).

---

## Headline takeaways

1. **Regulators are forcing ATM deployment in two footprint countries.** Hungary (MNB decree, June 2025) assigned nine banks specific municipalities for mandatory ATM installation through Q1 2026, with a legal requirement for an ATM in every settlement >1,000 inhabitants by end-2025 and >500 by end-2026. Austria's central bank itself tendered a framework for 60–120 ATMs. This is a new pattern — cash-access mandates creating non-discretionary ATM demand — and the single biggest 2024–26 procurement theme found.
2. **State-owned banks ran (and are still running) ATM fleet renewals**: CEC Bank (RO) has replaced 1,100+ units since ~2021 and the program explicitly continues "until the entire network is modernized"; Ukraine's Oschadbank/PrivatBank buy ATM parts, servicing and protection equipment continuously through Prozorro.
3. **Post offices are becoming bank-channel buyers**: ELTA (GR) is outsourcing heavily and adding banking/ATM services; Poșta Română contracted card-payment banking services; Bulgarian Posts was contractually integrated into the euro cash-supply chain.
4. **Bulgaria's euro changeover (completed 01.01.2026)** drove a one-off wave of ATM/POS adaptation through late 2025 — that capex is now spent; the follow-on opportunity is recycling/deposit functionality for residual lev handling and normal renewal cycles.

## What this baseline means for the weekly agent

- **Track to closure:** OeNB framework call-offs (AT); Hungarian banks' rollout contracts (HU); CEC's remaining fleet modernization (RO); Poșta Română Tezaur re-tender ~mid-2026 (RO); ELTA's first banking/ATM-related notice (GR).
- **Reconstruct when portal access improves:** Serbian BPŠ ATM renewal outcome; Bulgarian euro-changeover vendor map; Prozorro award/winner history for Oschadbank & PrivatBank; HP (Croatia) contract register.
- **Standing pattern to watch:** cash-access mandates (HU law, AT central-bank deployment, GR ELTA plan) are turning rural ATM coverage into regulated, tender-driven demand — the strongest structural tailwind for ATM hardware + managed services found in 2024–26.

## Method gaps (unchanged from 2026-06-12 weekly file)

TED v3 API requires POST; national portal search UIs (SEAP, ΚΗΜΔΗΣ, CAIS EOP, Prozorro, EOJN, Serbian portal, ePPS, etc.) are JavaScript-rendered and could not be fetched. A browser-based pass or per-portal saved-search e-mail alerts would let the next baseline revision fill the award-history columns (winner, value) that are mostly missing above. No values, dates or deadlines in this file are estimated.
