## What changed since last run

The biggest new development is Printec's confirmed 80.10% ownership of Cashflex/KEA via Printec Cash Network S.A., taking over operation of ~850 off-site Piraeus ATMs in Greece — a strategic XL position rather than a tender to chase. In Ukraine, PrivatBank's plan to buy 800 new recycler/multifunction ATMs in 2026 is now confirmed (following the 1,200 units bought in 2025), and Printec's incumbency there is documented via the 16/01/2025 contract for 1,140 NCR SelfServ recyclers worth 838.64M UAH — the clearest high-value follow-on in the dataset. Two new regulatory waves create fresh hardware demand: Hungary's ATM Act XVIII/2025 (Phase 2 mandating ~631 new ATMs in 500–1,000-pop settlements by end-2026) and Slovakia's Act 384/2025 cashless mandate (now postponed to 01/05/2026), driving POS/SoftPOS rollout. On the Greek public side, the new EUR 515.4m OPSEA digital-ID tender (Hyperfund/Ministry of Citizen Protection) is large but low win-probability for Printec. Verification corrections to note: the Banca Intesa Beograd Diebold/Payten ATM-replacement item is now contradicted (competitor win, not a Printec opening); the OeNB rural-ATM framework with PSA is contradicted as an opening; and the BORICA euro-cutover item is contradicted — none should be treated as actionable Printec opportunities.
