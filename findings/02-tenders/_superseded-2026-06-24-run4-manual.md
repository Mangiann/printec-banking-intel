## Glossary additions

- **ATM-as-a-Service (ATMaaS):** the ATM vendor (e.g. NCR Atleos) owns, deploys and operates the machines and the bank pays a per-transaction/per-service fee — capex shifts to opex. (Epirus Bank GR; OeNB/PSA AT in a similar "operation" model.)
- **Banknote-processing / sorting system:** an industrial central-bank machine that authenticates banknotes, sorts fit notes (for recirculation) from unfit (for destruction) at high speed. Typically OEM-locked (Giesecke+Devrient CPS/BPS class). (HNB V-31/32/33/34; ČNB BPS M7.)
- **MFM (multifunction machine):** a cash-recycling ATM that both dispenses and accepts/recirculates banknotes (and often FX). (CEC Bank RO.)
- **CIT (cash-in-transit):** armoured transport and escort of cash to/from branches and ATMs; "ATM replenishment" is the related service of refilling ATMs. (Poštanska štedionica RS; TEB XK.)
- **SIEM (Security Information and Event Management):** software/service that collects security logs and alerts on threats 24/7. (Central Bank of Cyprus Tender 02/2026.)
- **Soft-POS / mobile POS:** card-acceptance turned into software on a phone/handheld, or a portable card terminal. (Possible BH Pošta scope; FINA EFTPOS estate HR.)
- **ATM Act (Hungary, Act XVIII/2025) + MNB Decree 19/2025 (VI.26.):** law + implementing decree mandating ATMs in every small settlement, phased by population (>1,000 by 31/12/2025, >500 by 31/12/2026), assigning 9 named banks to settlement clusters. Contested at the Constitutional Court.
