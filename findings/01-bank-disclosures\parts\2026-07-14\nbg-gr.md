## Summary
- **Cosmos/Finacle core replacement**: migration to new Core Banking "nearing completion (May 2026)" per NBG 1Q26 results deck; press (fintechfutures/IBS) report the programme completed June 2026. Infosys Finacle core; IBM + Deloitte SIs; ~6m customers, 12m deposit accounts, >1.5m loans migrated; ~1,000 people / ~500,000 person-days; phase-1 (corporate/SME loans, ~60% of loan book) went live 2023. Finacle to extend to international subs incl. Cyprus.
- **Capex split**: "Sector-leading investments in IT and digital transformation of c€1b since 2020 (incl. CBS replacement)" — NBG 1Q26 deck.
- **ATM fleet (verified, corrects baseline 1,476)**: 1,360 ATMs at 31/12/2025 — 498 on-site, 862 off-site (AR2025). Entire ATM fleet upgraded; NFC contactless, mobile withdrawal, barcode across all ATMs. Plan: new upgraded ATM menu + expand cash-deposit functionality to more ATMs.
- **Branches**: 305 Units / 303 branches incl. 48 tellerless (31/12/2025); 49 tellerless in 1Q26 (trajectory 9→18→34→48→49 FY22→1Q26). -157 branches since FY18.
- **Self-service/cash automation**: EthnoDeposit ATM cash deposit + Smart Safe / Instant Cash for businesses; migration of transactions to ATM/APS continuing.
- **POS/acquiring**: NBG Pay (49% NBG) c302k terminals, c215k merchants, €3.3b gross transactions (1Q26).
- **eKYC/onboarding**: KYC Management Team (est. 2024); onboarding/KYC journey uplift; stated aim to streamline client on-boarding + introduce self-service functionalities.
- **Intl network**: 58 branches, 174 ATMs (Cyprus + North Macedonia).
