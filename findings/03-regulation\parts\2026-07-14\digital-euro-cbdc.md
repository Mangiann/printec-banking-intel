# Digital euro & CBDC — Regulation findings (run 2026-07-14)

Scope: ECB programme timeline; acceptance / ATM-wallet readiness. Footprint = AL, AT, BA, BG, CY, CZ, GR, HR, HU, KS/XK, ME, MK, RO, RS, SI, SK, UA.

## What is NEW / CHANGED since baseline (24/06/2026)

1. **ECB selected 36 PSPs for the digital-euro pilot — announced 14/07/2026** (ECB primary). >50 applications; notifications end-June 2026. Pilot runs H2-2027 for 12 months across ECB + 19 euro-area NCBs. Two roles: distributing PSPs and acquiring PSPs. Scope now explicitly includes P2P (online + offline) and **P2B at physical point-of-sale, SoftPOS, e-commerce, mobile** — i.e. real acceptance-device validation. Beta version, no legal-tender status.
   - **Footprint banks IN the pilot** (from ECB published list): Cyprus — Bank of Cyprus + JCC Payment Systems; Croatia — Raiffeisenbank Austria d.d. + Corvus Pay; Slovenia — Nova Ljubljanska banka (NLB); Slovakia — Tatra banka; Greece — Piraeus Bank, National Bank of Greece, Cooperative Bank of Chania; Austria — BAWAG P.S.K., Raiffeisen Bank International (RBI). Bank of Cyprus is an existing Printec reference customer (Glory TCR).

2. **EP plenary approved digital-euro negotiating mandate 09/07/2026 (416-169-22)**; first trilogue 13/07/2026 (rapporteur Fernando Navarrete, EPP). Target final agreement end-2026. New concrete elements: **minimum 24-month roll-out period after authorisation** before go-live; **non-euro EU banks/PSPs authorised to distribute** the digital euro (relevant to non-euro footprint CZ/HU/RO/BG etc.); holding limit to be set by Commission on ECB recommendation, reviewed >=every 2 years; merchant fees capped at existing card-acceptance cost. Retail issuance still pencilled 2029.

## Unchanged (baseline still valid, not re-reported as new)

- GC moved to next phase 30/10/2025; EUR3,000 tested holding limit; 2029 issuance target.
