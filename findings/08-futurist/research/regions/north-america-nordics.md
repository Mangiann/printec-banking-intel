# North America + Nordics 3-5yr (most-digital leading indicators)

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

These two regions are Printec's **leading indicator** for where its 17 CEE/SEE markets head next — they are the most-digital large economies on earth, so what stabilises (or doesn't) here previews the floor for the rest of the footprint. The 2027-2031 arc has three threads:

1. **Transactional cash has hit a low, stubborn floor — not zero.** In the US, cash has been flat at ~7 payments/month since 2020 and 14% of payment *count* in 2024; the Fed itself calls this a possible "floor" consumers choose not to fall below ([Fed Diary 2025, p.3-4](https://www.frbservices.org/news/research/2025-findings-from-the-diary-of-consumer-payment-choice)). Canada is even more striking — cash stable at 21% of purchase *volume* / 11% of value across 2020-2024, with cash *holdings rising* ([Bank of Canada MOP 2024](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2025/08/staff-discussion-paper-2025-12/)). The Nordics are the global frontier: Norway 2% of payments (2025), Sweden ~10% of in-store purchases ([Norges Bank 2025](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/publications/Financial-Infrastructure-Report/finansiell-infrastruktur-2025/web-report-financial-infrastructure-2025/); [Riksbank 2025](https://www.riksbank.se/en-gb/payments--cash/payments-in-sweden/payments-report-2025/)). Even at these floors, ~90% of US consumers intend to keep using cash and >94% use it as payment, backup or store of value ([Fed Diary 2025, p.1-2](https://www.frbservices.org/news/research/2025-findings-from-the-diary-of-consumer-payment-choice)).

2. **The decisive new force is RESILIENCE policy, and the Nordics are writing the playbook the EU/CEE will copy.** Sweden's Riksbank has set a hard target: by **01/07/2026** all adult cardholders must be able to pay offline for essential goods for up to **7 days** of disruption, is opening more banknote depots, and backs *legislating* a cash-acceptance obligation ([Riksbank 2025, p.5-7](https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/rapporter/betalningsrapport/2025/engelsk/payments-report-2025.pdf)). Norway already mandates a BankAxept offline backup enabling week-long card payments during outages, plus a statutory bank cash-service obligation ([Norges Bank 2025](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/publications/Financial-Infrastructure-Report/finansiell-infrastruktur-2025/web-report-financial-infrastructure-2025/)). This is the most-digital region deliberately *re-arming* cash and offline rails as critical infrastructure — a structural tailwind for resilient self-service.

3. **The investment dollar shifts from "how many machines" to "how smart, how outsourced, how AI-run."** Raw ATM counts decline in North America (US ~470k in 2019 → ~451.5k by 2022; global −1.4% in 2025) ([ATM Marketplace/coinlaw](https://coinlaw.io/atm-statistics/)), but the US ATM **managed-services** market still grows from $2.54bn (2024) to $3.61bn (2033) at ~4% CAGR ([ResearchAndMarkets 02/12/2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/12/02/3197739/28124/en/United-States-3-61-Bn-ATM-Managed-Services-Markets-2025-2033-by-Service-Type-Key-Locations-States-and-Company-Analysis.html)), and AI could add >$370bn/yr in retail-banking profit by 2030 with AI-first banks running 30-40% lower cost bases ([BCG 05/11/2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/300-billion-profit-potential-for-retail-banks-via-ai-by-2030-302604848.html)). Branches still acquire 72% of new current accounts / 92% of new balances in North America ([McKinsey, State of Retail Banking 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/the-state-of-retail-banking-profitability-and-growth-in-the-era-of-digital-and-ai)) — so the branch isn't dying, it's being re-tooled around self-service and video tellers.

**Net for 2027-2031:** the most-digital markets show that cash *transactions* keep shrinking toward a floor (US/Canada double-digit, Nordics low-single-digit) while machine fleets consolidate into fewer, multi-function, recycling, AI-managed, **resilience-hardened** units — and the surrounding digital rails (instant payments, wallets, A2A) capture the migrating value. Confidence: **high** on the floor + resilience direction (concrete central-bank mandates and dated targets); **medium** on exact 2031 cash-share levels (consulting trajectories beyond 2030 are largely gated).

## Key drivers

- **US cash has a "floor," not a cliff — 14% of payments, flat 7/month since 2020.** Cash is the #3 instrument for 5 straight years; lower-income (24%) and 55+ (19%) lean on it most ([Fed Diary 2025, p.1-4](https://www.frbservices.org/news/research/2025-findings-from-the-diary-of-consumer-payment-choice)).
- **Cash-as-store-of-value persists across the most-digital markets.** ~80% of US consumers hold cash on a given day (stable since 2018); >94% use cash as payment, backup or store of value; Canadians' nominal cash holdings *rose* in 2024 ([Fed Diary 2025, p.1-2](https://www.frbservices.org/news/research/2025-findings-from-the-diary-of-consumer-payment-choice); [Bank of Canada MOP 2024](https://www.bankofcanada.ca/2025/08/staff-discussion-paper-2025-12/)).
- **Norway is the world frontier — and still won't drop cash.** Cash = 2% of payments (2025, down from 3% in 2024); cash-in-circulation NOK ~38.5bn, just 1.4% of M1; yet banks carry a statutory cash-service obligation and a week-long offline card backup ([Norges Bank 2025](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/publications/Financial-Infrastructure-Report/finansiell-infrastruktur-2025/web-report-financial-infrastructure-2025/)).
- **Sweden: ~10% of in-store purchases cash, ~1% of GDP in circulation — the lowest globally — yet re-legislating cash.** ~70% of small businesses still accept cash (85% in essential-goods retail); Riksbank backs a statutory accept-cash duty and bank cash-service obligations ([Riksbank 2025, p.5-7, 18](https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/rapporter/betalningsrapport/2025/engelsk/payments-report-2025.pdf); [IJCB / Riksbank annual report 2024](https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/rapporter/arsredovisning/engelska/annual-report-2024.pdf)).
- **Hard, dated RESILIENCE mandates are the new demand signal.** Sweden target: offline card payments for essential goods, 7-day outages, by **01/07/2026**; Norway BankAxept offline backup live; Norway joining TIPS instant settlement with NBO INST operational **H1 2028** ([Riksbank 2025, p.5](https://www.riksbank.se/globalassets/media/rapporter/betalningsrapport/2025/engelsk/payments-report-2025.pdf); [Norges Bank 2025](https://www.norges-bank.no/en/news-events/publications/Financial-Infrastructure-Report/finansiell-infrastruktur-2025/web-report-financial-infrastructure-2025/)).
- **ATM fleets shrink, managed services + recycling grow.** US ATMs ~470k (2019) → ~451.5k (2022); global −1.4% (2025); over half of ATMs now off-site. But US ATM managed services $2.54bn (2024) → $3.61bn (2033); cash recycling cuts cash-in-transit ~50% ([coinlaw/ATM Marketplace](https://coinlaw.io/atm-statistics/); [ResearchAndMarkets 2025](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/12/02/3197739/28124/en/United-States-3-61-Bn-ATM-Managed-Services-Markets-2025-2033-by-Service-Type-Key-Locations-States-and-Company-Analysis.html); [NCR Atleos — interested party](https://www.ncratleos.com/insights/cash-recycling-improved-efficiency)).
- **Instant payments scale fast in the US — the rail cash value migrates to.** FedNow participants 900 (Jul 2024) → 1,400+ (Jul 2025); RTP ~1.5M payments/day in 2026 with a $10M limit; ~70% of businesses expect to adopt instant payments within two years ([FedNow at two](https://www.frbservices.org/news/fed360/issues/071625/fednow-service-two-years-growth-innovation); [PYMNTS RTP/FedNow 2025](https://www.pymnts.com/real-time-payments/2025/58percent-of-us-banks-use-both-rtp-and-fednow-for-instant-payments)).
- **Wallets/A2A keep eroding card and the last of cash by 2030.** US POS digital wallets 17% (2025) → 26% (2030); US in-store cash 10% (2025) → ~9% of transactions (2030); credit 40%→36%, debit 28%→25% ([Worldpay GPR 2026 via Payments Dive 06/04/2026](https://www.paymentsdive.com/news/us-digital-wallet-use-projected-to-grow-by-2030/816718/); [The Financial Brand on Worldpay](https://thefinancialbrand.com/news/payments-trends/digital-payments-dominate-globally-says-worldpay-study-187823)).
- **AI re-tools the branch/back office — the margin story.** >$370bn/yr retail-banking profit potential by 2030; AI-first banks 30-40% lower cost; branches still drive 72% of new accounts / 92% of new balances in North America ([BCG 05/11/2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/300-billion-profit-potential-for-retail-banks-via-ai-by-2030-302604848.html); [McKinsey State of Retail Banking 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/the-state-of-retail-banking-profitability-and-growth-in-the-era-of-digital-and-ai)).

## Printec implication

The most-digital markets are Printec's **dress rehearsal**: they prove that even at 2-14% cash shares, the demand doesn't vanish — it *transforms* into fewer-but-smarter machines, recurring managed services, and a regulatory premium on resilience. That maps directly onto Printec's portfolio:

- **Cash recycling + deposit automation (NCR Atleos) is the consolidation play.** As fleets shrink (US −4% over 2019-2022, global −1.4%/yr) but cash-in-circulation and store-of-value demand persist, banks collapse single-function dispensers into multi-function recyclers that cut cash-in-transit ~50%. Each unit Printec deploys does more work — the Nordic/NA pattern that will reach CEE/SEE next.
- **ATM-as-a-Service / managed services is the margin story confirmed by the data.** The US managed-services market grows ($2.54bn→$3.61bn) *while* machine counts fall — proof that the value migrates from boxes to run/manage/optimise contracts. This is recurring revenue Printec can sell across all 17 markets as banks hit the same complexity inflection.
- **Resilience is the killer new tailwind — and the Nordics hand Printec the spec sheet.** Sweden's 01/07/2026 offline-7-day target, Norway's BankAxept offline backup, and statutory cash-service obligations are exactly the requirements the EU Single Currency Package will push into CEE. Printec should productise: offline-capable POS/terminals, banking-hub / shared self-service infrastructure, hardened cash-cycle software, and contingency cash logistics — selling "guaranteed availability" as a managed outcome.
- **Self-service + video-teller branch transformation, AI-run back office.** With branches still acquiring 72% of new NA accounts but mobile leading daily banking, the branch becomes a self-service/advice hub (SelfServ-80-class multifunction units + video teller). The BCG/McKinsey AI thesis (>$370bn, 30-40% lower cost) points Printec's software/managed-services and onboarding lines toward AI-assisted, lower-cost operating models.
- **Hedge the transactional decline into digital rails.** US instant payments (FedNow 1,400+, RTP ~1.5M/day) and wallets (US POS 17%→26% by 2030) show where cash value goes. Printec's POS/payments, SoftPOS/acquiring, and digital onboarding lines are the capture vehicles for value leaving the cash rail within the same accounts.

Note (per Printec compliance framing): all figures here are public third-party projections; any deployment-grade or payment-related solution built on them must be flagged for human review and testing before being treated as production-ready, consistent with PCI DSS / DORA obligations.
