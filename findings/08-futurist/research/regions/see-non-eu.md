# SEE non-EU footprint 3-5yr (Serbia, Albania, Bosnia, Kosovo, Montenegro, N. Macedonia)

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc through 2031 for the SEE non-EU bloc (the "WB6") is **convergence-on-fast-forward**: a still-cash-intensive region whose payment rails are being yanked up to EU standard years ahead of actual EU membership, while cash remains structurally important but loses transactional share fast. The defining dynamic is **policy- and infrastructure-driven**, not organically consumer-led — which makes the 2027-2031 trajectory unusually predictable for an emerging region.

Three forces compound:

1. **SEPA accession is now a fact, not a plan.** Albania, Montenegro and North Macedonia joined SEPA schemes in October 2025; Serbia entered SEPA's geographic scope on 22/05/2025 with banks operationally live from May 2026 ([European Western Balkans, May 2025](https://europeanwesternbalkans.com/2025/05/23/serbia-becomes-a-part-of-the-single-euro-payments-area-sepa/); [World Bank, March 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/03/25/cheaper-and-faster-payments-sepa-opens-new-horizons-for-the-western-balkans)). Cross-border B2B transfer costs collapsed ~10x after launch (Montenegro: €73.40/transaction in 2024 → €6.15 Oct 2025-Jan 2026) ([World Bank, March 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/03/25/cheaper-and-faster-payments-sepa-opens-new-horizons-for-the-western-balkans)). Bosnia is the lone laggard — legislation does not yet meet SEPA requirements ([European Western Balkans, Feb 2025](https://europeanwesternbalkans.com/2025/02/03/western-balkan-countries-are-taking-further-steps-to-join-the-single-euro-payments-area/)).

2. **A shared TIPS-clone instant-payment backbone goes live ~mid-2026.** The central banks of Albania, BiH, Kosovo, Montenegro (N. Macedonia possibly later) signed an agreement (17/01/2025) for Banca d'Italia to supply a TIPS-software-based instant-payment settlement system, settling in local currencies and explicitly designed as a step into the EU financial system ([ECB MIP news, Jan 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/intro/news/html/ecb.mipnews250117_2.en.html)). Serbia already runs its own NBS IPS (live since 2018) with ~1-second execution and 87.2m transactions in 2024 ([NBS Overview 2023-2024, p.4](https://www.nbs.rs/export/sites/NBS_site/documents-eng/platni-sistem/pregled-pu-ien/pu-ien_e_IV_24.pdf)).

3. **National "go cashless" policy is escalating.** Albania — Europe's most cash-heavy economy (money outside banks 24.3% of M3, vs eurozone ~9.3%, July 2025) — launched "Cashless Albania 2030," targeting universal POS coverage and ≥60% of payments digital by 2028 ([balkanweb / Bank of Albania, 2025](https://www.balkanweb.com/en/masat-si-do-ulet-cash-i-ne-ekonomine-me-nivelin-me-te-larte-te-parase-jashte-bankave-ne-europe/)). Serbia's POS-subsidy programme and "Better Way" push already took cashless usage past 60% ([NALED, 2025](https://naled.rs/en/vest-vise-od-60-gradjana-se-okrenulo-bezgotovinskim-nacinima-placanja-10349)).

**Net for 2027-2031:** digital-payment penetration (currently ~69-77% of adults across WB6) keeps climbing toward euro-area norms; card/POS acceptance grows fast (Serbia POS terminals +17.7% in a single year; Albania POS +24.5% YoY Q3 2025); **but cash-in-circulation stays elevated** through the window — these economies still carry large informal sectors (Albania cash-out-of-banks 40.4% of lek M2) and high cash-for-value behaviour (Albania: cash still 65% of withdrawal *value* even as it falls below 43% of transaction *count*). The EU enlargement Growth Plan (€6bn Reform & Growth Facility 2024-2027; Montenegro targeting membership ~2028, Albania ~shortly after) underwrites continued modernization investment ([EC Growth Plan](https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/enlargement-policy/growth-plan-western-balkans_en)).

**Confidence: high** on direction (the SEPA/TIPS/policy triggers are committed and dated); **medium** on pace per country (Bosnia and Kosovo lag; no single public forecast gives WB6 cash-share trajectories to 2031, so the long-tail of cash demand is the main uncertainty).

## Key drivers

- **SEPA accession completed for 4 of 6, operational 2025-2026.** Albania, Montenegro, N. Macedonia joined Oct 2025; Serbia entered geographic scope 22/05/2025, 18 banks live by May 2026; Kosovo at pre-application, Bosnia not yet compliant ([European Western Balkans, May 2025](https://europeanwesternbalkans.com/2025/05/23/serbia-becomes-a-part-of-the-single-euro-payments-area-sepa/); [EC Enlargement, May 2025](https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/news/european-commission-welcomes-inclusion-serbia-single-euro-payments-area-2025-05-22_en)).
- **Cross-border payment cost collapse drives B2B digitalization.** Region historically paid ~6x EU rates to settle international payments; remittance cost 6.71% vs 3% SDG target (~€500m potential savings); SEPA cut B2B transfer cost ~10x post-launch ([World Bank brief](https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/eca/brief/advancing-the-modernization-and-integration-of-payment-systems-in-the-western-balkans); [World Bank, March 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/feature/2026/03/25/cheaper-and-faster-payments-sepa-opens-new-horizons-for-the-western-balkans)).
- **Shared TIPS-based instant-payment rail (~mid-2026).** Banca d'Italia supplies a TIPS clone to ALB/BIH/KOS/MNE (MKD later), settling in local currencies — a structural leap toward real-time, EU-compatible infrastructure ([ECB MIP news, Jan 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/intro/news/html/ecb.mipnews250117_2.en.html); [Central Banking, 2025](https://www.centralbanking.com/central-banks/payments/7963622/west-balkan-central-banks-to-create-instant-payments-system)).
- **National anti-cash policy (Albania the flagship).** "Cashless Albania 2030": universal POS coverage, ≥60% digital payments, all public institutions cashless by 2028; a Digital Payments Council with 12 measures and monthly monitoring; Mastercard free POS for 12 months from 01/05/2026 ([balkanweb / BoA, 2025](https://www.balkanweb.com/en/masat-si-do-ulet-cash-i-ne-ekonomine-me-nivelin-me-te-larte-te-parase-jashte-bankave-ne-europe/)).
- **Rising digital-payment penetration, but from a sub-EU base.** WB6 only 76% of adults hold a transaction account (vs 99% euro area); digital-payment use rose 13pp (ALB, SRB) to 29pp (BIH) over the decade; Serbia 77% and N. Macedonia 75% of adults made/received digital payments in 2024 (above ECA's 69%) ([World Bank WB Regular Economic Report No.28, Fall 2025, Box 1.3, p.13](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100625100539134/pdf/P512916-fe1dc2c8-b236-4cfe-ad18-88303a06122e.pdf)).
- **Cash stays a store of value / informal-economy medium.** Money-outside-banks (Aug 2025): Albania 24.3% of M3 (40.4% of lek M2); BiH 20.5% of M2; N. Macedonia 9.9% of M3; Serbia 7.6% of M3 — all above eurozone ~9.3% on the broadest measure, Albania far above ([balkanweb / BoA, 2025](https://www.balkanweb.com/en/masat-si-do-ulet-cash-i-ne-ekonomine-me-nivelin-me-te-larte-te-parase-jashte-bankave-ne-europe/)). A 10% rise in digital-payment share cuts the shadow economy ~2% ([World Bank brief](https://www.worldbank.org/en/region/eca/brief/advancing-the-modernization-and-integration-of-payment-systems-in-the-western-balkans)).
- **Acceptance network expanding fast.** Serbia: POS terminals +17.7% to 160,857 and ATMs +4.5% to 3,218 in 2024; card retail transactions +20.7%; issued cards 12.2m ([NBS Overview 2023-2024, p.3-4](https://www.nbs.rs/export/sites/NBS_site/documents-eng/platni-sistem/pregled-pu-ien/pu-ien_e_IV_24.pdf)). Albania POS terminals +24.5% YoY to 29,261 (Q3 2025); POS card payments +42% H1 2025 ([hashtag.al / BoA, 2025](https://www.hashtag.al/en/index.php/2025/11/28/pagesat-me-karte-u-rriten-me-ritme-te-larta-edhe-ne-tremujorin-e-trete/)).
- **Mobile-first leapfrog.** N. Macedonia: mobile apps = 71.3% of digital-channel transactions; e-payment volume +9%, value +20.9% YoY ([Central Banking / NBRM, 2025](https://www.centralbanking.com/central-banks/payments/7963622/west-balkan-central-banks-to-create-instant-payments-system)). Mobile-phone ownership >90% region-wide, smartphone 79-88% ([World Bank WB RER No.28, p.13](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099100625100539134/pdf/P512916-fe1dc2c8-b236-4cfe-ad18-88303a06122e.pdf)).
- **Serbia cashless momentum.** Card purchases +18% to 805m in 2025; online purchases +34.3% to 110.6m; cashless usage past 60% via Better Way / POS subsidies (~10,000 SMEs onboarded over 2.5yrs) ([SeeNews, 2025](https://seenews.com/news/card-purchases-in-serbia-rise-18-percent-in-2025-1291181); [NBS, 2025](https://www.nbs.rs/en/scripts/showcontent/index.html?id=21320&konverzija=no); [NALED, 2025](https://naled.rs/en/vest-vise-od-60-gradjana-se-okrenulo-bezgotovinskim-nacinima-placanja-10349)).
- **EU enlargement underwrites the modernization spend.** Growth Plan + €6bn Reform & Growth Facility (2024-2027); Montenegro targeting membership ~2028, Albania shortly after; bloc could add members by 2030 — meaning ongoing regulatory/payments alignment through the whole window ([EC Growth Plan](https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/enlargement-policy/growth-plan-western-balkans_en)).

## Printec implication

This region is the **clearest "build-out, not rationalize" story in Printec's footprint** — opposite to the mature-market ATM-shrink narrative. Demand is pulled toward:

- **POS / payment acceptance is the single biggest pull.** Universal-POS-coverage mandates (Albania by 2028), aggressive subsidy programmes (Serbia, ~10,000 SMEs onboarded; Mastercard free-POS in Albania from May 2026) and 17-25% annual POS-fleet growth mean a multi-year hardware + acceptance + SoftPOS deployment runway. This is Printec's POS/payments line directly.
- **Self-service & cash-deposit/recycling still expanding, not contracting.** Serbia ATMs *grew* 4.5% in 2024 and account/card penetration is below EU norms with cash still dominant by value (Albania 65%). The bloc is at the stage where banks add multi-function recyclers, branch self-service and onboarding kiosks to formalize cash and serve the still-unbanked 24% — favourable for Printec's NCR Atleos cash-recycling/ATM line and self-service kiosks well into 2031.
- **Instant-payments + SEPA integration = software/compliance & onboarding demand.** SEPA SCT/SCT Inst/SDD adoption and the TIPS-clone rail (live ~mid-2026) force banks across all six to re-plumb payment processing, ISO 20022 messaging, fraud/AML and customer onboarding — pulling Printec's software, security, compliance and digital-onboarding (eID) lines.
- **Formalization tailwind.** Anti-cash policy explicitly aims to shrink the shadow economy (10% more digital → ~2% smaller shadow economy), which converts informal cash flows into accounts, cards and traceable rails — expanding Printec's addressable transaction base structurally.
- **Sequencing watch:** Serbia and N. Macedonia are the most digitally mature (lead with POS/acquiring/software); Albania is the fastest-moving on policy (POS + onboarding surge to 2028); Bosnia and Kosovo lag (SEPA-incomplete) — likely later but larger catch-up opportunities for both cash infrastructure and acceptance.

Note (Printec compliance framing): figures here are public third-party data and government targets, not Printec forecasts; any payment-related or production-grade solution built on these should be flagged for human review and testing before being treated as production-ready. Targets such as "Cashless Albania 2030" are policy ambitions and should be treated as directional, not guaranteed.
