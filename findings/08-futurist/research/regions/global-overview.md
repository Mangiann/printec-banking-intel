# Global ATM/payments/self-service 3-5yr overview — the worldwide arc & what leads it

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The worldwide arc for 2027-2031 is a **managed contraction of the ATM fleet alongside a structural shift in what those machines do**, set against a payments market that keeps drifting toward digital wallets, instant/account-to-account (A2A) rails, and — in the euro area — a possible digital euro. The pace is gradual, not a cliff: the global ATM installed base has declined ~2% per year since 2018 and is forecast to fall only about 4% in total by end-2030 (Datos/RBR), i.e. roughly 1% a year — a slow rationalisation, not collapse. Confidence: **high** on direction, **medium-high** on magnitude (multiple independent advisory/regulator/vendor sources agree on the shape; exact numbers differ by methodology).

The defining feature of the period is **bifurcation**. Withdraw-only ATMs are being culled in mature markets (Western Europe, North America, Asia-Pacific), while **deposit-taking cash recyclers grow ~20% to >1.2 million units by 2029 and account for well over half of all ATM shipments** — banks swap many simple machines for fewer, smarter, full-function terminals that automate deposits and cut cash-in-transit and teller cost (Datos Insights). In parallel, the ATM itself is being re-platformed from a cash dispenser into a **software-led, multi-function self-service hub** (the explicit strategy of Printec's key partner NCR Atleos, whose ATM-as-a-Service book grew ~40%). Cash is simultaneously declining as a *payment instrument at POS* yet rising as a *store of value* (the "paradox of banknotes") — which is precisely why deposit/recycling and cash-management infrastructure stays relevant even as withdrawal volumes ease. Growth in raw ATM numbers concentrates in the Middle East & Africa (the fastest-growing region to end of decade) and, later in the window, a recovering Latin America.

What leads the arc: **mature markets lead the contraction-and-modernisation pattern** (fewer, recycling-capable, outsourced, software-rich machines), while **emerging markets lead net unit growth and digital-payment adoption**. For a CEE/SEE footprint, this means demand pulls toward recyclers, ATM-as-a-Service/managed services, and the software/security/compliance layer rather than vanilla cash dispensers.

## Key drivers

- **ATM fleet rationalisation, not extinction.** Global base ~2.9 million in 2024 (down ~2% YoY, consistent annual decline since 2018), forecast ~4% lower by end-2030; declines concentrated in Western Europe, North America and Asia-Pacific [Datos Insights — Advanced Terminals & Emerging Markets Reshape Global ATM Landscape](https://datos-insights.com/press-release/advanced-terminals-and-emerging-markets-reshape-global-atm-landscape/).
- **Recyclers are the growth engine within ATMs** — installed base up nearly 20% to >1.2 million by 2029; recyclers expected to be "well over half of all ATM shipments" with ~900,000 devices shipping over five years; driven by banks cutting teller dependence and cash-in-transit cost [Datos Insights — Recycling ATMs Defy Market Downturn](https://datos-insights.com/press-release/recycling-atms-defy-market-downturn-with-continued-growth/).
- **Cash declining as a payment instrument but rising as a store of value (the banknote paradox)** — euro cash in circulation reached €1.59tn end-2024 (~10% of GDP); new-banknote production grew ~3.7% CAGR 2019-24 even as POS cash use fell for over a decade [Cash Essentials — The New Cash Paradox](https://cashessentials.org/the-new-cash-paradox/).
- **Euro-area cash at POS keeps falling but stays widely held** — cash 52% of POS transactions by number in 2024 (down from 59% in 2022); 62% of consumers still consider having cash as an option important [ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.space2024~19d46f0f17.en.html).
- **Payments revenue still grows but mix shifts to lower-margin rails** — ~4% annual revenue growth through 2029 (to ~USD 3tn); global cash share of payments 46% in 2024 (down from 50% in 2023); digital wallets ~30% of global POS volume, led by India/Brazil/Nigeria [McKinsey Global Payments Report 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/global-payments-report).
- **Instant / account-to-account payments scale fast** — non-cash transactions forecast to reach 2,838bn by 2028 (from ~1,650bn in 2024); instant payments to be 22% of non-cash volume by 2028; APAC fastest-growing region [Capgemini World Payments Report 2025](https://www.capgemini.com/insights/research-library/world-payments-report-2025/).
- **Digital wallets near ubiquity; contactless value doubles** — digital-wallet users to reach ~5.6bn (two-thirds of global population) by 2029, from 4.4bn in 2025; contactless transaction value to reach ~USD 15.7tn by 2029 (from USD 7.4tn in 2024), with Soft POS extending acceptance to micro-merchants [Juniper Research — Contactless to hit $15.7tn](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/pressreleasescontactless-payment-transactions-to-hit-15tn_globally/); [Juniper Research — digital wallets two-thirds of population by 2029](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/over-two-thirds-of-the-global-population-to-own-a-digital-wallet-by-2029/).
- **Digital euro becomes a concrete planning factor for the footprint's eurozone-adjacent markets** — pilot in H2 2027, potential first issuance 2029, contingent on EU legislation in 2026; ~€1.3bn build cost, ~€320m/yr to operate; holding limits designed in [ECB — Eurosystem moving to next phase of digital euro project, 30/10/2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2025/html/ecb.pr251030~8c5b5beef0.en.html).
- **Financial inclusion still rising — more accounts to serve, often mobile-first** — 79% of adults worldwide held an account in 2024 (up from 74% in 2021); growth in developing economies led by mobile money; 86% of adults own a mobile phone [World Bank Global Findex 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/globalfindex/report).
- **Vendor strategy confirms the "software-led hub + outsourcing" thesis** — NCR Atleos (Printec's key partner) reports ATM-as-a-Service growth ~40%, first ATMaaS customers in Latin America and the Middle East, and an explicit strategy to "generate more service revenue from every machine" and transform ATMs into multi-function financial hubs [NCR Atleos Q3 2025 earnings (8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001974138/000162828025049563/ex-991ncratleosq32025earni.htm). *(Vendor source — interested party; treat as directional.)*

## Printec implication

The global arc pulls Printec's demand decisively away from plain cash-dispensing ATMs and toward five higher-value lines that all sit inside its portfolio:

1. **Cash recyclers / deposit-automation hardware** — the one ATM segment growing worldwide (>1.2M units by 2029, majority of shipments). Mature CEE/SEE banks will replace withdraw-only fleets with fewer, recycling-capable terminals — a direct hardware-plus-services refresh cycle for the next 3-5 years.
2. **ATM-as-a-Service / managed services** — the vendor data (NCR Atleos ~40% ATMaaS growth) and Printec's role as integrator make outsourced fleet management the structural margin opportunity as banks shed in-house ATM operations.
3. **Software & multi-function self-service** — re-platforming ATMs into onboarding/service hubs aligns with Printec's onboarding and self-service lines; demand shifts from "boxes" to "recurring software/service revenue per machine."
4. **Payments / POS & Soft POS** — contactless value doubling and Soft POS reaching micro-merchants supports Printec's POS/payments business, especially for SME acceptance across emerging CEE/SEE markets.
5. **Security & compliance** — instant/A2A growth, the digital euro pilot (2027) and rising account ownership raise the regulatory and fraud-control surface, pulling demand toward Printec's security and compliance/managed-services offerings.

Net: position around **"fewer but smarter machines + recurring services + payments acceptance + compliance,"** not unit volume of dispensers. The euro area's digital-euro path (pilot 2027, possible issuance 2029) is a planning input rather than a near-term revenue driver for the 2027-2031 window. *(Payment- and security-related implementations are production/security-sensitive and require human review and testing before deployment.)*
