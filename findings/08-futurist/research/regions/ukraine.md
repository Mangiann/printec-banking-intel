# Ukraine 3-5yr (war-conditional; reconstruction/digitisation scenarios)

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

Ukraine's transaction-technology trajectory through 2027-2031 is the most **scenario-dependent** of any market in the Printec footprint, but the underlying direction is unusually clear and one-way: **accelerated digitisation, deep EU payments integration, and a structurally smaller but resilience-critical cash/self-service estate.** Confidence on the *direction* is high; confidence on the *pace and size* is low and gated on when (and how) the war ends.

Through four years of full-scale war the financial system has not only held but digitised faster than peers: cashless payments now run at ~95% of card transactions by number and ~65% by value, POS terminals crossed 558k (85%+ contactless), and 148.7m cards are in issue ([NBU via UNN, 2025](https://unn.ua/en/news/ukrainians-increased-the-volume-of-cashless-transactions-by-10percent-in-2025)). The state-in-a-smartphone (Diia, ~23m users / ~81% of adults) gives Ukraine a digital-identity rail most Printec markets lack ([DigitalState.gov.ua, 2025](https://digitalstate.gov.ua/news/govtech/ponad-23-milyony-ukrayintsiv-uze-korystuiutsia-diyeiu-tsyfrova-derzava-stala-novoiu-normoiu)).

Three plausible 2027-2031 arcs:
- **Reconstruction/ceasefire baseline (most weight):** war ends or freezes ~2026-2027; GDP rebounds to ~3.5-4% (2027) and ~4.2-4.5% (2028); ~$588bn 10-year reconstruction programme unlocks bank-sector strengthening, state-bank privatisation, SEPA accession, and a re-equipment + branch/self-service rebuild cycle ([IMF WEO Apr-2026 via NV](https://english.nv.ua/business/imf-warns-ukraine-faces-another-weak-growth-year-in-2026-before-a-modest-2027-rebound-50600319.html); [World Bank RDNA, 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/02/23/updated-ukraine-recovery-and-reconstruction-needs-assessment-released)).
- **Frozen-conflict downside:** combat continues into 2027-2028; GDP growth ~0.5-1%; digitisation/resilience spend continues but capex on physical estate stays defensive ([IMF, 2026](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1ukrea2026001-source-pdf.pdf)).
- **Escalation tail:** unlikely-but-non-trivial; collapse of donor financing; planning-only for Printec.

Net: the market pulls toward **digital onboarding/identity, instant-payments & SEPA-compliant infrastructure, cash-recycling and energy-resilient self-service, and security/compliance** — with a large but back-loaded reconstruction equipment opportunity.

## Key drivers

- **~$588bn / 10-year reconstruction need (RDNA5, as of 31/12/2025), with the financial sector named a priority and ~40% of total costs targeted to private capital.** This frames a multi-year bank re-capitalisation, branch and self-service rebuild, and digital-finance build-out. [World Bank press release, 23/02/2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/02/23/updated-ukraine-recovery-and-reconstruction-needs-assessment-released); [Mindev.gov.ua, 2026](https://mindev.gov.ua/en/news/potreby-vidnovlennia-ukrainy-protiahom-nastupnykh-desiaty-rokiv-skladut-588-mlrd-dol-ssha)
- **War-conditional GDP rebound is the master variable.** IMF baseline: 2027 ~3.5%, 2028 ~4.2% (assuming war ends ~2026); downside (frozen conflict): ~1% (2027), ~0.5% (2028). World Bank: ceasefire-by-2027 → ~4% in 2027, ~4.5% in 2028 (below the once-hoped 7% first-year rebound, due to labour/infrastructure damage). [IMF Country Report 26/58, 2026](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1ukrea2026001-source-pdf.pdf); [IMF via NV, Apr-2026](https://english.nv.ua/business/imf-warns-ukraine-faces-another-weak-growth-year-in-2026-before-a-modest-2027-rebound-50600319.html)
- **SEPA accession = the single biggest payments catalyst for 2026-2028.** Cabinet approved the SEPA legislative package on 17/12/2025; awaits Verkhovna Rada; Balkan/Moldova precedent suggests ~6-12 months post-application. Saves €70-100m/yr in euro-transfer fees, ~€4,000/yr per SME (~120k EU-exporting SMEs), up to +0.3% GDP year-one. Pulls banks toward ISO 20022 + SCT/instant-payments compliance. [Euromaidan Press, 18/12/2025](https://euromaidanpress.com/2025/12/18/ukraine-save-100m-euro-transfers-sepa/); [Ukranews, 2025](https://ukranews.com/en/news/1123299-rada-announces-conditions-for-ukraine-s-accession-to-sepa)
- **Instant payments + ISO 20022 already being rolled out by NBU**, with all banks expected to support immediate transfers by end-2025 — directly relevant to acquiring/POS, switching and onboarding stacks. [NBU Instant Payments](https://bank.gov.ua/en/payments/ips); [NBU ISO 20022 project](https://bank.gov.ua/en/payments/project-iso20022)
- **Digitisation is structural, not cyclical: cashless ~95.5% by number / ~65.4% by value (2025); contactless ~85% of terminals; tokenised cards ~18.6m.** This raises demand for software/managed-services and contactless/tokenised acceptance, and constrains net ATM growth. [NBU via UNN, 2025](https://unn.ua/en/news/ukrainians-increased-the-volume-of-cashless-transactions-by-10percent-in-2025); [NBU via Mezha, 2025](https://mezha.net/eng/bukvy/ukraine-s-cashless-payment-card-transactions-rise-11-7-in-2025/)
- **Cash remains a wartime resilience backstop, not a growth segment.** NBU mandates ATMs be pre-stocked across denominations to avoid shortages during outages/holidays; ATM base broadly flat-to-declining (~15.6-15.7k, -0.6% in 2025). This favours energy-resilient, cash-recycling and high-availability self-service over net new units. [Visit Ukraine / NBU rules](https://visitukraine.today/blog/7511/how-banks-and-the-nbu-payment-system-will-operate-during-christmas-and-new-year-working-hours); [NBU via UNN on terminals/ATMs](https://unn.ua/en/news/number-of-payment-terminals-increased-by-more-than-71percent-nbu)
- **Diia digital identity is a unique onboarding rail.** ~23m users (~81% of adults), 33 digital documents incl. legally-equal digital passport, 70+ services, GovTech "Group A" leader; roadmap adds 100+ services and AI integration — a ready KYC/eID layer for digital account opening. [DigitalState.gov.ua, 2025](https://digitalstate.gov.ua/news/govtech/ponad-23-milyony-ukrayintsiv-uze-korystuiutsia-diyeiu-tsyfrova-derzava-stala-novoiu-normoiu); [UNDP, 2025](https://www.undp.org/ukraine/press-releases/63-ukrainians-use-state-e-services-user-numbers-grow-third-year-row-survey)
- **OECD financial-sector reform agenda points spend toward digital finance, banking-sector strengthening and SME access** — incl. gradual state-bank privatisation, which typically triggers technology renewal. [OECD, 2025](https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/stronger-financial-markets-and-institutions-for-ukraine-s-recovery_0c41c8ac-en/full-report/ukraine-s-financial-system-framework_c3ceabe7.html)
- **Rising fraud losses (NBU, 2025) sustain demand for fraud/security and compliance tooling** as transaction volumes climb. [AUB / NBU, 2025](https://aub.org.ua/en/why-losses-from-banking-fraudsters-increased-in-2025-an-analysis-by-the-nbu/)

## Printec implication

Ukraine pulls demand toward Printec's **software/managed-services and onboarding/identity** lines first, with a back-loaded hardware-rebuild upside:
- **Digital onboarding / eID (highest pull):** Diia's mature digital-identity rail + EU-accession KYC/AML alignment make Ukraine an ideal market for Printec's **onboarding and customer-management** products and managed digital account-opening.
- **Payments modernisation (near-term):** SEPA + ISO 20022 + instant payments create a hard, dated compliance deadline for acquirers and banks — demand for **POS/payment acceptance, switching, tokenised/contactless acceptance** and the associated managed services.
- **Self-service rebuilt for resilience, not growth:** flat-to-declining ATM count + outage exposure favour **cash-recycling, energy-resilient and high-availability self-service** and remote monitoring/managed services over net-new ATM volume — well aligned with the NCR Atleos cash-recycling and ATM-as-a-service proposition.
- **Security & compliance (steady, rising):** climbing transaction volumes and fraud losses sustain demand for **fraud, security and compliance** tooling.
- **Reconstruction (large but back-loaded, ceasefire-gated):** the ~$588bn programme and state-bank privatisation imply a multi-year branch/self-service re-equipment and core-modernisation cycle — material for Printec from ~2028 onward in the baseline scenario, near-zero in the frozen-conflict downside.
