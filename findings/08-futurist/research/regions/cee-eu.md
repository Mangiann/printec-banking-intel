# CEE EU footprint 3-5yr (Czechia, Slovakia, Hungary, Romania, Bulgaria, Croatia, Slovenia, Austria)

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc for Printec's eight CEE EU markets through 2031 is a **convergent two-speed story** — digital payments rails sprint ahead while cash remains structurally entrenched and is increasingly *protected by law*, not merely tolerated. Three forces define the window:

1. **The euro keeps expanding eastward, country by country.** Croatia joined in 2023, **Bulgaria became the 21st euro-area member on 01/01/2026** ([EU Council, 08/07/2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/07/08/bulgaria-ready-to-use-the-euro-from-1-january-2026-council-takes-final-steps/)), and **Romania targets ~2029-2030** (self-imposed 2029 now widely seen as slipping toward 2030+ on fiscal-deficit grounds) ([Romania & the euro, Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania_and_the_euro)). Each adoption forces a wholesale ATM/POS/cash-cycle conversion event (Croatia: ~70% of ATMs dispensing euro on day one, all by day 15; Bulgaria: full ATM cutover by 01/02/2026) — a recurring, deadline-driven hardware-and-software demand spike directly in Printec's wheelhouse ([ATM Marketplace](https://www.atmmarketplace.com/news/croatia-switches-to-euro/); [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/economy/1031798-bulgaria-transitions-to-euro-what-to-know-about-availability-of-bank-services)).

2. **Cash stays materially higher here than the euro-area average, and is being legislated as critical infrastructure.** SPACE 2024 puts Slovenia, Slovakia and Austria at **56% of spending by value in cash** and Croatia at 51% — well above the 39%-by-value euro-area mean ([Euronews / ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.euronews.com/business/2025/10/05/keeping-cash-across-europe-which-countries-use-the-most)). Hungary went furthest: a **14/04/2025 constitutional amendment makes cash payment a right**, and **Act XVIII of 2025 obliges providers to install/operate ATMs in every municipality** (fines HUF 2-200m), banning the discontinuation of existing withdrawal services ([CMS, 13/05/2025](https://cms.law/en/hun/legal-updates/new-hungarian-law-requires-atms-in-all-towns-and-villages)). The EU Single Currency Package (Council position 19/12/2025) layers an EU-wide cash-acceptance and cash-access obligation on top ([EU Council, 19/12/2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/19/single-currency-council-agrees-position-on-the-digital-euro-and-on-strengthening-the-role-of-cash/)).

3. **Digital rails accelerate fastest in the non-euro CEE markets.** Czechia's instant payments hit **>40% of all credit transfers** (40.8m in April 2026, +~50% YoY; 53% of Czechs now use them) ([CNB, 27/05/2026](https://www.cnb.cz/en/cnb-news/press-releases/Instant-payments-gain-popularity-most-Czechs-now-use-them-with-99-customer-satisfaction/)). Romania launched the **RoPay** national instant-mobile scheme (RoPay 2.0 live 01/09/2025) ([thepaypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/ropay-romania-launches-instant-mobile-payment-system)); Hungary's AFR instant system is mandatory for all banks; Slovakia's instant share reached 27% of SEPA transfers by end-2024 ([EPC](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/news-insights/insight/instant-payments-slovakia)).

**Net for 2027-2031:** withdrawal *volumes per machine* and standalone dispense-only ATM counts erode (the global FI-ATM base falls ~10% since 2017 and continues sliding to ~2.22m by 2030), but the *value* withdrawn rises and a wall of euro-conversion deadlines plus cash-access mandates keeps the regional installed base relevant and forces fleet *modernisation* toward recyclers and managed services ([Datos Insights / Euronet, Nov 2025](https://www.euronetatms.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Future-of-ATM-Economics.pdf), p.7-8). For euro-area members (AT, SI, SK, HR, BG) the **digital euro** (pilot H2-2027, possible issuance 2029) arrives within the window as a complement to cash, not a replacement ([ECB closing report, Oct 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/digital_euro/progress/html/ecb.deprp202510.en.html)). Confidence: **high** on direction (euro pipeline and cash-access mandates are codified law/dates); **medium** on pace (Romania's euro date and per-country ATM trajectories are uncertain; CEE-specific consulting forecasts are largely gated).

## Key drivers

- **Euro adoption pipeline = recurring forced-conversion events.** Croatia 2023, Bulgaria 01/01/2026 (21st member), Romania ~2029-2030. Bulgaria's ATMs deposited BGN / dispensed EUR through Jan 2026, fully EUR from 01/02/2026 — a complete cash-cycle reconfiguration ([EU Council, 08/07/2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/07/08/bulgaria-ready-to-use-the-euro-from-1-january-2026-council-takes-final-steps/); [ECB, Bulgaria adopts the euro](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/economic-bulletin/focus/2026/html/ecb.ebbox202508_01~b4379b735b.en.html); [BTA](https://www.bta.bg/en/news/economy/1031798-bulgaria-transitions-to-euro-what-to-know-about-availability-of-bank-services)).
- **CEE is structurally more cash-intensive than the euro-area average.** By value of spending, cash = 56% in Slovenia, Slovakia and Austria; 51% in Croatia (vs 39% euro-area). By transaction count Slovenia is among the highest at 64%; Croatia ~42% of POS ([Euronews / ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.euronews.com/business/2025/10/05/keeping-cash-across-europe-which-countries-use-the-most); [ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.space2024~19d46f0f17.en.html)).
- **Hungary: cash protected constitutionally + ATM-everywhere mandate.** 14/04/2025 constitutional right to pay in cash; Act XVIII of 2025 forces ATMs in every municipality, raises minimum withdrawal limit from HUF 75,000 to 150,000, and prohibits banks ceasing existing cash withdrawal services (effective 15/05/2025; fines HUF 2-200m) ([CMS, 13/05/2025](https://cms.law/en/hun/legal-updates/new-hungarian-law-requires-atms-in-all-towns-and-villages); [Lexology summary](https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=8bb0a51f-9651-4694-a147-5f83d2babace)).
- **EU-wide legal-tender / cash-access obligation.** Council (19/12/2025) backs effectively banning cash non-acceptance and requiring member-state remedial action where cash access deteriorates — directly underpinning ATM/branch self-service demand across all eight markets ([EU Council, 19/12/2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/19/single-currency-council-agrees-position-on-the-digital-euro-and-on-strengthening-the-role-of-cash/)).
- **Instant-payments surge (esp. non-euro CEE).** Czechia: instant >40% of credit transfers, 40.8m in April 2026 (+~50% YoY), 53% of population using ([CNB, 27/05/2026](https://www.cnb.cz/en/cnb-news/press-releases/Instant-payments-gain-popularity-most-Czechs-now-use-them-with-99-customer-satisfaction/)). Romania RoPay live 01/09/2025 ([thepaypers](https://thepaypers.com/fintech/news/ropay-romania-launches-instant-mobile-payment-system)). Slovakia 27% of SEPA transfers instant by end-2024 ([EPC](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/news-insights/insight/instant-payments-slovakia)). From Jan 2027 all banks must *accept* instant euro payments, sending 24/7 by July 2027 ([CNB](https://www.cnb.cz/en/cnb-news/press-releases/Instant-payments-gain-popularity-most-Czechs-now-use-them-with-99-customer-satisfaction/)).
- **Digital euro arrives within the window for euro-area CEE.** Pilot H2-2027 (12 months), possible first issuance 2029; ~EUR1.3bn dev cost, ~EUR320m/yr opex from 2029; Oesterreichische Nationalbank among the six NCBs building core components — designed to complement, not replace, cash ([ECB closing report, Oct 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/digital_euro/progress/html/ecb.deprp202510.en.html); [ECB, 30/10/2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2025/html/ecb.pr251030~8c5b5beef0.en.html)).
- **ATM economics force consolidation into smarter machines.** Global FI-ATM base −10% since 2017; cash-in-transit costs +~40% (2020-2025); ~75% of ATM operating cost is fixed; value withdrawn rising while withdrawal count falls — pushing banks toward fewer, multi-function, recycling ATMs and outsourced operation ([Datos Insights / Euronet, Nov 2025](https://www.euronetatms.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/The-Future-of-ATM-Economics.pdf), p.7-12).
- **Cash recycling is the growth pocket within a shrinking ATM market.** Over half of ATMs worldwide now accept automated deposits, most using recycling; global cash-recycling-ATM market projected from ~USD3.9bn (2023) to ~USD4.7bn (2030) at ~5% CAGR ([Allied Market Research](https://www.alliedmarketresearch.com/cash-recycling-atm-market-A14933)).
- **Digital-banking modernisation tailwind across CEE.** Mobile banking the fastest-growing European segment (~20% CAGR), cloud the largest (~60% share, ~22% CAGR), smartphone penetration ~80% — Poland, Romania, Hungary singled out as fast-transforming ([Market Data Forecast, Europe digital banking](https://www.marketdataforecast.com/market-reports/europe-digital-banking-market)).

## Printec implication

The CEE EU footprint is **the single best-aligned region in Printec's portfolio for the 2027-2031 thesis**: it combines above-average cash intensity, legally protected cash access, a steady drumbeat of euro-conversion deadlines, and fast-rising digital rails — pulling demand toward Printec's full stack rather than any single line.

- **Cash recycling & deposit automation (NCR Atleos) is the core pull.** With cash still 51-56% of spending by value in SI/SK/AT/HR and CIT costs +40%, banks rationalise dispense-only fleets into fewer multi-function recyclers — the one growing segment in a shrinking ATM market. Each recycler Printec deploys does more work at lower cash-handling cost, which is exactly what the ATM-economics squeeze demands.
- **Euro-adoption conversion projects are repeatable, deadline-driven revenue.** Bulgaria (2026) and the Romania pipeline (~2029-2030) each require wholesale ATM/POS/cash-cycle reconfiguration on a fixed timetable — Printec's hardware, software, integration and managed-services lines all monetise the event, and the Croatia/Bulgaria playbook is directly transferable to Romania.
- **Cash-access mandates de-risk the installed base.** Hungary's ATM-in-every-municipality law and the EU-wide cash-access obligation mean the *minimum* network is now legally defended — favouring Printec's branch self-service, banking-hub-style shared infrastructure, and managed-services (run/operate) model precisely where banks would otherwise cut.
- **ATM-as-a-Service / managed services is the margin and recurring-revenue story.** Rising fixed costs (~75% of ATM opex) push FIs to outsource operation; Printec's managed services convert the cost squeeze into multi-year recurring contracts across all eight markets.
- **Hedge into digital onboarding / POS / instant-payment rails for the digital-sprint side.** Czech instant payments >40% of transfers, Romania's RoPay, Hungary's AFR, and the 2027 instant-euro mandate plus digital euro (pilot 2027, issuance ~2029 in AT/SI/SK/HR/BG) point demand toward Printec's payments, software/compliance and digital-onboarding lines — capturing the value migrating off cash within the same accounts.

Note (per Printec compliance framing): all figures here are public third-party projections; any deployment-grade, security-sensitive or payment-related solution built on them must be flagged for human review and testing before being treated as production-ready, consistent with DORA/PCI DSS/GDPR/ISO 27001.
