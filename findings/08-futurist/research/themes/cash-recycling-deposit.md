# Cash recycling & deposit automation — recycler share of fleet, adoption curve

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc through 2031 is clear and high-confidence: while the **total** global ATM fleet is flat-to-contracting, the **recycler/deposit-automation share** of that fleet keeps climbing, and recyclers will represent the majority of new ATM shipments. The independent industry data house RBR/Datos Insights forecasts the worldwide recycler installed base to exceed **1.2 million machines by 2029** (nearly 20% growth over five years) with ~900,000 recycler devices shipping over 2024-2029 — and crucially that recyclers will account for **"well over half of all ATM shipments worldwide"** across the forecast period. So the dominant pattern is *substitution within a shrinking fleet*: cash points are consolidating, but the ones that remain are upgraded to deposit-capable recyclers. This is a transformation play (replacing dispense-only ATMs and branch teller cash handling) rather than a net-units-growth play in mature markets.

Three structural forces sustain this through 2031 even as digital payments rise: (1) the economics — cash handling can be up to ~50% of an ATM's total cost of ownership, and recyclers cut cash-in-transit visits by up to ~80% and replenishment effort by up to ~75%; (2) persistent cash demand and resilience expectations — euro-area cash is still 52% of point-of-sale transactions by number (2024) and 62% of consumers consider cash an important option; and (3) a hardening EU regulatory mandate (Council position, Dec 2025) requiring member states to guarantee access to cash *and deposit facilities* nationwide. Pace: steady mid-single-digit unit growth in the recycler base globally, with Asia-Pacific (esp. India) and North America as the volume growth engines and Europe/CEE running a mature *replacement-and-conversion* cycle. Confidence: high on direction and on the recycler-share-of-shipments inflection; medium on the precise installed-base figure (single primary forecaster, RBR/Datos, for the global series — triangulated qualitatively but not numerically by a second independent house in public sources).

## Key drivers

- **Recyclers now win the majority of ATM shipments** even as the overall ATM market contracts — installed base forecast >1.2M by 2029, ~20% five-year growth, ~900k units shipping, "well over half of all ATM shipments worldwide" [Datos Insights / RBR — Recycling ATMs Defy Market Downturn](https://datos-insights.com/press-release/recycling-atms-defy-market-downturn-with-continued-growth/).
- **Compelling unit economics**: cash handling is up to ~50% of an ATM's total cost of ownership; recyclers deliver ~20% lower TCO, up to ~75% less replenishment effort, and up to ~80% fewer cash-in-transit visits per year [ABA Banking Journal — Recycling the narrative on cash, 01/2026](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2026/01/recycling-the-narrative-on-cash/); [Diebold Nixdorf — Cash Recycling is About to Shake Up US Banking](https://www.dieboldnixdorf.com/en-us/banking/insights/blog/cash-recycling-is-about-to-shake-up-us-banking/).
- **Deposit volumes at automated endpoints are rising fast**, validating the recycler thesis: note deposits at connected ATMs grew +24% YoY (2025 vs 2024) and +39% over five years in North America; banknotes are ~70% of all deposits [ABA Banking Journal — Recycling the narrative on cash, 01/2026](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2026/01/recycling-the-narrative-on-cash/).
- **Cash remains structurally important**, sustaining the deposit/withdrawal cycle recyclers serve: cash is 52% of euro-area point-of-sale transactions by number (down from 59% in 2022) and 39% by value; 62% of consumers consider cash an important payment option (up from 60%) [ECB SPACE 2024 press release, 19/12/2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2024/html/ecb.pr241219~172b929461.en.html).
- **Businesses already deposit primarily via machines**: cash-in machines are the most popular corporate cash-deposit method (56%), ahead of the bank counter (49%) — the SME deposit-automation use case recyclers target [ECB SPACE 2024 — INSIGHT EU Monitoring summary](https://ieu-monitoring.com/editorial/space-study-cards-dominate-digital-payments-apps-rise-cash-remains-key/520157); [ECB SPACE 2024 press release](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2024/html/ecb.pr241219~172b929461.en.html).
- **EU regulatory tailwind**: the Council's December 2025 negotiating position requires member states to monitor and safeguard access to cash *and deposit facilities* nationwide (urban and rural), prohibit unjustified cash refusal, and ensure resilience — directly underpinning recycler/deposit-automation demand [Council of the EU — position on digital euro and strengthening cash, 19/12/2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/19/single-currency-council-agrees-position-on-the-digital-euro-and-on-strengthening-the-role-of-cash/); [ECB — Access to and acceptance of cash](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/cash_strategy/acceptance-cash/html/index.en.html).
- **ATM-as-a-Service bundles the upgrade**: vendors report accelerating recycler-led hardware demand sold through outsourced/managed models — NCR Atleos self-service banking revenue +~12% YoY (Q1 2026), ATMaaS +~30%, hardware +~23%, with recycling cited as a growth driver [NCR Atleos Q1 FY2026 earnings release (SEC 8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001974138/000197413826000004/ex-991ncratleosearningsrel.htm); [NCR Atleos — Cash Recycling ATM](https://www.ncratleos.com/banking/atm-itm/hardware/cash-recycling-atms).

## Printec implication

This pulls demand squarely toward Printec's **cash-recycling / deposit-automation hardware (NCR Atleos recyclers and multi-function ATMs/ITMs)** sold inside a **managed-services / ATM-as-a-Service wrapper** — the highest-conviction long-term product line in the portfolio. In Printec's CEE/SEE footprint the play is *fleet conversion*, not net new units: most cash points already exist and are dispense-only, so the addressable demand is replacing/upgrading them to recyclers and capturing the recurring service, cash-cycle optimisation and CIT-reduction revenue that follows. The +56% corporate "cash-in machine" deposit behaviour and the EU access-to-cash/deposit-facility mandate together open a parallel SME and retail-deposit-automation channel (closed cash ecosystem, smart safes, branch teller-cash replacement). Positioning Printec as the partner that runs the *whole cash cycle as a service* — rather than selling boxes — is what monetises this trend through 2031.

Note: any client-facing forecast figures sourced from market-research aggregators (e.g. Allied/Verified/Business Research market-size CAGRs) should be flagged for human verification before use, as their numbers diverged widely (5%-10%+ CAGR, USD 1.5B-5.8B) and lack the methodological transparency of RBR/Datos and ECB; the series above deliberately relies on the more authoritative sources.
