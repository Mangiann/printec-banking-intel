# Cash-to-digital shift & cash resilience — cash-in-circulation, withdrawal volumes/values, cash-access policy, by region 2027-2031

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc through 2031 is a **bifurcation, not a disappearance**. Two curves diverge:

1. **Transactional cash keeps falling, steadily, not cliff-like.** Globally cash fell to ~46% of payments in 2025 from 50% in 2023 — roughly a 4%/year erosion ([McKinsey 2025 Global Payments Report](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/global-payments-report)). In the euro area, cash at point of sale dropped from 59% of transactions (2022) to 52% (2024) by number, and from 42% to 39% by value ([ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2024/html/ecb.pr241219~172b929461.en.html)). The UK is the leading edge: cash is forecast to be only ~4% of all payments by 2034 ([UK Finance Payment Markets 2025](https://www.ukfinance.org.uk/policy-and-guidance/reports-and-publications/uk-cash-and-cash-machines-2025)). Extrapolating the euro-area pace, POS cash-by-number likely falls into the low-to-mid 40s% by 2027 and high-30s% by 2031 — still material, especially for small-value and P2P.

2. **Cash demand (store of value) and cash-as-resilience-backstop hold or grow.** This is the "paradox of banknotes": euro cash in circulation is ~€1.6trn, ~10% of euro-area GDP, and has held above 10% of GDP for a decade despite falling transactional use ([ECB Economic Bulletin 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/economic-bulletin/articles/2025/html/ecb.ebart202506_02~1a773e2ca3.en.html); [BIS commentary 2026](https://www.bis.org/statistics/payment_stats/commentary2604.pdf)). Crises spike demand sharply (COVID: +€140bn net issuance by end-2020; Ukraine invasion: +36% daily issuance in bordering countries) ([ECB Economic Bulletin 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/economic-bulletin/articles/2025/html/ecb.ebart202506_02~1a773e2ca3.en.html)).

The decisive 2027-2031 wildcard is **policy + geopolitics turning cash into critical national infrastructure**. The EU's Single Currency Package (Council position 19/12/2025) moves toward effectively banning cash non-acceptance and mandating member-state action where cash access deteriorates ([EU Council, 19/12/2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/19/single-currency-council-agrees-position-on-the-digital-euro-and-on-strengthening-the-role-of-cash/)). Sweden's Riksbank now tells households to hold cash and is legislating a cash obligation for essential goods, framing cash as "total defence" ([Riksbank / paymentexpert 2026](https://paymentexpert.com/2026/03/05/riksbank-promotes-cash-usage/)). Poland's NBP urges citizens to keep cash for outages ([TVP World](https://tvpworld.com/92154868/bank-urges-poles-to-keep-cash-over-more-frequent-digital-outages)).

**Net for 2027-2031:** withdrawal *volumes and ATM counts* shrink (UK ATMs −5% in 2025; Poland back to 2014 levels), but the *obligation to keep cash accessible and resilient* strengthens, and cash-in-circulation value stays high. Confidence: **high** on the directional split; **medium** on exact pace (paywalled consulting forecasts and the unresolved EU/digital-euro legislative path widen the cone). The digital euro (pilot H2-2027, possible issuance 2029) is explicitly designed to *complement, not replace* cash, so it does not collapse cash demand within the window ([ECB, June 2026](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/key/date/2026/html/ecb.sp260603_1~2801335beb.en.html)).

## Key drivers

- **Structural decline in transactional cash, ~4%/yr globally.** Cash = 46% of global payments in 2025, down from 50% in 2023; declining ~4% p.a. as wallets/A2A grow ([McKinsey 2025 Global Payments Report](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/global-payments-report)).
- **Euro-area POS shift, but cash still dominant for small/P2P.** Cash 52% of POS transactions by number in 2024 (was 59% in 2022); 22% of consumers still prefer cash and ~two-thirds want to keep it as an option ([ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2024/html/ecb.pr241219~172b929461.en.html)).
- **The banknote paradox — store-of-value demand persists.** Cash-in-circulation stays ~10% of euro-area GDP and ~9% of GDP across advanced economies despite falling use ([ECB Economic Bulletin 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/economic-bulletin/articles/2025/html/ecb.ebart202506_02~1a773e2ca3.en.html); [BIS commentary 2026](https://www.bis.org/statistics/payment_stats/commentary2604.pdf)).
- **Crisis resilience is a structural new tailwind.** Pandemic, war, and outages drive sharp cash spikes (April 2025 Iberian blackout: card spend −41/42% in affected areas, ATM withdrawals surged in unaffected areas) ([ECB Economic Bulletin 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/economic-bulletin/articles/2025/html/ecb.ebart202506_02~1a773e2ca3.en.html)).
- **EU legislating cash acceptance + access.** Council (19/12/2025) backs effectively banning cash non-acceptance and requiring remedial action when access is insufficient, with member-state compensation to providers ([EU Council 19/12/2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/19/single-currency-council-agrees-position-on-the-digital-euro-and-on-strengthening-the-role-of-cash/)).
- **Acceptance erosion among merchants is the real risk to cash, not consumer preference.** Euro-area company cash acceptance fell from 96% (2021) to 88% (2024) ([ECB, June 2026](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/key/date/2026/html/ecb.sp260603_1~2801335beb.en.html)).
- **Nordic / total-defence preparedness mandates.** Riksbank recommends ~SEK 1,000/adult in cash, offline card payments live by 01/07/2026, and a legislated cash obligation for essential goods ([Riksbank / fintech.global 2026](https://fintech.global/2026/03/06/riksbank-advises-cash-reserves-for-payment-resilience/); [paymentexpert 2026](https://paymentexpert.com/2026/03/05/riksbank-promotes-cash-usage/)).
- **CEE/SEE stays more cash-intensive than the euro-area average (Printec's core footprint).** Poland's cash share of transactions ~42.7% in Q2 2024; ATM withdrawal value still rising in nominal terms (PLN 104.9bn Q4 2024 → PLN 109.1bn Q4 2025) even as machine counts fall ([cashless.pl / NBP 2025](https://www.cashless.pl/18897-number-of-atms-in-poland)). Greece: 50% prefer cash for P2P ([ECB SPACE 2024 country data](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.space2024~19d46f0f17.en.html)).
- **ATM network rationalisation + shift to recycling/managed services.** UK ATMs −5% in 2025 (44,569 → 42,403); LINK forecasts ~20,000 free-to-use ATMs by 2032, still deemed sufficient ([UK Finance / LINK 2025](https://www.link.co.uk/news/average-uk-adult-withdrew-1-352-from-atms-in-2025)). Recycling can cut cash-handling cost up to ~50% ([NCR Atleos](https://www.ncratleos.com/insights/cash-recycling-improved-efficiency)) — vendor claim, treat as interested party.
- **Emerging-market leapfrog accelerates digital, lowering cash share there fastest.** 42% of adults in low/middle-income economies made a digital merchant payment in 2024, up from 35% in 2021 ([World Bank Global Findex 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/07/16/mobile-phone-technology-powers-saving-surge-in-developing-economies)).
- **Digital euro complements (not replaces) cash; arrives within window.** Pilot H2-2027 (12 months), possible issuance 2029; holding limits (analysed €500–€3,000) cap deposit flight; designed to preserve cash ([ECB preparation closing report 2025](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/digital_euro/progress/html/ecb.deprp202510.en.html); [ECB June 2026](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/key/date/2026/html/ecb.sp260603_1~2801335beb.en.html)).

## Printec implication

The data point **away from "ATM dispense-only" volume and toward intelligent, resilient cash infrastructure plus the digital rails that surround it** — Printec sits on the right side of both curves:

- **Cash-recycling & deposit automation (NCR Atleos partnership) is the core growth pull.** Falling withdrawal *volumes* + rising cash-handling cost pressure + still-high cash-in-circulation = banks consolidate ATM fleets into fewer, smarter, multi-function recyclers. Recycling cuts cash-handling cost up to ~50%, so each unit Printec deploys does more work. This is structurally favoured even as raw machine counts shrink (UK −5%/yr, Poland back to 2014 levels).
- **ATM-as-a-Service / managed services is the margin story.** As FIs hit a "critical inflection point" in ATM management complexity, they outsource run/manage/optimise — a recurring-revenue managed-services line for Printec across its 17 CEE/SEE markets, where cash intensity (Poland ~43%, Greece P2P 50%) keeps the installed base relevant longer than in the UK/Nordics.
- **Cash-access & resilience mandates are a regulatory tailwind for Printec's whole product line.** EU legal-tender/access-to-cash obligations, plus Sweden/Poland preparedness messaging, mean banks and states must *guarantee* cash access — driving demand for branch self-service, banking-hub-style shared infrastructure, offline-capable terminals, and cash-cycle software. Printec's CEE footprint is precisely where mandated minimum cash-access networks will be defended, not abandoned.
- **Hedge into digital onboarding/POS/payments for the transactional-decline side.** The 4%/yr secular fall and digital-euro arrival (pilot 2027, issuance ~2029) point demand toward Printec's POS/payments, digital onboarding and software/compliance lines — capturing the value migrating off cash rails within the same accounts.

Note (per Printec compliance framing): forecast figures here are public third-party projections; any deployment-grade or payment-related solution built on these should be flagged for human review and testing before being treated as production-ready.
