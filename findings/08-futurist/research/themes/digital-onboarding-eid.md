# Digital onboarding, eID / digital identity wallets (EUDI), eKYC

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The 2027-2031 window is when digital identity stops being a national patchwork and becomes a regulated, cross-border default in the EU — directly reshaping how banks onboard customers across Printec's footprint. The pivot point is eIDAS 2.0: the European Digital Identity (EUDI) Wallet must be offered by every member state by the end of 2026, and from late December 2027 banks and other "obligated" regulated private-sector relying parties are legally required to accept it for strong authentication and identification ([EU Commission – EUDI Wallet implementation](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eudi-wallet-implementation); [Deloitte Luxembourg – eIDAS 2.0 reshaping financial services](https://www.deloitte.com/lu/en/our-thinking/future-of-advice/eidas-2-0-digital-identity-revolution-reshaping-financial-services.html)). Layered on top, the EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR) enters full application in July 2027, hard-wiring stronger identity/KYC obligations into the same timeframe ([Deloitte Luxembourg](https://www.deloitte.com/lu/en/our-thinking/future-of-advice/eidas-2-0-digital-identity-revolution-reshaping-financial-services.html)).

The arc: 2026-2027 = wallet issuance + mandatory acceptance begins (compliance-driven build-out); 2028-2031 = scale, with the Commission targeting 80% of EU citizens using a digital ID by 2030 — a target most independent analysts believe will slip ~2 years to ~2032 ([ABI Research](https://www.abiresearch.com/market-research/insight/7783456-the-european-commissions-goal-of-eudi-wall)). Confidence: HIGH on direction and regulatory dates (primary EU sources), MEDIUM on adoption pace (analysts split between the 2030 official target and 2032 realistic case), MEDIUM-HIGH on the parallel structural driver: AI-generated deepfake fraud is breaking the "document + selfie" eKYC pattern, forcing a move to layered, wallet-anchored verification just as the regulation lands.

Net: demand pulls strongly toward bank-grade digital onboarding/eKYC platforms that are EUDI-Wallet-ready, with biometric liveness + deepfake defence as a now-mandatory layer rather than a differentiator.

## Key drivers

- **eIDAS 2.0 legal mandate with a hard 2027 deadline.** EUDI Wallet in force since 20/05/2024; at least one wallet per member state by end-2026; mandatory acceptance by banks and regulated private-sector relying parties from late December 2027 ([EU Commission – EUDI Wallet implementation](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eudi-wallet-implementation)); ([Deloitte Luxembourg](https://www.deloitte.com/lu/en/our-thinking/future-of-advice/eidas-2-0-digital-identity-revolution-reshaping-financial-services.html)).
- **AMLR full application July 2027** reinforces identity/KYC obligations in the same window, compounding the compliance push ([Deloitte Luxembourg](https://www.deloitte.com/lu/en/our-thinking/future-of-advice/eidas-2-0-digital-identity-revolution-reshaping-financial-services.html)).
- **Hard ROI on onboarding.** Early EUDI adopters report 40-60% reductions in onboarding abandonment; KYC automation can cut related operational costs 40-60%, and onboarding times in payments/financial services could fall by as much as 90% ([Deloitte Luxembourg](https://www.deloitte.com/lu/en/our-thinking/future-of-advice/eidas-2-0-digital-identity-revolution-reshaping-financial-services.html)).
- **Onboarding is broken today — large headroom.** Only 3% of banks' marketing teams consider onboarding seamless (1% in Europe); just 29% of onboarding data collection is fully automated with AI, yet 41% of executives plan to prioritise digital onboarding ([Capgemini World Retail Banking Report 2025](https://www.capgemini.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/WRBR_Final_25_weblock-version.pdf)).
- **Deepfake fraud is breaking legacy eKYC.** Gartner predicted 30% of enterprises would consider standalone identity verification unreliable by 2026 due to AI deepfakes; deepfake attacks hit identity systems roughly every 5 minutes in 2024 and document forgeries jumped 244% in a year ([Gartner press release, 01/02/2024](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-02-01-gartner-predicts-30-percent-of-enterprises-will-consider-identity-verification-and-authentication-solutions-unreliable-in-isolation-due-to-deepfakes-by-2026); [Biometric Update / QuantoSei summary 2026](https://news.quantosei.com/2026/06/03/liveness-detection-kyc-verification-2026/)). Deloitte projects US generative-AI fraud reaching $40bn by 2027, up from $12.3bn in 2023 ([Gartner/Deloitte coverage](https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2024-02-01-gartner-predicts-30-percent-of-enterprises-will-consider-identity-verification-and-authentication-solutions-unreliable-in-isolation-due-to-deepfakes-by-2026)).
- **EUDI banking use cases validated by EU large-scale pilots.** POTENTIAL (Apr 2023-Sep 2025) tested bank account opening as one of six core use cases; NOBID piloted wallet-based payment authorisation across Nordics + Italy/Germany, with "largely positive" user feedback but flagged real UX friction (PIN setup, multi-provider flows, data-sharing transparency) — i.e. integration/UX work remains ([EU Commission – EUDI Wallet implementation](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/eudi-wallet-implementation); [NOBID Consortium, 08/09/2025](https://www.nobidconsortium.com/nobid-wraps-up-successful-pilot-under-the-european-digital-identity-wallet-programme/)).
- **CEE is a live proving ground (Printec-relevant).** Poland's government mObywatel app passed 10 million users by end-July 2025; bank-issued mojeID is used by ~22 million people and accepted by most banks and offices — a striking benchmark for bank-anchored digital ID — with mObywatel 3.0 targeting EUDI/eIDAS 2.0 alignment and ~20m users by 2031 ([Biometric Update, 08/2025](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202508/poland-campaigns-to-boost-the-use-of-its-digital-id-platform-mobywatel); [Identity Week](https://identityweek.net/poland-is-developing-mobywatel-3-0-projecting-20-million-users-by-2031/)).
- **Macro inclusion driver.** McKinsey Global Institute estimates full digital-ID coverage could unlock economic value of 3-13% of GDP by 2030 (~3% mature economies, ~6% emerging), over half accruing to individuals — underpinning sustained government and bank investment ([McKinsey Global Institute](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/digital-identification-a-key-to-inclusive-growth)). World Bank ID4D: ~800m people still lack official ID and ~2.8bn lack a government-recognised digital identity ([World Bank ID4D blog](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/digital-development/global-progress-in-identification--3-findings-from-the-latest-da)).

## Printec implication

This theme pulls demand squarely toward Printec's **digital onboarding / eKYC** offering and its **security & compliance** managed services — and gives them a hard regulatory deadline (Dec 2027) that converts "nice to have" into "must comply" across 17 CEE/SEE markets. Three concrete pulls:

1. **EUDI-Wallet-ready onboarding.** Banks in Printec's footprint must accept the EUDI Wallet for identification/authentication by Dec 2027. Printec's onboarding stack should add a wallet-acceptance/relying-party connector (PID + bank-credential verification, QES) so client banks can comply without building it themselves — a clear, time-boxed integration revenue line.
2. **Deepfake-resilient eKYC as a layer, not a feature.** With legacy document+selfie now bypassable, demand shifts to layered liveness + biometric binding + injection-attack detection. Printec should position eKYC as a managed, continuously-updated defence (recurring revenue), not a one-off install — and flag to clients that any deployed verification code is security-sensitive and requires human review/testing before production.
3. **Bank-anchored ID as the regional pattern.** Poland's mojeID (22m bank-issued users vs 10m government) shows banks themselves can own the digital-ID rail. Printec can broker the bank-as-identity-provider opportunity in markets where the state wallet lags, bridging national eID, EUDI compliance, and onboarding.

Note (compliance): all identity-verification and onboarding code Printec ships is payment/security-sensitive and must be flagged for human review and testing before deployment; treat GDPR/PCI DSS/AMLR alignment as a gating requirement and route data-subject-rights or breach questions to the DPO/Compliance team.
