# SoftPOS / Tap-to-Phone & acquiring fragmentation; merchant payment acceptance

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc is clear and convergent across vendors, analysts and the ECB: by 2027-2031 software-based card acceptance (SoftPOS / Tap-to-Phone) moves from niche to a **mainstream, default acceptance channel** for micro- and small merchants, while dedicated terminal hardware does NOT disappear but is squeezed at the entry tier. Multiple independent sources triangulate on the same direction:

- **Explosive but small-base SoftPOS growth.** Juniper Research forecasts global SoftPOS transaction value rising from **USD 23.9bn (2025) to USD 540bn (2030)** — a ~2,150% increase — and the installed base of SoftPOS-enabled smartphones reaching **61 million by 2028 (+683% vs 2023)**. Note these are very large percentage moves off a small base; SoftPOS is still a minority of the broader mPOS pool (Juniper: mPOS USD 2.7tn → USD 4.9tn 2025-2030).
- **The enabling rails are now near-universal.** Apple Tap to Pay on iPhone reached 43 countries/regions by Sept 2025 and — critically for Printec — now covers essentially the entire CEE/SEE footprint (Czechia, Romania, Bulgaria, Hungary, Poland, Slovakia, Slovenia, Croatia, Cyprus, Greece, the Baltics). Visa reports +200% YoY Tap-to-Phone growth globally (FY23-FY24); Mastercard's Tap on Phone is live in 110+ markets. The Android+iOS pincer removes the last hardware excuse for small-merchant acceptance.
- **Cash is structurally receding at POS** (the demand engine under SoftPOS): ECB SPACE shows euro-area cash falling from 72% of POS transactions (2019) → 59% (2022) → **52% (2024)** by number, with mobile-device payments doubling to 6%. This is gradual, not a cliff — cash is sticky in CEE/SEE — but the direction underpins ~10-year acceptance growth.
- **Pace & confidence:** Direction = HIGH confidence (every source class agrees). Magnitude of SoftPOS dollar forecasts = MEDIUM confidence (analyst point-estimates diverge widely and are interested/early-base). Hardware displacement timing = MEDIUM-LOW (certification cost, receipt/PIN needs, and merchant inertia slow absolute cannibalisation).

The second, equally important arc is **acquiring fragmentation flipping toward consolidation + software-led control**. The merchant-acceptance value chain is moving from terminal-centric to software-centric: vertical SaaS/ISVs increasingly own the merchant relationship and embed payments (and SoftPOS) as a feature, while PSP/acquirer M&A accelerates (Adyen-Talon.One €750m, Mollie-GoCardless €1.1bn, CVC-Nexi, Worldline retrenching to Europe). For a hardware-led integrator like Printec, the strategic risk is being disintermediated by software platforms; the opportunity is to supply the certified, managed-service "rails" (SoftPOS SDK, MPoC compliance, device fleet, cash-handling for the residual cash economy) under those platforms.

## Key drivers

- SoftPOS transaction value forecast to grow ~2,150% from USD 23.9bn (2025) to USD 540bn (2030), driven by democratising acceptance for micro/SMB transitioning from cash. [Juniper Research](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/soft-pos-transactions-to-accelerate-by-2150/)
- SoftPOS-enabled smartphones forecast to reach 61 million by 2028, +683% vs 2023, driven by Apple's Tap to Pay roll-out in merchant-dense markets (Brazil, France, UK). [Juniper Research](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/global-soft-pos-transaction-value-to-experience-massive-growth-of-1-050-by-2028-driven-by-apple-s-tap-to-pay-roll-out/)
- Broader mPOS transaction value to grow 83% from USD 2.7tn (2025) to USD 4.9tn (2030); Juniper notes mPOS hardware remains more resilient than SoftPOS, which mainly displaces cash in small/occasional transactions — i.e. SoftPOS complements rather than wholly replaces terminals. [Juniper Research](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/mpos-transactions-to-grow-by-83-percent/)
- Visa Tap to Phone adoption +200% YoY globally and +234% in top markets (US, UK, Brazil); "millions of sellers," ~30% of them new small businesses (VisaNet FY23-FY24). [Visa Newsroom](https://usa.visa.com/about-visa/newsroom/press-releases.releaseId.21271.html)
- Mastercard Tap on Phone live in 110+ markets; Cloud Commerce moves ~90% of contactless kernel functionality to the cloud, extending acceptance to lower-cost devices. [Mastercard](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2025/october/Mastercard-Merchant-Cloud-simplify-and-support-commerce-growth-in-global-acceptance-ecosystem.html)
- Apple Tap to Pay on iPhone reached 43 countries/regions by Sept 2025, covering nearly all of Printec's CEE/SEE footprint; supporting acquirers include Adyen, SumUp, Revolut, Viva, Worldline, Global Payments, Nets, Stripe, Mollie. [Apple Newsroom](https://www.apple.com/uk/newsroom/2025/09/apple-expands-tap-to-pay-on-iphone-to-five-more-countries-across-europe/)
- Euro-area cash use at POS fell from 72% (2019) → 59% (2022) → 52% (2024) by number of transactions; mobile-device payments doubled from 3% to 6% (2022→2024). The secular cash-to-digital shift is the demand engine but remains gradual. [ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.space2024~19d46f0f17.en.html)
- Contactless card payments in the euro area rose 15.5% to 29.5bn transactions in H2 2024 (value €0.8tn, +15.1%), confirming tap-to-pay is now the dominant card-present mode that SoftPOS rides on. [ECB Payments Statistics 2024 H2](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/stats/paysec/html/ecb.pis2024h2~5ada0087d2.en.html)
- PCI MPoC v1.1 (Nov 2024) is the formal global security standard for software card acceptance; certification is costly/complex (per-model fees and testing backlogs), which favours scaled, compliance-capable providers over fragmented entrants — a barrier-to-entry tailwind for a managed-service vendor. [Fime — PCI MPoC](https://www.fime.com/blog/blog-15/post/pci-mpoc-what-you-need-to-know-about-the-global-security-standard-530)
- Acquiring is consolidating and software is capturing the merchant relationship: Adyen-Talon.One (€750m), Mollie-GoCardless (€1.1bn), CVC-Nexi, Worldline retrenching to Europe; ~two-thirds of US ISVs consolidating payment suppliers (Adyen survey). SoftPOS risks becoming "a feature inside someone else's product." [Business of Payments, May 2026](https://businessofpayments.substack.com/p/business-of-payments-may-2026)
- Rising memory-chip costs are pushing up traditional terminal prices, structurally improving SoftPOS's relative economics. [Business of Payments, May 2026](https://businessofpayments.substack.com/p/business-of-payments-may-2026)
- Merchant acquiring services forecast ~8.7% CAGR, surpassing USD 160bn revenue by 2026; overall payments revenue to reach USD 3.1tn by 2028 (slower ~5%/yr growth), with acquirers pushed toward intelligent routing, real-time settlement and multirail (card + A2A + stablecoin). [McKinsey Global Payments Report 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/global-payments-report)
- Instant / account-to-account payments to reach 22% of non-cash transaction volume by 2028 (EU IPR mandate live Oct 2025) — a medium-term competitive threat to card-based acceptance economics that SoftPOS providers must hedge. [Capgemini World Payments Report 2025](https://www.capgemini.com/insights/research-library/world-payments-report-2025/)
- CEE is a leading SoftPOS growth region: Eastern Europe ~USD 7.4bn / 30% of one market sizing in 2025 at the highest regional CAGR; Poland is a top-tier global SoftPOS market, aided by the national cashless programme (up to PLN 1,000 terminal reimbursement). [Grand View Research — SoftPOS](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/softpos-market-report)

## Printec implication

Demand is pulled toward **SoftPOS / Tap-to-Phone as a managed, certified acceptance offering** rather than entry-tier terminal hardware sales. Concretely:

1. **Add/scale a SoftPOS line** (own SDK or white-labelled, PCI MPoC-certified) bundled into Printec's POS/payments and onboarding portfolio — targeting micro/SMB, mobile/field staff, queue-busting and pop-up/event acceptance across the 17-country footprint. Apple Tap to Pay now covers nearly all of those markets, so the consumer-side rail is ready.
2. **Defend the entry-terminal book**: expect erosion of low-end standalone-terminal demand (rising chip-driven hardware prices accelerate SoftPOS substitution). Reposition hardware toward smart/Android POS and managed device fleets where receipts, PIN-on-glass and integration still need hardware.
3. **Compliance & security as the moat**: PCI MPoC complexity favours scaled providers — Printec can sell certification, security and managed-service wrap to banks/ISVs/PSPs who want SoftPOS but not the compliance burden. This is consistent with Printec's security/compliance positioning (flag any acceptance code/security config as requiring human review and testing before deployment).
4. **Channel risk to manage**: vertical SaaS/ISVs are capturing the merchant relationship and acquiring is consolidating; Printec should partner to be the rails/managed-service layer beneath platforms rather than competing for the merchant front-end. The residual cash economy (still 52% of euro-area POS by number, higher in CEE/SEE) keeps Printec's cash-handling/ATM-recycling relevant as a hedge during the transition.
