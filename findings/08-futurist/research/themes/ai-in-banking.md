# AI in banking — fraud, service, ops, predictive maintenance, gen-AI

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc through 2027-2031 is a shift from AI as a productivity *assistant* (copilots, chatbots, fraud-scoring models) to AI as an *operator* — autonomous "agentic" systems embedded in regulated workflows (payments, compliance, risk, service dispatch). Confidence in the *direction* is high and broadly triangulated across consulting, regulators and vendors; confidence in the *pace and magnitude* is moderate, because a large gap persists between strategic intent and production-scale value capture (MIT-cited "only ~5% of pilots delivered significant value at scale"; ~23% of banks past pilot per Accenture-cited Q1 2026 data).

Four sub-trends matter for a transaction-technology vendor like Printec:
- **Gen-AI value is concentrated and material but mostly productivity, not revenue** — McKinsey sizes USD 200-340bn/yr of potential value for banking (9-15% of operating profit), largely productivity. Citi sizes a ~9% / USD 170bn profit uplift by 2028.
- **Fraud/financial-crime is the most defensible near-term ROI** — generative/behavioural AI is already delivering step-changes (Mastercard: up to +300% detection, up to -200% false positives). This is also the one Annex-III use case the EU AI Act *exempts* from "high-risk", lowering the compliance barrier relative to credit scoring.
- **Predictive maintenance & AI-assisted field service is real and shipping at fleet scale** — NCR Atleos (Printec's key partner) reports 95%+ diagnostic accuracy, 13% global reduction in service revisits and a predictive-maintenance PoC underway across 600,000+ ATMs. This directly upgrades Printec's managed-services margin.
- **Regulation becomes a hard gate from 2026-2027** — EU AI Act high-risk obligations (credit scoring, biometric ID) bite from 02/08/2026 (legacy systems 02/08/2027; possible Digital Omnibus slip to 12/2027), layered on DORA/GDPR. This is itself a demand driver for compliant, auditable, human-oversight tooling.

Net: AI pulls banking demand toward (a) AI-enhanced fraud/security at the endpoint, (b) AI-driven predictive maintenance / managed services, and (c) agentic self-service and onboarding — all squarely in Printec's portfolio.

## Key drivers

- Gen-AI could add USD 200-340bn annually to banking (9-15% of operating profit), largely from productivity, on top of a global cross-industry potential of USD 2.6-4.4tn/yr across 63 use cases — [McKinsey, Economic potential of generative AI](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/the-economic-potential-of-generative-ai-the-next-productivity-frontier) ; [McKinsey, Capturing the full value of gen AI in banking](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/capturing-the-full-value-of-generative-ai-in-banking).
- Citi estimates AI could boost banking-industry profits ~9% (~USD 170bn) by 2028, and finds banking the sector most exposed to AI job displacement (54% of roles at risk, +12% augmented) — [The Financial Brand on Citi GPS report](https://thefinancialbrand.com/news/artificial-intelligence-banking/catching-up-and-moving-forward-with-genai-in-banking-179666) ; [Investment News on Citi](https://www.investmentnews.com/industry-news/the-ai-jobs-threat-is-biggest-for-banking-says-citi/254657).
- Adoption is shifting decisively from pilots to production: 77% of banks have launched/soft-launched gen-AI in 2025 (vs 61% in 2023) and 47% have rolled out applications (vs 10% in 2023) — [EY-Parthenon GenAI in banking survey](https://www.ey.com/en_us/insights/banking-capital-markets/ai-in-banking-ey-parthenon-genai-survey-insights).
- Agentic AI is the next wave: Gartner names it the top strategic tech trend and projects AI systems completing multi-day work without human oversight by 2027; financial-services AI investment projected to reach ~USD 97bn by 2027 (WEF/Accenture) — [Gartner, Banking Predicts 2026](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/7201830) ; [Accenture, Agentic AI and the future of work in FS](https://bankingblog.accenture.com/agentic-ai-future-of-work).
- Capgemini frames 2025-onward as the move from ML → gen-AI → autonomous agents; banks could optimise up to 66% of time on operations/documentation/compliance/onboarding via gen-AI copilots, yet 61% of customers still contact a human because they were unhappy with chatbot resolutions — [Capgemini World Retail Banking Report 2025](https://www.capgemini.com/insights/research-library/world-retail-banking-report/) (PDF: [WRBR 2025](https://www.capgemini.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/WRBR_Final_25_weblock-version.pdf)).
- Fraud is the highest-conviction ROI: Mastercard reports generative AI lifting fraud detection rates by up to 300% and cutting false positives by up to ~200%, plus 300% faster identification of at-risk merchants — [Mastercard press release, May 2024](https://www.mastercard.com/us/en/news-and-trends/press/2024/may/mastercard-accelerates-card-fraud-detection-with-generative-ai-technology.html).
- Predictive maintenance / AI field-service is live at fleet scale at NCR Atleos (Printec's key partner): 95%+ diagnostic accuracy, 13% global reduction in service revisits, 50% drop in North America tech-support cases, 87% engineer adoption / 35,000+ uses per week across 600,000+ ATMs, with an AI predictive-maintenance PoC underway — [NCR Atleos / ATMIA Peter Kulik award (StockTitan)](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/NATL/ncr-atleos-wins-atmia-s-peter-kulik-innovation-award-for-its-ai-7wead4rsihx2.html).
- Regulation hardens into a gate and a demand driver: EU AI Act classifies credit scoring/creditworthiness and biometric ID as high-risk (Annex III), but exempts AI used for financial-fraud detection; high-risk obligations apply from 02/08/2026 (legacy systems to 02/08/2027), with penalties up to EUR 15m / 3% of global turnover — [EU AI Act, Annex III](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/annex/3/) ; [European Commission AI Act framework](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai).
- Central banks/regulators legitimise and pressure-test AI use (fraud, AML, supervision) while flagging gen-AI's *amplification* of fraud (deepfakes, synthetic identity) and systemic risk — [BIS speech, AI and central banks, Oct 2025](https://www.bis.org/speeches/sp251009.htm) ; [FSB, Financial Stability Implications of AI, Nov 2024 (BIS exec summary)](https://www.bis.org/fsi/fsisummaries/exsum_23904.htm).
- AI-specific bank spend grows ~3x faster than total bank IT: Gartner forecasts banking/investment-services AI *software* spend rising 18.8% in 2024 to ~EUR 31.3bn and reaching ~EUR 55.2bn by 2027 (5-yr CAGR ~19.9%), within total bank IT spend of ~EUR 857.5bn in 2026 heading to ~EUR 1.1tn by 2029 — [Gartner, Enterprise IT spending for banking 4Q25](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/7431362) ; [Gartner, AI software spending in banking 2023-2027](https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/5318563).

## Printec implication

AI is a tailwind across Printec's core lines rather than a threat:
- **Managed services / ATM-as-a-Service fleet:** the NCR Atleos predictive-maintenance and AI-assisted service stack is the single most directly bankable trend for Printec — fewer revisits, higher uptime, lower tech-support cost lift managed-services margin and let Printec sell "AI-optimised availability" SLAs across its 17-country, NCR-Atleos-anchored fleet. Highest near-term, lowest-regulatory-friction opportunity.
- **Security / fraud at the endpoint:** AI video analytics (skimmer/attack detection), behavioural biometrics and gen-AI fraud scoring pull demand toward Printec's security and anti-fraud offerings — and fraud detection is the EU-AI-Act-exempt use case, so it deploys faster than credit-scoring AI.
- **Self-service & onboarding:** agentic / conversational AI and AI-guided self-service (voice, digital-human assistants, automated KYC/onboarding where Capgemini sees up to 66% time saved) pull demand toward Printec's self-service and onboarding platforms — but the Capgemini "61% still want a human" signal argues for *assisted*, not fully-autonomous, deployments.
- **Compliance as a product:** the EU AI Act + DORA + GDPR stack makes auditable, human-oversight, logged AI a buying criterion — Printec can position compliant-by-design AI tooling as a differentiator for CEE/SEE banks lagging on AI governance.
