# Self-service & assisted-service growth (kiosks, video teller, in-branch automation)

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc through 2031 is a structural shift of the physical channel from staffed transaction processing to **automated self-service plus remote-human "assisted service."** The branch is not disappearing as fast as headlines suggest, but its interior is being rebuilt around machines: cash-recycling ATMs, teller cash recyclers (TCRs), interactive/video teller machines (ITMs), onboarding kiosks and interactive walls, with the floor staff repositioned to advisory. The consensus design target across vendors and consultants is to migrate **80-90% of routine teller transactions** onto self/assisted-service formats, while keeping a thin layer of human advice for complex needs.

Pace and confidence:
- **High confidence** that the *mix* shifts decisively toward self/assisted service: every independent strand (McKinsey, Diebold Nixdorf, NCR Atleos, Datos Insights/RBR, ECB) points the same way, and the underlying cash-usage and labour-cost drivers are durable.
- **High confidence** that ITM/video-teller and cash-recycling segments *grow* even as the total ATM fleet *shrinks* — recyclers are forecast to exceed half of all ATM shipments and the ITM market to roughly triple by the early 2030s.
- **Medium confidence** on absolute unit counts: the public, named-vendor ITM/kiosk market sizes come largely from commercial market-research houses (Astute Analytica, Grand View) whose methodologies are not transparent; treat the levels as directional and the *trend* as well-supported. Total-ATM and recycler forecasts from Datos Insights/RBR are the most credible series.
- Mature markets (Western Europe, North America, parts of CEE) lead this transition; emerging CEE/SEE and APAC markets still add machines, favouring recyclers and assisted-service to extend reach where branches close.

Net: demand moves *away* from staffed branches and simple cash-dispense ATMs, and *toward* recycling-capable, assisted-service-capable, software-and-managed-service-rich endpoints — the core of Printec's footprint.

## Key drivers

- **Consulting design target: 90% of simple activity to self/assisted service, ITMs as 24/7 "branch in a box."** McKinsey's "bank branch for the digital age" describes smart branches with teller counters and most back-office gone, a self-service zone of ATMs, ITMs, service terminals and interactive walls, and ITMs that add a remote human link to extend hours to 24/7 — explicitly targeting migration of >90% of simple customer activities to assisted or self-service formats. [McKinsey – A bank branch for the digital age](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/a-bank-branch-for-the-digital-age)
- **Vendor roadmap: migrate up to 80% of teller transactions to ATMs with on-demand video/chat.** Diebold Nixdorf's August 2025 Branch Automation portfolio is built to move up to 80% of traditional teller transactions onto ATMs/TCRs via core integration, with video/chat assistance on demand and managed services on a cloud-native platform — turning branches into "advisory-led service hubs." [Diebold Nixdorf press release, 25/08/2025](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/diebold-nixdorf-unveils-new-branch-automation-solutions-portfolio-302536926.html)
- **Cash recyclers are the one growing hardware segment, even as the ATM fleet shrinks.** Datos Insights/RBR forecasts global ATM numbers falling to ~2.9 million by end-2028, while recycling ATMs rise ~20% to more than 1.2 million by 2029 and account for over half of all ATM shipments in the forecast period; recyclers underpin assisted/self-service cash automation. [Datos Insights – Recycling ATMs defy market downturn](https://datos-insights.com/press-release/recycling-atms-defy-market-downturn-with-continued-growth/) · [Datos Insights – Advanced terminals reshape global ATM landscape](https://datos-insights.com/press-release/advanced-terminals-and-emerging-markets-reshape-global-atm-landscape/)
- **Vendor financials confirm the money is moving to services and recurring revenue, not box-shifting.** NCR Atleos (Printec's key partner) reported FY2025 Self-Service Banking revenue +7%, ATM-as-a-Service +33%, ATMaaS annualized recurring revenue of ~$/£288m, with ITMs positioned as self-service plus live video-teller assistance. [NCR Atleos FY2025 results, 26/02/2026](https://investor.ncratleos.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/173/ncr-atleos-corporation-reports-strong-results-in-2025) · [NCR Atleos – Interactive Teller Machine](https://www.ncratleos.com/insights/what-is-an-interactive-teller-machine)
- **Declining cash-at-POS but persistent cash *importance* keeps assisted cash access strategically required.** ECB SPACE 2024: cash share of POS transactions fell to 52% by volume (from 72% in 2019) and 39% by value, yet 62% of consumers still consider cash important and perceived access to cash deteriorated — pushing banks to maintain reach via shared/assisted self-service rather than full staffed branches. [ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.space2024~19d46f0f17.en.html) · [ECB press release, 19/12/2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/pr/date/2024/html/ecb.pr241219~172b929461.en.html)
- **Labour shortages, branch closures and extended-hours economics drive ITM/video-teller adoption.** Real deployments show the value case: a $500m credit union moved 75% of branch transactions to ITM drive-thru saving ~$240,000/year in labour; community banks use ITMs to extend hours and reach amid staffing shortages; in the US in 2025, 1 in 4 closed branches were in low-population ZIPs, making remote-assisted endpoints the way to keep coverage. [Tidal Commerce – ITM pros, cons & future](https://www.tidalcommerce.com/learn/interactive-teller-machines) · [The Financial Brand – branch closures forecast](https://thefinancialbrand.com/news/banking-branch-transformation/bank-branch-closures-will-surge-again-heres-why-147050)
- **Customer-experience pressure favours seamless "start-anywhere, finish-anywhere" omnichannel including the assisted physical touchpoint.** Capgemini's retail-banking research finds low satisfaction with bank contact (only ~32% satisfied in the 2024 read) and pushes end-to-end omnichannel journeys — the rationale for video-teller/kiosk that bridges digital and branch. [Capgemini World Retail Banking Report 2025](https://www.capgemini.com/insights/research-library/world-retail-banking-report/)

## Printec implication

This theme pulls demand squarely toward Printec's core, partner-aligned product lines:
- **Cash-recycling ATMs and teller cash recyclers (TCRs)** — the only growing hardware segment; Printec's NCR Atleos partnership positions it to refresh declining single-function fleets into recyclers across its 17 CEE/SEE markets.
- **Interactive/video teller machines (ITMs) and assisted-service kiosks** — the "branch in a box" with remote human help directly answers branch-closure and labour-shortage pressure in CEE/SEE; a natural extension of Printec's self-service and in-branch automation lines.
- **ATM-/self-service-as-a-Service and managed services** — the value is migrating from hardware to recurring software + managed services (mirroring NCR Atleos's +33% ATMaaS growth); Printec's managed-services offering is where margin and stickiness sit.
- **Onboarding kiosks + compliance/security** — assisted self-service onboarding (KYC at the kiosk) ties to Printec's onboarding, security and compliance lines, and to the "advisory hub" branch model.

Strategic read: position Printec as the partner that converts shrinking, ageing branch/ATM estates into a smaller number of higher-value recycling + assisted-service endpoints delivered as a managed service — the single most durable demand vector in the physical channel through 2031.
