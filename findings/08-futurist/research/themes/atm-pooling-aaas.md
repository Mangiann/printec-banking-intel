# ATM pooling / ATM-as-a-Service / IAD estate transfers / managed ATM operations

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc is one of **decoupling ownership from operation**. Over 2027-2031 the global ATM installed base stays broadly flat-to-shrinking, but who *owns and runs* those machines shifts decisively away from individual banks toward three overlapping operating models: (1) **shared "pooling" utilities** (one neutral network serving many banks' cardholders); (2) **ATM-as-a-Service (ATMaaS)** — banks keep the customer relationship but outsource the entire device lifecycle to a vendor on a per-transaction/subscription basis; and (3) **bank-to-IAD estate transfers** — banks sell or hand operation of their fleets to independent ATM deployers. The economic engine is the same in all three: cash handling is 4-10% of European bank operating costs, and pooling/outsourcing cuts ATM network cost by up to ~40%, so as cash usage falls but cash *access* becomes legally mandated, banks rationalise by sharing or outsourcing rather than simply removing machines.

Pace and confidence: the **structural direction is High-confidence** — it is already visible in mature markets (Netherlands, Belgium, Sweden, Spain, France, UK) and the vendor/market data converges on a ~9-12% CAGR for ATMaaS/managed services through the early 2030s on a low-single-digit overall ATM market. The **CEE/SEE timing is Medium confidence** — Printec's footprint is one cycle behind Western Europe, but two 2025 accelerants are now in the data: (a) NCR Atleos (Printec's key partner) is winning ATMaaS at scale in Europe including in-footprint (Greece/Epirus Bank, ING Spain), and (b) regulators are mandating cash access *and explicitly permitting joint operation* — Hungary's Act XVIII of 2025 requires ATMs in every municipality and allows banks to install/operate them jointly, the textbook precondition for pooling. Net: ATMaaS and managed operations are the fastest-growing slice of an otherwise flat ATM market, and pooling moves from a Western-European phenomenon into the footprint over the window.

## Key drivers

- **ATMaaS / managed-services is the growth segment of a flat ATM market.** ATM-as-a-Service is forecast at ~9-12.5% CAGR while the overall ATM market grows only ~3.6% — i.e. outsourced operation grows ~3x the device market. ATMaaS sized at ~USD 9.14bn (2025) rising to ~USD 21.72bn (2035), 9.04% CAGR. [Business Research Insights — ATMaaS](https://www.businessresearchinsights.com/market-reports/atm-as-a-service-market-102493) · [Grand View — ATM market 3.6% CAGR](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/atm-market)
- **ATM managed-services market roughly doubling to the mid-2030s.** ~USD 9.04bn (2025) → ~USD 20.85bn (2034) at ~10% CAGR, with Europe ~USD 1.69bn / 18.7% share in 2025; an independent estimate puts it at USD 8.45bn (2024) → USD 12.29bn (2033) at 4.25% CAGR — the spread itself signals an emerging, fast-but-uncertain category. [Fortune Business Insights — ATM Managed Services](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/atm-managed-services-market-113472) · [ATM Marketplace / Research & Markets](https://www.atmmarketplace.com/news/atm-managed-services-market-to-reach-1229b-by-2033/)
- **Pooling cuts ATM network cost by up to ~40% and physically consolidates fleets** — cash handling is 4-10% of European bank opex (up to 7% in cash-intensive Germany/Austria). Sweden's Bankomat cut the shared fleet from 2,700 to 1,200 ATMs; Netherlands' Geldmaat from 6,400 to 3,800 — same access, far fewer machines. [Tietoevry — ATM Pooling](https://www.tietoevry.com/en/campaigns/2025/atm-pooling/)
- **The vendor proof point — NCR Atleos (Printec's key partner) is the #1 ATMaaS/managed-services provider and ATMaaS is its fastest-growing line.** It services 600,000+ ATMs across 140+ countries; ATMaaS revenue rose 32% YoY in Q2 2025 (to ~USD 62m vs ~USD 47m), backlog up 105% YoY, and Q4 2025 set a record with >USD 200m of ATMaaS contract value booked in a single quarter. Recent European wins: ING Spain, Lloyds, and in-footprint Epirus Bank (Greece). [NCR Atleos Q2 2025 slides](https://www.investing.com/news/company-news/ncr-atleos-q2-2025-slides-atmaas-surge-drives-9-eps-growth-outlook-reaffirmed-93CH-4174778) · [NCR Atleos ATMaaS](https://www.ncratleos.com/banking/atm-as-a-service)
- **NCR Atleos guides its serviced/installed base toward outsourced/managed models**, ~590,000 (2024) → ~620,000 units within 3-5 years, deliberately weighted to managed and outsourced solutions rather than device sales. [Quartr — NCR Atleos investor summary](https://quartr.com/companies/ncr-atleos-corporation_15638) · [NCR Atleos 2025 results](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260226147008/en/NCR-Atleos-Corporation-Reports-Strong-Results-in-2025)
- **Bank-to-IAD estate transfers are now mainstream in Western Europe and reach into the footprint.** IADs hold ~18% of ATMs globally; Brink's France took ~7,500 ATMs from BPCE (2019 deal, transferred 2022) to reach a 20% share of the entire French market; Euronet runs Swedbank's 1,000+ Baltic ATMs (2024) and operates an IAD/shared network across 32+ European countries. In Poland, Euronet and Planet Cash built fleets largely from bank terminal acquisitions. [Datos Insights via Financial IT — IADs resilient](https://financialit.net/news/atms/independent-atm-deployers-remain-resilient-thanks-to-new-deals-with-banks) · [Euronet — IAD expansion Belgium/Mexico](https://www.euronetatms.com/euronet-expands-independent-atm-network-into-belgium-and-mexico/)
- **Pooling utilities are proliferating and now combine pooling WITH ATMaaS** — Geldmaat (NL, 3 banks, ~3,800 ATMs), Batopin (BE, 4 banks, run as pooling + ATMaaS via Auriga), Bankomat (SE, 5 banks), TAM (Turkey, 2021, ~⅓ of Turkish ATMs), TecBan/Banco24Horas (Brazil, 23,000+ ATMs by end-2024), plus Euronet's multi-bank shared network in Spain (8+ banks added). [Datos Insights — Pooling resources](https://datos-insights.com/blog/atm-pooling-atmass-optimising-networks-reducing-costs/) · [Euronet — 8 more Spanish banks](https://www.atmmarketplace.com/news/euronet-worldwide-adds-8-more-banks-to-its-atm-shared-network-in-spain/)
- **Regulation is the structural accelerant — and it explicitly enables pooling.** Hungary's Act XVIII of 2025 (after an April 2025 constitutional amendment making cash a right) obliges payment service providers to operate ATMs in every municipality (first round: settlements >2,000 inhabitants) AND permits **joint installation and operation** — the legal precondition for pooling — inside Printec's footprint. At EU level, the provisional PSD3/PSR agreement (27 Nov 2025) and the Commission's legal-tender/cash-acceptance proposal push to preserve cash access as branches and bank-owned ATMs shrink. [CMS Law-Now — Hungary ATM Act](https://cms-lawnow.com/en/ealerts/2025/05/new-hungarian-law-requires-atms-in-all-towns-and-villages) · [ECB — Access to and acceptance of cash](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/euro/cash_strategy/acceptance-cash/html/index.en.html)

## Printec implication

This theme is the strongest pull toward Printec's **managed ATM operations / ATM-as-a-Service and cash-management services** lines, and it reframes the hardware business. As banks across CEE/SEE move from owning to outsourcing, demand shifts from one-off ATM box sales toward **multi-year recurring contracts**: end-to-end ATM lifecycle management (monitoring, first/second-line maintenance, software, cash forecasting/CIT orchestration, compliance) priced per-device or per-transaction. Printec should productise an **ATMaaS offer co-built on its NCR Atleos partnership** — riding Atleos' #1 ATMaaS position and its in-footprint wins (Epirus Bank/Greece) to capture local operation, integration and field-service scope. Three concrete plays: (1) position as the **neutral operator for emerging pooling utilities** where multiple footprint banks share a network — the Geldmaat/Batopin model is the template, and Hungary's Act XVIII legally invites it; (2) target **bank-to-IAD / estate-transfer deals** as banks shed branch-attached estates, competing with or partnering alongside Euronet-style IADs by offering managed operation rather than estate ownership; (3) layer **AI-enabled predictive maintenance and cash optimisation** (Atleos Intelligent Dispatch/Diagnostics analogue) as the differentiator that makes recurring managed-services margins defensible. The risk to flag: if a pan-regional IAD (Euronet) or Atleos-direct ATMaaS captures the operating layer, Printec is relegated to subcontracted field service — so the strategic move is to own the customer-facing managed-services contract, not just the wrench.
