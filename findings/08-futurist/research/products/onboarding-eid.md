# 3-5yr demand outlook: digital onboarding / eID / e-signature

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

Demand for digital onboarding, eID and e-signature is set for **structurally strong, regulation-forced growth** across 2027-2031 — this is one of the most policy-driven, high-confidence growth vectors in Printec's portfolio. The arc is steep and the pace accelerates into the back half of the window, because in the EU two regulatory clocks converge precisely in Printec's footprint:

- **eIDAS 2.0 / EU Digital Identity (EUDI) Wallet**: every Member State must offer at least one wallet by Dec 2026, and by **Dec 2027 banks/financial services (and other regulated sectors) must accept the wallet** for strong-authentication use cases. The Digital Decade sets an **80% citizen-uptake of a digital ID target by 2030** (vs ~52% private-use eID in 2025). ([EU Commission digital-strategy](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/electronic-identification), [Wikipedia EUDI Wallet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EU_Digital_Identity_Wallet))
- **AML Regulation (AMLR, EU 2024/1624)** applies directly from **10 July 2027**, with AMLA supervision from 2028. It mandates an "eIDAS-first" approach to customer due diligence and explicitly blesses fully remote onboarding (liveness, document capture, time-stamped audit trails) — removing the in-branch/video-call default for low-risk customers. ([Signicat AMLR](https://www.signicat.com/blog/amlr-explained-what-changes-in-2027), [EY AMLA/AMLR](https://www.ey.com/en_nl/insights/financial-services/how-eu-leaders-can-stay-ahead-of-amla-and-amlr))

The global market data reinforce the same direction independently of EU policy: identity-verification, digital-signature and digital-account-opening markets are all forecast to grow at **15-40% CAGR** into 2030. Confidence is **HIGH** on direction and on the EU regulatory triggers (legislated, dated); confidence is **MEDIUM** on the magnitude of vendor/analyst market-size figures, which vary 2-3x by firm and should be read as a growth signal, not a precise euro number.

Net: this is a **rising, compounding demand pool** for Printec — pulled hardest by EU regulatory deadlines in 2027 and the 2030 Digital Decade target — with mature/Western markets leading and the lower-eID-adoption CEE/SEE markets (Romania 10%, Bulgaria 12%, Slovakia 14% eID use in 2025) representing the largest *catch-up* upside.

## Key drivers

- **eIDAS 2.0 hard deadlines**: wallet availability in every Member State by end-2026; mandatory acceptance by banking/finance and other regulated relying parties by end-2027 — turning the wallet into a default onboarding channel where adoption is high. ([eIDAS readiness timeline](https://eidasreadiness.com/eidas-2-timeline), [EU Commission Regulation page](https://ec.europa.eu/digital-building-blocks/sites/spaces/EUDIGITALIDENTITYWALLET/pages/915931811/The+European+Digital+Identity+Regulation))
- **Digital Decade 2030 target of 80% citizen uptake of a digital ID** (and 100% access to secure eID), the EUDI Wallet being the primary instrument — a large gap to today's ~52% private-use baseline that demand must close. ([Consilium Path to the Digital Decade](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/policies/path-to-the-digital-decade-the-eu-s-plan-to-achieve-a-digital-europe-by-2030/), [EU digital-strategy eID](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/electronic-identification))
- **AMLR direct effect from 10 July 2027 + AMLA from 2028**: a single EU rulebook that mandates electronic identification at "substantial/high" assurance and authorises fully remote onboarding — converting compliance into a procurement trigger for digital onboarding/eID/e-signature stacks. ([Signicat AMLR](https://www.signicat.com/blog/amlr-explained-what-changes-in-2027), [Fourthline AMLA prep](https://www.fourthline.com/blog/how-to-prepare-for-amla-compliance-what-financial-institutions-must-do-before-2027))
- **Onboarding abandonment economics**: in regulated markets such as banking, nearly one in two prospective customers abandons onboarding due to poor UX — a direct revenue driver for friction-reducing eID/e-signature onboarding. ([Signicat / EUDI onboarding](https://www.signicat.com/blog/eudi-wallet-digital-onboarding))
- **Verification volume explosion**: ~75bn digital ID verification checks globally in 2024, ~86bn in 2025; spend on IDV checks rising 74% to ~$26bn by 2029 — the underlying transaction tide Printec's onboarding/eID products ride. ([Juniper press](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/pressreleasesdigital-id-verification-spend-to-exceed-26bn/), [Biometric Update](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202412/world-to-spend-26b-on-idv-checks-by-2029-juniper))
- **Digital wallet ubiquity**: 5.6bn people (>67% of the global population) forecast to use digital wallets by 2029, up from 52.6% in 2024 — the device-side rail on which eID wallets and e-signature ride. ([Biometric Update / Juniper](https://www.biometricupdate.com/202501/juniper-5-6b-people-to-have-digital-wallets-by-2029))
- **Inclusion / catch-up tailwind**: ~850m people still lack official ID and ~3.3bn lack a digital ID usable for online transactions — a long-run demand floor in emerging markets; McKinsey estimates digital ID can unlock economic value of ~3% of GDP in mature and ~6% in emerging economies by 2030. ([World Bank blog](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/digital-development/850-million-people-globally-dont-have-id-why-matters-and-what-we-can-do-about), [McKinsey MGI](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/digital-identification-a-key-to-inclusive-growth))
- **CEE/SEE catch-up dispersion is the upside**: 2025 eID private-use ranges from 99% (Denmark) to 10% (Romania), 12% (Bulgaria), 14% (Slovakia), 15% (Germany) — the low-adoption end sits squarely in Printec's footprint and faces the same 2027/2030 deadlines, implying the steepest growth curves. ([Eurostat e-gov & eID](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=E-government_and_electronic_identification))

## Printec implication

This vector pulls demand directly toward Printec's **digital onboarding / eID / e-signature and KYC/AML compliance products** — the highest-conviction software-and-services growth lane in the portfolio over 2027-2031. Concretely:

- **EUDI Wallet enablement is the marquee opportunity.** Banks in all 17 footprint countries face a hard end-2027 deadline to *accept* the wallet. Printec should position as the integration/relying-party layer connecting bank onboarding flows to national EUDI wallets and other eIDs (the "eID hub" pattern), plus wallet-based step-up authentication for existing customers.
- **AMLR 2027 is a forced procurement window.** The single rulebook + "eIDAS-first" CDD turns compliance into a budgeted, dated buying event for remote onboarding with liveness, document verification and time-stamped audit trails — a natural managed-service and software bundle for Printec.
- **CEE/SEE catch-up is Printec's structural edge.** The footprint contains both the EU's lowest eID-adoption markets (Romania, Bulgaria, Slovakia) and the same 2027/2030 deadlines — the steepest demand curves sit inside Printec's geography, where it already has bank relationships and field/managed-services presence.
- **Bridges the cash-to-digital pivot.** Onboarding/eID/e-signature is the credible high-growth software offset to flat-to-declining ATM hardware demand, deepening recurring-revenue and managed-services share of the mix.

Note (per Printec policy): any onboarding/eID/e-signature code or architecture is security- and payment-sensitive and must undergo human security review and testing before deployment; do not treat vendor or analyst figures above as production decisions without DPO/Compliance review for GDPR/AMLR/eIDAS alignment.
