# 3-5yr demand outlook: monitoring, telemetry, transaction analytics

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

Demand for monitoring, telemetry and transaction analytics is on a **strong, multi-year upward arc** even as the physical estate Printec services (ATMs, branches, self-service) plateaus or gently shrinks. The two trends are causally linked, not contradictory: as banks thin branches and ATM unit counts decline (~2%/yr globally), the *remaining* devices must hit higher availability targets with fewer field staff, which pulls spend out of "break-fix" hardware and into **remote monitoring, telemetry-driven predictive maintenance and managed services**. In parallel, the regulatory and fraud environment (EU Instant Payments Regulation / Verification of Payee, surging AI-enabled fraud) is forcing a structural shift from static rule-sets to **real-time, AI-based transaction monitoring** across the whole payments stack.

Three reinforcing engines drive the outlook:
- **Device/estate telemetry & predictive maintenance** — vendor model (NCR Atleos ATM-as-a-Service, CARE, Intelligent Diagnostics) is the template; the broader predictive-maintenance market grows ~11-25% CAGR depending on scope.
- **Transaction monitoring / AML / fraud analytics** — the most directly relevant software market, growing ~16% CAGR to roughly USD 42-48bn by 2030-31, with cloud and AI the fastest sub-segments.
- **AIOps / observability** for the IT and channel layer underneath, growing ~15-30% CAGR.

**Confidence: HIGH** on direction (all three source families agree); **MEDIUM** on the precise size of the slice Printec can address, because market-research absolute figures vary 2-3x by scope and CEE/SEE-specific numbers are not separately published. Objectively, this is the demand category most insulated from the cash-decline headwind — it is *fed* by it.

## Key drivers

- **ATM unit counts decline but availability bars rise — forcing telemetry/remote ops.** Global ATM count fell ~2% in 2024 to ~2.9m and is forecast to keep falling through end-2028; yet the ATM *market value* still grows ~3.6% CAGR to USD 31.64bn by 2030 — value migrates from hardware boxes to software, monitoring and managed services. [Datos/RBR via Digital Transactions](https://www.digitaltransactions.net/deployers-are-driving-growth-in-the-u-s-but-overall-the-atm-count-is-still-sliding/) · [Grand View ATM market press release](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/press-release/global-atm-market)
- **The dominant vendor is explicitly re-platforming around AI monitoring.** NCR Atleos (Printec's key partner) delivered 5.8 million additional hours of ATM availability in 2025, a 23% YoY improvement across revisits/retrips/outliers, via AI-driven Intelligent Dispatch, Intelligent Diagnostics, remote resolution (eliminating truck rolls) and fleet-level CARE telemetry — across a fleet of 600,000+ serviced ATMs. This is the demand signal Printec resells/operates locally. [NCR Atleos newsroom](https://www.ncratleos.com/news/ncr-atleos-delivers-5-8-million-hours-of-atm-availability-to-customers-through-advanced-service-innovation-and-continuous-improvement)
- **EU Instant Payments Regulation + Verification of Payee = a hard regulatory demand pull for real-time transaction analytics.** VoP became mandatory for euro-area PSPs on 09/10/2025 and extends to non-euro EU/EEA PSPs by 09/07/2027 — squarely covering Printec's CEE/SEE footprint over the outlook window. It mandates real-time payee name/IBAN matching on all SEPA credit transfers, explicitly to cut fraud and erroneous transfers. [PwC Legal — VoP under IPR](https://legal.pwc.de/en/news/articles/verification-of-payee-requirements-vop-under-the-eus-instant-payments-regulation-ipr) · [European Payments Council — VoP rulebook in force](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/document-library/press-releases/verification-payee-scheme-rulebook-now-force)
- **Fraud is growing faster than transaction volumes, and shifting to AI-vs-AI.** Banking fraud losses are forecast to rise 153% from USD 23bn (2025) to USD 58.3bn (2030); fraud-detection & prevention platform spend already exceeds USD 11.8bn in 2025 (up from 9.3bn in 2021). McKinsey/BCG argue static rule-sets are "architecturally incompatible" with adaptive AI fraud, mandating continuous model retraining and real-time monitoring. [Juniper — fraud to cost FIs $58.3bn by 2030](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/fraud-to-cost-financial-institutions-58bn/) · [McKinsey — agentic AI in financial crime](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/risk-and-resilience/our-insights/how-agentic-ai-can-change-the-way-banks-fight-financial-crime)
- **Cash still matters but is declining at PoS — keeping ATMs/recyclers in service while making each device's uptime more economically critical.** Euro-area cash at PoS fell to 52% of transactions (2024) from 59% (2022); 79% (2016). A shrinking-but-essential cash estate is the textbook case for telemetry-led "do more with fewer, higher-uptime devices." [ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.space2024~19d46f0f17.en.html)
- **AI's banking value case is concentrated in exactly these functions.** BCG estimates AI could unlock ~USD 370bn profit potential for retail banks by 2030, with cost reduction of 30-40% concentrated in service, compliance, risk and exceptions — i.e. the monitoring/analytics layer. [BCG — $370bn AI profit potential](https://www.bcg.com/press/5november2025-300-billion-profit-potential-retail-banks-via-ai)
- **The underlying IT-operations layer (AIOps/observability) is itself a fast-growing demand category** that channel-monitoring platforms ride on, growing ~15-30% CAGR through 2030. Gartner: "there is no future of IT Operations that does not include AIOps." [Grand View AIOps market](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/aiops-platform-market) · [IBM on Gartner AIOps guide](https://www.ibm.com/think/insights/gartner-market-guide-for-aiops-essential-reading-for-itops-and-sre)

## Printec implication

This is the strongest "pull" category in the Printec portfolio for 2027-2031, and the one that converts the cash-decline narrative from a threat into a tailwind. Demand is pulled toward:
- **Managed services + remote monitoring / telemetry for the device estate** — re-selling and locally operating NCR Atleos ATM-as-a-Service, CARE-style fleet telemetry, Intelligent Diagnostics and predictive maintenance, so that CEE/SEE banks hit 98.5%+ availability while cutting field-service truck rolls. This is the natural defence as ATM unit counts soften: monetise *uptime and operations*, not boxes.
- **Real-time transaction analytics / fraud & AML monitoring** — a regulation-mandated, fast-growing software demand (VoP by 2027 across non-euro EU footprint; AI fraud surge). Printec can position monitoring/transaction-analytics and onboarding/security as a compliance-driven, recurring-revenue layer adjacent to its payments and onboarding lines.
- **Recurring, software/services revenue mix** — mirrors the explicit Atleos strategy shift toward network, software, maintenance and managed-services revenue; supports Printec's EBITDA quality by trading declining one-off hardware margin for sticky telemetry/analytics subscriptions.

Caveat per compliance posture: any fraud/AML/transaction-monitoring or payment-related capability Printec builds or resells is security- and payment-sensitive and must undergo human review and testing (PCI DSS, DORA, GDPR) before being presented as production-ready.
