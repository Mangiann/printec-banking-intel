# 3-5yr demand outlook: ATMs & cash recyclers / intelligent deposit

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc is a **declining unit count of total ATMs but a rising share — and rising absolute base — of cash recyclers / intelligent-deposit machines**. Two forces run in opposite directions and both favour Printec's product mix:

1. **Total ATM installed base shrinks slowly.** The global base has fallen every year since 2018; it was ~2.9 million in 2024 and is forecast to fall a further ~4% by the end of the decade (Datos Insights/RBR). This is a managed contraction, not a collapse — mature Western European and North American bank fleets are being optimised down, while MEA and Latin America grow.
2. **Within that shrinking base, recyclers/intelligent deposit take over.** For the first time, over half of the world's ATMs now accept automated deposits, most using recycling technology. The recycler base is forecast to grow almost 20% over five years to **>1.2 million units by 2029**, with recyclers accounting for **well over half of all ATM shipments worldwide** over the forecast period (Datos Insights, Jan 2025). So even as gross machine counts fall, the addressable demand for the higher-value, multi-function deposit/recycling unit Printec sells is structurally rising.

Underpinning the floor under cash hardware: the **"paradox of banknotes"** — euro cash in circulation hit a record €1.59 trillion (≈10% of euro-area GDP) at end-2024 even as transactional cash share fell, because cash is held as a store of value (ECB). And the EU is moving to **legislate access-to-cash and mandatory cash acceptance** (Single Currency Package / Council position Dec 2025), which obliges Member States to maintain "sufficient and effective" cash access — a regulatory backstop against fleet attrition in Printec's EU markets.

**Pace:** Gradual, not abrupt — single-digit annual ATM-count declines; high-single-digit growth in the recycler segment. **Confidence: high** on direction (regulators, multiple analysts, and vendors all agree); **medium** on exact magnitudes (private analyst forecasts not independently re-derivable, market-sizing reports diverge 8–10% CAGR).

**CEE/SEE note (Printec's footprint):** Central & Eastern Europe was one of the few regions to show a **marginal increase in installed base in 2024**, and IAD ATM numbers are rising particularly in Poland and Uzbekistan — i.e. Printec's home regions are running counter to the mature-market decline, which lengthens the local demand runway.

## Key drivers

- **Recyclers are now the majority deposit technology and the growth engine of the contracting market** — >50% of ATMs accept automated deposits, recycler base to exceed 1.2M by 2029 (~20% growth), recyclers = "well over half of all ATM shipments worldwide" over the forecast period; ~900k recycler shipments expected over five years. [Datos Insights — Recycling ATMs defy market downturn](https://datos-insights.com/press-release/recycling-atms-defy-market-downturn-with-continued-growth/)
- **Total ATM base shrinks but slowly** — 2.9M in 2024 (−2% YoY), falling every year since 2018, ~4% further decline forecast by end of decade; MEA fastest-growing region, Latin America returns to growth, Western Europe declining on bank removals. [Datos Insights — Advanced terminals and emerging markets reshape global ATM landscape](https://datos-insights.com/press-release/advanced-terminals-and-emerging-markets-reshape-global-atm-landscape/)
- **Cash usage at POS declines but remains the most-used method** — euro-area cash share of POS volume fell 72%→59%→52% (2019/2022/2024) and by value 47%→42%→39%; yet cash was still used in ~50% of POS transactions in 2024 and 62% want to keep the cash option. [ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.space2024~19d46f0f17.en.html)
- **The banknote paradox keeps cash hardware relevant** — euro cash in circulation at record €1.59T (~10% of GDP) at end-2024; the "demise of cash has not materialised" and the decline is slower than predicted. [ECB — Cash is alive… and somewhat young?](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/economic-bulletin/articles/2025/html/ecb.ebart202505_03~d74cb56069.en.html)
- **Regulatory backstop on cash access** — EU Single Currency Package obliges Member States to ensure "sufficient and effective access to cash" and (Council Dec 2025 position) to generally ban non-acceptance of cash; explicit concern about ATM/branch closures harming access. [EU Commission — Single Currency Package](https://ec.europa.eu/commission/presscorner/detail/en/ip_23_3501) · [Council — agrees position on digital euro and strengthening role of cash, 19/12/2025](https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/19/single-currency-council-agrees-position-on-the-digital-euro-and-on-strengthening-the-role-of-cash/)
- **Branch transformation pulls deposit functions onto self-service** — record UK branch closures (410 in 2024, 433 in 2025), driving migration of teller transactions to recyclers/ASSTs; teller cash recyclers (TCRs) and video-assisted terminals adopted to enable "universal banker" branch redesign. Cash handling/replenishment = ~50% of ATM network operating cost, the core economic case for recycling. [CoinLaw — Bank branch closure statistics](https://coinlaw.io/bank-branch-closure-statistics/) · [ATM Marketplace — 2025 self-service outlook](https://www.atmmarketplace.com/blogs/a-2025-self-service-outlook-advancements-shaping-the-future-of-banking/)
- **Key partner vendor confirms the recycling/as-a-service tilt** — NCR Atleos repositioning as a "software-led ATM-as-a-Service" company; ATMaaS revenue +32% YoY to $62M in Q2 2025, 33.2k ATMaaS units by Sep-2025; multi-function SelfServ 80 series (dispense + deposit + recycling). Treat as an interested party. [NCR Atleos Q2 2025 results](https://investor.ncratleos.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/87/ncr-atleos-corporation-reports-second-quarter-2025-results)

## Printec implication

Demand is pulled decisively toward **cash recyclers / intelligent-deposit machines and the managed services around them**, not single-function cash dispensers. Concretely:

- **Lead with recyclers and intelligent-deposit (NCR Atleos SelfServ 80-series, cash-recycling units), not dispense-only ATMs.** This is the only growth segment of an otherwise shrinking hardware market, and it carries higher value per unit and a richer software/service attach.
- **Push ATM-as-a-Service / managed-services.** With cash handling/replenishment ≈50% of ATM operating cost and the partner (NCR Atleos) pivoting to ATMaaS, Printec's managed-services line is the natural monetisation of the fleet-optimisation theme — fewer machines, each more functional and outsourced.
- **Branch-transformation hardware (TCRs, video-assisted/ASST terminals)** is a parallel demand pull as branches close/redesign — a cross-sell from the ATM line into the in-branch automation Printec already does.
- **CEE/SEE timing advantage:** Printec's core markets are still flat-to-growing in installed base and rising in IAD deployment, so the mature-market decline narrative does NOT yet apply locally — the recycler upgrade cycle is the near-term opportunity, with the EU access-to-cash regulation providing a demand floor across its euro-area markets.

Note: vendor figures (NCR Atleos) are an interested party and any payment/security-related deployment specifics require human review and testing before being treated as production-ready.
