# 3-5yr demand outlook: managed services / ATM-as-a-Service / outsourcing

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

**Direction: structurally rising, and the fastest-growing demand vector in the whole ATM value chain — even as the underlying hardware market grows only low-single-digits.** The 2027-2031 window is when ATM outsourcing crosses from "early-majority" into mainstream banking practice. The arc is clear and supported across vendor, advisory and regulator sources: banks are decoupling from *owning* the cash channel while still needing it to run, and they are buying the channel as a managed outcome instead. This shows up as a widening gap between the slow-growing ATM hardware market (~3% CAGR) and the much faster managed-services / outsourcing layer (~6-8% CAGR) — and within that, full ATM-as-a-Service (ATMaaS) is the fastest sub-segment of all (vendor bookings growing 30-40% y/y off a small base).

**Pace:** Adoption is at an inflection. The most recent industry survey (KAL / ATM Marketplace 2025/26) puts *full* ATM-network outsourcing at 19% of banks today with another 24% planning to adopt within two years — i.e. a near-doubling of the addressable installed base is being signalled into exactly the 2027-2028 window. Total-cost-of-ownership pressure (cash ops = up to ~10% of bank operating costs per McKinsey) plus rising-but-non-strategic ATM reliance (51% of banks say reliance is *up*) is the demand engine.

**Confidence: high on direction, medium on the precise size.** The direction is corroborated by independent advisory (McKinsey, Deloitte), regulator data (ECB SPACE — cash still 52% of POS transactions by count, so the channel is not disappearing), a named industry survey (KAL/ATM Marketplace), several market-research houses, *and* a hard structural signal: Brink's agreed (Feb 2026) to acquire NCR Atleos for $6.6bn explicitly to build a recurring-revenue cash-management/ATMaaS platform, targeted to close Q1 2027. The size estimate is medium-confidence only because market-research houses disagree by ~2x on absolute market value depending on scope definition (managed services vs. broad outsourcing). The trend, not the level, is the reliable signal.

## Key drivers

- **Cash is declining but structurally permanent — banks want the channel without owning it.** ECB SPACE 2024: cash is still used in 52% of euro-area POS transactions by number (down from 59% in 2022) and 39% by value; 62% of consumers say cash-as-an-option remains important. A channel that is shrinking-but-essential and non-differentiating is the textbook outsourcing candidate. [ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.space2024~19d46f0f17.en.html)
- **Cost pressure is the primary trigger.** McKinsey notes cash operations can account for up to ~10% of banks' operating costs; Deloitte (cited in industry coverage) estimates outsourcing cuts total ATM operational cost by 15-30% via scale and specialisation. [McKinsey nonpersonnel cost](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/a-new-frontier-for-transforming-nonpersonnel-cost-management-in-banking) · [Brink's AMS / Deloitte cite](https://brinks-ams.com/cash-trends-in-atm-managed-services/)
- **Adoption is at an inflection point into the 2027-28 window.** KAL/ATM Marketplace 2025/26 survey: 19% of banks now use full ATM-network outsourcing, +24% plan to adopt ATMaaS within two years; ATM reliance is *rising* for 51% of banks (vs 31% the prior year), and hardware maintenance, cash management and operational monitoring are the most-outsourced functions. [KAL 2026 ATM trends](https://www.kal.com/en/kal-atm-news/atm-industry-insights/top-2026-atm-trends-for-customer-focused-banking)
- **The "ATM Continuum" framing legitimises a staged migration.** NCR Atleos + Datos Insights (Mar 2025) define four stops — traditional DIY → managed services → advanced services → full ATMaaS — giving banks an on-ramp (start with managed services, end at full outsourcing). This staged model is exactly how mid-tier banks/credit unions that "lack resources to maintain and upgrade a proprietary fleet" enter. [NCR Atleos/Datos paper](https://www.ncratleos.com/news/ncr-atleos-unveils-comprehensive-paper-on-optimizing-atm-operations-through-atm-as-a-service-atmaas)
- **The dominant vendor is restructuring its whole business around recurring outsourcing revenue.** NCR Atleos 2025: Self-Service Banking revenue +7%, *ATMaaS revenue +33-37% y/y*, ATMaaS annualised recurring revenue reached $288M, record Q4 bookings >$200M quarterly contract value; recurring revenue was ~71% of total ($3.08bn). [NCR Atleos 2025 results](https://investor.ncratleos.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/173/ncr-atleos-corporation-reports-strong-results-in-2025)
- **Structural consolidation bet: Brink's to acquire NCR Atleos for $6.6bn (announced Feb 2026, targeted close Q1 2027).** Combined ~$10bn revenue entity built to deliver "software, maintenance, repairs, cash logistics, and total ATM outsourcing services" on a subscription basis — an explicit market wager that outsourcing/ATMaaS is the durable demand vector. *Treat as an interested-party signal, but a very strong one.* [Brink's/Atleos $6.6bn](https://www.ncratleos.com/news/brinks-to-acquire-ncr-atleos-for-6-6-billion-creating-leading-financial-technology-infrastructure-company)
- **CEE/SEE relevance is concrete, not hypothetical.** NCR Atleos is modernising/expanding Epirus Bank's ATM network across Greece on the ATMaaS model + Cashzone network, deployment started Nov 2025 — Printec's core territory and partner. [Founders/ATMaaS momentum coverage](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/ncr-atleos-natl-turning-atm-170627467.html)
- **AI/cloud operations raise the outsourcing value proposition.** Predictive maintenance, AI diagnostics and cloud estate analytics let outsourcers deliver uptime gains in-house banks can't match — NCR Atleos added 5.8M hours of ATM availability in 2025 (23% y/y improvement) via AI-enabled diagnostics. [NCR Atleos 5.8M uptime hours](https://www.stocktitan.net/news/NATL/ncr-atleos-delivers-5-8-million-hours-of-atm-availability-to-htrxztkie2p9.html)

## Printec implication

This pulls demand squarely toward **Printec Managed Services / ATM-as-a-Service / outsourcing** — the highest-growth, highest-recurring-margin part of Printec's portfolio and the strategic centre of gravity for 2027-2031. The structural story works directly in Printec's favour for three reasons:

1. **Demand is shifting from one-off hardware sales to recurring, outcome-based contracts.** Printec already offers ATM/ITM hardware, multivendor software, security, monitoring, cash optimisation *and* managed services — i.e. it can sell the whole "ATM Continuum" and walk CEE/SEE banks up it from managed services to full ATMaaS. The differentiated, defensible margin is in the recurring services layer, not the box.

2. **Printec's NCR Atleos partnership is now an even stronger asset.** The dominant vendor is restructuring around ATMaaS (33-37% y/y growth) and merging into a $10bn cash-management platform; Printec is the localised, in-country delivery, support and integration partner across 17 CEE/SEE markets. The Epirus Bank (Greece) ATMaaS rollout shows this demand is live in Printec's own footprint. Printec should position itself as the regional ATMaaS delivery engine — the entity that makes the global ATMaaS proposition real for mid-tier CEE/SEE banks and credit unions that lack the scale to run estates themselves.

3. **Mid-tier banks are the sweet spot.** The advisory consensus is that mid-tier banks/credit unions without the capital or staff to keep upgrading a proprietary fleet are the natural ATMaaS buyers — which describes much of Printec's CEE/SEE bank base. The recommendation: lead with a staged "Continuum" sales motion (managed monitoring → cash optimisation → advanced services → full ATMaaS), anchored on TCO reduction (15-30%) and uptime/AI-diagnostics value, capturing the 24% of banks signalling adoption into 2027-28.

*Caveat for the operator: any ATMaaS / cash-logistics proposition in this region carries PCI DSS, DORA (third-party ICT outsourcing/concentration risk) and GDPR obligations — outsourcing the channel does not outsource the regulatory accountability. Contract design and the DORA "critical third-party" framing should be reviewed with Compliance before go-to-market.*
