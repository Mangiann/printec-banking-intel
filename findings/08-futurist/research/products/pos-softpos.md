# 3-5yr demand outlook: POS terminals, SoftPOS, terminal management

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc through 2027-2031 is **steady value growth, unit-base maturity in developed markets, and a structural mix-shift toward Android smart-POS and software**. Three overlapping movements define the period:

1. **Cash keeps ceding ground at the point of sale, which sustains acceptance-device demand.** The ECB SPACE study shows euro-area POS cash share (by number of transactions) falling from 59% (2022) to 52% (2024), with cards (45% by value) and mobile apps (7%, up from 4%) absorbing the difference. This long, slow displacement is the demand floor for terminals across Printec's CEE/SEE footprint, where cash starts from a higher base than the EU average — meaning the runway is longer than in Western Europe. Confidence: high.

2. **The terminal estate is going Android, NFC-saturated, and connected.** Roughly half of POS terminals sold in 2025 were Android, the NFC-ready installed base is heading to ~97% saturation by 2029, and cellular-connected terminals grow ~7.6% CAGR to 247M by 2029 (Berg Insight). The hardware market value still grows (~8.6% CAGR per Mordor) but the centre of gravity moves to software, cloud management, and value-added services on the device. Confidence: high.

3. **SoftPOS / tap-to-phone is the fastest-growing acceptance channel but remains additive, not a hardware-killer this decade.** Transaction value explodes (Juniper: $23.9bn 2025 → $540bn 2030, +2,150%) and the dedicated-SoftPOS software market grows ~23% CAGR (Grand View). But the absolute base is tiny (<15M SoftPOS smartphones in use in 2025) versus ~185M cellular terminals — so SoftPOS expands the *acceptance universe* (micro-merchants, mobile/field, queue-busting, pop-up) far more than it cannibalises the installed traditional/smart-POS base over the forecast window. Confidence: medium-high (transaction-value forecasts are interested-party/vendor-tilted and vary widely; the directional signal is robust).

Net for the period: terminal **units** plateau-to-modest-growth in mature segments and grow in emerging/CEE, terminal **value** grows mid-to-high single digits, and the **software + management + SoftPOS** layers are where double-digit growth concentrates. The risk to the hardware story is faster-than-expected SoftPOS substitution at the low end plus A2A/instant-payment rails (potentially offsetting 15-25% of future card-transaction growth per Capgemini) eroding the card-acceptance volume that justifies terminal refresh cycles.

## Key drivers

- Cash share at euro-area POS fell 59%→52% (by volume, 2022→2024); cards 45% and mobile apps 7% by value — the structural displacement that underwrites acceptance-device demand, with a longer runway in higher-cash CEE/SEE markets — [ECB SPACE 2024](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/ecb.space2024~19d46f0f17.en.html).
- Global non-cash transaction volume rising from ~1.65 trillion (2024) toward ~3.5 trillion (2029); card share of the mix declining (65%→52%, 2020→2024) even as absolute card transactions keep growing — more transactions to acquire, but per-transaction economics compress — [Capgemini World Payments Report 2026](https://www.capgemini.com/insights/research-library/world-payments-report/).
- Instant/A2A payments projected at ~22% of non-cash volume by 2028 and could offset 15-25% of future card-transaction-volume growth — a structural headwind to the card-acceptance demand that drives terminal refresh — [Capgemini WPR 2026 via FutureCFO](https://futurecfo.net/instant-payments-to-account-for-22-of-non-cash-transactions-by-2028-report-predicts/).
- Merchant acquiring revenue growing ~8.7% CAGR toward $160bn+ by 2026; acquirers shifting from acceptance to "autonomous payment infrastructure" (smart routing, real-time settlement, automated compliance) — pulls value into the software/management layer on top of terminals — [McKinsey Global Payments Report 2025](https://www.mckinsey.com/industries/financial-services/our-insights/global-payments-report).
- ~Half of POS terminals sold in 2025 were Android; NFC-ready terminals heading to ~97% saturation by 2029 — the estate becomes a programmable app-platform, raising the value of remote management, app distribution and lifecycle services — [Berg Insight via IoT Business News, Apr 2026](https://iotbusinessnews.com/2026/04/29/cellular-pos-terminals-to-reach-247-million-by-2029-as-retail-iot-expands/).
- SoftPOS transaction value forecast to grow ~2,150% (from $23.9bn in 2025 to $540bn in 2030), democratising acceptance for micro/small merchants — additive channel expanding the acceptance universe — [Juniper Research, May 2025](https://www.juniperresearch.com/press/soft-pos-transactions-to-accelerate-by-2150/).
- Dedicated SoftPOS software market ~$365M (2024) → ~$1.24bn (2030) at ~23% CAGR; micro & small businesses = 63% of demand; Europe growing ~24% CAGR — [Grand View Research SoftPOS](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/press-release/global-softpos-market).
- Tap-to-Pay-on-iPhone and Android SoftPOS now live across most of Printec's footprint (Croatia, Czechia, Cyprus, Hungary, Poland, Slovenia, Slovakia, Austria) — the regulatory/scheme rails for SoftPOS are already present in-region — [Worldline Tap on Mobile](https://worldline.com/en/home/main-navigation/solutions/merchants/tap-on-mobile).
- POS terminal market value ~$121bn (2025) → ~$197bn (2031) at ~8.58% CAGR; contactless +9.3%, mobile/portable +9.7%, software +9.8% CAGR — software and mobile outgrow the hardware average — [Mordor Intelligence POS Terminal Market](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/point-of-sale-terminal-market).

## Printec implication

The demand pulls toward **a software-and-services-led acceptance proposition rather than box-shifting**:

- **POS terminals (Android smart-POS):** Refresh and replacement demand is real and value-growing, but the win is selling Android smart-POS as a programmable platform — bundled with app distribution, value-added services, and lifecycle management — not commodity terminals. This is the highest-volume near-term Printec opportunity and aligns with the NCR Atleos / partner-channel hardware model.
- **SoftPOS / tap-to-phone:** Pursue as a *footprint-extension* product targeting micro/SME, mobile/field, hospitality and queue-busting use cases — the segment is small in absolute value today but the fastest-growing and already scheme-/regulation-enabled across Printec's countries. It widens the merchant funnel (acquiring SMEs that would never buy a terminal) and feeds the managed-services and onboarding businesses.
- **Terminal management (the strongest strategic pull):** An Android, NFC-saturated, cellular-connected estate makes **remote/cloud terminal estate management, security/compliance (PCI), app lifecycle and monitoring** a recurring-revenue layer that grows independent of hardware unit counts. This is where Printec can defend margin as hardware commoditises and SoftPOS dilutes per-device economics — and it ties directly to Printec's managed-services, security and compliance lines. Given PCI DSS and payment-sensitivity, any terminal-management/SoftPOS deployment code or architecture should be treated as requiring human review and testing before production.
