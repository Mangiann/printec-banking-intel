# 3-5yr demand outlook: payment security, HSMs, key management, physical security

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

The arc is a **structural, multi-year uplift in demand for payment cryptography and key management, driven by two compounding mandates that both land squarely inside Printec's 2027-2031 window** — the post-quantum cryptography (PQC) migration and the continued tightening of PCI key-management rules — layered on top of a **steadily growing payment-HSM base** and a **persistent, evolving physical/logical attack surface on cash hardware**. All three of Printec's security sub-lines (payment security/HSM, key management, physical security) are pulled upward, but with different shapes:

1. **HSMs & key management: secular growth, accelerating late-decade.** The global HSM market is forecast to roughly double from ~USD 1.66bn (2025) to ~USD 3.28bn (2030) at ~14.5% CAGR, with **payment processing the single largest application (~46% of HSM demand)** (MarketsandMarkets). The growth driver mix shifts over the window: PCI key-block enforcement (already mandatory) sustains a refresh cycle near-term, while the **PQC migration becomes the dominant capex driver from ~2028 onward** as NIST deprecates RSA/ECC by 2030 and disallows them by 2035, and EU/financial-sector roadmaps target 2030-2032 for critical payment systems. Crucially, EMV's own RSA→ECC migration is only completing ~2030, so PQC arrives *before* the prior migration finishes — a "telescoping" effect that forces a hardware/firmware crypto-agility refresh across the estate (NIST, G7 CEG, Cryptomathic).

2. **Deployment model tilts toward cloud / HSM-as-a-service.** On-premises still dominates (~68% of value in 2024), but payment-HSM-as-a-service is the faster-growing layer (ABI Research projects payment-HSM revenue to ~USD 681m by 2028; "Payment HSM-as-a-Service" ~USD 1.95bn (2024) → ~USD 6.14bn (2033), ~13.4% CAGR per Growth Market Reports). The pull is toward managed, PCI-outsourced cryptographic services rather than pure box sales.

3. **Physical & logical security: high, geographically migrating threat, sustaining hardware/retrofit demand.** European ATM physical attacks rose ~24% in 2023 (3,728→4,637 incidents), with explosive attacks still ~700/yr ("two ATMs blown up every day") and a pronounced eastward migration of gangs from the Netherlands into Germany — i.e. *toward* Printec's CEE/SEE footprint. Logical/jackpotting attacks surged in the US (>700 attacks and >USD 20m losses in 2025 per FBI/IC3) while Europe's confirmed ATM-malware incidents fell to near-zero on the back of Europol/EAST hardening guidance — evidence that *security upgrades work and are being bought*, which is itself a demand signal for anti-attack retrofits, ink-staining/glue, gas-suppression and endpoint-hardening software (EAST, FBI/IC3, govinfosecurity).

**Pace:** Steady-to-accelerating. HSM/key-management ~12-15% CAGR through 2030, front-loaded by PCI compliance and back-loaded by PQC. Physical-security demand is event-driven and lumpy but structurally elevated. **Confidence: high** on direction (regulators NIST/EU, the PCI Council, multiple analysts and vendors all align); **medium** on magnitudes (private market-sizing reports diverge and are not independently re-derivable; vendor figures are interested parties).

**CEE/SEE note (Printec's footprint):** The eastward migration of explosive/ram-raid gangs, combined with EU PQC roadmap deadlines that bind all Member States, means Printec's home markets face the *same* regulatory crypto-migration clock as Western Europe but a *rising* physical-attack exposure — lengthening the demand runway for both the cryptographic and the physical security lines locally.

## Key drivers

- **PQC migration is now a dated regulatory mandate, not a research topic.** NIST IR 8547 deprecates RSA/ECC (112-bit, e.g. RSA-2048, P-256) by 2030 and *disallows* all quantum-vulnerable asymmetric crypto by 2035; replacement standards are ML-KEM/ML-DSA/SLH-DSA/FN-DSA. [NIST IR 8547 (initial public draft)](https://nvlpubs.nist.gov/nistpubs/ir/2024/NIST.IR.8547.ipd.pdf) · [NIST PQC project](https://csrc.nist.gov/projects/post-quantum-cryptography)
- **The financial sector has its own, earlier deadlines.** EU coordinated roadmap: national strategies/crypto inventories by 31/12/2026, high-risk use cases (incl. critical financial systems) on PQC by 31/12/2030, remainder by 2035; the G7 Cyber Expert Group's Jan-2026 roadmap targets 2030-2032 for critical financial-system migration; UK NCSC phases crypto-discovery by 2028, high-priority by 2031, full transition by 2035. [Cryptomathic — Banker's Guide to Quantum-Safe Crypto, Part 1](https://www.cryptomathic.com/a-bankers-guide-to-quantum-safe-cryptography-part-1-the-compliance-mandate-for-pqc-migration-cryptomathic) · [The Quantum Insider — PQC migration timelines](https://thequantuminsider.com/2026/05/08/post-quantum-migration-timelines-government-industry-impact/)
- **"Harvest now, decrypt later" pulls migration forward** — adversaries store encrypted data today to decrypt once quantum is mature, so long-lived payment keys/PINs need re-keying ahead of the deprecation dates. NIST estimates a large, fully-resourced migration takes 3-5 years — i.e. estates must start now to hit 2030. [Cryptomathic — Banker's Guide Part 1](https://www.cryptomathic.com/a-bankers-guide-to-quantum-safe-cryptography-part-1-the-compliance-mandate-for-pqc-migration-cryptomathic)
- **EMV's own RSA→ECC migration completes only ~2030, colliding with PQC** — institutions may skip ECC and go RSA→PQC directly, forcing a crypto-agile HSM/firmware refresh rather than an incremental one. [postquantum.com — Payments and the race to quantum safety](https://postquantum.com/post-quantum/payments-quantum-pqc/)
- **PCI key-management rules already mandate HSM-grade controls and key blocks.** PCI PTS HSM v4.0 + PCI PIN v3.1 strongly mandate key blocks; PCI DSS 4.0 future-dated cryptographic requirements became mandatory on 31/03/2025; in practice a PCI-PTS-v4.0 or FIPS 140-2/3 Level 3+ HSM is the clearest path to satisfying PCI DSS Req 3.5/3.6. [PCI SSC — HSM Security Requirements v4.0 (PDF)](https://listings.pcisecuritystandards.org/documents/PCI_HSM_Security_Requirements_v4.pdf) · [Utimaco — PCI mandates use of key blocks](https://utimaco.com/news/blog-posts/pci-mandates-use-key-blocks-encryption-symmetric-keys)
- **HSM market is large, payment-led and growing double-digit.** ~USD 1.66bn (2025) → ~USD 3.28bn (2030) at ~14.5% CAGR; payment processing ~46% of HSM applications and the largest segment; on-prem ~68% of 2024 value but cloud/HSMaaS the fastest-growing layer. [MarketsandMarkets — HSM market worth $3.28bn by 2030](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hardware-security-modules-market-worth-3-28-billion-by-2030---exclusive-report-by-marketsandmarkets-302386944.html) · [MarketsandMarkets — HSM market report](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/hardware-security-modules-market-162277475.html)
- **Payment HSMs are shifting to cloud / as-a-service** — ABI Research projects worldwide payment-HSM revenue ~USD 527.9m (2024) → ~USD 681.3m (2028), with HSMaaS trust growing 2027-2028; cloud providers now offer "total outsourcing" of PCI compliance, easing adoption (esp. neo/challenger banks). [ABI Research — Payment HSMs are going cloud-based-as-a-service](https://www.abiresearch.com/research-highlight/payment-hsms-evolution)
- **Tokenization is becoming the default payment-security primitive, growing demand for the key/HSM infrastructure beneath it.** Tokenization market ~USD 3.5bn (2024) → ~USD 12-13.5bn (2030) at ~20-26% CAGR; Visa reports ~50% of global e-commerce volume token-protected; Mastercard processes >4bn tokenized transactions/month (~30% of volume) and targets 100% of online transactions tokenized by 2030. [Grand View Research — Tokenization market](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/tokenization-market-report) · [Mordor Intelligence — Tokenization solution market](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/tokenization-solution-market)
- **Digital euro will require quantum-safe crypto and secure elements from inception**, with token-based offline payments via tamper-resistant secure elements on cards/phones — a new, regulator-mandated cryptographic key-management workload for issuing PSPs in Printec's euro-area markets. [EDPB — Digital euro token-based offline modality (PDF)](https://www.edpb.europa.eu/system/files/2025-10/digitaleurotokenbasedofflinemodality_en.pdf) · [Venari — PQC threats, financial services](http://www.venarisecurity.com/post-quantum-cryptography-guide/quantum-threats-financial-services-uk/)
- **Physical attacks on ATMs remain elevated and are migrating eastward into Printec's region.** European ATM physical attacks +24% in 2023 (3,728→4,637); explosive attacks ~714/yr ("two ATMs blown up every day"), ~61% of the €9m physical-attack losses; gangs migrating from NL into Germany as defences harden. [ASIS — Mitigate ATM crime risks](https://www.asisonline.org/security-management-magazine/articles/2024/09/banks/ATM-crime-risks/) · [Sherwood News — Europe ATM bombings](https://sherwood.news/business/europe-atm-bombings-explosives-cash-robberies/) · [CNN — Criminals looting ATMs in Europe](https://edition.cnn.com/2024/10/27/europe/criminals-atm-robberies-europe-intl)
- **Logical/jackpotting attacks: surging in the US, suppressed in Europe by hardening — proving the security-upgrade demand case.** FBI/IC3: >1,900 US jackpotting attacks since 2020, >700 in 2025, >USD 20m losses in 2025; in Europe confirmed ATM-malware incidents fell from 3 (H2-2024) to 0 (H1-2025) after Europol/EAST hardening guidance, while H1-2025 still saw 613 physical attacks (~USD 12.2m). ## Printec implication

Demand is pulled toward **a "crypto-agility + managed key-management" security proposition wrapped around Printec's hardware estate**, plus a durable **physical-security retrofit** line. Concretely:

- **Lead with PQC-ready / crypto-agile payment HSMs and key-management services (payment security line).** The 2026-2030 regulatory clock (NIST, EU financial roadmap, PCI) makes a crypto refresh non-discretionary for every issuer/acquirer in the footprint. Position Printec as the partner that inventories keys, manages the RSA→ECC→PQC migration, and supplies/operates PCI-PTS-v4.0/FIPS-L3+ HSMs — ideally as a *managed/HSM-as-a-service* offering that absorbs PCI burden, matching the cloud/as-a-service tilt the analysts flag.
- **Attach key-block remediation and tokenization key-infrastructure now.** PCI key-block enforcement and the ~20-26% CAGR tokenization wave both create near-term, compliance-driven demand for the key-management layer beneath tokens — a software/services attach to existing payment processing relationships.
- **Sustain and grow the physical-security line (anti-explosive, ink/glue staining, gas-suppression, secure enclosures) — especially in CEE/SEE.** The eastward migration of explosive/ram-raid gangs into Germany and adjacent markets, with explosives causing ~61% of losses, means Printec's home regions are an *increasing* target. The European malware-to-zero result shows hardening sells and works — a strong proof-point for selling endpoint-hardening software and physical countermeasures together as the "secure ATM" bundle.
- **Digital-euro readiness is a forward option.** Quantum-safe crypto and secure-element key management mandated from inception create a future PSP-side workload Printec can pre-position for, tied to its onboarding/payments lines.

Note: all payment/security-related configurations, HSM deployments, key-migration plans and physical-countermeasure specifications require human review and testing before being treated as production-ready, and regulatory-interpretation questions (PCI/DORA/GDPR scope, breach handling) should go to Printec's DPO/Compliance team. Vendor and private market-sizing figures are interested parties and should be treated as indicative, not verified.
