# 3-5yr demand outlook: AML/KYC, transaction monitoring, compliance suite

## Direction & summary (2027-2031)

Demand for AML/KYC, transaction monitoring and broader compliance-suite software is set for **structurally strong, double-digit growth across 2027-2031**, driven less by market choice than by a stacked, dated wall of EU regulation that lands squarely inside Printec's footprint. This is one of the highest-confidence growth vectors in the portfolio on *direction*, with the EU acting as the pacesetter: the single EU **AML Regulation (AMLR) applies directly from 10 July 2027**, and the new **Anti-Money Laundering Authority (AMLA, Frankfurt) begins direct supervision of ~40 institutions in 2028** — turning compliance modernisation into a budgeted, deadline-driven procurement event rather than a discretionary upgrade. ([Indicium AMLR roadmap](https://www.indicium.ag/en/resources/amlr-2027-compliance-roadmap-banks-financial-institutions), [Freshfields AMLA work programme](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/risk-and-compliance/unveiling-amlas-blueprint-a-snapshot-of-the-2026-2028-work-programme-and-key-re-102mm45))

Independent market sizing reinforces the same arc. Across the cluster of related markets, analysts converge on **~15-22% CAGR through 2030/2031**: the global AML market is put at ~17.8% CAGR (MarketsandMarkets, USD 4.13bn→9.38bn) or ~16.2% (Grand View, USD 1.73bn→4.24bn); transaction monitoring at ~15.7% (Mordor, USD 19.98bn 2025→48bn 2031); KYC software at ~22% (TBRC, USD 4.69bn→12.7bn); and RegTech overall at ~20-23%. ([MarketsandMarkets AML](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/PressReleases/anti-money-laundering-solutions.asp), [Grand View AML press](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/press-release/global-anti-money-laundering-market), [Mordor transaction monitoring](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/transaction-monitoring-market))

The *pace* accelerates into 2027-2028 as the EU deadlines bite and adds a second structural driver — **AI/ML re-platforming of legacy monitoring engines** to cut the chronic false-positive burden — and a third — **real-time payments forcing real-time screening** (EU Instant Payments Regulation mandates at-least-daily sanctions screening rather than transaction-time checks from 2025). Confidence is **HIGH** on direction and on the legislated EU triggers (dated, in force); **MEDIUM** on absolute market-size magnitudes, which differ ~2x between firms by scope and should be read as a consistent growth *signal*, not a precise euro number.

Net: a **rising, compounding, regulation-forced demand pool** for Printec, where the CEE/SEE footprint — historically lighter on AML tooling and now subject to the same single rulebook and AMLA oversight — represents the steepest catch-up curves.

## Key drivers

- **AMLR single rulebook applies directly from 10 July 2027** — directly applicable customer due diligence, PEP, UBO transparency and an EU-wide EUR 10,000 cash limit, replacing fragmented national directives and forcing systems modernisation across all member states. ([Deloitte Legal — EU AML package](https://www.deloittelegal.de/dl/en/services/legal/perspectives/eu-aml-paket-wendepunkt-geldwaeschepraevention.html), [Indicium AMLR roadmap](https://www.indicium.ag/en/resources/amlr-2027-compliance-roadmap-banks-financial-institutions))
- **AMLA (Frankfurt) operational from 2025/26, direct supervision of ~40 institutions from 2028** — harmonised EU-level supervision with data collection from March 2026 and first selection in 2027, raising the supervisory bar (and tooling expectations) for the largest cross-border banks, many present in Printec's region. ([Freshfields AMLA blueprint](https://www.freshfields.com/en/our-thinking/blogs/risk-and-compliance/unveiling-amlas-blueprint-a-snapshot-of-the-2026-2028-work-programme-and-key-re-102mm45), [PwC IE — EU AMLA](https://www.pwc.ie/services/audit-assurance/insights/eu-new-anti-money-laundering-authority.html))
- **EU Instant Payments Regulation re-shapes screening** — PSPs must screen customers at least daily (and immediately after sanctions-list changes) rather than at transaction time, from 2025; instant settlement in 10 seconds removes the time buffer that legacy batch monitoring relied on. ([EY — EU instant payments](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/insights/financial-services/emeia/eu-instant-payments-regulation-five-key-hurdles-for-banks-to-clear), [ACTICO — instant payments & sanctions screening](https://www.actico.com/blog-en/the-eu-is-redefining-how-instant-payments-and-sanctions-screening-are-handled/))
- **AI/ML re-platforming of monitoring to cut false positives** — FCA reports 75% of UK firms already use AI with another 10% adopting within three years; AI transaction monitoring is cited as cutting false positives by ~50% with ~96% detection accuracy, the core efficiency case for replacing rules-only engines through 2027-2030. ([EY — AI reshaping transaction monitoring](https://www.ey.com/en_se/insights/financial-services/how-ai-is-reshaping-the-future-of-transaction-monitoring), [Sumsub — AI in AML 2026](https://sumsub.com/blog/ai-in-anti-money-laundering-and-compliance/))
- **Cloud shift in deployment** — cloud is the fastest-growing AML deployment mode and already ~63% of transaction-monitoring spend (2025), favouring SaaS/managed-service delivery over on-prem — a structural pull toward Printec's managed-services model. ([Mordor transaction monitoring](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/transaction-monitoring-market), [Grand View RegTech](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/regulatory-technology-market))
- **Services grow fastest within AML** — the services segment is forecast to register the highest CAGR (~17.3%) within the AML market, validating the implementation/managed-compliance opportunity beyond pure software licences. ([MarketsandMarkets AML report](https://www.marketsandmarkets.com/Market-Reports/anti-money-laundering-solutions-market-95490454.html))
- **Transaction monitoring is the AML core and the largest AML product segment** — it held the largest AML product revenue share (Grand View) and ~42% of the transaction-monitoring market is AML-driven (Mordor), with BFSI ~56% of demand — the centre of gravity for compliance spend. ([Grand View AML press](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/press-release/global-anti-money-laundering-market), [Mordor transaction monitoring](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/transaction-monitoring-market))
- **Europe a high-growth RegTech region with rising compliance cost base** — Europe ~28% of global RegTech revenue and growing ~15% CAGR; MiCA crypto rules and beneficial-ownership reporting peak 2025-2027, layering further obligations. ([Grand View RegTech](https://www.grandviewresearch.com/industry-analysis/regulatory-technology-market), [Mordor RegTech](https://www.mordorintelligence.com/industry-reports/global-regtech-industry))

## Printec implication

This vector pulls demand directly toward Printec's **AML/KYC, transaction-monitoring and compliance-suite software-and-services lane** — a high-conviction, recurring-revenue growth offset to flat-to-declining ATM hardware. Concretely:

- **AMLR 2027 + AMLA 2028 are a forced procurement window across all 17 footprint countries.** A single, directly applicable EU rulebook plus harmonised supervision converts compliance into a dated, budgeted buying event. Printec should position as the implementation/managed-compliance partner for banks re-platforming CDD, sanctions screening, transaction monitoring and SAR/case-management ahead of these deadlines.
- **Managed/SaaS delivery is the structural tailwind.** Cloud is already ~63% of monitoring spend and services carry the fastest CAGR — both favour Printec's managed-services model over one-off software licences, and play to its installed bank relationships and field presence in CEE/SEE.
- **Real-time payments make this adjacent to Printec's payments franchise.** EU Instant Payments forces at-least-daily sanctions screening and real-time controls — a natural cross-sell where Printec already touches payment rails and onboarding.
- **AI re-platforming is the modernisation hook.** The ~50% false-positive-reduction case lets Printec lead with an ROI narrative (cut investigator load) rather than a pure cost-of-compliance one — easing budget approval.
- **Tight adjacency to the eID/onboarding lane** (see `onboarding-eid.md`): AMLR's "eIDAS-first" CDD links KYC onboarding and AML monitoring into one compliance stack Printec can bundle.

Note (per Printec policy): AML/KYC and sanctions-screening code and architecture are security-, compliance- and payment-sensitive and must undergo human security review and testing before deployment. Treat all vendor/analyst figures above as a planning signal, not a production decision; route data-subject-rights, breach-handling and AMLR/AMLA/DORA/GDPR interpretation to the DPO/Compliance team before acting. Market-size figures vary ~2x by firm and are directional.
