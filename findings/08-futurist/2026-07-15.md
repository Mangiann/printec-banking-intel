## Per-region outlook 2027–2031 (footprint highlighted; mature markets as leading indicators)

**Leading indicators (mature → CEE follows with a lag):** Western Europe/Nordics show the destination — POS cash share down to 52% by number (from 59% in 2022, [ECB SPACE](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/ecb_surveys/space/html/index.en.html)), branch/ATM rationalisation, instant-by-default with VoP live, EUDI wallets in rollout, digital-euro decision pending. CEE/SEE replays this 2–4 years behind, telescoped by EU deadlines.

- **Greece** — footprint frontrunner: instant payments +73% YoY (122.1m in 2025), IRIS now cross-border via EuroPA (Jul 2026), high contactless. 2027–31 = consolidation + EUDI acceptance; strongest VoP/instant/wallet + SoftPOS demand.
- **Bulgaria** — euro membership (01/01/2026) forces the pace: TARGET2/TIPS done, but VoP/SCT-Inst on the non-euro clock (receive 9 Jan 2027, send + IBAN-name check 9 Jul 2027) → concentrated 2026–27 remediation on instant rails + VoP + euro-cash lifecycle, then normalises.
- **Croatia / Slovenia / Slovakia / Cyprus** (euro-area) — already inside the instant/VoP/EUDI perimeter; steady, continuous mature-market convergence.
- **Romania** — largest absolute runway: most cash-reliant EU market (>70% cash) yet RoPay live and urban digital adoption fast; euro deferred to ~2030+ (EDP to 2030). RRF/cohesion-funded core, instant, contactless, digital onboarding.
- **Western Balkans (Serbia, N. Macedonia, Albania, BiH, Montenegro, Kosovo)** — Reform-&-Growth Facility (€5.5bn, digitalisation pillar) + EBRD/EU digital guarantees the funding engine; Serbia's IPS a bright spot; accession-driven SEPA/EUDI alignment pulls identity + instant projects, gated by disbursement pace.
- **Czechia / Hungary** — digitally advanced but euro deliberately deferred; modernisation continues on domestic rails/AI/fraud, not euro-migration.
