## What changed since last run

The biggest new item is Albania's 2026 fiscal package, which mandates POS terminals for all taxpayers (accommodation/transport/public institutions by 30/05/2026, all other businesses by 31/12/2026) while cutting cash limits — a state-driven acceptance mandate that maps directly to Printec's POS and acquiring opportunity (opp_size XL). Newly quantified is Kosovo's structural POS gap: only 6.7% of businesses with financial accounts have a terminal (6,234 of 90,365), the single clearest greenfield acceptance opportunity in the region. The TIPS Clone instant-payments wave is now firmly multi-country and new this run — confirmed Letters of Intent in Bosnia & Herzegovina and Kosovo, with go-live dates in Montenegro (July 2026), Albania (slipped to autumn 2026), feeding instant-payments rail and ISO 20022 integration work. On the hardware side, Serbia's ATM fleet returned to growth (+3.6% y/y to 3,336 in Q1 2026, reversing the Q3 2025 dip) and POS reached 187,957 (+10.3%). On corrections: North Macedonia's end-2025 card/contactless figures came back contradicted, and the BiH 2025 device estimates (~1,868 ATMs / ~37,895 POS) could not be confirmed — only the 2024 primary figures (1,767 ATMs / 35,035 POS) stand; Ukraine's ATM/Power Banking and Montenegro/Serbia instant-payment numbers were only partially confirmed.

## EU footprint — what the scripted series says (trend read)

- **Branches still contracting almost everywhere.** Of the 10 EU footprint countries only **Croatia grew** (+1.0%); Cyprus was flat; the other eight fell, led by **Bulgaria −12.2%** (sharpest in the EU, euro-changeover driven) and **Hungary −7.2%** / **Romania −5.9%**. The structural read is unchanged from last run: branch rationalisation → demand for self-service kiosks, off-site ATMs and cash recyclers to backfill closed branches.
- **ATMs are mixed, not uniformly falling.** Up: **GR +13.5% ◇, HR +13.3% ◇ (noisy), HU +4.6% ◇, SK +2.1% ◇, CY +1.8% ◇.** Down/flat: AT, RO, SI, CZ, BG. The two big risers (GR, HR) carry data-quality caveats (deployer-driven / ECB PTN noise) and should be reconciled against national sources (HBA, HNB).
- **Card cash withdrawals fall in every EU footprint country** — from −1.8% (RO, cash resilient) to **−10.7% (CZ)**. Falling cash throughput + bank cost pressure is the core cash-recycling / OptiCash sales argument.
- **POS terminals grow in every market except Cyprus (series break) and Slovenia (flat)** — led by **BG +12.9% ◇** and **AT +9.1% ◇**. POS estate growth → acquiring / terminal-management (TMS) opportunities (Verifone/Castles).
