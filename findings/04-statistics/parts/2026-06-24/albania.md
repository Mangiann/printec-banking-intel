## Full-year 2025 series (BoA, published Feb 2026) — VERIFIED, still current
- Active ATMs: 1,091 at end-2025, +8% y/y. (monitor.al 26/02/2026)
- Deposit-capable ATMs: 430 at end-2025, +9% y/y = 39% of fleet. ATM cash deposits ~2.2m (+109%), value Lek 104.8bn (+~110%). Strong intelligent-deposit/recycler signal.
- POS terminals: 31,263 at end-2025, +27.7% y/y (29,261 at end-Q3, +24.5%).
- Card payments at POS: 27.2m txns (+38.7%), value Lek 105.75bn (+27%). First time card POS payments exceeded ATM cash withdrawals in volume (51% vs <43% over 9M).
- Active cards: 1.65m (+9.4%); debit 1.46m (~88%); credit ~120k (+5.6%).
