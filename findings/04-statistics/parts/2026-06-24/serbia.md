# Serbia (NBS) — Payment & Cash Statistics — 2026-06-24

Primary source: National Bank of Serbia (NBS) "Overview of data on the provision of payment services and e-money issuance Q1 2026" (pub June 2026) + IPS NBS statistics + NBS macro/financial-sector report.

## Headline figures — NEW Q1 2026 (verified this run from primary PDF)

- **ATMs: +3.6% y/y in Q1 2026 → 3,336 machines** (Tanjug citing NBS, 11/06/2026). REVERSES the slight Q3 2025 dip (3,197, -0.4%). Fleet is growing again. NBS PDF confirms "number of ATMs went up by 3.6%".
- **POS: 187,957 terminals at end-Q1 2026 (+17,523 / +10.3% y/y)**; virtual POS +20.2%. NBS Q1 2026 PDF, Table I.1.2.
- **Cards issued: 13,343,642 at end-Q1 2026 (+8.0% y/y, +991k)**; 7.1m registered users. NBS Q1 2026 PDF.
- **Card purchases in Serbia Q1 2026: 195,192,834 domestic-card txns (+18.7% y/y)**; foreign-tourist card use +18.6%; total 208.6m purchases.
- **Online card/e-money purchases Q1 2026: 32,918,968 (+39.7% y/y)**; dinar online purchases +44.5%.
- **m-banking users: 5,337,237 (+10.3% y/y)**; e-banking 5,061,586 (+5.7%); m-banking transactions +28.1% (39.36m); e-banking txns -2.5%.
- **IPS instant payments Q1 2026: 31,880,379 txns (+30.8% y/y; 2.6x Q1 2021), value RSD 392.8bn (+33.3% y/y; 3.6x Q1 2022)**; avg ticket RSD 12,320; avg 354,226 payments/day (+83k vs Q1 2025). Daily record 18 May 2026; value record 30 Dec 2025. (NBS PDF + ips.nbs.rs + Informer 24/06/2026.)
- **Digital onboarding / eKYC NEW: 139,244 distance contracts in Q1 2026 (+49.16% y/y)**; 105,103 concluded electronically (+54.91%); 34,141 via video identification (+33.85%); distance loan contracts +112.7%. Strong Namirial/eKYC signal.

## Structural / branch & banks (refines baseline)
- **Branches: 1,316 in Q1 2025** (down from 1,730 in 2015, -24%/-414); **19 banks** (from 30 in 2015); 21,886 employees. Source: NBS "Makroekonomska kretanja i kretanja u finansijskom sektoru" May 2025, via Magazin Biznis 05/06/2025. This replaces baseline's unverified ~1,401.
- Sector total assets EUR 55.7bn, deposits EUR 42.7bn (Q1 2025); foreign banks 78% market share.
