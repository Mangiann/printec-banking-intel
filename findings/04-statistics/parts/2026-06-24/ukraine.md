# Ukraine (NBU) — Banking Infrastructure Findings — run 2026-06-24

## Summary
NBU published Q1 2026 card-market data on 09/06/2026 (bank.gov.ua, Ukrainian; corroborated by FinClub & RBC-Ukraine). Network picture vs baseline (Sep 2025):
- ATMs ~15.7k, essentially flat YTD (-0.2%); deposit/recycling ATMs only 17.8% of fleet.
- POS terminals 563.4k (+0.9% YTD); 90.9% contactless.
- Acceptance points (merchant outlets) 654.5k (+6.6% YTD).
- Cashless: 96% by count, 67.3% by amount; 2.2bn cashless card txns worth UAH 1.23tn in Q1 (~+15% y/y).
- Cards issued 151.7m (+2.0%); active cards 61.4m (-6.2%).
- Branches: 4,934 at 1 Oct 2025 (-98 YTD; -167 full 2025); 744 temporarily suspended. Leaders: Oschadbank 1,142 (+626 suspended), PrivatBank 1,096.
- Power Banking ~2,400 energy-independent branches across >370 settlements, national ATM roaming; Power Banking filter now in Diia app (Feb 2026).

## Signal rows
1. ATM fleet ~15.7k flat, deposit-capable only 17.8% -> recycler/intelligent-deposit gap (NCR recyclers, x-core). FinClub citing NBU, 09/06/2026.
2. POS 563.4k, 90.9% contactless, acceptance points 654.5k +6.6% -> terminal estate & TMS (Verifone/Castles). NBU UA release 09/06/2026.
3. Cashless 96% count / 67.3% value; 2.2bn txns, UAH 1.23tn -> txn monitoring & AML (INETCO, Siron). NBU 09/06/2026.
4. Branches 4,934 (1 Oct 2025), 744 suspended -> self-service migration, kiosks. RBC-Ukraine 14/10/2025.
5. Power Banking ~2,400 resilient branches + ATM roaming -> ATM telemetry/monitoring, resilient SST, field services. bank.gov.ua / Minfin 25/02/2026.
