## Strategic context
- Montenegro = **first Western Balkans country to join SEPA** (report frames this as 2024/early-2025 milestone).
- National **instant-payments project** under way, built on ECB **TIPS framework ("TIPS Clone")** in partnership with **Banca d'Italia** and World Bank support.

## NEW / CHANGED this run (payment-system modernization — verified primary CBCG sources)
- RTS/X: new-generation national payment system launched (most significant change since 2005), ISO 20022-aligned. Online clearing for interbank txns up to EUR 1,000 processed intraday. Weekday hours extended 18/08/2025 (RTGS to 20:00, DNS to 19:30, six clearing cycles/day).
- Weekend/holiday operation: from 20/10/2025 the payment system runs Sat/Sun and public holidays (+118 days/year; ~EUR 32m capital freed). Source: CBCG press release 20/10/2025.
- TIPS Clone (instant payments, 24/7/365): go-live now expected JULY 2026 (cooperation Banca d'Italia + World Bank). Letter of Intent signed by Govs Radović/Panetta in Rome.
- SEPA go-live: full Montenegrin banking-sector cutover 06/10/2025 (Montenegro joined SEPA geographical area 21/11/2024, first Western Balkans country).
- SEPA fee caps effective 06/10/2025: first daily transfer <=EUR 200 capped at EUR 0.02; e-transfer <=EUR 20,000 max EUR 1.99; >EUR 20,000 max EUR 25; branch <=EUR 20,000 max EUR 3.99; >EUR 20,000 max EUR 50. CBCG projects ~EUR 13.9m/yr savings. Source: CBCG press release.
- Note: 2025 Annual Report (with 2025 terminal counts) NOT yet published as of 24/06/2025; 2024 figures above remain latest. Monthly RTGS/DNS stats current to May 2026 but carry NO ATM/POS/card counts.

## Printec mapping
- ATM +23% (118 net new units) — NCR Atleos ATMs, x-core multivendor software, telemetry/monitoring, cash recyclers, field/managed services.
- POS +15% (~2,465 net new) — Verifone/Castles terminals + TMS, acquiring.
- TIPS Clone / instant payments + SEPA — INETCO transaction monitoring, HSM/PCI (Thales), AML/KYC.
