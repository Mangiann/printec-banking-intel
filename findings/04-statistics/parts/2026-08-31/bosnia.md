### 10. European Commission (DG NEAR) — eIDAS / e-identity gap — 04/11/2025 (primary, Medium, opp M, win Low, new=True)
THE eKYC LANE IS LEGALLY BLOCKED, and the EC says so. The EC's Bosnia and Herzegovina Report 2025 (SWD, pub. 04/11/2025) records under Chapter 10 that 'No progress was made in adopting a Law on electronic identity and trust services for electronic transactions in line with the EU acquis', that there is still no single countrywide supervisory body, and that there was 'no progress in ensuring the interoperability of electronic signature throughout the country'; BiH is at an 'early stage of preparation' on digital transformation. Under Chapter 4 the EC told BiH to 'adopt legislation to align with the Payment Services Directive 2, the Electronic Money Directive 2 and the SEPA Regulation' and to 'set up a countrywide central register of beneficial ownership' — the first of those has now been DONE (FBiH laws end-July 2026; RS earlier), the second has not and is a named FATF grey-list item. Consistent with this, the Association of Banks of BiH publicly argues that remote/biometric video identification for account opening should be allowed but that 'there is currently no legal solution for this in BiH'. NEXT TRIGGER: the EC's 2026 enlargement package is due ~Oct/Nov 2026 (the 2025 one landed 04/11/2025) and will re-score both items.

*Implies:* Namirial eKYC/e-signature and any EUDI-wallet play cannot be sold as a compliant remote-onboarding product in BiH until an e-identity/trust-services law exists — but the pipeline is real and the banks are the demand side. Position now as (a) assisted/branch-side digital onboarding and e-signature that works under current law, (b) an EUDI-ready roadmap, and (c) beneficial-ownership/KYC data quality tooling tied to the FATF action plan.
*Source:* [European Commission — Bosnia and Herzegovina Report 2025 (SWD accompanying COM(2025) 690)](https://enlargement.ec.europa.eu/document/download/5d8fc547-f8f8-456f-84e3-b38998acfafd_en?filename=bosnia-and-herzegovina-report-2025.pdf)
