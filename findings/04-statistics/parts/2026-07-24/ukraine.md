# Ukraine (NBU) — Banking Infrastructure Findings — run 2026-07-27

## Summary
Fresh angles this run surfaced three NEW operational catalysts on top of the Q1 2026 card-market stats:
1. **2000 UAH banknote launch 04/09/2026** — NBU will, for the first time, pre-configure the WHOLE acceptance estate: ~38,700 devices (~15,750 ATMs + POS/SST), free technical access opens Aug 2026 (Minfin, 17/07/2026). Fleet-wide firmware/cash-module/BV update + field-service surge.
2. **Anonymous cash top-up ban from 26/06/2026** — NBU Resolution №67 kills anonymous deposits at self-service payment terminals (PTKS); every deposit now needs phone-number OTP verification, full +380 number on receipt. AML/eKYC + txn-monitoring driver.
3. **Cyber Resolution №143** (adopted 13/12/2025, comply by 13/12/2026) — mandatory infosec/cyber programme (MFA, logging, segmentation, asset inventory) for insurers/credit unions/fincos/pawnshops; plus NBU's March-2026 list of important payment-infrastructure objects (Visa, Mastercard, PrivatMoney, NovaPay, Ukrainian Payment System; processors UPC, TAS Link, AC DC) under enhanced continuity/security rules.

Q1 2026 card stats (NBU, primary, desktop-UA fetch): ATMs 15.7k (-0.2% YTD); POS 563.4k (+~1%); acceptance points 654.5k (+6.6%); contactless POS 90.9%; cashless 96% by count / 67.3% by value; 2.21bn cashless txns worth UAH 1.23tn (+5.9% count / +14.8% value y/y); in-store card txns 1,640.4m / UAH 644.1bn; cards issued 151.7m (+2%); active cards 61.4m (-6.2%, social-programme roll-off); 57.1% of active cards contactless; 20.6m tokenized.
