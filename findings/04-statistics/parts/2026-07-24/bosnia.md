# Bosnia & Herzegovina (CBBiH) — Statistics part — run 2026-07-27 (checkpoint 2026-07-24 path)

## Summary
2025 card-operations figures re-confirmed vs primary CBBiH press (12/03/2026):
- Card transactions **215,077,126** (2024: 186,045,013); value **21,102,734,732 KM** (+2.68bn / +14.5%).
- Channel value split: **ATM 64% (13,592,663,612 KM), POS 29% (6,127,307,206 KM), Internet 7% (1,382,763,915 KM)**.
- Cash-heavy: ATM withdrawals dominant channel; citizens still withdraw most cash from ATMs.
- Active cards **2,657,020** (+71,601). Mastercard 1,804,426; Visa 841,038. **25 banks** provide card services / 21 issuers.
- Avg transaction value 98 KM.
- Device counts (**1,868 ATMs / 37,895 POS**, 2025) carried from baseline — NOT re-echoed in value-focused March 2026 press; kept Low pending FBA/ABRS or CBBiH portal re-pull.

## NEW this run — INSTANT PAYMENTS WENT LIVE (was "planned July 2026" in baseline)
- CBBiH domestic **instant payment system launched 20/07/2026** — "most significant payment-infrastructure modernization in 25 years."
- 24/7/365, funds available in seconds, lower fees than žiro-clearing.
- First 3 banks LIVE: **Intesa Sanpaolo Banka BiH, Raiffeisen Bank BiH, Sparkasse Bank BiH** (~25% of sector assets); other banks join progressively as they finish technical adaptation.
- Built on European TIPS model adapted for Western Balkans by **Banca d'Italia** with ECB consent; **EU/EC funded EUR 2M**.
- Phase 2 roadmap: **QR-code payments, POS support, online shopping, cross-border**.
- Sources: Forbes N1 BiH 20/07/2026; bigportal.ba; frontal.ba; zenicablog.com.

## Printec mapping
