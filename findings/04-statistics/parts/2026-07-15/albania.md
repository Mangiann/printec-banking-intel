## NEW Q1-2026 prints (BoA, published 13/05/2026 via monitor.al / hashtag.al) — is_new
- POS terminals: 32,224 end-Q1-2026, +28% y/y — RECORD (was 31,263 end-2025). Card/TMS/acquiring signal.
- Card payments at POS: 7.58m txns (+30% y/y), value Lek 27.9bn (+22%). 4th consecutive quarter POS payments > ATM cash withdrawals in number.
- Active cards: 1.66m (+7.8%); debit 1.47m (+8.7%, 88%); credit ~120k (+2%).
- Digital transfers (hashtag.al 18/05/2026): 67% of bank transfers via internet/mobile in Q1-2026; internet-linked accounts 1.95m (+44%) = 49% of active accounts (39% in 2024); remote txns 3.06m (+26%), value Lek 528.4bn (+31%).

## SEPA / open banking (BIS keynote, BoA Holta Zaçaj, ~19/05/2026) — is_new
- SEPA: joined Nov-2025, live 07/10/2025, 11 banks. First 6 months: 363k SEPA transfers, value EUR 4.3bn; cross-border SME cost fell 0.76%→0.08%; 68% initiated online.
- Open banking: 10 e-money institutions licensed; 2 implement open banking concept.
- Adoption: 78% of adults own a bank account (40% in 2018); 29 electronic payments/adult (3x vs 2020); 2025 full-year 67% of bank payments by card, 20% via internet/mobile.

## POS mandate rollout (deadline 30/05/2026 now PASSED) — is_new
- Accommodation/transport/public-capital entities required POS by 30/05/2026; all other businesses by 31/12/2026. ~1 in 4 of ~143,000 businesses had POS as of the deadline window (~32,000 terminals distributed). Readiness lagged (urban transport ticketing/hotels flagged not ready).
- Mastercard free-POS program: 12 months free from 01/05/2026; national program cap ~4,750 terminals or 30/04/2026.
- Interchange caps 0.2% debit / 0.3% credit; consumer surcharge ban; target merchant fee ~1%.
