# Serbia (NBS) — Statistics run 2026-07-15

Primary sources: NBS "Overview of data on the provision of payment services and e-money issuance Q1 2026" (published June 2026); NBS Bank Supervision investor presentation "May 2026 Macroeconomic Developments" (banking data as of 31/03/2026); NBS Monetary statistics table SBMS01 "Main monetary aggregates" (updated 23/06/2026).

## Confirmed Q1-2026 (NBS payment-services overview, published Jun-2026)
- POS terminals: 187,957 (+10.3% y/y; +17,523 devices). Virtual POS +20.2%.
- ATMs: +3.6% y/y (~3,336; exact count sits in image-only Table I.1.2, narrative confirms +3.6%).
- Cards issued: 13,343,642 (+8.0% y/y).
- Card txns at Serbian merchants: 195,192,834 (+18.7%); foreign-issued card use +18.6% (tourist spend sustained).
- IPS instant: 31,880,379 txns (+30.8%), value RSD 392.8bn (+33.3%).
- m-banking users 5,337,237 (+10.3%); m-banking txns 39,355,785 (+28.1%); e-banking users 5,061,586 (+5.7%); e-banking txns -2.5%.
- Online card+e-money purchases: 32,918,968 (+39.7%); dinar online +44.5%.
- Distance/eKYC contracts: 139,244 (+49.16%); e-concluded 105,103 (+54.91%); video-ID 34,141 (+33.85%); 705 non-bank video-ID.

## NEW / fresher this run
- Bank branches: 1,288 as of 31/03/2026 (Q1-2026) and end-2025, from 1,337 (2024) / 1,341 (2023). Baseline had 1,316 (Q1-2025). 19 banks, 21,773 employees. Structural branch contraction continues. (NBS invest presentation, data 31/03/2026.)
- Currency in circulation (SBMS01, RSD m, end-period): Dec-2024 399,640; Dec-2025 430,183 (+7.6% y/y); latest May-2026 412,593. Cash-in-circulation still rising nominally despite digital growth.

## Printec mapping
- ATM growth +3.6% + branch contraction to 1,288 = branch-to-self-service migration -> NCR recyclers/intelligent-deposit, x-core multivendor (Raiffeisen Serbia reference), telemetry.
- POS +10.3% to 187,957 + strong fee-driven adoption -> Verifone/Castles terminals + TMS.
- eKYC 139k (+49%), video-ID 34k -> Namirial digital onboarding.
- IPS instant +30.8% -> INETCO transaction monitoring, HSM.
- Cash still growing (RSD 430bn) with fewer branches -> OptiCash cash optimisation, TCR recyclers, cash-cycle automation.
