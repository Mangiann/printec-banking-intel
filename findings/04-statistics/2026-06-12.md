# Agent 4 — Market Statistics — Findings 2026-06-12

**First run (no previous findings file existed — all items below are baseline/new).**
**Method:** ECB press releases + direct pulls from the ECB Data Portal API (branch and terminal series), national central banks (BNR, NBS, MNB, NBU, BNB) and national press citing central-bank data. Searches run in English plus Romanian, Serbian, Hungarian and Greek terms.
**Rules applied:** no extrapolation; every figure carries source, URL and reference period; confidence capped at Medium (single-agent).
**Historical context:** full 2023–2025 time series (ATMs, POS, branches per country) in `historical-series-2023-2025.md` in this folder.

**Jargon used below (plain language):**
- *Cash recycling ATM* — a machine that accepts deposited banknotes, checks them, and re-dispenses them to the next customer, cutting cash-in-transit costs.
- *Instant credit transfer (instant payment)* — an account-to-account transfer that settles in seconds, 24/7 (SEPA Instant in the euro area; IPS in Serbia, IRIS in Greece, RoPay in Romania, qvik in Hungary).
- *POS terminal* — the device (or software) a merchant uses to accept card/wallet payments.
- *Multi-acquiring double counting* — when several payment firms serve the same terminal, ECB statistics may count it more than once; treat POS counts as trend indicators, not exact estate sizes.
- *Dual circulation period* — the short window after a euro changeover when both old and new currency are accepted in shops.
