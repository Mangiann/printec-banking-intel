## What changed since last run

The biggest new opportunity is PrivatBank (Ukraine), which plans to buy 800 more deposit/recycling ATMs in 2026 after purchasing 1,200 units in 2025 for ~UAH 1bn — a rare confirmed XL hardware-replacement pipeline. Albania's mandatory-POS regime moved from pending to live: the 30/05/2026 deadline for accommodation/transport/public entities has now passed and all other businesses are due by 31/12/2026, with Mastercard's free-POS offer running from 01/05/2026, opening a large terminal-deployment window. Regionally, TIPS-clone instant-payment go-lives are now concrete for 20/07/2026 (Bosnia & Herzegovina and Montenegro confirmed; Montenegro's Parliament adopted the enabling law 42-0), while Kosovo's is only partially confirmed. On verification: North Macedonia's deposit-capable ATM growth was corrected from "+23%" to +16.6% y/y (473 units, 41.6% of fleet), the tokenised/wallet-card surge and Ukraine's ~17.8% deposit-capable ATM share could not be fully confirmed, and the Kosovo branch count (235) and ARC/KBA remote-onboarding regulation remain unconfirmed.

## EU footprint — what the scripted series says (trend read)

- **Branches still contracting almost everywhere.** Of the 10 EU footprint countries only **Croatia grew** (+1.0%); Cyprus was flat; the other eight fell, led by **Bulgaria −12.2%** (sharpest in the EU, euro-changeover driven) and **Hungary −7.2%** / **Romania −5.9%**. - **ATMs are mixed, not uniformly falling.** Up: **GR +13.5% ◇, HR +13.3% ◇ (noisy), HU +4.6% ◇, SK +2.1% ◇, CY +1.8% ◇.** Down/flat: AT, RO, SI, CZ, BG. The two big risers (GR, HR) carry data-quality caveats (deployer-driven / ECB PTN noise) and should be reconciled against national sources (HBA, HNB).
- **Card cash withdrawals fall in every EU footprint country** — from −1.8% (RO, cash resilient) to **−10.7% (CZ)**. Falling cash throughput + bank cost pressure is the core cash-recycling / OptiCash sales argument.
- **POS terminals grow in every market except Cyprus (series break) and Slovenia (flat)** — led by **BG +12.9% ◇** and **AT +9.1% ◇**. POS estate growth → acquiring / terminal-management (TMS) opportunities (Verifone/Castles).
