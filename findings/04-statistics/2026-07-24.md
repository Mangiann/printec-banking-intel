## What changed since last run

The biggest development this run is that regional instant payments went live: the Balkan "TIPS Clone" platform (built by Banca d'Italia with ECB backing) launched on 20/07/2026, with Bosnia's IPS BiH going live the same day and Kosovo actively preparing via KIPS/TIPS workshops — a fresh window to pitch instant-payment terminal readiness and integration services. Terminal estates are expanding fastest in Montenegro (POS +20% y/y to 22,566 at Q4-25, ATMs +20% to 751, cash-deposit ATMs +47% since end-2024) and Albania (record 31,263 POS, +27.7%), signalling near-term acquiring and deposit-ATM demand. Serbia confirms the durable pattern of a saturated ATM fleet (3,336, +3.6%) alongside strongly growing POS (187,957, +10.3%), pointing to a software/services rather than hardware ATM play. Ukraine's NBU pre-configuring its entire ~38,700-device acceptance estate for the new 2000 UAH banknote (04/09/2026) is a notable cash-handling opportunity, though that device count is only partially confirmed. On corrections: two prior baselines were contradicted (the Albanian ~1,011 ATM estate and Serbia's Q3-2025 card-txn/foreign-card figures), and the previously reported BiH estate of ~1,868 ATMs / ~37,895 POS could not be confirmed this run.
