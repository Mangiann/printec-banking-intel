## What changed since last run

Three regulatory and structural items dominate. Hungary's ATM deployment law (Act XVIII of 2025) was re-verified in a consolidated version in force from 14/05/2026 as amended by Act XIV of 2026, and the phase-2 obligation stands: settlements of 500–1,000 inhabitants must have working ATM cash withdrawal by end-2026 — with the striking corollary that Hungarian bank hiring shows almost no ATM/self-service capability (only two of 16 nationwide "ATM" hits on profession.hu sit at banks), so the build must be bought in. In Slovakia, ČSOB SK's own 03/06/2026 release confirms a shared network of nearly 600 ATMs with 365.bank and Poštová banka from 01/07/2026, alongside a market-wide dismantling of branch cash desks across all five large banks and roughly 1,000 deposit ATMs now run by seven banks. In Czechia, ČSOB has five concurrent card-acquiring vacancies posted in August 2026, all Prague-based — a clear acquiring build — while UniCredit's February 2026 entry took the KB/MONETA/Air Bank shared deposit-ATM network to nearly 1,000 machines. On the Balkan side, RBI's takeover of Addiko closed on 56.16% acceptance with the Bosnia/Montenegro units carved out to Alta Group, putting estates in Croatia, Slovenia, Serbia, BiH and Montenegro into play.

Verification corrected several baseline figures: the digital-euro pilot does not start until H2-2027 (not 2026) per the ECB's own page; MBH Bank's HUF 10bn ATM programme target of 1,400+ locations is dated end-2025, not current; Raiffeisen Romania's careers domain moved (raiffeisen.jobful.ro is NXDOMAIN, now cariere.raiffeisen.ro); NETOPIA's BNR payment-institution entry, Banca Transilvania's H1-2026 figures, Postbank's dev.bg cluster and Snappi's vacancy count were all contradicted as originally stated and restated here. Two items could not be confirmed at all: Hungary's reported doubling of the free monthly ATM withdrawal allowance to HUF 300,000 from 01/02/2026, and NLB's competing takeover attempt for Addiko. Slovenia's ZDUPS bill also advanced past its 17/08 consultation close — the Ministry of Finance published a continued-dialogue note on 24/08/2026 — with a proposed minimum of 60 ATMs per 100,000 inhabitants still in the draft.

### The structural read

Two capability gaps are worth more than any single posting. **Hungary** must put working ATMs into every settlement above 500 inhabitants by 31/12/2026 under Act XVIII of 2025, yet a nationwide profession.hu search returns 16 "ATM" hits of which only **two** sit at banks — the capability to deliver that programme is not being hired, so it has to be bought in. **Slovakia** shows the same shape from the other direction: branch cash desks are being dismantled across all five large banks (Slovenská sporiteľňa alone has 93 of 128 branches without one) while a Slovakia-wide profesia.sk search finds just **two** live vacancies containing "bankomatov" against 21 for "AML". Cash is moving to the machine in both markets without the banks staffing for it.

Against that, the clearest *funded* demand signals are ČSOB Czechia's five August card-acquiring roles, UBB's Head of Card Products/POS & ATM Unit in Bulgaria, and RBA Croatia's jump from 2 to 21 roles around ICT risk and DORA. The clearest cooling is Romanian: BCR down 50% in ten weeks with no self-service role at all, Banca Transilvania's two cards-architecture roles gone, and BRD publishing nothing at all.
