## What changed since last run

Two large, genuinely new signals lead this run. Eurobank unveiled its "Banking Forward" programme (29/06/2026) — an approximately EUR 1bn technology investment for 2025-2028, its largest ever, spanning cloud, AI and GenAI assistants; verification confirmed the event and the ~EUR 1bn figure. Piraeus was named (ECB, 14/07/2026) among 36 PSPs for the Eurosystem digital-euro pilot, which runs for 12 months in H2-2027, opening a clear infrastructure and payments-integration window. Also new and confirmed, IRIS Payments connected to the pan-European EuroPA network via DIAS from 30/06/2026, enabling cross-border IBAN-less instant P2P transfers (Eurobank/Piraeus/Viva.com involved). On the leadership side, two changes matter: NBG Cyprus appointed Nicolas Prentzas as GM/COO with an IT and digital-transformation mandate, and Eurobank elevated Iakovos Giannaklis to Deputy CEO / Head of Retail & Digital Banking. Note two items remain unconfirmed by verification — the Alpha Bank ~34 open-roles listing and the Piraeus IT Security Specialist careers posting — so treat both as low-confidence. The NBG/Mellon Hyosung recycling-ATM award and the Piraeus Diebold Nixdorf ATM migration are confirmed but not new, and both reflect competitor hardware already installed.
