## Glossary additions (new terms this run)

- **Videobanking** — a self-service device (or branch "capsule") that connects the customer to a remote human teller by video; lets a bank keep service in a location without an on-site cashier. (CEC RO, MBH HU)
- **Cash recycling / recycling ATM** — an ATM that re-dispenses notes deposited by other customers, cutting cash-replenishment trips; "multifunction device" in some ads. (Raiffeisen RS, Tatra SK, Komerční CZ)
- **Issuer-processor** — a vendor that runs the back-end of card issuing (authorizing/processing card transactions) for a bank; e.g. Paymentology behind Snappi. (Piraeus GR — competitor stack)
- **DORA** — EU Digital Operational Resilience Act; rules forcing banks to harden ICT systems and manage third-party (vendor) risk, with a Jan-2025 application date and ongoing supervisory work — drives "operational resilience / ICT risk / third-party risk" hiring. (Alpha GR, OTP SI)
- **Cashier-less branch** ("pénztár nélküli" / "pobočka budoucnosti") — a branch with no manual cash desk; cash handled entirely by self-service deposit/recycling machines. (Raiffeisen HU, Komerční CZ)
