- For other countries, signals below come from indexed news of hiring programs, not individual postings. Marked accordingly.
- Per playbook rule 5, all confidence is capped at Medium (single-agent findings).
- Dates on infostud listings are **application deadlines**, not posting dates; all listings were live when seen on 2026-06-12.

## Plain-language glossary used here

- **PMO** — project management office; the team that coordinates a portfolio of change projects. PMO hiring usually precedes a wave of projects.
- **Payment operations (platni promet)** — the back-office processing of transfers/payments; its head decides processing systems and outsourcing.
- **BPO** — business process outsourcing; a bank pays an external firm to run a function (call center, fraud checks).
- **Voluntary exit program** — paid early-retirement scheme banks use to shrink/reshape staff.
