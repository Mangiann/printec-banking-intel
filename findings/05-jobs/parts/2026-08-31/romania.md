# Romania - Agent 5 (jobs & leadership) - run 31/08/2026

**Status:** complete | **Signals:** 18 | Fresh run (no checkpoint to resume)

## Headline

Romania's story this month is **not hiring, it is the payments rail**. RoPay (the national QR/A2A instant-payment scheme) reached 11 participating institutions; BRD launched merchant and QR payments on 06/08; ING pushed SoftPOS bill payment with ENGIE on 12/08; and acquiring is moving outside the banks as EuPlatesc connected directly to TRANSFOND (24/08) and NETOPIA won a BNR payment-institution licence (IP-RO-0018, 10/07). Underneath it, Law 239/2025 has since 01/01/2026 forced near-universal card/electronic acceptance, and BNR data show POS terminals up 161,396 in six months - while Romania's ATM estate GREW to 11,136, against the CEE trend.

On jobs, all three readable bank boards SHRANK (BT 29->20, BCR 29->18, Raiffeisen ~54->18 on a new portal). The most useful single role change: Raiffeisen's cards product-manager vacancy has been **rescoped to cover issuing AND merchant acceptance**, and BT's **Enterprise Architect Cards Domain has been filled**.
