## Ministrstvo za finance / Banka Slovenije (ZDUPS bill) (Slovenia) - 17/07/2026 - opp XL / win Medium
NEW second public draft of the Zakon o dostopnosti in uporabi placilnih sredstev (ZDUPS - Law on Accessibility and Use of Payment Means) published 17/07/2026, consultation open to 17/08/2026 (13/08/2026 for municipalities via ZMOS). Draft mandates: at least 60 ATMs per 100,000 inhabitants nationally; an ATM within 5 km of the centre of every settlement above 1,000 residents; a bank branch or Post Office banking point within 5 km for settlements above 3,000 residents; at least 40 branches per 100,000 inhabitants; 95% of residents within 5 km road distance of cash; max 3% of the ATM requirement substitutable by cash-in-shop/cashback points; five free withdrawals OR DEPOSITS per month at any bank's ATM; and merchants must accept euro cash AND offer at least one electronic payment method (exemptions: turnover under EUR 10,000, first-year start-ups, charity, no safe connectivity). Government timetable on the e-Demokracija register (evidenca 2026-1611-0028, Ministrstvo za finance): adoption and entry into force targeted Q4-2026. Plain language: a cash-access law that legally forces banks to run a minimum ATM and branch estate, and forces shops to take cards.

- Implies: Direct, sizeable ATM-deployment and cash-automation opportunity: banks that have been thinning their networks will have to re-deploy machines into low-density settlements. Free DEPOSITS at ATMs pushes banks toward deposit-capable and recycling machines (NCR intelligent-deposit / recyclers, Glory-Sesami TCRs) rather than dispense-only, plus x-core multivendor ATM software, ATM telemetry/monitoring, cash optimisation (NCR APTRA OptiCash) to run a bigger, less profitable estate economically, physical ATM security for unmanned rural sites, and managed/field services for a geographically dispersed fleet. The merchant e-payment obligation is a POS/softPOS acceptance opportunity for acquirers.
- Likelihood: High - bill is on the Government's own Q4-2026 adoption timetable and has Banka Slovenije co-authorship; banks will need supplier commitments before the law bites | Confidence: Medium | new: True
- Deadline: 17/08/2026
- Source: [e-Uprava / e-Demokracija - Predlog predpisa: Zakon o dostopnosti in uporabi placilnih sredstev (id 18855) + ZMOS bill page (jul 26)](https://e-uprava.gov.si/si/drzava-in-druzba/e-demokracija/predlogi-predpisov/predlog-predpisa.html?id=18855) (primary)
