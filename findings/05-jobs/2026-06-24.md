## What changed since last run

The biggest new development is Hungary's MNB Act XVIII mandate: 1,039 ATMs must be deployed across 9 named banks by 31/12/2026 (~HUF 4.156bn), with confirmed per-bank allocations (MBH +195, K&H +117, Erste +104) — a hard, deadline-driven ATM hardware/services play. In Ukraine, leadership and self-service momentum is strong: Yuriy Katsion confirmed as Oschadbank Chairman (26/01/2026) and PrivatBank's confirmed ATM-recycler FX cash service (USD/EUR→UAH, 24/7) is expanding the recycler estate. In Cyprus, Bank of Cyprus's confirmed CDBbank portfolio acquisition (~EUR150m loans / ~EUR500m deposits) and Alpha Bank Cyprus's confirmed AstroBank post-merger systems unification both signal integration-driven channel work. Verification corrected several baseline items: the NLB Reinhard Holl/CTO appointment, the Erste Hungary, Komercni banka, CIB Bank (Luigi Fuzio), AikBank, and UniCredit Bank Serbia signals were all contradicted, and OTP Hungary's specific ATM counts (~1,900 / ~500 deposit-capable) could only be partially confirmed.
