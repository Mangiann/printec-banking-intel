Third, the **Greek ATM fee-cap law and the Cashflex structure** keep Printec's majority-owned ~850-unit off-site fleet (Cashflex, ~80%) the highest-certainty in-footprint opportunity. ## Jargon added (plain language)
- **ATM recycler / recycling ATM (MFM / cash-in-out):** a machine that both dispenses and accepts cash and reuses deposited notes, removing the need for a separate cash-in terminal; many also do over-the-counter FX.
- **TCR (teller cash recycler):** a back-office machine that counts, validates and recycles cash so a teller does not handle notes manually.
- **CDM:** cash-deposit machine; an ATM variant that accepts (but may not dispense) cash.
- **Core banking system (CBS):** the central system holding accounts, balances and transactions; replacing it forces every ATM/POS/branch device to be reconnected.
- **x-core:** Printec's vendor-agnostic multivendor ATM application/management software that runs across mixed-OEM fleets.
- **OptiCash / APTRA OptiSuite:** NCR cash-forecasting and replenishment-optimisation software that reduces cash-in-transit and idle-cash cost.
- **eKYC:** electronic Know-Your-Customer — remote identity verification (document scan, liveness, e-signature) for digital account opening (Printec sells Namirial).
- **TMS (Terminal Management System):** software that remotely configures, updates and monitors card-payment (POS) terminals.
- **HSM:** Hardware Security Module — a tamper-resistant device that stores cryptographic keys and processes PIN/card crypto (Printec sells Thales).
- **SoftPOS:** software that turns an NFC smartphone/tablet into a contactless card terminal, no dedicated hardware.
- **DORA:** EU Digital Operational Resilience Act (in force 17/01/2025) — mandates ICT risk management, incident reporting and a register of all ICT third-party providers (so banks must hold DORA-compliant vendor contracts).
- **AMLR / AMLA:** the EU single AML rulebook (applicable 10/07/2027) and the new EU Anti-Money-Laundering Authority — drivers of AML/KYC system upgrades.
- **SEPA / SCT Inst / ISO 20022:** the single euro payments area, its 10-second instant-credit-transfer scheme, and the structured payment-message standard that ATM/POS/back-office systems must support.
- **TIPS Clone:** a Banca d'Italia-built replica of the ECB's instant-payment settlement platform being deployed across Western Balkan central banks (Albania, BiH, Kosovo, Montenegro, North Macedonia).
- **VES / VSS / VEP:** voluntary exit/severance/separation schemes — bank staff-reduction programmes that increase demand for self-service automation.
- **Act XVIII (Hungary) / Law 239/2025 (Romania) / eKasa (Slovakia):** national mandates forcing, respectively, ATM coverage in every municipality, universal card acceptance, and cashless-payment acceptance — each a hardware-procurement driver.
