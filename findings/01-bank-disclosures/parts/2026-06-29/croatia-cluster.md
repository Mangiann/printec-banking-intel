Banks: ZABA (UniCredit), PBZ (Intesa), Erste HR, OTP HR. Focus: ATM fleets, OptiCash (Erste incumbency), recyclers, POS/Nexi, vendors.

## What changed / is new this run
- **National ATM Network mandate (NEW, regulatory, highest value):** Croatia's Law on Comparability of Fees / account switching / basic accounts (final draft to Parliament 15/05/2025) mandates an interoperable national ATM network functional from **01/01/2027**. Government + HNB + banks to sign a cooperation memorandum; cross-bank withdrawal fees abolished once live; until then ≥2 free monthly withdrawals at any ATM. HUB has signalled a possible **~50% cut in ATM count**. Source: glas-slavonije 16/05/2025, Dubrovnik Times, HUB statement.
- **HNB primary fleet data (end-2024):** Croatia total **3,978 ATMs** (declining: 4,894 in 2020 → 4,692 → 4,184 → 4,277 → 3,978), **142,354 EFTPOS** + 490 cash-only EFTPOS. 102.89 ATMs/100k. PI1 table refreshed 27/05/2026. Source: HNB Payment Cards & Card Transactions, Payment Statistics 2024 (Jul-2025).
- **Erste HR Printec/NCR OptiCash incumbency reconfirmed** (case study: 600+ ATMs, Printec NCR channel partner, 99.2% availability). 2015-era deployment — renewal/refresh + recycler-fleet expansion window.
