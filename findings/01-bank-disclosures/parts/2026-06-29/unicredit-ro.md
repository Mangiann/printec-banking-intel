# UniCredit Bank Romania — findings 2026-06-29

## Summary
The Alpha Bank Romania merger is legally complete (15/08/2025). The combined bank is now Romania's 3rd-largest by assets (~11% assets, 13% loans, 11% deposits), ~300 branches, ~900 ATMs, 4,154 bank / 4,475 group employees (31/12/2025, BVB annual report). FY2025 group net profit RON 1,485m, bank RON 1,262m; revenues RON 4,168m (+18.27%); tangible+intangible assets RON 860m→1,275m on Alpha asset integration.

Key Printec-relevant developments:
- **ATM harmonisation live since 01/04/2025**: cross-bank fee parity + interoperability (withdrawal, deposit, balance) across the combined ~900-ATM fleet (Euronet ATMs excluded). Two legacy ATM estates (UniCredit + Alpha) now operated as one network — multivendor ATM-software/managed-services/OptiCash opportunity.
- **Branch rationalisation ~300 → ~200 by end-2026** while retaining ~900 ATMs → self-service migration (recyclers/multifunction BNA, cash automation).
- **Prime by UniCredit Bank** launched May 2026 across 9 CEE markets incl. Romania; includes "exclusive Prime zones in selected branches" → branch reformatting.
- **Google Cloud 10-yr partnership** (12/05/2025), 13 markets incl. Romania, 15m customers; migrates legacy systems + AI (Vertex/Gemini); explicitly names financial-crime prevention → AML/transaction-monitoring angle.
- AML/eKYC: 2025 cooperation agreement with DGEP (Persons Records Directorate) to streamline KYC; digital onboarding simplified → eKYC/AML.
- Group-wide AI governance set up in 2025 to deliver case studies through 2026.
