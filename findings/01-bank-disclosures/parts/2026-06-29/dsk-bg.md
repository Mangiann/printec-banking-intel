# DSK Bank (OTP Group) — Bulgaria — 2026-06-29

Focus: post-euro ATM/CDM strategy, AI assistant, fleet vendor, IT capex.
Bulgaria joined the euro area on 01/01/2026; DSK is 2nd-largest BG bank by assets, #1 retail.

## Verified primary network & financials (OTP 4Q25 deck, pdftotext extract)
- DSK Group 31/12/2025: **274 branches** (2024: 278), **955 ATMs** (2024: 962, ~flat), **20,994 POS** (2024: 19,643, +6.9%), **11,731 headcount** (2024: 11,404).
- Profit after tax HUF 211.3bn (+5% y/y), ROE 20.3%, total assets HUF 8,507bn (2025).
- Euro transition "went smoothly"; deposit inflows surged pre-accession; double-digit retail loan growth.

## Key signals
1. **Euro ATM/CDM conversion** — withdrawals euro-only from 01/01/2026 (min €10 note); deposit ATMs accept BGN only until 31/01/2026, then gradual euro cash-in conversion from Feb 2026. ~955 ATM fleet reconfigured. (DSK primary 31/12/2025 + 16/12/2025 notices)
2. **Very small cash-in/CDM footprint** — only 19 cash-deposit ATMs as of Jan 2024 (Profit.bg). Large recycler/CDM white space vs 955-unit fleet, now under euro-driven upgrade pressure. = Printec recycler/CDM + OptiCash opportunity.
3. **BPC SmartVista** is the card/payments processing platform (10-yr relationship); ran the full BGN→EUR redenomination across cards, POS, ATM scenarios, 2.6m debit + 300k credit accounts. Competitor to Printec acquiring/TMS layer; defines integration boundary. (FinancialIT, 21/04/2026)
4. **AI assistant (OpenAI-based)** — "first fully AI-based assistant in Bulgaria," launched 14/07/2025 on website + DSK Direct, mobile planned; GenAI, <10s responses; Petko Rangelov (Head of Digital, Data & Tech). Roadmap → personal banking advisor. (DSK primary)
5. **Spitch "D-bot" voice/omnichannel virtual assistant** — Bulgarian NLU, 60+ query types, 24/7; +80% response speed, -19% cost/interaction. (Spitch case study)
6. **Digital stack** — Backbase (Azure) + VeriPark Dynamics 365 CRM (from 2022 baseline). Visa exclusive issuing, full portfolio→Visa by end-2026 (baseline).
7. **Fleet OEM unconfirmed** — no public DSK ATM tender / named OEM contract; legacy mixed (DSK + SocGen Expressbank merger 2020). Follow-up needed.
