# Albania — Bank Disclosures (2026-06-29)

Focus: NCR SS80 (Printec/OTP), fleet refresh, Creatio, digital banks. EN + Albanian.

## What changed vs baseline (2026-06-24)
- **JET Bank LIVE (NEW, highest-value):** Albania's first 100% digital-only bank officially launched operations + mobile app **25/06/2026**. Full BoA license March 2026 (preliminary approval 03/12/2025 — first new-bank license in 20 years). Built in 6 months by ~70 staff; 75,000+ waitlist. Stack: Oracle Banking (core), **Backbase** (engagement OS), Mastercard, AWS, Microsoft Azure, **Jumio (eKYC)**, **Facilization** (integration). Digital-only = no ATM/branch fleet → NOT a hardware target, but a competitor-platform signal and an eKYC/AML benchmark.
- **Tirana Bank–Backbase** 5-yr digital transformation (announced 27/03/2025; Endava delivery partner); "largest tech investment in bank history," triple digital base. 84 ATMs (2024, +23.5% YoY), 33 branches. No ATM/self-service vendor named — fleet-refresh whitespace.
- **OTP Bank Albania NCR SS80** confirmed via Printec post (NCR Atleos SelfServ 80, cash deposit + recycler, phased 2025-2026). Unit count still undisclosed at source.
- **Credins Bank**: Creatio (selected Jan 2025, partner Facilization) + Deda Bit core (Bankup, SEPA done). 87 ATMs (2024, +20.8% YoY), 52 branches, 2nd-largest bank (16.63% assets).
