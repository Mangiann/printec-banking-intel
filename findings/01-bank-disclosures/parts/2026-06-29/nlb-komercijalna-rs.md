# NLB Komercijalna banka (Serbia) — ATM replacement & off-branch services

Run date: 2026-06-29. Languages: EN + SR.
Focus: 300-machine multifunction ATM replacement; >90% services off-branch.

## Summary so far
- NLB Komercijalna banka (NLBKB) = 3rd-largest bank in Serbia by assets (NLB Group acquired Komercijalna banka, completed Dec 2020; merger/integration May 2022).
- Bank publicly states it was the FIRST bank in Serbia to replace its ENTIRE ATM network with latest-gen multifunctional machines (deposit daily takings + withdraw, all-channel integration, contactless, QR reader). Source: International Banker feature.
- Digital transformation: ~two-thirds of total investment budget goes to technology (Exec Board member Bojana Kaličanin Stojanović, Biznis.rs, 25/03/2026).
- "300 machines" exact figure and ">90% services off-branch" exact figure NOT yet confirmed at a primary source; searching procurement portal + AR.
- Vendor of the multifunctional ATMs: NOT yet confirmed (Printec is NCR Atleos distributor in Serbia; AikBank Serbia is a confirmed Printec NCR win — relevant competitive context).
