# Optima bank S.A. (Greece) — 2026-06-29

Lean digital-first Greek bank (founded 2019, Vardinogiannis/Motor Oil-linked, ATHEX-listed Oct 2023). Among Europe's most efficient banks: cost-to-core-income 24.5% (lowest in Greece), ROTE 25.3%. Explosive growth via corporate lending; tiny physical footprint (~29 branches, no own large ATM fleet — relies on the DIAS shared network). This is the OPPOSITE of an ATM/cash-automation buyer: capex skews to digital/core/integration, not self-service hardware.

## Key primary figures
- **FY2025** (press release 03/03/2026): net profit €170.0m (+21% YoY); total income €297.6m; NII €210m (+11%); net fee income €61.8m (+49%); gross loans €5.1bn (+40%); deposits €6.3bn (+36%); AUM €5.6bn; ROTE 25.3%; cost-to-core-income 24.5%; NPE 1.31%; TCR 14.87%, CET1 12.19%; L/D 80.2%.
- **2026 guidance**: loans ~€6.2bn (+€1.1bn), deposits ~€7.7bn (+€1.4bn), net profit >€195m.
- **Q1 2026**: net profit €47.5m (+22%); loans €5.5bn (+38%); deposits €6.4bn (+34%).
- **Headcount** 31/12/2024: 550 bank / 575 group (vs 478/500 in 2023).
- **Branches**: ~29 (21 Athens, 3 Thessaloniki, rest Patras/Larissa/Corinth/Crete); plan ~10 new branches over 3 years ("phygital" model, paperless w/ tablets + digital signatures).
- **Digital stack**: Netinfo digital-banking platform live Jul 2021, integration layer by Profile Software on WSO2; Optima mobile app, IRIS payments, Apple/Google Pay, 3-step mobile digital onboarding (eKYC).
- **Euroxx**: non-binding offer for up to 80.84% for up to €65.5m (all-cash), close 2H 2026 — pivot into investment banking/brokerage, not retail hardware.
