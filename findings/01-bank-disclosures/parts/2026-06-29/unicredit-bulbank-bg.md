# UniCredit Bulbank (Bulgaria) — 2026-06-29

Focus: Branch of the Future cashless, post-euro ATM replacement, Prime, Google Cloud.

## Context
- Bulgaria adopted the EURO on 01/01/2026 (21st eurozone member). UniCredit Bulbank switched ATMs to euro-only withdrawals from 01/01/2026 and euro-only deposits from 04/01/2026; technical outage 31/12 21:00 – 01/01 01:00. (BTA, Sofia Globe, bank notices)
- 4th/5th largest BG bank; FY2024 net profit BGN 947.2m (+17.4% y/y, standalone); 125 branches at 31/12/2024 (per FY2024 AR); ~1m customers; 760k digital active / 720k mobile active users (~70% penetration). Only Top-5 BG bank with fully organic growth (no M&A).

## Key signals
1. **Post-euro ATM replacement / obsolescence** — FY2024 AR: replaced 73 obsolete ATMs + installed 36 new ATMs; "ATM obsolescence" named as an ICT risk; 2025 focus = self-service zones + cashless branches. ATM deposits +31% volume; #1 BG market share in ATM-deposited amounts. Live fleet ~755 ATMs (contacts page). Euro changeover = forced full-fleet reconfiguration → recycler/hardware refresh window. → Printec ATM/self-service + cash recyclers + OptiCash + managed services.
2. **Branch of the Future / cashless branches** — pilot since 2013 (BG was UniCredit group pilot country); first fully cashless branch (Krasno Selo, Sofia) + 10 more rolled out; cash desk ops -64% over decade; cash service migrated to 24/7 self-service zones. 2025 plan continues self-service-zone + cashless-branch expansion. → recyclers/TCRs, ATM placement, managed services.
3. **Prime** (launched ~18/05/2026) — new personal-banking model for affluent clients (>€50k assets; or salary €3,100 Sofia/€2,600 country); target ~10% of base = 100,000 individuals; rolled out simultaneously across 9 CEE markets incl. Bulgaria; dedicated Prime section in Bulbank Mobile + personal banker. → digital onboarding/eKYC, channel integration (advisory-led branches need self-service for cash).
4. **Google Cloud** — UniCredit Group signed 10-yr MoU with Google Cloud 12/05/2025; migrates app landscape incl. legacy across 13 banks (incl. Bulbank) progressively; Vertex AI/Gemini for AI incl. financial-crime prevention. → cloud-native AML/transaction-monitoring + HSM/key-management opportunity.
5. **Digital onboarding/eKYC** — 20+ fully digital end-to-end processes; remote ID via Evrotrust (selfie + ID scan) + BORICA B-Trust QES; "bank office in the phone" (Capital.bg, 04/05/2026, paywalled). → eKYC/digital onboarding adjacency (incumbent Evrotrust/BORICA = competitor stack).
6. POS/acquiring growth — POS fleet +10.5%, acquiring volumes +20%, txns +23% (FY2024); 5,519 POS replaced + 4,606 configured. → POS/acquiring.
7. Antifraud/transaction monitoring saved ~BGN 2.5m core + BGN 3m card antifraud (FY2024). → AML/transaction monitoring.
