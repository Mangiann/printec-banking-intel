# UBB (KBC) Bulgaria — 2026-06-29

UBB is now the **largest bank in Bulgaria** by assets (~BGN 38.4bn, ~19.6% share, Sept 2025), ~176 branches, ~937 ATMs (~1,000 reported), part of KBC Group. Bulgaria adopted the euro 01/01/2026.

## What changed vs baseline (2026-06-24)
- **Eurohubs CHANGED**: now **33 Eurohubs across 21 cities** (baseline said 35 in 23). Coin-counting machines, recommended for >200 coins. Sofia 10 sites, Plovdiv/Varna/Burgas 2 each.
- **Post-euro ATM ops confirmed**: 01–31/01/2026 ATMs accept BGN deposits / dispense EUR only; **from 01/02/2026 fully euro**. Free lev exchange at branches for 6 months after adoption (to ~30/06/2026); BNB + select banks only after 12 months.
- **Cashless branches confirmed**: Ring Mall (first cashless, 160 m², extended hours) + **Stamboliyski Sofia branch went cashless 11/11/2024** (self-service zone only). New-generation/phygital format. Vendors undisclosed.
- **Kate AI quantified (2024 Report to Society)**: 200,000+ users in 2024, **>65% autonomy**, 71% satisfaction. Baseline Sept-2025 figure: 70-75% autonomy / 1M mobile users / 84% of mobile users. Voice payments + Blink P2P via Kate (2024). Kate 2.0 (gen-AI) timing still unconfirmed for BG.
- **Digital onboarding**: 62% of new clients onboarded fully digitally Jan–May 2026 (>50% earlier 2025).
