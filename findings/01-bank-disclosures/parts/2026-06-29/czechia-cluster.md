# Czechia bank cluster — disclosures (run 2026-06-29)

Banks: Ceska sporitelna (CS), CSOB, Komercni banka (KB), Raiffeisenbank CZ, plus shared-network partners Moneta, Air Bank, UniCredit CZ.
Focus: shared deposit-ATM networks, recyclers, Temenos (KB), KAL incumbency, DORA/AMLR.

## STRUCTURAL CONTEXT
Czech commercial banks are PRIVATE; ATM/self-service procurement is bilateral RFP, not public tender (confirmed in 02-tenders/czechia-nen). Pursue banks directly.

## KEY THEME: TWO COMPETING SHARED DEPOSIT-ATM NETWORKS
1. **Network A (KB / Moneta / Air Bank / UniCredit)** — deposit-capable shared network. Launched cash deposit Aug 2024 (~800 deposit ATMs); UniCredit joined 16/02/2026 adding 144 deposit ATMs -> "nearly 1,000" deposit ATMs. Jan 2026: 155,000+ shared deposits, ~CZK 2bn. Single-deposit limit raised CZK 300k->350k. New: deposit via tokenised card on phone/smartwatch (Feb 2026). ~120 ATMs relocated to underserved areas. Total shared withdrawal fleet ~2,000 ATMs (Moneta states "nearly 2,000").
2. **Network B ("Vklad na kartu" — CS / CSOB / Raiffeisenbank + Mastercard & Visa)** — world-first card-rail cross-bank deposit. Pilot Jun 2025, full live 16/09/2025; >1,000 deposit ATMs. Czechia first country where Mastercard/Visa offer this. CS is the only bank also enabling deposits to other banks' accounts.

## VENDOR / INCUMBENCY MAP (competitive landscape for Printec)
- **CS**: KAL Kalignite full suite (K3A, KTC, KTH) since 2016; world-first Kalignite Hypervisor in production (Jan 2020); migrated hundreds of NCR machines to Hypervisor (as of Jun 2024). Fleet 1,516 ATMs+payment machines end-2025 (down 95 YoY). KAL = multivendor software incumbent.
- **KB**: Temenos core (live; >1m of 1.6m retail clients migrated by end-2024, full retail target end-2025, corporate by end-2027; LTIMindtree partner). ATM hardware NCR + Diebold Nixdorf (DN added 2017 for deposit/recycling); piloting DN Series 300. ~800 deposit ATMs in shared network.
- **CSOB**: Diebold Nixdorf partner since 2001; DN AllConnect Managed Services runs entire ~1,025 self-service fleet; ~200 DN Series recyclers (2022); 239+ deposit ATMs growing ~20%/yr. Czech Post 3,200 branches for cash.
- **Raiffeisenbank CZ**: 158 ATMs / ~134 deposit ATMs; 116 branches end-2025; 2.1m clients. Member of Network B.

## REGULATORY
- DORA: in force 17/01/2025; transposed via Act No. 31/2025 Coll. (Act on Digitalization of Financial Market), effective 15/02/2025. CNB supervises.
- AMLR (EU 2024/1624) + AMLD6: apply 2027; AMLA stands up 2026.

## PRINTEC ANGLES
- Hard incumbency: CS=KAL, KB/CSOB=Diebold Nixdorf direct + DN managed services. Printec wedge is narrow on hardware/software; better on cash-cycle optimisation (OptiCash) for fast-growing recycler/deposit volumes, monitoring (INETCO), HSM/security, AML/transaction monitoring for DORA/AMLR.
