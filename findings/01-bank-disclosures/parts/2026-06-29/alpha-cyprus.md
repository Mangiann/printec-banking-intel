# Alpha Bank Cyprus (ex-AstroBank) — 2026-06-29

## Summary
Alpha Bank completed AstroBank acquisition 31/10/2025 (deal signed 24/06/2025); combined entity = Cyprus' 3rd-largest bank, total assets >€6.6bn, loans >€2bn, deposits >€5.6bn. Full operational integration (digital platforms, cards, apps, systems) to be completed gradually within 2026. Target: recurring net profit >€100m (double profitability).

Cyprus Voluntary Exit/Retirement Scheme: max compensation €225,000 (€200k tax-free, excess taxed 20%); +10% for staff outside Nicosia/Limassol and DoValue transferees; ETYK consultation complete. - **BRANCH REORGANIZATION announced 18/03/2026** — Makedonitissa branch operations relocated/merged into Engomi (ex-AstroBank) branch. Part of retail transformation.
- **RETAIL TRANSFORMATION rolled out (~02/04/2026)** — 5 directorates / 25 regions (mirrors Alpha Greece model); branches → advisory/development hubs; routine tx pushed to remote/self-service; **CONTACTLESS ATM TRANSACTIONS introduced** for faster service. CEO Vassilis Psaltis.
- **ATM model clarified — STRATEGIC**: ex-AstroBank ATM network is OUTSOURCED to Euronet (330+ ATMs, free withdrawals on Euronet fleet); ex-AstroBank does NOT own a recycling/self-service fleet. Instant Cash cardless withdrawal works on ex-AstroBank + Euronet ATMs only (limits €600/day, €3,000/month). Legacy Alpha CY runs own ATMs at its 12 branches (Alpha 360 ATM; 3DES). Consolidation of two ATM models (Euronet-outsourced vs Alpha-owned) is the open question for 2026.
