# CrediaBank (ex-Attica Bank) — 2026-06-29

CrediaBank = merged Attica Bank + Pancreta (Pagkritia) Bank, rebranded 10/07/2025 (AGM); trades on ATHEX as CREDIA from 21/08/2025. Greece's "5th banking pillar." CEO Eleni Vrettou.

## Key developments this run (all NEW vs baseline — baseline had no CrediaBank entry)

1. **Rebranding complete** — Attica→CrediaBank, IT/digital operational merger of Attica + Pancreta completed Sept 2025 (unified core/customer base). "digital-first / mobile-first" transformation program.
2. **HSBC Malta acquisition** — put-option agreement 16/09/2025 to buy 70.03% of HSBC Bank Malta for €200m cash (implies €286m for 100%; 0.48x P/TBV). MTO for minorities at €1.44/share. HSBC Malta = 2nd-largest Maltese bank, ~€8bn assets, ~€6bn deposits, ~24% asset share, ~200k clients, ~1,100 staff. Close expected 2H 2026 (MFSA/ECB/Bank of Greece approvals). Doubles group assets. Badwill ~€200m. Staff retained ≥2yrs.
3. **Euronet ATM + acquiring transfer** — definitive agreement announced 30/12/2025–07/01/2026. Euronet acquires CrediaBank merchant-acquiring business; **CrediaBank's in-branch + off-site ATM network transferred to Euronet Card Services to manage/operate**; customers get free access to Euronet's ~2,500-ATM Greek network. 3-year exclusive payments/digital-wallet partnership (acquiring, ATM, card issuing, A2A wallet w/ IRIS rails). Close Q3 2026. Combined merchant volume ~$22bn / 240k+ merchants → Euronet now largest payments provider in Greece.
4. **New Experience branch concept** — appointment-free "phygital" branches (Kolonaki/Skoufa Athens, Heraklion Crete, Thessaloniki 8 Tsimiski). Card-based customer ID on entry, **TCR machines** for cash, **VTS / video-teller (CrediaConnect)**, VR product-explorer (built w/ FORTH), full accessibility (sign-language, Braille, neurodiversity). ALL branches to be renovated under concept by end-2026. Group footprint = 66 branches + 5 business centres.

## Printec implications
- **ATM fleet transferred to Euronet (Printec competitor in ATM-as-a-service)** — NEGATIVE for any direct CrediaBank ATM hardware/managed-services pitch in Greece; the fleet is now Euronet-operated. ATM opportunity at CrediaBank Greece is effectively foreclosed for 3+ yrs.
- **New Experience branch TCRs + VTS** — cash recyclers (TCR) and video-teller hardware being rolled across the whole network by end-2026. Vendor undisclosed → live in-branch self-service opportunity (Glory/NCR TCRs, eKYC, card-ID). Highest-certainty Printec angle at CrediaBank.
- **HSBC Malta (new ECB jurisdiction)** — ~1,100 staff, ~24% share bank to be integrated 2026-27. Channel/ATM/branch tech, AML/compliance harmonisation, eKYC, transaction monitoring all in play post-close. Malta is outside Printec's 17-country footprint but a net-new opportunity.
- **Digital onboarding/eKYC + AML** — unified post-merger platform + Malta integration = onboarding/AML consolidation work.
