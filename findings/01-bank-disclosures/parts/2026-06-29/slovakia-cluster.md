# Slovakia cluster — SLSP / VÚB / Tatra (+ ČSOB, Slovenská pošta)

Run: 2026-06-29. Focus: deposit-ATM expansion, ATM 2.0 coin deposit, eKasa POS mandate, OptiCash/APTRA.
Vendor landscape (verified): Slovak customer-ATM suppliers = Diebold/Wincor Nixdorf (direct), NCR (via PRINTEC), OKI (via Albacon). Source: kurzy-online / co-to-je.sk.

## Key findings (NEW / CHANGED vs 2026-06-24 baseline)

1. **Tatra banka "bankomat 2.0" — coin-deposit ATMs.** Launched Mar 2025 at 21 ATMs accepting all coin denominations (1c–€2), free, daily cap 300 coins/account, card+PIN only. By Mar 2026 grown to 40+ devices, "continuously increasing." Third-generation ATMs. ČSOB had coin deposit first. Tatra fleet ~330 cash-recycle ATMs / 303 in SK. (techbyte.sk Mar-2025; tatrabanka.sk blog; CHANGED — coin count was not in baseline; baseline had Tatra 294/6 recyclers.)
   → Printec: coin recyclers (Glory/DN iCASH), self-service hardware, field services.

2. **SLSP recycling-ATM estate now ~350** (was ~300 in baseline; first deposit ATM 2015 Košice). Recyclers reuse deposited notes for withdrawal → OptiCash ROI story. (touchit.sk; slsp.sk vkladomat.)
   → Printec: OptiCash/APTRA cash optimisation, recyclers, managed services.

3. **VÚB core-banking replacement underway** — VÚB publicly states it is "changing its core banking system, a project that affects almost all areas of the bank." Part of Intesa Sanpaolo group Isytech cloud + Retail+ programme (€5bn group digital spend; ISP closing 1,500 branches group-wide). Major channel-integration opening post-CBS. VÚB deposit ATMs at 110+ locations, no coin. (vub.sk; finreport.sk; ISP AR2025.)
   → Printec: channel/self-service integration to new core, x-core, digital onboarding.

4. **eKasa / cashless-acceptance mandate FIRM.** Act 384/2025 Coll. on Sales Records: from 01/01/2026 eKasa-only online registers + QR receipt + posted receipt-rights notice; from 01/05/2026 every eKasa merchant must offer a cashless option (card/online transfer/QR) for purchases >€1. Fines €500–15,000 (some sources €1,500–20,000). Subsidised terminal scheme (12 months fee-free) applications to 30/06/2026. Nationwide POS/SoftPOS demand driver. (fiscal-requirements.com; vatupdate; slsp.sk biznis; peniaze.sk.)
   → Printec: POS terminals (Verifone/Castles), SoftPOS, acquiring/TMS.

5. **Slovenská pošta self-service cash kiosks** — pilot still 5 branches (Bratislava x2, Košice, Štúrovo, Žilina): deposit+withdrawal, ePoukaz, Pay-by-square bill pay, cash or card. Under evaluation; NO scale-up tender published yet. Concurrent branch closures continue. (posta.sk; sita.sk; refresher.) STILL PRE-TENDER.
   → Printec: self-service kiosks + recyclers + field service.

6. **ČSOB SK** — 109 deposit ATMs, 40 recyclers (allow €5-note withdraw), coin deposit available (one of two banks with €5 notes alongside Tatra). (csob.sk; finsider.)
