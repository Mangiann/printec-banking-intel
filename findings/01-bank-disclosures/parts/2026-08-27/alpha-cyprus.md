# Alpha Bank Cyprus (ex-AstroBank) — bank disclosures, run 27/08/2026

**Status:** complete · **Signals:** 12 · all new or materially changed vs the 30/07/2026 findings file.

## Headline

Alpha Bank's own Q2 2026 deck (31/07/2026) now puts a **date** on what the last run could only call "during 2026": the AstroBank **full integration date is H2 2026**. As at 27/08/2026 the bank is still running two separate e-banking platforms, two card estates and (per the Association of Cyprus Banks) ~25 branches / ~26 ATMs. Nine branch pairs were merged on 18/03/2026, bill payment was pulled off the counter on 02/02/2026, and a EUR 225,000-cap voluntary exit scheme was announced on 22/06/2026 (EUR 18m provision in Q2 2026). The Central Bank of Cyprus, in its 22/07/2026 payment statistics, explicitly forecasts ATM-network rationalisation as a consequence of the 2025 bank acquisitions — the regulator is calling Printec's shot for it.

Fewer, higher-value withdrawals plus halved counter withdrawals means cash volume per surviving machine rises - the economic case for recyclers.
- **Likelihood:** CBC expects visible ATM-count change 'within the coming months' from 22/07/2026; next release (H1 2026 data) lands around Feb 2027 and will show it | **Confidence:** Medium | **Size:** M | **Win:** Medium
