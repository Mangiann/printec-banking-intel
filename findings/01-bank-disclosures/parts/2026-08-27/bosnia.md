## Headline

- **IPS BiH went live 20/07/2026** on the Bank of Italy TIPS-clone platform (EU-funded, EUR 2m) with only **3 of ~20 banks** — Intesa Sanpaolo, Raiffeisen, Sparkasse (~25% of sector assets). Nova banka joined 28/07/2026, ATOS Bank 17/08/2026 → **5 participants**. Every remaining bank is a dated integration project.
- **SEPA application filed 06/08/2026**, after both entity parliaments passed the harmonised financial-law package (FBiH House of Representatives 28/07/2026). CBBH explicitly states banks must now realign IT to SEPA standards; first transactions ≥6 months after admission. XL-sized, dated.
- **Regulator-verified fleet data at 30/06/2026** (new this run): FBiH-HQ banks **1,400 ATMs / 28,834 POS**; RS-HQ banks **490 ATMs / 12,483 POS**. BiH-wide ≈ **1,890 ATMs / 41,317 POS**, vs ≈1,863 / 38,269 at 31/12/2025 — **POS +8% in six months, ATMs +1.4%**.
- **Seven FBiH banks report ZERO own POS terminals** (Sparkasse, BBI, Addiko, ProCredit, Privredna, Union, KIB) — clean acquiring entry points.
- **FATF grey-listing 19/06/2026** is converting into named audit mandates: ASA Banka commissioned an external **ICT audit**; NLB Sarajevo commissioned separate **financial / AML-CFT / ICT** audits for 2026.
- **ASA Banka**: KM 50m bond (Mar-2026), new **Business Strategy 2026–2029**, 100 ATMs, 59 units, 697 staff — best-timed mid-size target (win: High).

## Gaps worth naming

- **No Temenos (or any core-banking) evidence found** in BiH this run — unverified, not disproven.

### 1. Central Bank of BiH (CBBH) / IPS BiH (Bosnia & Herzegovina)

**Signal.** CBBH launched the domestic Instant Payment System (IPS BiH) on 20/07/2026, built on the Bank of Italy TIPS-clone platform adapted for the Western Balkans with ECB consent, EU-funded with EUR 2m. Launch participants were only three banks - Intesa Sanpaolo Banka BiH, Raiffeisen Bank BiH and Sparkasse Bank BiH - together roughly one quarter of banking-sector assets. CBBH says QR-code payments and later cross-border instant payments are planned additions.

**Source.** Central Bank of Bosnia and Herzegovina - press release — https://www.cbbh.ba/press/ShowNews/1798?lang=en — 20/07/2026 (primary)

**Implies for Printec.** A live 24/7 instant rail with only 3 of ~20 banks on it at launch is a dated, funded integration backlog. Every non-participant bank needs ISO 20022 connectivity, 24/7 payment processing, real-time fraud/AML screening and later QR acceptance - Printec transaction monitoring, AML/compliance and POS/QR acceptance.

**Likelihood.** Very likely - remaining banks must join through 2026-27; two more already did within a month · **Confidence.** Medium · **Opp size.** L · **Win.** Medium

**Follow-up.** Track each new joiner announcement on cbbh.ba/press/AllNews; ask non-participants (UniCredit, NLB, Addiko, ASA, BBI, ProCredit, ZiraatBank, Union) about IPS integration and fraud-screening plans

### 3. CBBH / SEPA (Bosnia & Herzegovina)

**Signal.** CBBH formally submitted BiH's application to join the Single Euro Payments Area on 06/08/2026, after both entity parliaments adopted the harmonised financial-law package (RS National Assembly, then FBiH House of Representatives on 28/07/2026). CBBH states commercial banks must now align their IT and business systems with SEPA standards and join the European payment schemes, and that first SEPA transactions are expected no earlier than six months after admission. World Bank estimates fee savings of around EUR 100m for BiH.

**Source.** CBBH press release - 'CBBH officially applied for joining the SEPA' — https://www.cbbh.ba/press/ShowNews/1808?lang=en — 06/08/2026 (primary)

**Implies for Printec.** An explicit, central-bank-stated requirement for every BiH bank to re-plumb core payment IT to SEPA/ISO 20022 within a defined window. Drives payment-hub, message-transformation, sanctions/AML screening and managed-services demand.

**Likelihood.** Very likely - application filed; bank-side work can only start after admission, so budgets form late 2026 / 2027 · **Confidence.** Medium · **Opp size.** XL · **Win.** Medium

**Follow-up.** Watch European Payments Council decision on BiH admission; then approach banks in the 6-month technical-preparation window

### 6. FBiH Banking Agency (FBA) - FATF grey list (Bosnia & Herzegovina)

**Signal.** FBA published a statement dated 19/06/2026 confirming BiH was placed on the FATF increased-monitoring ('grey') list at the FATF plenary of 19/06/2026 following the MONEYVAL assessment. FBA argues the classification is unrelated to banking-system stability, points to European Commission Implementing Decision 2021/1753 on equivalence of BiH supervisory requirements, and says the current classification 'may have limited effects on the efficiency of banking services in international business'. It says the reasons lie in the wider legal and institutional framework outside the banking system and that BiH must implement the FATF Action Plan.

**Source.** Agencija za bankarstvo FBiH - 'Moneyval: Informacija o procjeni i ogranicenim efektima na bankarski sistem u vezi sa klasifikacijom na sivu listu' — https://www.fba.ba/bs/moneyval-informacija-o-procjeni-i-ogranicenim-efektima-na-bankarski-sistem-u-vezi-sa-klasifikacijom-na-sivu-listu — 19/06/2026 (regulator)

**Implies for Printec.** Grey-listing forces correspondent banks to apply enhanced due diligence on BiH counterparties, which typically pulls forward AML/KYC, sanctions screening and transaction-monitoring investment at BiH banks. Direct fit for Printec AML/compliance, transaction monitoring and digital onboarding/eKYC.

**Likelihood.** Likely - action-plan implementation runs through at least 2027 reporting cycles · **Confidence.** Medium · **Opp size.** L · **Win.** Medium
