# NBG (National Bank of Greece) — bank disclosures, run 27/08/2026

**slug:** `nbg-gr` · **status:** complete · **signals:** 14

## Headline

- Core banking replacement (project **Cosmos**, Infosys Finacle) **finished** — final Customer go-live May 2026. The channel/self-service layer is the next wave.

- Branches flat at **304** but **49 tellerless**; staffing cut via a Voluntary Exit Scheme. Automation inside a retained network.

- **1,360 ATMs** at 31/12/2025 (HBA), **63% off-site** — the largest off-site fleet in Greece. NBG has publicly refused the Piraeus/Cashflex outsourcing route.

- From NBG's own locator (27/08/2026): **~442 of 1,323 ATM points are cash-out only** — the quantified recycler white space. **300 of 306 branches** carry an APS.

- Subsidiary **Stopanska Banka (North Macedonia)**: 154 ATMs, only **62 cash-in capable** — a market the previous run never covered.
