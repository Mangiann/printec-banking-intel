# ProCredit Holding (XK/BG/RS/BA/RO/AL/MK/UA) - bank disclosure findings, run 27/08/2026

**Status:** complete | **Signals:** 19 | **Priority:** never reached in the 30/07/2026 run - this is the first coverage of ProCredit.

## Headline

ProCredit's H1-2026 (13/08/2026) is a bank openly spending its margin on a retail build-out: profit EUR 38.5m (down from 47.0m), cost-income ratio 71.2%, and the report naming **staff, IT, marketing and the branch network** as the reason. Hard IT spend is EUR 18.9m for the half (+5.4%), with depreciation up 14.9% to EUR 16.5m on 'expansion and modernisation of the retail infrastructure'.

The strategic shape matters more than the numbers: ProCredit runs **tiny branch estates with always-on machine rooms**. Counted from the banks' own locators on 27/08/2026:

| Market | Customer sites | Structure |
|---|---|---|
| Kosovo | 29 | 15 branch+ATM, 13 standalone ATM, 1 ATM Zone |
| Bulgaria | 18 | 17 offices (14 with 24/7 self-service) + 1 standalone Zone 24/7 |
| Serbia | 16 | 15 branch+Zona 24/7, 1 standalone Zona 24/7 |
| Bosnia | 9 | 4 tagged AutomatedTeller, 5 branch; all deposit-capable |
| Albania | 10 | head office + 9 agencies, staffed ~30h/week |
| Romania | 8 | 1 HQ, 3 branches, 4 Zone 24/7, 1 self-service zone |

## The three things to act on

1. **LIVE TENDER - ProCredit Bank Serbia, procurement 878/26, fraud monitoring application, deadline 24/08/2026.** Published on the bank's own supplier page. It closed three days before this run. It proves the group buys fraud/AML tooling externally rather than building it at Quipu. Contact srb.nabavka@procredit-group.com now about status and the invitation list.

2. **Make-vs-buy is now resolved.** The mobile apps are built in-house by Quipu (confirmed by the Google Play package ids com.quipugmbh.*, and by Bosnia migrating off the local Ping/ELBA app). Do not pitch channel software. What IS bought externally: identity/e-signature per market (Evrotrust and B-Trust in Bulgaria, OneID in North Macedonia), fraud monitoring (Serbia tender), and all hardware/consumables (Kosovo tendered UPS units and banknote bags in 2026).

3. **Serbia is substituting merchant cashback for ATMs.** 'Brzi kes' gives up to RSD 5,000 cash at ProCredit POS terminals across named retail chains, marketed with the line that one should not chase an ATM. Sell acquiring into Serbia, not ATMs - and watch whether the model is exported to other ProCredit markets.
