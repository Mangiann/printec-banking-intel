# Optima bank (Greece) — bank disclosures, run 27/08/2026

**Slug:** `optima-gr` · **Status:** complete · **Signals:** 11 · all `is_new = true` (first coverage of this bank in the 27/08/2026 run)

## The story in five lines

Optima is the extreme case of the lean digital-first bank, and the H1-2026 numbers published on 30/07/2026 make that concrete: **EUR 5.8bn of loans and EUR 7.1bn of deposits, both +37% YoY, run on exactly 30 branches and 663 people**, at a 22.0% cost-to-core-income ratio. It raised FY-2026 guidance to **>EUR 210m net profit** on the same footprint.

The channel picture is the interesting part for Printec. Optima's own interim statements say each branch has **one external ATM, for "cash withdrawals or balance inquiries"** — the locator confirms 29 ATM-tagged sites out of 32, no off-site machines, **and no deposit or recycling capability anywhere**. Its tariff of 20/07/2026 prices withdrawals at EUR 0 across every Greek bank's ATM, so it has no reason to build cash-OUT capacity — the whole opening is cash-IN.

The constraint is equally clear: **H1-2026 capex additions were EUR 737k (fixed assets) plus EUR 1.1m (intangibles)** — both slightly *below* H1-2025. Nothing gets sold here as capex. And there is **no merchant acquiring / POS business at all**, so that product line has no route in.

### 8. Optima bank S.A. — Greece

Optima's tariff confirms it free-rides the national ATM network rather than building one. Basic tariff ANX158.55, issued 20/07/2026 with changes effective 01/09/2026, prices debit-card cash withdrawals at EUR 0 at Optima's own ATMs AND at other banks' ATMs in Greece and the EEA, EUR 0.75 at independent (non-bank) ATM-network operators in Greece/EEA - explicitly waived to EUR 0 in municipal units where only one ATM exists - and EUR 0.75 outside Greece/EEA. Legal entities: EUR 0 at Optima ATMs, EUR 0.75 at other banks' and independent operators' ATMs.

- **Source:** Optima bank, Basic tariff of commissions ANX158.55/20-07-2026 (Greek) — https://www.optimabank.gr/media/qosdawqq/anx158_basiko_timologio.pdf (20/07/2026, *primary*)
- **Implies:** Because Greek law zero-rated interbank ATM withdrawals, Optima gets nationwide cash-out coverage for free and has no commercial reason to expand its 29-machine fleet for withdrawals. Any Printec ATM case must therefore be built on cash-IN (deposit/recycling) and on branch teller-replacement economics, never on withdrawal coverage.
- **Likelihood:** Regime in force; Optima has priced to it · **Confidence:** Medium · **Opp size:** S · **Win:** Low
- **Follow-up:** Re-pull ANX158 after 01/09/2026 to confirm the effective-dated changes landed as published.

### 10. Greek Ministry of National Economy and Finance (General Secretariat for the Financial Sector) — Greece

Cash access is moving from ATMs to retail. Answering a parliamentary question tabled 15/06/2026 by PASOK MP Ioannis Tsimaris about the sudden removal of the only ATM in Delvinaki, Pogoni, Secretary General for the Financial Sector and Private Debt Management Theoni Alampasi stated that the revised EU payment-services framework Greece negotiated in Council will allow legal persons to provide cash-dispensing services through retail outlets EVEN WITHOUT a prior purchase of goods or services, on a voluntary basis for merchants, aimed at areas with limited ATM or branch access. The same reply reiterates that siting branches and ATMs remains a commercial decision of the banks. Reported 21/08/2026.

- **Source:** Dimokratiki tis Rodou, reporting the Ministry's written answer (21/08/2026) — https://www.dimokratiki.gr/21-08-2026/metrita-apo-to-kafenio-anti-gia-atm-to-schedio-tis-kyvernisis-gia-tin-eparchia-ke-pos-tha-efarmosti/ (21/08/2026, *press*)
- **Implies:** A purchase-independent cash-at-retail regime creates a new Printec product surface in Greece - cashback-enabled POS/POI terminals, retail cash-handling and reconciliation, and cash-in-transit/managed cash services for merchant sites - and it is the exact mechanism an ATM-light bank such as Optima could use to claim rural cash access without buying machines.
