## Headline

- **Estate re-counted from the bank's own locator, not from a press release.** `cec.ro/sucursale?localitate=` (empty filter) returns the whole national estate server-rendered. De-duplicated: **1,244 ATMs, 256 MFMs, 963 agentii, 48 sucursale**. The 1,011 territorial units reconcile *exactly* with the Pillar 3 disclosure — which validates the method. Machines are up (1,484 → 1,500) but **deposit-capable machines are DOWN (264 → 256)**: the cash-in gap widened.
- **Cash2Card (04/08/2026)** — CEC opened its MFM network to *any* Romanian bank's Visa/Mastercard debit card, free, for non-customers too. It has turned its cash-in estate into shared national infrastructure. This is the strongest recycler business case at this bank.
- **The missing denominator, found:** the FY2025 Investor Presentation (June 2026, never opened by the last run) discloses **24/7 self-service zones = 46 units** and **cashless units = 5**, against 1,011 territorial units. 46 of 1,011 is the conversion pipeline.
- **Temenos moved:** "Core Banking System (**pilot launched, migration in progress**)" — first concrete status in 18 months. Also **>92% of 2025 investment was IT** (CBS, CRM, ERP, core treasury), and FIS Quantum / Pluridio are named as the treasury stack.
- **Bill B409/2026 cleared its last hurdle:** BNR (17/08/2026, Governor Isarescu) says it **does not oppose** the mandatory custom-withdrawal-amount rule, and asks for consultation with the banking system on terminal technical characteristics; the Competition Council (18/08/2026) has no objection. A 90-day compliance window across every ATM in Romania.
- **Timing:** CEC does **not** report in the August cluster. H1-2026 interim information is filed for **30/09/2026**. Diary it.
