# UBB (KBC Bulgaria) — bank disclosures, run 27/08/2026

**Status:** complete · **Signals:** 14 · all Medium confidence (Orchestrator promotes)

## Headline

- UBB's own locator feed, re-pulled 27/08/2026: **737 ATM sites, only 244 deposit-capable** (249 flagged bulk-note-acceptor), i.e. **~493 cash-out-only ATMs** — the baseline white space is confirmed, not closing.

- **172 branch/office pins; only 5 are 'Безкасов клон' (cashless branch)** and **34 are 'Евро Хъб'** coin-exchange branches with high-tech coin counters.

- UBB's own FY2025 report: **171 branches + 18 business centres at 31/12/2025** (from 176+18), **#1 in Bulgaria by assets**, C/I 41.9%, core banking **Temenos T24**, ATM/POS depreciated over **3 years**.

- Euro changeover, quantified: **BGN 406,129,089 via 245 deposit ATMs vs ~BGN 2.3bn over branch counters**, 564,063 branch visitors, **~370 tonnes of coin**.

- Dated triggers: free lev deposits end **30/09/2026**; **Verification of Payee mandatory for all Bulgarian banks 01/01/2027**.
