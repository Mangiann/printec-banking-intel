# Optima bank S.A. (Greece) — 2026-07-24

Lean digital-first Greek bank (founded 2019, Motor Oil/Vardinogiannis-linked, ATHEX-listed Oct 2023). Among Europe's most efficient banks (cost-to-core-income ~23-24%, ROTE ~25%). Growth engine is corporate lending; physical footprint tiny (~30 branches, no independent large ATM fleet — customers steered to DIAS shared network + digital). This remains the OPPOSITE of an ATM/cash-automation buyer: capex is software/intangibles-weighted, not self-service hardware.

## What is NEW / materially changed vs 2026-06-29 baseline
- **Branch network now 30** (was ~29); staff **627** (more current than the 550 bank/575 group at 31/12/2024). CEO Kyparissis reaffirms staying in the same low-footprint business model. Source: bankingnews 12/05/2026.
- **€3m share buyback programme** approved at the 05/05/2026 AGM (NEW); dividend €0.23/share, €50.9m total, 30% payout ratio.
- **Euroxx timeline firmed up**: CEO says completion expected "within 2-3 months" (i.e. late Q3/Q4 2026), ~80% of shares paid in cash; still a non-binding/exclusive-negotiation structure as of the public record. Pivot into brokerage/investment banking/asset management, not retail hardware. ~20% commission uplift expected.
- **FY2024 capex disclosed** (from Annual Financial Report 31/12/2024, English notes pack): additions to fixed assets (PP&E) **€1.8m group** (2023: €1.5m); additions to **intangible assets €3.7m** (2023: €3.0m). Confirms hardware capex is minimal and software/digital dominates — reinforces LOW ATM/self-service demand thesis with a hard primary figure.

## Printec relevance (LOW)
Lean digital-first model = minimal ATM/cash-automation demand; capex proven software-weighted (€3.7m intangibles vs €1.8m fixed assets FY2024). Best (modest) angles: AML/transaction-monitoring + HSM/security as it scales into capital markets via Euroxx (new brokerage/AML surface); digital onboarding/eKYC if ever insourced; a handful of branch TCRs/recyclers if the ~10 planned new branches add cash-deposit.
