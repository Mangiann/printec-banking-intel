# National Bank of Greece (NBG) — findings 2026-07-14

Focus: Cosmos/Finacle core go-live, capex split, ATM/branch fleet, self-service, vendors.
Primary sources used: NBG 1Q26 Results Presentation (8 May 2026), NBG own ATM pages, Mellon press release, Hellenic Ministry of Finance, Greek press (To Vima / Ta Nea), fintechfutures.

## Key verified / new
- **Cosmos / Finacle core banking – COMPLETE.** NBG 1Q26 deck (8/5/2026): "Core Banking System migration near completion (May 2026)". fintechfutures (Jun-2026) reports it COMPLETED — "largest technology project ever in the Greek banking sector". Started 2023; vendors Infosys Finacle + IBM + Deloitte; migrated >6m customers, 12m deposit accounts, >1.5m loans; 1,000+ people, ~500,000 person-days. Now in run/optimise: GenAI (Sophia chatbot, speech-to-text), Appian workflows, CRM, EDW→cloud. is_new (completion now confirmed).
- **IT/digital capex c€1b since 2020 incl. CBS replacement** (1Q26 deck). Phase shifting from build to run/optimise. FY25/1Q26 opex up ~8% YoY driven by tech + human capital.
- **ATM fleet ~1,300+.** NBG English ATM page: "more than 1,300 machines". Greek press (To Vima/Ta Nea, Jul-2025): 1,416 ATMs = 529 in-branch + 887 off-site. Corrects baseline 1,360; the '1,476' figure still NOT confirmed — do not carry.
- **In-branch ATM stack = Printec COMPETITOR.** Mellon Technologies deal (16/1/2024): 700 new Hyosung Series 8 recycling ATMs + dispensers, KAL K3A app + KTC management software. Locks NBG's in-branch self-service channel to Mellon/Hyosung/KAL.
- **ATM fee cap (regulatory driver).** Hellenic Min. of Finance: from 11/8/2025 zero fee on own-network + single-ATM-municipality withdrawals; €1.50 cap on third-party ATMs (amends Art.48 L.5167/2024). Sector fee hit ~€150m/yr (To Vima). Systemic banks "do not rule out" selling off-site ATM fleets à la Piraeus–Cashflex — NBG has 887 off-site ATMs exposed. (NBG-specific sale NOT confirmed.)
- **Branch/self-service transformation.** 49 tellerless branches in 1Q26 (48 FY25, 34 FY24, 18 FY23, 9 FY22); branch optimisation -157. Onboarding/KYC flagged as core journey being enhanced. >4.6m digital subscribers, >3.3m active.
