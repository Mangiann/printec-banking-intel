# Bosnia & Herzegovina — Bank Disclosures (2026-07-14)

Target banks: UniCredit, Raiffeisen, NLB, Addiko, ASA, Sparkasse, BBI. Focus: ASA fleet modernisation, deposit ATMs, Temenos cores, instant payments, growing ATM market.

## Headline NEW signals
- **NLB voluntary public takeover of Addiko Bank AG** — EUR 29.00/share (25.8% premium), announced 09/04/2026. NLB intends to integrate all Addiko subsidiaries in 5 overlapping markets (incl. BiH); may divest non-EU subs. Materially earnings accretive from year 2. → Post-merger ATM fleet + channel harmonisation, core/self-service integration, managed services. (NLB Group press release)
- **Instant payments go-live 20/07/2026** — CBBH TIPS Clone, interbank only in phase 1, max 5,000 KM/transaction, full rollout phased to 31/07/2027, to align with ECB TIPS for cross-border later. → instant-payment rails, real-time fraud/AML transaction monitoring, HSM. (CBBH ShowNews/1713, 12/09/2025; klix.ba / Glas Srpske)
- **Raiffeisen Bank dd BiH live on Temenos Core** — retail current accounts migrated off legacy, ~500k customers, delivery partner Tech Mahindra, Red Hat OpenShift hybrid cloud. Published 21/05/2025. → ATM/channel middleware re-integration to new core, managed services. (Temenos press release)

## Deposit ATM / fleet signals
- **UniCredit Bank BiH** — largest bank (~25% share, 8.7B KM assets FBiH). 145 contactless ATMs; cash deposit on part of network. UniCredit Banja Luka: 49 contactless ATMs, deposit on part of network. → recycler upgrade, deposit automation, managed services.
- **BBI Digital Smart ATMs** — 20+ deposit-capable touch-screen ATMs, rollout ongoing since 27/01/2023; vendor undisclosed. BBI runs 150+ ATMs within BH Mreža. → deposit automation / recycler supply, vendor-displacement opportunity.
- **ASA Banka** — leading domestic bank, 3rd largest in FBiH; ~3.7B KM assets, ~65M KM net profit 2025 (press aggregate, Low). Grew via Vakufska (2021) + Sberbank BH acquisitions → legacy fleet to harmonise. → fleet standardisation, ATM/self-service, digital onboarding.
- **BH Mreža / BAMCARD shared network** — ~220 ATMs pooled across BBI, Privredna banka Sarajevo, Union Banka, ASA; KVIKO cardless. → pooled ATM-as-a-service / managed services angle.
