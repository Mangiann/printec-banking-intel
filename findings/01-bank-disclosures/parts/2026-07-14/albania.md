# Albania — Bank Disclosures (2026-07-14, run B)

Focus: NCR SS80 (Printec/OTP), fleet refresh, Creatio, digital banks. EN + Albanian.
Printec = local NCR Atleos partner in Albania (software, LEK currency templates, maintenance).

## What changed / deepened vs baseline (2026-06-29)
- **NCR market dominance quantified (NEW specificity):** NCR Atleos case study (Union Bank of Albania) states NCR holds ~95% ATM market share in Albania and **only 2 of 13 banks are NOT on the NCR SelfServ 80 Series** — i.e. 11 of 13 run SS80. Printec Albania is the local partner installing NCR software + providing maintenance for the fleet. Very strong structural tailwind for Printec managed services / recycler upgrades across nearly the whole market.
- **Bank of Albania keynote, Balkan Payment Forum (NEW primary regulator source, 14/05/2026):** First Deputy Governor Holta Zaçaj. SEPA (joined Nov-2025) — first 6 months: 363,000 transfers, €4.3bn; small-transfer cost 0.76%→0.08% (23x drop); 68% of outgoing SEPA online. 67% of bank payments via card (2025); 29 e-payments/adult/yr (3x vs 2020); 78% adults banked (vs 40% in 2018). 10 e-money institutions licensed; 2 implementing open banking; PSD2 transposed 2020; direct debit scheme introduced. Cybersecurity/fraud flagged → AML/transaction-monitoring/HSM demand.
- **Instant payments (TIPS Clone) re-verified:** Banca d'Italia TIPS clone, tentative June-2026 deadline seen as ambitious; realistic go-live **autumn 2026**; enables unified centralized QR payments.
- **POS mandate re-verified + fee detail (NEW):** Law 79/2025 (Jan-2026). Mandatory POS by **30/05/2026** (accommodation, transport, public institutions/SOEs) and **31/12/2026** (all other businesses). Domestic card commissions up to 2.70% (VISA/MC), 3.20% international, 30 lek fixed fee, 5,000 lek install, 1,000 lek/mo maintenance, terminal up to 50,000 lek. Major POS/acquiring/TMS opportunity.
- **OTP Bank Albania — fleet sized:** ~230 ATMs (thebanks.eu). Printec incumbent on NCR SS80 (deposit+recycler), phased 2025-26. Largest confirmed Printec hardware footprint in-country.
- **Raiffeisen AL 2026 CEO interview (29/04/2026):** €3.4bn assets, €76.4m net profit, ROE 17.7% (#1 of top-5). First bank with Apple Pay; 30,000+ digital-wallet users; Raiffeisen ON 345,000 users; 186 ATMs (2024, market ATM leader). Digital transformation named top 2026 priority. No ATM vendor named → fleet-refresh whitespace.
- **Credins Bank — re-verified:** Creatio (partner Facilization) CRM/digital-transformation underway; no public 2026 go-live date surfaced.
- **JET Bank — re-verified:** first 100% digital bank, license Mar-2026, live Jun-2026, Backbase AI-native OS, owned by Jet Holding B.V. (Amsterdam), 75,000+ waitlist. No fleet — eKYC/AML competitor-platform benchmark.
