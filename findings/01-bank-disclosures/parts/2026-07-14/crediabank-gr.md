# CrediaBank (ex-Attica Bank) — Bank Disclosures, run 2026-07-14

Slug: crediabank-gr. Greece's 5th-largest bank, formed from Attica Bank + Pancreta merger, rebranded to CrediaBank. CEO Eleni Vrettou. ~300k active customers, 65 retail branches + 5 business centers.

## Headline developments (all NEW/CHANGED vs baseline)

1. **HSBC Malta acquisition** — 70.03% stake for EUR200m cash (0.48x H1-2025 TBV). Put option 16/09/2025; definitive agreement 23/12/2025. Doubles CrediaBank assets, first market outside Greece. HSBC Malta: EUR7.9bn assets, EUR6.2bn deposits, ~200k clients, 22.5% CET1, 2.5% NPE (30/06/2025). Needs MFSA + Bank of Greece + ECB approval; completion expected end-2026. Mandatory takeover offer to minorities at EUR1.44/share. Keeps Malta Stock Exchange listing; retains staff 2+ years. Capital-neutral, own resources.

2. **Euronet acquiring + ATM transfer (COMPETITIVE ALERT for Printec)** — Definitive agreement announced 07/01/2026. CrediaBank sells its merchant-acquiring business to Euronet (epay) and transfers its ATM network to Euronet Card Services. Euronet to fully manage the acquired in-branch + off-site bank ATMs (extended participation agreement); CrediaBank customers get fee-free access to Euronet's 2,500+ Greek ATMs. Euronet also provides card issuing (credit/debit/prepaid) + 3-yr exclusive payments/A2A digital wallet on IRIS rails. Combined ops >$22bn/yr, 240,000+ merchants. Close expected Q3 2026. This DISPLACES Printec from ATM hardware/managed-services and POS/acquiring at CrediaBank.

3. **CrediaBank ATM fleet size** — 141 ATMs (80 in-branch, 61 off-site) per bank site (May-2026) — the fleet moving to Euronet operation.

4. **"New Experience" branch concept** — First-in-Greece concept; flagship Kolonaki (Skoufa 3), then Heraklion, Thessaloniki (Tsimiski 8), Panepistimiou, Chalandri. Full renovation of all 65 branches by end-2026. Digital self-service points, personalized welcome at entry, accessibility tech, human-centric. "Multiple millions of euros" investment (Gen. Dir. S. Iliadis). Rebrand of 65 branches Attica→CrediaBank completed by end-Oct-2025.
