# Raiffeisen Bank Romania — findings 2026-07-14

Focus: ATM/POS fleet, cloud, voice-guidance, Garanti BBVA integration.

## Key signals
- **Garanti BBVA Romania acquisition** — RBI/Raiffeisen Bank S.A. agreed to buy 100% of Garanti Bank S.A. + Motoractive IFN for **EUR 591m**, closing expected **Q4 2026**; plan to **merge** acquired units with RO operations for cost synergies. Garanti ~EUR4bn assets, ~2% share. Combined RO becomes #3 bank. Source: rbinternational.com press release 28/03/2026. IMPLIES fleet harmonisation + self-service/software consolidation opportunity (but ATM/POS estate outsourced to Euronet — competitive barrier).
- **Voice guidance (ghidare vocală)** extended **nationally** across ATMs + MFMs for cash withdrawals/balance, Q1 2026 (per Q1-2026 results + accessibility page phased rollout to end-2025). Driven by **Law 232/2022** (EAA transposition, applicable 28/06/2025; ATMs/self-service terminals in scope; 20-yr grandfather / transition to 28/06/2030).
- **Fleet**: ~770 ATMs, 373 MFMs, >42,000 POS, 264 branches (Q1 2026 branches figure; 770/373 from 2020 disclosure — verify current).
- **ATM/POS outsourced to Euronet** since 2003 (end-to-end mgmt) — competitive barrier for Printec managed-ATM services at Raiffeisen RO.
- **Cloud**: target 80% apps in cloud by 2025 (AWS + Azure), 160+ workloads migrated; Backbase digital channels. App-layer, not self-service HW.
