Lean digital-first Greek bank (founded 2019, Motor Oil / Vardinogiannis-linked, ATHEX-listed Oct 2023).
**Big event this run: H1 2026 results published 30/07/2026** — full primary pack opened locally

## Genuinely NEW this run (beyond the previous pass)

1. **Fortinet is the whole network-security estate.** Optima's own open vacancy
   "Network engineer – Fortinet specialist | Infrastructure | IT operations" (IT dept, Athens)
   names FortiGate NGFW clusters (Active-Active/Active-Passive) with SD-WAN, **FortiWeb** (WAF),
   **FortiADC** (load balancer), **FortiMail**, **FortiAuthenticator**, **FortiManager**,
   **FortiAnalyzer**, plus site-to-site VPN tunnels between data centres and branches, and asks for
   NSE 4 / NSE 5. → This *scopes the open "firewall management and network security policy platform"
   evaluation disclosed in the FY2025 report*: the winning platform must sit on FortiManager/FortiGate
   (FortiManager-native or Tufin/AlgoSec-class). Recovered from the site's Umbraco careers API, a
   source environment not used before.
2. **Accessibility gap, on the record.** New primary page `/support-area/prosvasimotita/` claims
   compliance with **Law 4994/2022 and Directive (EU) 2019/882** yet says only *"the majority of the
   bank's ATMs are accessible from wheelchairs"*; the FY2025 report says *"Most ATMs are wheelchair
   accessible"*; the IR presentation quantifies only **60% of branches accessible to PWD**. **No
   claim of voice-guided / audio ATMs anywhere** — while NBG (837 units, 100% by end-2026), Alpha
   (Printec since 2021) and Eurobank (100%) all claim it. Small, cheap, reputationally urgent
   retrofit on ~29 machines.
3. **Brand-new price list ANX158.55, in force 20/07/2026** (and ANX216.31 for e-transactions, same
   date): debit-card ATM withdrawals **€0 at Optima's own AND all other banks' ATMs in Greece/EEA**;
   **€0.75 at third-party ATM-network operators** in Greece/EEA (€0 in municipal units with only one
   ATM); €0.75 outside Greece/EEA. Card fee changes flagged effective 1.9.2026. Optima's 29 ATMs earn
   nothing and serve rivals' customers free → pure cost centre → managed-ATM argument.
4. **ATM count independently corroborated**: the Greek branch-locator HTML carries exactly **29
   "ATM post"** tags across **32 location pages**, matching the interim report's *"Each branch of the
   Bank's network has an external automatic teller machine (ATM) ... cash withdrawals or balance
   inquiries"* — i.e. **withdrawal/balance only, no deposit, no recycling, no off-site fleet**.
5. **AML/compliance function is hiring** ("AML/CFT & Authorities Investigations", plus "Junior IT
   auditor", "Project manager, Officer - IT", "Microsoft 365 & Azure specialist") into the Bank of
   Greece's 2026 supervisory priority of on-site inspection of customer transaction-monitoring
   systems. Zero ATM/self-service/cash-operations roles anywhere in the list.
6. **H1 2026 numbers and raised guidance** (see rows) — FY2026 net profit target lifted from >€195m
   to **>€210m**, loans +€1.35bn, deposits +€1.7bn, cost-to-core-income <30% (1H26 actual 22.6%).
   Guidance explicitly excludes Euroxx.
7. **Capex still tiny and falling**: FY2025 fixed assets €1.9m + intangibles €3.1m (≈€5.0m total vs

8. **Euroxx firmed to "expected completion by YE 26"** in the IR deck, yet **absent from the interim
   notes pack entirely** — not contractually closed.
