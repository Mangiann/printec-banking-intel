# Piraeus Financial Holdings — 2026-07-30 (second pass, primary-source deepening)

## Summary
The earlier pass this run mined the H1.2026 results (29/07/2026). This pass went to sources that pass had **not** opened: the **Capital Markets Day deck of 05/03/2026**, the **assured Sustainability Statement 2025**, the **audited Annual Financial Report 31/12/2025**, the **ECB digital euro pilot register**, and Cashflex's own site re-fetched with a desktop UA. All PDFs were downloaded and text-extracted locally with pdfminer (the AFR is 800 pages and carries a "do not extract" metadata flag, overridden).

Three findings materially change the picture:

1. **The fleet is bigger than the decks say.** The assured sustainability statement reports **1,641 ATMs in Greece at 31/12/2025** (2,092 in 2024), not the "c.1,500" repeated in the CMD and H1 decks. **1,197** offer deposit with immediate availability (1,201 in 2024 — flat), so **444 units are still withdrawal-only**. **1,172** have voice guidance (1,097 in 2024, **+75 yoy**), leaving **469 units** un-retrofitted against an accessibility obligation.
2. **Branches are not flat going forward.** The same statement commits to keeping branches **above 300 units for four years** and to reducing the physical network by **2% CAGR** over that period — i.e. 368 → roughly 340 by 2029. The quarterly "flat 368" is a starting point, not a policy.
3. **The AI programme now has hard economics.** CMD: **~€800m capex 2026-30, ~80% AI/technology**, plus ~€50m for Ethniki (a "five-year capex of €850m"); AI capacity release booked as **~€50m staff + ~€20m G&A** savings; OpEx walk shows explicit −€40m and −€20m AI lines. Agentic AI pilots are named in **Compliance (triage review) and Risk (monitoring, early warning)** — the AML/transaction-monitoring adjacency, being built with Accenture/Newra.

Also new: **Piraeus is one of 36 PSPs in the ECB digital euro pilot** (14/07/2026; H2-2027 start, 12 months) — one of only three Greek entrants. **ISO/IEC 42001** certificate is dated (TÜV NORD Hellas, effective 01/04/2026, three years, scope = AI governance framework + Digital Assistant + Employee Assistant). **Software additions €116m in 2025** (€102m in 2024), cost base €1,215m. **Snappi**: Paymentology appointed issuer-processor 30/03/2026; CMD target 300k clients in 12 months; and the SSM delayed the €494m capital return from 15/06 to 07/08/2026 over how the 55%→68% Snappi stake increase was approved.

**Cashflex re-verified**: the audited BoD report restates the 22/05/2025 sale of 80.1% of KEA to Printec Cash Network; the Cashflex site still offers **withdrawal / balance / PIN only — no deposit** — publishes no unit count, and has posted no news since 01/10/2025.
