## Headline additions this pass

1. **Cosmos has a hard finish date: 26/05/2026.** The last deposit-account categories and customer profiles migrated on 26/05/2026; the programme closed "early June 2026". Scale: >1,000 people across three continents, ~500,000 person-days, €1bn over five years. NBG's COO is named for the first time in our record: **Stratos Molyviatis**.
2. **Finacle is going to ALL NBG international subsidiaries — and "a lot of which are long-standing users of Temenos' T24".** That is an explicit multi-country core-replacement pipeline (NBG Cyprus, Stopanska Banka in North Macedonia) with channel/ATM/POS re-integration attached.
3. **NBG Cyprus put an ops-and-technology executive in charge**: Nicolas Prentzas, General Manager & COO from 09/06/2026 (Cambridge computer-science background, previously built digital banking platforms). NBG Cyprus is small and corporate-only: 2 branches (Nicosia, Limassol), >140 staff — no ATM fleet, so the play there is onboarding/KYC and corporate channels, not hardware.
4. **The post-core workstream is now explicit and funded**: Appian workflows, CRM, EDW migration to cloud, a NEW Mobile Banking platform, a NEW Business Internet Banking, the "Next" youth app, and **"Live Banking"** — marketed on nbg.gr as "the full branch experience, 100% digital" with remote relationship managers, alongside in-branch RMs for high-potential clients.
5. **NBG already sells merchant cash automation**: ΕΘΝΟDeposit deposit cards for ATM cash-in, **Smart Safe devices installed at the customer's own premises**, and "Instant Cash". The device vendor is NOT disclosed anywhere on nbg.gr — a standing identification task.
6. **VoP and IPR are live** in NBG Mobile Banking (Verification of Payee before transfer; Instant Payment Requests) under the EU Instant Payments Regulation.
7. **NBG–Allianz**: MoU 07/05/2026 for a **30% stake in Allianz European Reliance** plus a **10-year exclusive bancassurance** deal (auto 5-year extension), distributed through NBG's physical *and* digital channels.
8. **Qivalis re-verified from NBG's own press release (20/05/2026)**: NBG is a shareholder in the 37-bank / 15-country euro-stablecoin JV seeking a DNB EMI licence, MiCA EMT targeted 2H2026, reserves ≥40% bank deposits.
9. **Competitor land-grab in Greek ATMaaS**: Epirus Bank handed its whole ATM modernisation/expansion to **NCR Atleos under ATMaaS + Cashzone co-branding** (13/01/2026; deployment from Nov-2025, ~3-month phased rollout, Atleos does site selection, install, maintenance and operational support). With CrediaBank→Euronet and DirectPay/iXchange→Cashzone, Printec's Cashflex is now one of three operator platforms competing for exactly the carve-out we want from NBG's 862 off-site ATMs.
10. **IRIS at the physical checkout is still tiny**: ~330,000 in-store IRIS transactions worth just over €14m in H1-2026, against 4.6m IRIS users, >600k enrolled professionals, ~1.2m POS and 70,000 e-shops. DIAS says the unlocks are **NFC tap-to-pay** and **loyalty**; IRIS joined **EuroPA** in late June 2026 (Spain, Portugal, Italy), targeting 18 countries / ~176m users by end-2026, e-commerce in 2027.

## Re-verified / unchanged
- The audited FY25 network table (1,360 ATMs; 498 on-site / 862 off-site; 303 branches; 48 tellerless) is reconfirmed from the Greek 2025 Annual Financial Report.
- **nbg.gr still advertises "356 branches and 1,476 ATMs"** (checked 30/07/2026) — still stale marketing copy, still contradicted by the audited figures.
- The Mellon/Hyosung/KAL award (16/01/2024) is confirmed as **700 Hyosung Series-8 recyclers and dispensers for IN-BRANCH operations**, with KAL K3A + KTC. It does not cover the off-site estate.

## Printec read
- The **862 off-site ATMs** remain the single sharpest opportunity, but the window is closing: Atleos and Euronet are both executing exactly this trade in Greece right now.
- **Subsidiary core replacement (Cyprus, Stopanska Banka)** is a genuinely new, multi-year integration pipeline — channels, ATM/POS drivers, reconciliation.
- **Smart Safe / ΕΘΝΟDeposit** is a live, un-vendored merchant cash-automation line: direct Printec cash-automation fit and the vendor is unnamed.
- **In-branch customer-management + Live Banking** is branch-automation surface that does not require displacing Mellon's hardware.
- De-prioritise transaction monitoring (NBG builds in-house) and core banking (Infosys/IBM/Deloitte locked).
