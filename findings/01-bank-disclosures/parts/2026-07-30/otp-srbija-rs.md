# OTP banka Srbija — 2026-07-30 run

**Headline:** the 274-ATM baseline is stale. The bank's own live locator dataset now lists **310 ATM locations**, of which **248 are deposit-capable** and **176 accept EUR deposits**. OTP simultaneously **left Payten/ASEE's MoneyGet shared ATM network on 01/01/2026** and says it is focusing on expanding its **own** ATM and branch estate. The branch-adaptation tender machine is running at ~1 tender every 2–4 weeks (17 opened Jan–Jul 2026). Separately, NBS's approved-machine register (updated 29/07/2026) confirms **Printec d.o.o. is the NBS-registered representative for the NCR Atleos recycler line in Serbia**, with fresh approvals dated 26/02/2026 and 14/04/2026 — Printec is regulatory-ready to bid on exactly the cash-in build-out OTP is doing.

## Printec angle
- **Cash automation is the play.** 62 of 310 devices are still cash-out only, the business "uplata pazara" deposit cap is only RSD 50,000/day, and only 45 sites carry a 24/7 zone. That is a recycler/TCR replacement list.
- **The Payten incumbency just cracked.** Payten stays the card processor (via Chip Card) and is DN's Serbian agent, but it has lost the shared-ATM relationship. Printec's NCR Atleos NBS approvals are the counter-position.
- **SEPA + agentic payments** pull in AML/transaction-monitoring, tokenisation and HSM scope.
