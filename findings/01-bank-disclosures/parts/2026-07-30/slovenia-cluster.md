# Slovenia cluster — NLB, OTP banka d.d., Intesa SI, Bankart, Addiko SI (run 2026-07-30)

## Headline
**The NLB ATM/CDS procurement is no longer "coming" — it already happened.** The NLB Group Annual Report 2025 (published 09/04/2026) states in plain language that during 2025 *"Tenders for ATM and Cash Deposit Machines (CDS) were finalised."* The Investor Day commitment of 07/05/2026 is therefore the **execution** phase of an already-awarded programme, not a pre-tender signal. Identifying the winning OEM/integrator and the per-country lot split is now the single highest-value question for Slovenia.

Second headline: **NLB's Addiko bid was materially restructured** — EUR 37.00/share (not EUR 29.00), threshold cut to 50% + 1 share (9,750,001 shares) on 15/07/2026, acceptance period extended to 29/07/2026 17:00 CET. RBI stood at 55.5% on 20/07/2026. The outcome was not public as of 31/07/2026.

Third: **Bank of Slovenia's own PX statistical series** (updated 18/06/2026) gives the hard Slovenian estate: **1,330 ATMs and 46,659 POS at 2026Q1**, with only **993 ATMs accepting contactless** — i.e. roughly a quarter of the national fleet is still non-contactless and is a replacement candidate.

## What could NOT be confirmed
