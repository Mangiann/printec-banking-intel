# Alpha Bank Cyprus (ex-AstroBank) — run 2026-07-30

**Headline this run:** the ex-AstroBank cutover is no longer "sometime in 2026" — the bank is telling customers to prepare *during August*, cards are already being reissued ahead of it, and the Central Bank of Cyprus has itself flagged that post-merger ATM rationalisation is expected "within the coming months". A Cyprus-specific Voluntary Exit Scheme (max €225,000) landed on 22/06/2026, separate from the Greek VSS in the baseline.

## What is new vs baseline
- **VES Cyprus, 22/06/2026** — max €225,000 (€200,000 tax-free), +10% for staff outside Nicosia/Limassol or ex-doValue transferees. Reported sizing: 80 exits + 50 seconded to Cerberus (Low confidence, not yet primary).
- **Migration timing** — "prepare during August", Alpha 360 Mobile/Web are the target platforms, access only after cutover.
- **Full card reissue** — new cards delivered before migration, old cards deactivated, new card numbers break recurring mandates.
- **CBC Payment Statistics H2-2025** (resolves the previously corrupt PDF) — 396 ATMs in Cyprus (398 a year earlier), ~73% contactless vs 38% euro area, average withdrawal €390, OTC withdrawals nearly halved in 3 years, and footnote 11 explicitly expects ATM-network rationalisation after the 2025 acquisitions.
- **FY2025 audited statements** — 23 branches at 31/12/2025 (2024: 12); 491 average employees; 317 AstroBank staff transferred; software additions only €4.031m; acquired AstroBank PP&E fair-valued at €1.688m; six strategic pillars including a **Branch Network Redesign Program** and a systems-upgrade pillar.
- **Branch consolidation live** — 12/02/2026 announcement merging Makedonitissa into Engomi (ex-AstroBank) and Agios Ioannis (ex-AstroBank) into Omonias, effective 28/02/2026; a further reorganisation announcement dated 18/03/2026.
- **General T&C 11th edition, 21/07/2026** — effective at earliest late September 2026, drops "ACTC" from the automated-transaction-network definitions, adds push-notification PIN delivery, and carries transitional provisions for ex-AstroBank Miles & More cards. Consistent with a Q3/Q4-2026 cutover.
- **Contactless gap** — the Cyprus Alpha 360 ATM page advertises deposits (cash and cheque) but NOT contactless/cardless, while Alpha Greece is rolling contactless ATMs out in 2026 and ~73% of Cypriot ATMs are already contactless.
