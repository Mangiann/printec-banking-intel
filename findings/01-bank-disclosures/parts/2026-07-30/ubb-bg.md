# UBB (KBC) Bulgaria — run 30/07/2026

## Headline corrections to the baseline
- **937 ATMs is NOT supported.** UBB's own live locator API (`https://www.ubb.bg/locations/pins/desktop:1`) returned **738 ATM pins** and **172 branch pins** on 30/07/2026.
- The ">780 ATMs / one of the two largest networks" line still on ubb.bg sits on a **news page dated 05/09/2012** — stale, must not be carried as a 2026 figure.
- Audited FY2025 (statements approved 21/04/2026): **171 branches + 18 business centres** (2024: 176 + 18), **3,901 FTE** (2024: 3,914).

## Cash / self-service picture
- Only **246 of 738** ATM pins carry the "cash deposit at ATM" feature; **705** are flagged 24/7 access. Roughly two-thirds of the fleet is cash-out only.
- **34 branches** flagged "Euro Hub" (coin-counting machines) in the locator; the 27/01/2026 press item listed **33**.
- **5 branches** flagged "cashless" (Безкасов клон); 5 joint UBB/KBC branches, 3 public vaults, 4 extended-hours.
- Six months of euro (03/07/2026): **BGN 2.3bn** exchanged, **370 tonnes of coins**, **564,063** branch visitors — but only **BGN 406m** deposited through deposit ATMs. Free BGN deposits extended to **30/09/2026**.
- New pricing plans (30/06/2026) make deposits at UBB ATMs free in every plan tier.

## Digital
- Kate autonomy **68% → 75%** in 2025, **5.1m interactions**, work equal to ~**32 FTE**; 1m UBB Mobile clients. Kate 2.0 (LLM) is the 2026 upgrade.
- **62%** of new clients opened accounts fully digitally Jan–May 2026 (market ~30%).
- 2026 stated priority: acceleration of digitalisation/automation with particular focus on **core banking system T24** capabilities.
- Verification of Payee published 15/07/2026; mandatory for all Bulgarian banks from **01/01/2027**.
