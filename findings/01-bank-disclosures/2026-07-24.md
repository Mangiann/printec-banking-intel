## What changed since last run

The single highest-value new item is the crystallization of Cashflex ownership: Printec Cash Network holds a confirmed 80.10% (Piraeus 19.90%) of the entity operating roughly 850 former Piraeus off-site ATMs, which now sets its own fee policy (€1.50/withdrawal) — a captive XL fleet Printec effectively controls. Alpha Bank is the other big mover, with three confirmed new signals: 11% normalised-earnings growth guidance for 2026 plus an H2 Investor Day, a six-deal M&A integration programme (Alpha Trust, FlexFin, AstroBank and others) landing full benefit in 2027, and newly confirmed self-service APS kiosks across most branches — all pointing to near-term self-service and integration spend. CrediaBank also turned strongly profitable in Q1 2026 (recurring net profit €13.0m, fee income +55% YoY) while advancing its "New Experience" branch refurbishment (~40% of 66 branches) and its HSBC Malta acquisition. On verification caveats: the widely-cited NBG 529 in-branch / 887 off-site ATM split could not be confirmed (only the ~1,414 total stands), the Piraeus post-transfer fleet figures are only partially confirmed, and CrediaBank's €300m share capital increase claim was contradicted by verification.
