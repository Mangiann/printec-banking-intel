## What changed since last run

This is the first run, so there is no baseline. The headline picture: Q1 2026 reporting season (April–May 2026) shows banks across the footprint in active spending mode rather than pure cost-cutting. The four standout signals for Printec: (1) **Alpha Bank (Greece) launched a retail transformation in April 2026 that explicitly invests in the branch network and introduces contactless ATMs** — a direct branch/ATM opportunity window; (2) **PrivatBank (Ukraine) is in the middle of a large ATM fleet renewal** — 1,200 ATMs bought in 2025 and a stated plan for ~800 more in 2026; (3) **Banca Transilvania (Romania) budgeted ~RON 1.02 billion of 2026 investment, mostly IT/digital, up ~21% year-on-year**; (4) **Bulgaria's euro changeover (1 Jan 2026) is entering its final phase** — free lev exchange at banks ends 30 June 2026, after which Bulgarian banks are likely to rationalize cash/ATM operations that were sized for the conversion peak. Group-level disclosures (OTP, Erste, KBC, RBI) all confirm IT spend as a rising cost line, which supports active vendor budgets across their CEE subsidiaries.

## Jargon used above (plain language)

**Core banking system** — the bank's central software holding all accounts and transactions; replacing it is the biggest IT project a bank does. **Phygital** — branch model mixing digital self-service with human advisors. **Voluntary separation scheme** — paid program for staff to leave voluntarily; usually precedes network/operating-model change. **Digital penetration** — share of customers actively using the bank's app/online channels. **ROTE** — return on tangible equity, a profitability measure; high ROTE = budget capacity.
