## NEW / CHANGED signals

1. **CIR (EU) 2026/798 — remote onboarding of EUDI wallet users (NEW).** Adopted 07/04/2026, published OJ 08/04/2026 under Art.5a(24) eIDAS2. Sets the legally mandated reference standard for *remote* onboarding to EUDI wallets: eID at LoA "substantial" + additional remote procedures = LoA "high", conformance assessed against **ETSI TS 119 461 v2.1.1 (2025-02)**. Directly applicable in all MS/EEA. This is the technical floor that bank eKYC/AML onboarding flows must meet from 2026-27. EUR-Lex: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_impl/2026/798/oj/eng/pdf (08/04/2026). → Printec digital onboarding/eKYC + AML/compliance.

2. **Per-market EUDI readiness snapshot, July 2026 (deepened).** Austria (eAusweise/ID Austria) and Slovakia (eDOKLADY) = "existing app: upgrade confirmed"; Greece (Gov.gr Wallet) upgrade confirmed. Croatia, Czechia, Slovenia, Romania = announced, no public sandbox. Bulgaria = announced with dev repo. Cyprus (IDMe.cy) and Hungary (DÁP) = upgrade NOT yet confirmed. Denmark AltID in production 03/06/2026; Germany staged rollout early-2027. <1/3 MS at readiness benchmark vs 24/12/2026 deadline. Source: eideasy blog 07/2026 https://www.eideasy.com/blog/eu-digital-identity-wallets-july-2026 → onboarding integration per Printec market.

3. **BiH state-level draft Law on Electronic Identification & Trust Services (NEW).** Council of Ministers BiH draft in public consultation, flagged "significant for EU integration" — the state-level counterpart competing with the RS parallel law. javnarasprava.ba/bih/Zakon/1513 → foundational for compliant remote eID/eKYC in BiH.

4. **BiH Constitutional Court vs RS parallel eID law (CHANGED/deepened).** RS Law on Electronic Identification, Trust Services & Electronic Document (Off. Gazette RS 49/26, dated 06/02/2026) re-enacts digital-signing/e-wallet provisions the Court already annulled in **case U-1/25 (March 2026)**. New constitutionality request + interim-measure proposal filed 09/06/2026 by HoP President Ademović; as of mid-July 2026 still under review, NO interim measure yet issued on the eID law. Sources: Sarajevo Times 18/06/2026; raport.ba. IDDEEA jurisdiction dispute. → uncertainty freezes BiH remote-eID onboarding market.

5. **Wallet onboarding economics / bank prep (supporting, NEW).** Large-scale-pilot data: wallet-based onboarding ~EUR 3-8/customer vs EUR 70-100 for live-video KYC. Banks running onboarding architecture reviews May–Sept 2026; mandatory bank acceptance ~Nov/Dec 2027 (Art.5f). Source: biometricupdate (OneSpan LSP) 09/2026. → Printec eKYC value case.
