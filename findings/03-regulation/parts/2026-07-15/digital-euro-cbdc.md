## What is genuinely NEW vs baseline (acceptance/ATM-wallet readiness)

The baseline captured the political/pilot milestones but MISSED the ECB scheme-level ATM & terminal
technical workstreams — the most Printec-relevant layer. Surfaced this run from ECB primary sources:

1. **ECB RDG call for experts (18/03/2026, deadline 10/04/2026)** — Rulebook Development Group
   **Workstream G5 = "implementation specifications specifically for ATMs and payment terminals"**
   (communication technologies + offline integration; ATM/terminal provision expertise required), and
   **Workstream B1 = certification & approval framework** for payment/acceptance solutions and PSP
   infrastructure. Direct hit on Printec ATM/self-service + POS/acquiring + managed services.
2. **ECB signs agreements with 3 European standard setters (24/04/2026)** to facilitate digital-euro
   payments: ECPC (CPACE NFC tap-to-pay), **nexo standards (POS merchant acceptance AND ATM cash-machine
   transactions)**, Berlin Group (alias/mobile-number payments). These become the acceptance-device spec
   basis — reduces terminal-upgrade need, standardises ATM/POS integration.
3. **Sixth RDG progress report (06/07/2026)** published — ongoing rulebook incl. ATM/terminal specs.
4. **Trilogue kickoff**: EP negotiating mandate approved 416–169 (22 abst.) on 09/07/2026; first trilogue
   13/07/2026 under Irish Presidency; aim political deal end-2026; **at-least-24-month rollout clock**;
   **mandatory merchant acceptance as legal tender** (exemptions: micro-enterprises / self-employed not
   already taking digital payments) — an acceptance-readiness demand driver. Holding limit to be set by
   Commission on ECB recommendation, reviewed >=every 2 years; businesses may hold only ~24h.
