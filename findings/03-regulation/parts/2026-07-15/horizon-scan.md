# Horizon scan — emerging regulation beyond the watchlist (2026-07-15)

Run 1 day after baseline (2026-07-14). Job: discover NEW/newly-relevant regulation across the 17-country CEE/SEE footprint that the ten fixed workstreams do NOT cover, and flag promotion candidates. Confidence capped at Medium.

## Top new promotion candidates

### 1. Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) — Reg (EU) 2024/2847 — NEW, not in baseline
Product-cybersecurity law for "products with digital elements." ATMs, POS terminals, self-service kiosks, HSMs and connected security devices are in scope; manufacturer/integrator obligations (secure-by-design, SBOM, vulnerability handling, CE marking) fall on vendors like Printec.
- Conformity-assessment-body provisions apply **11/06/2026**.
- Vulnerability/incident **reporting obligations apply 11/09/2026** (24h early warning / 72h notification / 14-day final report to ENISA single platform).
- Main essential-requirements + CE-marking obligations apply **11/12/2027**.
- Fines up to €15m or 2.5% global turnover.
- Source: EC "Shaping Europe's digital future" CRA summary — https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/cra-summary
- **DESERVES ITS OWN WORKSTREAM.** Directly hits Printec as a hardware+software manufacturer; also a managed-security/vulnerability-management opportunity for bank clients.

### 2. NIS2 (Dir (EU) 2022/2555) national transposition — NEW status
Banks = "essential entities" with risk-management + supply-chain security + 24h/72h incident-reporting duties. Transposition still uneven across footprint.
- Bulgaria's amended Cybersecurity Act **entered into force 13/02/2026** (BG had been referred to CJEU May-2025).
- Romania: bill approved, framework not yet published.
- Source: EC NIS2 transposition tracker — https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/nis-transposition
- Overlaps DORA for FS but catches broader entity set. Printec managed-security/SOC + secure device supply.
