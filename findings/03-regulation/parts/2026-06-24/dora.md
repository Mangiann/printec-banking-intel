## Plain-language note
- **DORA** (Reg. EU 2022/2554, in force 17 Jan 2025): EU rulebook forcing banks/PSPs to manage ICT risk, report major ICT incidents, keep a register of ICT outsourcing, and test resilience.
- **Register of Information (RoI)** — Art. 28/29: a structured register of every ICT third-party contract, submitted to the national regulator (EBA format).
- **TLPT (Threat-Led Penetration Testing)** — Art. 26-27: realistic red-team attack simulations on the largest banks, run under the **TIBER-EU** framework, at least every 3 years.
- **CTPP (Critical ICT Third-Party Provider)** — Art. 31: ICT providers (cloud, core-banking, payments) designated by the ESAs as systemically critical and placed under direct EU oversight with a Lead Overseer.
