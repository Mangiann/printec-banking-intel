# eIDAS 2 / EUDI Wallet & eID — 2026-06-24

**Plain language:** *eIDAS 2* (Regulation (EU) 2024/1183) requires every EU member state to issue at least one **EUDI Wallet** (an EU Digital Identity Wallet — a phone app holding government-verified identity + attributes) by **end-Dec 2026**. From **end-2027**, banks and other "obliged" private relying parties that must verify customers must **accept** the wallet for strong customer authentication and onboarding. *LoA* = Level of Assurance ("substantial" or "high"); *eKYC* = electronic Know-Your-Customer (verifying a customer's identity remotely, e.g. at account opening).

## Headline NEW item vs 2026-06-15 baseline
- **Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/798 of 7 April 2026** (OJ 8 Apr 2026; in force 20 days after publication) — sets the reference standards for **fully-remote onboarding** to an EUDI Wallet: a "substantial" eID + layered remote procedures (NFC chip read of ID, passive/active chip auth, liveness + biometric face matching, FAR/FRR risk targets) = overall "high" LoA, per **ETSI TS 119 461 V2.1.1**. This resolves the prior "Catch-22" (wallets needed high-LoA enrolment but no remote method was defined). Directly enables **remote eKYC** because **AMLR Art. 22(6)(b)** accepts substantial/high eID + qualified trust services as valid remote identity verification. AMLR (Reg. 2024/1624) applies **10/07/2027**.

## Status of baseline items
- Dec-2026 member-state wallet deadline: CONFIRMED, still pending. <1/3 of states meet readiness benchmark (Apr-2026).
- Banks accept wallet: end-2027 (one year after Dec-2026), tied to AMLR 10/07/2027.
- BiH remote-eID: state app **e-IDDEEA** (IDDEEA) live; QES from Jun-2024; national digital-ID app launched ~Jul-2025. **Republika Srpska parallel law** (RS Official Gazette 49/2026, 6 Feb 2026) disputed/unconstitutional per state — Art. 23 cross-border auth "nodes" risk visa-free regime. Fragmentation risk for any BiH bank remote-onboarding rollout.

## Market rollout snapshot (Apr-2026)
- Live/upgrade path: Greece (Gov.gr Wallet, EUDI upgrade; bank-account-opening a target use case; Greece+Cyprus cross-border link), Austria, Czech, Poland, Slovakia, Cyprus.
- Lagging / not started: **Bulgaria, Croatia, Malta, Romania** (Romania: ROeID SSO platform only).
- Non-EU footprint: Serbia eID + ConsentID (remote high-assurance auth, cloud QES) live; Albania announced EUDI wallet for 2026; WB candidates aligning for future interop.
