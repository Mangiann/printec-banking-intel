## Top candidates for promotion to their own workstream next run
1. **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) migration** — EU coordinated roadmap (23/06/2025): all MS start transition by end-2026, critical financial infra quantum-safe by end-2030; Europol financial-sector PQC prioritisation guidance (Jan-2026); TIBER-EU 2.0 adds crypto-agility. Maps directly to **Thales HSM/PCI** (HSM/key migration, crypto-agility), ATM/POS cryptogram upgrades. STRONGEST new Printec-mappable candidate.
2. **EU AI Act high-risk obligations (credit scoring, AML, fraud AI)** — Annex III high-risk; Digital Omnibus (Commission proposal 19/11/2025) defers high-risk application; deadline reportedly moved toward Dec-2027. Maps to **FICO scoring, IMTF Siron AML, INETCO transaction monitoring** (explainability, data governance, human oversight).
3. **Consumer Credit Directive 2 (CCD2, Dir (EU) 2023/2225)** — transposition due 20/11/2025 (23/27 MS missed), applies 20/11/2026; hardened creditworthiness + eKYC/identity. Maps to **Namirial digital onboarding/eKYC**, open-banking income verification.
4. **Digital Omnibus (DORA/NIS2/GDPR/eIDAS incident-reporting single portal)** — changes DORA-adjacent incident reporting (72->96h, single ENISA portal). Touches existing DORA workstream but the Omnibus itself is new.
5. **Mandatory cash acceptance / legal tender of euro cash (Single Currency Package)** — Council position 19/12/2025; ECB Opinion 22/01/2026. Maps to **cash automation/recyclers, ATMs, access-to-cash**.
6. **FiDA / Financial Data Access (open finance)** — trilogue ongoing, adoption expected 2026; track only.
