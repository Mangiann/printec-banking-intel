## Footprint exposure
Directly binds EU members in the 17-country footprint: AT, BG, CY, CZ, GR, HR, HU, RO, SI, SK. Non-EU footprint (AL, BA, ME, MK, RS, XK, UA) not directly bound but candidate-country payment-law alignment likely follows. Budgets should commit 6-12 months before ~2028 application → NOW (H2-2026 / 2027) for fraud-monitoring and SCA stack.

## Printec mapping
Strongest hook = fraud-liability shift: PSPs liable for APP-scam / spoofing losses and for IBAN-name (VoP) mismatch redress → drives demand for real-time transaction monitoring (INETCO), fraud analytics (FICO), AML/KYC (IMTF Siron). SCA hardening → Namirial eKYC, Thales HSM/PCI. Open-banking API/dashboard requirements are largely outside Printec core (API infra), note-only.
