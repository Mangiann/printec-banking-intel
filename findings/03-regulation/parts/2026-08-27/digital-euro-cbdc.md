# Digital euro & CBDC — Agent 3 (Regulation), run 27/08/2026

## Headline
1. **ATM digital-euro requirements EXIST but are formally DEPRIORITISED.** Rulebook v0.91 Annex D1 #4 (Acceptance device) carries a full ATM section (manual funding by cash deposit, manual defunding by cash withdrawal, balance/transaction-history enquiry, each via card OR QR) — and states verbatim that "ATM use cases are deprioritised and will be addressed at a later stage". No 2026-27 ATM CAPEX trigger.
2. **RDG workstream G5 has a workplan but NO published output.** Its mandate names *nexo* as candidate reference implementation for digital euro at ATMs, offline digital euro into existing **certified** POS/ATM infrastructure, and POS/ATM security standards.
3. **The pilot has NO ATM and NO physical-POS use case** — acceptance is SoftPOS only. Four use cases, 19 NCBs (GR, HR, CY, AT, SI, SK in footprint; **Bulgaria absent** despite BNB joining the Eurosystem 01/01/2026).
4. **The real ATM money is in the third file, not the digital euro file**: the Legal Tender of Euro Banknotes and Coins Regulation, now in trilogue uncontested, empowers the Commission to set common indicators including **density of cash access points per population** and to impose country-specific implementing acts where access is insufficient.
5. **Legislative clock**: ECON 23/06/2026 → EP plenary mandate 09/07/2026 (416/169/22) → trilogues with the Irish Presidency, target political agreement end-2026. EP mandate requires a **roll-out period of at least 24 months** after authorisation. **No euro holding-limit figure is fixed in either mandate** — the ceiling is delegated to the Commission on ECB recommendation, reviewed at least every two years.
6. Non-euro footprint CBDC: CZ (CNB) no decision + no legal basis; RS legal basis exists (Sl. glasnik RS 19/2025) but no implementing act; UA e-hryvnia still options analysis. No spend triggers.

Next hard date in this workstream: **13/10/2026 — 21st ERPB general technical session on the digital euro**, agenda item: collective discussion of the digital euro **rollout strategy**.
