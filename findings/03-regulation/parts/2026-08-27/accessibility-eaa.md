## Hungarian Parliament / MNB - Act XIV of 2026 amendment to the ATM Act (ATMtv.) (Hungary) — NEW/CHANGED
- **Signal:** NEW SINCE THE BASELINE: Act XVIII of 2025 on the installation of ATMs was AMENDED by Act XIV of 2026 s.96(1)-(2), in force 14/05/2026. The amendment rewrote s.3(1)(a) and inserted new subsections (1a), (1b) and (1c), creating an ATM DECOMMISSIONING AND RELOCATION FREEZE: a credit institution operating an ATM may not cease operating an ATM that already existed when the Act entered into force - 'ide nem ertve az ATM csserejet' [not counting replacement of the ATM]; under (1a) it may move an existing ATM only in a justified case and only if, after removal, it still operates an ATM within the same settlement or Budapest district; under (1b) 'Ha az ATM csereje egy adott helyszinen kizarolag technologiai, muszaki okok miatt - igy kulonosen a kihelyezendo uj ATM meretei, vedelmi kovetelmenyei miatt - nem valosithato meg, akkor a csere ATM kizarolag az erintett telepulesen, fovarosi kerueleten belul telepitheto' [if replacing the ATM at a given site cannot be done for purely technological or technical reasons - in particular the DIMENSIONS of the new ATM to be deployed and its SECURITY REQUIREMENTS - the replacement ATM may only be installed within the same settlement or district]; (1c) defines justified cases (deployment in a settlement with no ATM, demonstrable average withdrawal-volume grounds, or landlord termination of the site lease). Breach of s.3(1) carries an MNB fine of HUF 2m-200m (s.3(2)). Branch cash-payout services are subject to the same no-closure rule.
- **Source:** Nemzeti Jogszabalytar - 2025. evi XVIII. torveny az automata bankjegykiado gepek telepiteserol, consolidated text as at 14/05/2026, with amendment notes citing 2026. evi XIV. torveny 96. s (1)-(2) (primary-tier) 14/05/2026 — https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2025-18-00-00
- **Implies:** This is an unusually direct hardware signal. ## Drzavni zbor RS / Trzni inspektorat RS (TIRS) + Banka Slovenije - ZDPSI-A (Slovenia) — NEW/CHANGED
- **Signal:** NEW SINCE THE BASELINE AND SEPARATE FROM THE CASH-ACCESS BILL: Slovenia AMENDED its EAA transposition. The Zakon o spremembah in dopolnitvah Zakona o dostopnosti do proizvodov in storitev za invalide (ZDPSI-A) was passed by the Drzavni zbor on 11/02/2026, promulgated 19/02/2026 and published in Uradni list RS st. 14/2026 of 24/02/2026 (item 563), entering into force 25/02/2026. It amends the original ZDPSI (Uradni list RS st. 14/23, applicable from 28/06/2025). The commercially decisive change is Art.1, which inserts a new Art.3(5): 'Pogoji glede dostopnosti za proizvode in storitve iz tega zakona predstavljajo obvezne zahteve glede dostopnosti, ki se v postopkih oddaje javnih narocil upostevajo pri oblikovanju tehnicnih specifikacij javnega narocila' [the accessibility conditions for the products and services under this Act constitute MANDATORY accessibility requirements which are taken into account in PUBLIC PROCUREMENT procedures when drawing up the technical specifications of the contract]. Art.13 inserts a new Art.27a giving TIRS a defined procedure where an operator invokes the fundamental-alteration / disproportionate-burden defence, and Art.12 adds Art.27(5)-(9) obliging TIRS to notify the Commission and other Member States of restrictive measures and, in Art.27(9), to ensure appropriate restrictive measures INCLUDING WITHDRAWAL OF THE PRODUCT FROM THE MARKET. Art.4 lets the minister responsible for disability protection prescribe functional-performance criteria as an alternative to a technical condition. Art.17(1) restates the transitional rule that self-service terminals lawfully used before 28/06/2025 may run to the end of their economic life but no longer than 20 years after entry into use; Art.18 grandfathers procurement procedures whose contract notice was sent for publication before the amendment took effect. Under the base ZDPSI, TIRS is the product market-surveillance authority (Art.26(2)) and Banka Slovenije, TIRS and the Information Commissioner share supervision of consumer banking services (Art.30(5)); product fines are EUR 3,000-40,000 for a legal-person manufacturer/importer and EUR 1,200-3,000 for distributors (Art.34).
- **Source:** Uradni list RS, st. 14/2026 of 24/02/2026, item 563 - Zakon o spremembah in dopolnitvah Zakona o dostopnosti do proizvodov in storitev za invalide (ZDPSI-A), full official text (primary-tier) 24/02/2026 — https://www.uradni-list.si/glasilo-uradni-list-rs/vsebina/2026-01-0563
- **Implies:** Slovenia is now a DOUBLE trigger for Printec and the two bills interlock. ZDPSI-A makes EAA accessibility a mandatory technical specification in every Slovenian public tender - so any ATM, kiosk, TCR or payment-terminal bid to a state-owned or public-sector buyer (and Posta Slovenije, which the separate ZDUPS draft would make a cash-point operator) must now carry EN 301 549 evidence in the technical specification or be non-compliant on its face. That is a hard qualification gate Printec can clear with NCR Atleos accessibility documentation and a competitor without it cannot. Meanwhile TIRS can withdraw a non-compliant terminal model from the Slovenian market. Combine with the ZDUPS cash-access bill: if the geographic floor survives, NLB, OTP banka Slovenije and Posta Slovenije must ADD cash points and every added machine must be EAA-compliant from day one, with no legacy derogation.
- **Likelihood:** In force since 25/02/2026 and it bites at the moment a tender is drafted, so it is live in every current Slovenian procurement.
