# eIDAS 2 / EUDI wallet & eID - Agent 3 Regulation, run 27/08/2026

**Headline:** the EU implementing-act layer hardened this summer (ARF v3.0.0 on 23/07/2026, CIR 2026/1731 in force 11/08/2026), while national wallet SUPPLY is publicly conceded to be late - Austria's State Secretary told the Budget Committee on 29/06/2026 that *no* member state will have implemented the EUDI Wallet requirements by end-2026. The bank-side dates do not move with it. The most important find this run is **CIR (EU) 2025/848, which applies from 24/12/2026** and requires banks acting as wallet-relying parties to be REGISTERED and to hold access certificates - a full year before the 24/12/2027 Art.5f(2) acceptance duty everyone is planning to.

**Debts closed:** Austria (primary, parliamentary). Croatia (partly - TLv6 migration 29/04/2026 confirmed primary; no government wallet timetable exists, Q4-2026 remains press-only).

**Also new:** CIR (EU) 2026/798 of 07/04/2026 makes ETSI TS 119 461 V2.1.1 remote identity proofing the legal on-ramp to the wallet - the single most directly monetisable act for the Namirial eKYC line - and North Macedonia's bill Art.20-m(2) expressly names *banking and financial services* as relying parties that must accept the wallet from 01/06/2027.
