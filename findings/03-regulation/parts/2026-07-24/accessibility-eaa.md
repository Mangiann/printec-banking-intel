# EAA / Accessibility — checkpoint 2026-07-24

Workstream: European Accessibility Act (Dir. 2019/882) — ATMs / payment terminals / self-service; 28/06/2025 obligations, enforcement & retrofit demand per market.

## Current picture (verified this run)
- EAA obligations in force since 28/06/2025 across 27 MS; consumer banking + self-service terminals (ATMs, payment terminals) in scope. Standards: EN 301 549 (hardware/ATMs) + WCAG 2.1 AA (digital).
- Legacy self-service terminals lawfully in use before 28/06/2025 may run to end of economic life, max 20 yrs (→ 28/06/2045); MS may shorten. New machines placed on market after 28/06/2025 must be fully compliant + CE-declared.
- Per-market fines now mapped (national transposition): Austria €80k (BaFG), Czech ~CZK 2M (~€80k), Hungary ~HUF 30M (~€75k), Greece €10k–100k (EETT), Croatia €50k, Romania €5k–50k (ANCOM), Poland ~PLN 100k (~€25k), Bulgaria ~BGN 5k–50k (~€2.5k–25k, lowest in EU).
- Retrofit demand driver: every provider must declare fleet accessibility (CE declaration) → speech guidance, headphone jack, tactile/braille EPP, illuminated interaction points, "accessibility mode" reach-down interface. Vendors (Diebold Nixdorf) marketing retrofittable "accessibility mode" — competitive signal for Printec self-service/managed-services.
- Non-EU CEE/SEE Printec markets (RS, MK, AL, ME) aligning to EU banking acquis (SEPA joined 2025) — EAA-style accessibility likely to follow via candidate alignment; no binding obligation yet.
