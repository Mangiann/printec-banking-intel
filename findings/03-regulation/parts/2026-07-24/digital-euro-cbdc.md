## What is genuinely NEW vs baseline

1. **Cipollone speech, Federcasse annual assembly, Rome, 17/07/2026** — digital euro app functions
   to be delivered via a **software development kit (SDK) for banks to integrate into their existing
   apps** ("leave no-one behind"). This is the app/integration layer — direct hit on Printec digital
   onboarding/eKYC and managed services (SDK adoption/app integration for banks). Source: Central Banking.
2. **Named pilot PSPs surfaced** (ECB PR itself withholds names; press lists them): incl.
   **Raiffeisen Bank International** (Austria, large CEE/SEE footprint — Printec territory),
   **UniCredit**, **Cooperative Bank of Chania** (Greek co-operative), plus Deutsche Bank, Nexi,
   Worldline, Revolut, Stripe, Adyen, SumUp, Poste Italiane, MPS, Banca Sella, isybank. 36 PSPs from
   16 of 21 euro-area states; **19 participating NCBs incl. Croatia, Greece, Austria, Slovenia, Slovakia**
   (Printec markets). Acquiring PSPs = enable merchant acceptance → POS/acquiring readiness.
3. **Digital euro scheme rulebook v0.91 published 02/07/2026** (derdgpr260702) — incorporates
   June–Oct 2025 market-consultation feedback; adds UX minimum standards, brand rules, dispute
   management, onboarding, and **testing/certification framework** (Workstream B1). Non-binding draft,
   NOT for implementation. Certification/testing = Printec ATM/POS conformance + managed services angle.
4. **Trilogue substance** (Freshfields analysis, July 2026): holding-limit debate now centres on a
   concrete **~€1,500–€3,000 range**; co-legislators asked ECB to **test hypothetical holding limits up
   to €3,000/person**. Compensation: transitional uniform merchant-fee caps → cost-based; Council wants
   firm **10-year transition cap** + national-level fee caps. Both mandate **online AND offline** versions;
   mandatory acceptance with micro/small-enterprise carve-out. Acceptance-mandate = POS/terminal demand.
