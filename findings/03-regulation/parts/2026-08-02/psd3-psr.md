# PSD3 / PSR — Agent 3 Regulation workstream, run 02/08/2026

## Headline

**The PSD3/PSR clock has slipped, and the final text is now readable in full.**

1. **Slip.** The EP Legislative Observatory procedure file for the PSR (2023/0210(COD)) still shows *"Awaiting Council's 1st reading position"* as of 02/08/2026, with the last completed step being ECON approval on 05/05/2026 and an **indicative plenary sitting date of 14/12/2026**. There is no OJ publication. Every law-firm note repeating "OJ by Q2/Q3 2026" is now out of date. The ECON chair pushed for adoption "by September 2026 at the latest"; the Parliament's own forecast says December.
2. **Dates settled.** PSR Article 112: general application **21 months** after entry into force; **Articles 50 and 57 (VoP and VoP liability) at 27 months**; Articles 85a and 108a from entry into force. PSD3 Article 49: transposition **21 months** — *not* the 18 months in the 24/07/2026 baseline.
3. **Article 59 corrected.** The refund window is **15 business days**, not the 10 recorded in the baseline. Burden of proof sits on the PSP, which must first invite the consumer to explain events.
4. **New ATM/self-service hooks nobody is writing about**: PSR Art 88 (accessible SCA, free of charge, for non-smartphone customers), PSR Art 7 (mandatory at-ATM fee and FX mark-up display), PSD3 Art 38 (registration regime for independent ATM deployers, grandfathered to 27 months), PSD3 Art 37 (cash at retail without purchase, EUR 150 cap, SCA required).
5. **Footprint reality**: no national competent authority in the 17-country footprint has opened PSD3 transposition work, because there is nothing to transpose yet. Serbia (NBS draft law 05/06/2026) and Ukraine (NBU Resolutions 80/81/82) are implementing the *previous* generation — PSD2 + Reg 2024/886 — a full cycle behind.
