## 1. All Bulgarian traders, plus banks' tariff/price disclosures, ATM screens/receipts and POS terminal receipts (Bulgaria)

**Signal.** [LIVE DEADLINE] Bulgaria's DUAL PRICE DISPLAY (prices shown simultaneously in euro AND lev) ENDS 08/08/2026. Law on the Introduction of the Euro in the Republic of Bulgaria (ZVERB), Art.15(2) as amended by Darjaven Vestnik No.65/2025: the dual-marking period 'zapochva na 8 avgust 2025 g. i priklyuchva na 8 avgust 2026 g.' [begins 8 August 2025 and ends 8 August 2026]. Penalties (ZVERB Art.59): breach of Art.15(1) BGN 400-5,000 for individuals / BGN 600-7,000 property sanction for legal persons (BGN 1,200-14,000 on repeat); breach of Art.15(4) BGN 1,000-10,000 / legal persons BGN 5,000-100,000 (BGN 10,000-200,000 repeat), and for an undertaking with prior-year turnover above BGN 50m up to 0.5% of turnover, 1% on repeat within the dual-display period, capped at BGN 1,000,000. Enforced by KZP (Komisiya za zashtita na potrebitelite - Consumer Protection Commission) and the NRA; KZP restated the 08/08/2026 end date in its 29/01/2026 guidance 'Kakvo sledva za potrebiteli i targovtsi ot 1 fevruari 2026 godina'.

**Implies.** (a) WHO: every Bulgarian bank (branch tariffs, statements, ATM on-screen text and receipts, POS/e-commerce receipts) and every card-accepting merchant on a Printec-managed POS estate. (b) WHAT: a software/content release across the estate - POS terminal application + TMS (Terminal Management System) push to drop the lev leg from receipts and prompts; ATM screen and receipt template changes through NCR x-core multivendor software; document-management updates to tariffs and pre-contractual information. (c) WHEN: budget is already committed - this is an execution week, and the sell is the change-release, regression test and rollback plan as a managed service.

## 2. All Bulgarian credit institutions (UniCredit Bulbank, DSK, UBB, Postbank, Fibank, ProCredit, Allianz Bank etc.) and Bulgarian Posts EAD (Bulgaria)

**Signal.** Residual lev-exchange calendar, verified against the statute. ## 3. Bulgarian banks and their cash-in-transit / cash-handling service providers (Bulgaria)

**Signal.** [STANDING - dated 31/07/2025; in force 01/01/2026] BNB Ordinance No.18 of 31/07/2025 on quality control of euro banknotes and coins in cash circulation (Darjaven Vestnik No.68 of 19/08/2025, in force from 01/01/2026) applies the Eurosystem recirculation regime in Bulgaria. Art.28(1): banks and service providers may process euro banknotes and coins ONLY on machines that have passed manufacturer tests and are on the list published by the ECB (banknotes) or by the European Commission (coins). Art.28(2): those machines are subject to BNB control tests. Art.28(3): BNB does NOT test ATMs or self-service machines that do not identify the user. Art.28(4): self-service machines may be loaded only with banknotes checked for authenticity and fitness under ECB Decision ECB/2010/14. Art.29: documented staff training is mandatory and an untrained employee may not operate the machine. Art.30-31: a BNB recognition control test passes ONLY at 100% correct identification of the counterfeit test set; a fitness test passes only if at most 5% of unfit notes in the test set are categorised as fit. Art.32: a listed machine that FAILS a BNB control test is WITHDRAWN FROM OPERATION and placed under observation for one month, and the service operator must apply for a re-test before it may be used again; repeated failure means permanent withdrawal. Art.35: banks, service providers, financial institutions and bureaux de change must adopt internal rules. Art.36: banks and service providers report to BNB through the BNB Integrated Information System per Annex IV of ECB/2010/14 and Art.12 of Regulation (EU) No 1210/2010, must notify any cash-handling outsourcing agreement (conclusion, amendment or termination) within 10 working days, and must declare and sample any anti-theft banknote-staining chemical used.

**Implies.** (a) WHO: every Bulgarian bank running recyclers, intelligent-deposit ATMs or back-office sorters, plus every CIT/cash-handling outsourcer they use. (b) WHAT: this is the strongest hardware driver in the workstream - ECB-listed, fitness-capable NCR recyclers and Glory / Sesami TCRs; because a failed BNB control test takes a machine off-line for at least a month, SLA-backed field service (Printec's 500+ engineers), sensor calibration, firmware currency and ATM/device telemetry and monitoring stop being nice-to-have and become contractual; the Annex IV reporting duty and the 10-working-day outsourcing notification are a data/managed-service sell; Printec as an outsourced service provider is itself directly in scope of Art.28-29 and Art.36. (c) WHEN: spend is in-year - BNB control tests are already running through 2026, so remediation cannot be deferred to a 2027 budget.

## 4. All Bulgarian ATM deployers - banks and independent ATM deployers (Bulgaria)

**Signal.** [STANDING - dated 31/07/2025] BNB Ordinance No.46 of 31/07/2025 on initial frontloading and sub-frontloading with euro banknotes, euro coins and euro coin starter kits (DV No.68 of 19/08/2025), Art.18, sets a BINDING ATM DENOMINATION MIX: ATMs with TWO banknote cassettes must be loaded with EUR 10 and EUR 20; ATMs with THREE cassettes with EUR 10 and EUR 20, optionally also EUR 5 and/or EUR 50; ATMs with FOUR cassettes with EUR 10 and EUR 20, optionally also EUR 5 and/or EUR 50 and/or EUR 100, where (a) withdrawals of EUR 100 or less must ALWAYS be served from EUR 5, 10, 20 and/or 50 notes and (b) only withdrawals above EUR 100 may use EUR 50 and/or EUR 100. Art.18(2) and ZVERB Art.23(6): from the euro introduction date, cash withdrawals from self-service machines including ATMs dispense ONLY euro banknotes. Art.19: banks report frontloading/sub-frontloading data to BNB in the manner set by BNB instructions and must IMMEDIATELY notify any euro cash released into circulation early. Art.20: BNB may carry out on-site and documentary inspections of banks, their agents and sub-frontloaded merchants, with a formal inspection protocol. Sanction for breaching ZVERB Art.23(6): BGN 500-3,000 for individuals and a BGN 5,000-30,000 property sanction for legal persons, doubling to BGN 10,000-60,000 on repeat (ZVERB Art.59(1)(4) and Art.59(5)(11)).

**Implies.** (a) WHO: every bank and independent deployer running an ATM in Bulgaria. (b) WHAT: the denomination rule directly drives cassette-count decisions - a 2-cassette ATM cannot legally offer EUR 5, 50 or 100, so any bank wanting a low-denomination or high-value proposition needs 3- or 4-cassette NCR hardware or a recycler; NCR APTRA OptiCash forecasting has to be re-parameterised for a constrained denomination MIX, not just volume; NCR x-core multivendor software must enforce the sub-EUR-100 rule in the withdrawal menu logic. (c) WHEN: this feeds the 2026-2027 ATM refresh cycle - banks are sizing swap-out lists now, in the first full year of euro operation.

## 5. Bulgarian credit institutions - post-changeover reporting to BNB (Bulgaria)

**Signal.** [STANDING - dated 31/07/2025] BNB Ordinance No.47 of 31/07/2025 on the provision of information by credit institutions under ZVERB Art.26 and on withholding and expert examination of lev banknotes and coins (DV No.68 of 19/08/2025; Chapter Four in force from the euro introduction date) sets the post-changeover reporting duties. Art.2(1)-(2): for the FIRST MONTH after euro introduction banks reported WEEKLY, by 12:00 on the first working day of the following week, the total value of lev banknotes and coins they held, submitted electronically through an information system. Art.2(3): a bank must notify BNB IN ADVANCE of the type and amount of any fee it introduces under ZVERB Art.26(6). Art.2(4): a bank must notify BNB IN ADVANCE of the date from which it discontinues cash lev-euro exchange under Art.26(9) - i.e. ahead of 01/01/2027. Art.2(5)-(6): the same advance notification applies to fees on immediate exchange of DAMAGED lev notes after 6 months and to discontinuing that service after 12 months. Art.2(7): the Deputy Governor heading the Issue Department may require further exchange-related information. Chapter Four: BNB and banks must withhold any lev note/coin suspected counterfeit, raise a two-copy protocol on the prescribed form (Annex 1), deliver it to BNB's National Analysis Centre (Cash Centre, 10 Mihail Tenev St, 1784 Sofia) within 3 WORKING DAYS at the presenter's cost; BNB completes the technical authenticity examination within 20 WORKING DAYS, its finding is not appealable, counterfeits are destroyed without compensation and genuine notes are reimbursed IN EURO.
