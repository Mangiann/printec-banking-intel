## Printec read-across
- **ATM**: NCR Atleos fleet + **x-core multivendor software** must gain digital euro funding/defunding and NFC/QR initiation. G5 is the spec that decides how.
- **POS**: Verifone/Castles estates + **TMS** re-parameterisation and B1 re-certification; softPOS/mPOS explicitly in B1 scope.
- **Security**: Thales HSM / remote key injection for the B1 security certification scope.
- **Cash**: the legal-tender-of-cash limb protects ATM/recycler demand — Glory/Sesami TCRs, NCR recyclers, **APTRA OptiCash**, telemetry & monitoring for the "resilience/continuity" obligation.
- **Budget window**: political agreement targeted end-2026 → specs and certification 2027 → pilot H2-2027 → issuance 2029. Bank capex cases must land in the **2027 budget round**.
