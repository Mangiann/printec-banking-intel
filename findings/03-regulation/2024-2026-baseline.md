## Patterns observed (2024 → mid-2026)

1. **~12-month procurement lead time before hard deadlines.** Technical specs land ~12–18 months before each deadline (DORA Batch 1 Jan 2024 → Jan 2025; AMLA RTS Jul 2026 → AMLR Jul 2027), and bank buying clusters in the 6–12 months prior. Predictive use: the next confirmed deadline cluster (IPR non-euro Jan/Jul 2027, AMLR Jul 2027) puts peak vendor selection in **H2 2026 – H1 2027** for CZ, HU, RO.
2. **Two-wave deadline structure: euro area first, non-euro ~2 years later.** IPR receive: Jan 2025 → Jan 2027; send+VoP: Oct 2025 → Jul 2027. Whatever euro-area banks bought in 2024–25 (payment hubs, VoP services, 24/7 ops), CZ/HU/RO banks must buy in 2026–27 — the same vendors and propositions can be re-run with a 2-year lag.
3. **Enforcement ramps ~12 months after application, shifting spend from build to assurance.** DORA applied Jan 2025 → CTPP designation + TLPT waves + SREP integration in late 2025/2026. EAA applied Jun 2025 → audits ramping 2026. Implication: compliance-assurance services, testing, and remediation sell best in year 2 of any regulation.
4. **EU rules cascade to the non-EU footprint with a 1–3 year lag via SEPA/accession.** Montenegro+Albania (Nov 2024) → Serbia (May 2025) → AL/MD/ME/MK scheme adherence (Oct 2025) → Serbian ORD (May 2026) → Ukraine (application in progress, accession ~2027–28). The Western Balkans + UA are now replaying the euro area's 2014–2017 SEPA/ISO 20022 modernization — a predictable multi-year pipeline.
5. **National mandates exceed EU minimums and create sharper, earlier local deadlines.** Greece made IRIS acceptance mandatory with fines (Nov–Dec 2025), going beyond IPR; Hungary added a NIS2 audit obligation; Romania built RoPay ahead of its 2027 IPR dates. Watch national legislation, not just Brussels — local mandates produced the fastest terminal/POS spend of the window (Greece).
6. **Currency/infrastructure changeovers compress years of capex into ~6 months.** Bulgaria: convergence green light Jun 2025 → full TARGET onboarding + 96% ATM conversion by Jan 2026. If/when the next changeover or major migration is announced (digital euro pilots 2027, future euro candidates), expect the same compressed buying window and pre-position early.
7. **Long-runway items move slowly, then all at once.** PSD3 sat in negotiation through 2024–25, then agreement (Nov 2025) → final texts (Apr 2026) → OJ (mid-2026) in 8 months. Digital euro: two years of reports, then phase change in one Governing Council decision (Oct 2025). Monitor for the inflection event, not the steady-state reports.
