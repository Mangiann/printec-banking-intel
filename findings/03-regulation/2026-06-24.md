## What changed since last run

The biggest new development is the ECB's 18/03/2026 call for external experts to join two Rulebook Development Group workstreams — notably G5, the implementation specifications for ATMs and payment terminals (communication technologies, certification) — which puts Printec's core hardware/integration competence directly in the digital-euro standard-setting path. Second, the digital euro programme advanced concretely: the ECB pilot's call for expression of interest closed 14/05/2026 with ~10-30 PSP selections notified by end-June 2026, and the ECON committee adopted its single-currency-package position on 23/06/2026 (digital euro 43-14-1), so the framework is firming from "preparation" toward live build. Third, AMLA's rulebook and supervision machinery is now on a hard clock — final RTS to the Commission by 10/07/2026 and the ~40 directly-supervised entities to be picked through Aug-Oct 2026 — driving near-term AML/KYC remediation demand across the footprint. New regional items also matter: FATF grey-listed Bosnia & Herzegovina (19/06/2026), Bulgaria opened its mandatory 6-month free lev/euro cash-exchange window, and Romania enacted national DORA via OUG 14/2026 (in force 11/03/2026). On baselines, verification reclassified the "Hungary proposal to widen DORA scope" as already-enacted law (in force 17/01/2025, not a pending 2026 proposal); the CEE EAA fine-ceiling figures were contradicted and should not be relied on; and the SEPA pre-09/04/2027 euro-introduction transitional rule for Bulgaria remains unconfirmed.
