## What changed since last run

The biggest new development is the ECB's 14/07/2026 selection of 36 PSPs for the digital-euro pilot (H2-2027, 12 months), which names several footprint-country institutions — most valuably existing Printec references Bank of Cyprus and JCC Payment Systems (CY), plus Piraeus (GR), NLB (SI), Tatra banka (SK) and Raiffeisenbank Austria/Corvus Pay (HR) — giving a concrete, warm-lead entry point. Second, the DORA subcontracting RTS (Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/532) is now confirmed as published in the OJ on 02/07/2025 and in force from 22/07/2025, hardening Printec's own ICT-third-party obligations across all EU footprint banks. Third, the PSD3/PSR package advanced to formal adoption (provisional agreement 27/11/2025, Council I-item note 17/04/2026), resetting fraud-liability and open-banking rules for footprint PSPs. Also newly confirmed is the Western Balkans TIPS-clone instant-payment platform targeting ~mid-2026 go-live for AL/BA/XK/ME. On verification corrections: the Alpha Bank voice-guidance reference is only partially confirmed and is standing (dated Jan 2021), with the "100+ ATMs" figure narrowed to Alpha's official 102; the T2 ISO-migration signal (T2-0170-SYS) was contradicted; and the Bulgarian "first MiCA CASP licence" timing was contradicted (Alaric Securities ~08/01/2026, not April 2026).
