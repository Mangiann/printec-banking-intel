## Jargon added this run (plain language)

- **TIPS clone / Western Balkans IPS** — a copy of the Eurosystem's TIPS instant-settlement platform, built and run by Banca d'Italia for non-EU Western-Balkans central banks so their banks can settle instant payments in real time, 24/7, in local currencies. *(also appended to `glossary.md`)*
- **EuroPA / EPI** — two pan-European account-to-account payment networks. EuroPA links national instant schemes (Greece's IRIS, Italy's Bancomat Pay, Spain's Bizum, Portugal's MB WAY); EPI runs the "Wero" wallet. Connecting IRIS to them enables cross-border instant payments. *(also appended to `glossary.md`)*
- **SREP / Pillar 2** — the ECB's annual Supervisory Review and Evaluation Process; a weak score can force a bank to hold extra capital ("Pillar 2"). From 2026 digital-operational-resilience (DORA) performance feeds this score — i.e. weak ICT resilience now costs capital. *(also appended to `glossary.md`)*

## Corrections to the 2026-06-12 file

1. **Hungary NIS2 — banks are excluded; the previous "Hungarian banks must pass audits this month" is wrong for banks.** Hungary's Cybersecurity Act (Act LXIX of 2024, in force 1 Jan 2025) **omits banking and financial-market-infrastructure sectors from NIS2**; for those, **DORA (Reg. EU 2022/2554) applies instead**, with the **central bank MNB as the competent authority**. The 30 Jun 2026 SZTFH audit deadline therefore does **not** bind Hungarian banks directly. The date conflict flagged last run (31 Dec 2025 vs 30 Jun 2026) is **resolved**: 30 Jun 2026 is correct, having been **extended by six months** (SZTFH/MKIK proposal announced 5 May 2025). Net effect for Printec: Hungarian **bank** cyber spend is DORA-driven (via MNB), not NIS2-audit-driven. *(Sources: [CMS Law-Now](https://cms-lawnow.com/en/ealerts/2025/01/hungary-introduces-new-comprehensive-laws-for-nis2-transposition); [Régens extension](https://www.regens.com/en/-/six-month-extension-cybersecurity-audit-sztfh-proposal).)* — Confidence Medium. Worth tracking: CMS reports Hungary has **proposed widening the scope of financial entities under DORA** ([CMS](https://cms.law/en/hun/legal-updates/hungary-proposes-widening-scope-of-financial-entities-under-dora-regulation)) — could pull more HU entities into DORA obligations; verify next run.

2. **Bulgaria IPR transition — partially resolved.** The precise non-euro obligation table is now in hand (Osborne Clarke, above). Bulgaria adopted the euro on **1 Jan 2026** (sole legal currency since 1 Feb 2026) and its banks were already on **TIPS via BORICA since Dec 2024**, so BG banks are on euro-area-equivalent instant rails today. The **exact Art. 5a transitional date** that applies to a country adopting the euro *after* the euro-area deadlines (Jan/Oct 2025) had passed is **still not pinned to a primary source** — but the practical read is unchanged and actionable: BG's remaining instant-payments sell is **VoP + volume-driven capacity**, not basic rails. Still flagged for a primary-source (regulation text / BNB) check.
