## What changed since last run

The single highest-value new item is the PSR fraud-liability regime under Article 59: PSPs must now fully refund consumers for impersonation ("spoofing") fraud within 10 business days of notification plus a police report, with the burden of proof shifting to the PSP (XL, High win) — a direct pull for fraud-detection and dispute-handling tooling, though the underlying PSD3/PSR file is still only "Close to adoption" (EP plenary, Council formal adoption, signature and OJ publication all pending as of the 20/06/2026 Legislative Train update). On the instant-payments front, the TIPS Clone shared infrastructure went live 20/07/2026 with Montenegro (all 11 banks) and Bosnia and Herzegovina as co-first participants, and a second onboarding window in November 2026 is set to bring in Albania, Kosovo and North Macedonia, opening a fresh regional integration pipeline. Verification of Payee is now confirmed LIVE for euro-area PSPs since 09/10/2025 (non-euro PSPs phased to 09/07/2027), making IBAN/name-matching a near-term deliverable rather than a forecast. The digital euro programme also advanced concretely: the ECB named 36 pilot PSPs across 16 euro-area states (14/07/2026) and Cipollone confirmed an SDK-based app delivery model, while trilogues opened 13/07/2026 targeting political agreement by end-2026. Note two verification caveats: the Romanian EAA fine figures (Law 232/2022) were contradicted by verification and should not be relied on, and several digital-euro/VoP participant and volume details remain only partially confirmed.

## Top opportunities (ranked)

1. PSR Article 59 fraud-liability regime (EU) — pitch fraud-detection / spoofing-refund dispute automation (XL, High).
2. EAA self-service terminal conformance, 27 Member States (EU-wide) — pitch EN 301 549-compliant accessible ATM upgrades/retrofits (XL, Medium).
