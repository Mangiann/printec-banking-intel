## What changed since last run

The single highest-value new development is the ECB's call for RDG Workstream G5 experts on digital-euro implementation specs for ATMs and payment terminals (deadline 10/04/2026) — a direct hardware/integration opening for Printec, reinforced by the sixth RDG progress report (06/07/2026) advancing the G5 ATM/terminal and B1 certification workstreams. Second, the Greek accessibility front sharpened: PASOK MP Petros Pappas filed a parliamentary referral (14/05/2026) demanding universal ATM/bank accessibility, adding regulatory pressure in a core Printec market alongside the WCAG 2.2-aligned draft EN 301 549 V4.1.0. Third, Montenegro's TIPS-Clone is now the first confirmed Western Balkans instant-payments go-live (20/07/2026), with Parliament adopting enabling Law on Payment Transactions amendments 42-0 on 10/07/2026, while the broader regional TIPS-Clone rollout (AL/BA/XK/MK) still lacks published dates. Fourth, the SWIFT CBPR+ Nov-2026 release now confirms a mandatory ability to receive camt.110 investigation requests via Case Management, a concrete cross-border messaging build. On baselines, verification could NOT confirm the AMLA group-wide RTS item (contradicted) and left the digital-euro legislative-mandate, PSD3/PSR "close to adoption" status, and several EUDI-wallet readiness figures only partially confirmed; the Bulgarian cash-changeover tally (29.4bn lev = 94.48%) was confirmed.
