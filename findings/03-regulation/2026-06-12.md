## Jargon used below (plain language)

- **VoP (Verification of Payee)** — before a transfer is sent, the bank must check that the recipient's name matches the IBAN and warn the payer of mismatches; anti-fraud measure mandated by the Instant Payments Regulation.
- **CTPP (Critical ICT Third-Party Provider)** — a tech vendor (cloud, software) formally designated under DORA as critical to the EU financial system and put under direct EU supervisory oversight.
- **TLPT (Threat-Led Penetration Testing)** — mandatory "ethical hacker" attack simulations on major banks' live systems, at least every 3 years under DORA.
- **AMLA / AMLR** — the EU's new anti-money-laundering authority (Frankfurt) and the directly-applicable AML Regulation that replaces national AML rules from 10 July 2027.
- **EAA (European Accessibility Act)** — EU law requiring ATMs/self-service terminals and banking services placed on the market after 28 June 2025 to be usable by people with disabilities.
- **Structured addresses (CBPR+)** — ISO 20022 requirement that address data in payment messages use defined fields instead of free text; free-text addresses are rejected after the November 2026 cutoff.
- **ORD (Operational Readiness Date)** — the date from which a country's payment providers may start sending/receiving live SEPA transactions after joining the scheme.
