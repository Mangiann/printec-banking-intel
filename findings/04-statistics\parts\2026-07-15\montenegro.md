# Montenegro (CBCG) — findings 2026-07-15

Focus: ATM/POS/cards from 2024 Annual Report (primary), plus 2025 developments.

## Verified 2024 device counts (PRIMARY — CBCG Godišnji izvještaj o radu 2024, Table 8.7)
- ATMs (bankomati): 626 end-2024, +23% y/y (2023: 508; 2022: 500)
- POS terminals: 18,775 end-2024, +15% y/y (2023: 16,310; 2022: 14,814)
- E-money acceptance terminals: 3,990 end-2024, +4% y/y (2023: 3,823; 2022: 3,484)
- Cards issued (plastic, Table 8.6): 529,551 end-2024, +8% y/y (2023: 490,637; 2022: 525,849 — 2023 dip = one PSP withdrew a card brand)
- Card txns (payment function, Table 8.8): 55,806,484, +15%; value EUR 1,179.59m, +16%. 82.08% at physical POS, 17.92% remote (internet+mobile). E-money txns 101.35k (+19%).
- Mobile-pay users: 258,691 (+45%); internet-pay users: 286,485 (+33%) [Table 8.3]

## 2025 developments (NEW/CHANGED vs baseline)
- 2025 Annual Report ADOPTED by CBCG Council 02/06/2026 (PDF not yet published). Bank assets >EUR 7.9bn, loans +14.24%, NPL 2.67% (record low).
- SEPA: first transactions 07/10/2025; ~EUR 4.8m savings in first 7 months.
- Payment System Law amendments ADOPTED by Parliament (42 in favour, Jul-2026) — clears TIPS Clone launch.
- TIPS Clone instant payments go-live 20/07/2026; technical build by Banca d'Italia, ECB-approved; max EUR 1,000/txn, funds in <=10s, 24/7, mandatory Verification of Payee.
- RTS/X ISO-20022 live May-2025; weekend operation from 20/10/2025.
