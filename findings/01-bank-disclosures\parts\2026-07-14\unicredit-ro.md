# UniCredit Bank Romania - findings (2026-07-14)

Focus: Alpha Bank RO merger, ATM harmonisation, Prime, Google Cloud. Languages: EN + RO.

## Summary
- Alpha Bank RO merger COMPLETE 18/08/2025 (not 2026 as baseline implied). Combined: ~300 branches, ~900 ATMs, >4,800 employees; 11% assets / 13% loans / 11% deposits. Alpha retains 9.9% stake; UniCredit paid ~EUR255m cash + 9.9% equity for 90.1% of Alpha Bank RO. Source: UniCredit Group PR 18/08/2025 + BVB UCB27.
- ATM interoperability live since April 2025 (pre-merger): shared networks at same cost. Now consolidating into one ~900-unit fleet -> ATM/self-service harmonisation opportunity (legacy Alpha fleet onto UniCredit standard).
- Prime by UniCredit launched ~13/05/2026 across 9 CEE markets incl. Romania. Includes "exclusive Prime zones in selected branches" + dedicated in-app PRIME section -> branch transformation / self-service redesign. Research on 11,000+ clients.
- Google Cloud 10-yr partnership (MoU 12/05/2025) across 13 core markets incl. Romania: migrate legacy systems to GCP, Vertex AI + Gemini, financial crime prevention. Single-partner approach -> AML/monitoring signal AND competitive constraint on Printec software.
- Lupu at ZF Bankers Summit 03/06/2026: post-merger consolidation/productivity phase; will continue investing in technology and client experience, especially retail.
