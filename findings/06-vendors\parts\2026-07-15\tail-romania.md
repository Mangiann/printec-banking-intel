# Long-tail & NEW competitors — Romania — 2026-07-15

Analyst: Printec banking market-research (subagent). Slug: tail-romania. Confidence capped MEDIUM.
Scope: LOCAL long tail + new-competitor discovery in Romania across POS/acquiring/soft-POS, self-service/cash automation, CIT/ATM-service, RegTech. Global names' own units excluded. Namirial & IMTF excluded (partners).

## KEY NEW / CHANGED (is_new=true)
1. **Netopia Payments — GRANTED BNR payment-institution licence (13/07/2026).** Materially changed vs baseline ("advancing authorization"). Now regulated PI; processes >30% of RO online card payments, >25,000 merchants; CEO Raluca Micu (ex-BNR Payments Supervision head 2018-2026). Threat: RO acquiring/gateway. Tier regional. Sources: romania-insider.com 13/07/2026; economedia.ro; bankingnews.ro.
2. **SelfPay (ZebraPay) — 14,000+ payment stations, +277 new locations in first half May-2026, +15% daily tx volume; "Rapid Transition Program" for retailers; SelfPay Now super-app ~1M users.** Cash-deposit-on-card + bill-pay self-service = displaces Printec bank kiosks/cash-in. Tier local-peer. Sources: rofin.tech (May-2026); selfpay.ro Raport Anual 2025 (May-2026).
3. **Global Operation Center — SOLE bidder, BNR centralized security system, 12.43M lei (~€2.5M) / 24 months; published 11/01/2026, bid deadline 12/02/2026; award PENDING.** Physical surveillance/CCTV/access — marginal Printec (ATM physical security/managed services) fit. Tier local-peer. Sources: profit.ro 26/02/2026; forbes.ro; bursa.ro 11/01/2026.
4. **CEC Bank — RoPay P2M (Person-to-Merchant) launched 17/03/2026.** Account-to-account instant merchant collection via QR (Transfond national instant-payments rail), physical + online — a card-bypass rail eroding Printec POS/acquiring. Tier local-peer. Source: profit.ro 17/03/2026.
5. **ING Bank RO SoftPOS — adopted by 1,000+ merchants (up from ~300 earlier in year).** Bank in-house tap-on-phone erodes Printec Verifone/Castles terminal base. Tier local-peer. Sources: nocash.ro; ing.ro; revistabiz.ro.
6. **Banca Transilvania — BT POS "Tap to Pay on iPhone" (06/05/2026).** In-house iOS SoftPOS, no hardware — displaces physical POS. Tier local-peer. Source: thinkbiz.ro 06/05/2026.
7. **Symphopay — RO payment-orchestration fintech, 2026 "from validation to controlled scaling".** Merchant checkout/orchestration long-tail name. Tier local-peer. Source: wall-street.ro.
8. **Primăria Sector 1 Bucharest — SoftPOS/Tap-to-Phone market pilot with Visa RO, council vote 09/07/2026.** Municipal card-acceptance opening (proximity/markets). Context. Source: bankingnews.ro.
