For North Macedonia's A3 regulatory workstream, nbrm.mk is a single, dense, self-contained origin point: the bank publishes its own binding instruments in two parallel registers — supervision decisions (одлуки од областа на супервизијата) for banking and the ЗПУПС regulation set (including a dedicated index of the twelve payments bylaws) for payments — plus a layer of implementing упатства carrying the reporting deadlines, and statutory registers of banks, payment institutions and e-money institutions that reveal licensing decisions in effect. The main structural weaknesses are the pre-adoption stage (no NBRM consultation hub was found; ENER is the likely but unconfirmed proxy) and the absence of any machine-readable feed, which forces HTML/PDF diffing. Collection must use a browser-grade fetcher — a plain fetch of an NBRM regulation index returned HTTP 403, which is a bot-defence hypothesis rather than evidence the page is dead.

For North Macedonia there is no ECB-style NBRM consultation portal: extensive Macedonian- and English-language searching surfaced only adopted decisions, consolidated texts and Council-session press releases on nbrm.mk, never a draft open for comment with a published deadline. The genuine consultation venue for banking and payments PRIMARY law is ENER (ener.gov.mk), the statutory national e-register where ministries must post draft laws with a Regulatory Impact Assessment (ПВР) and an open comment window — confirmed by live financial-sector items such as the draft amendments to the Law on Foreign Exchange Operations. For NBRM BYLAWS the earliest public signals are instead the annual Програма за работа and the 3-year Стратегиски план (forward-looking regulatory calendars), the NPAA EU-alignment matrices which date obligations by chapter and responsible institution, and industry channels (Македонска банкарска асоцијација) where NBRM consults banks non-publicly. Critically, the whole nbrm.mk host returns HTTP 403 to plain HTTP clients while remaining fully search-indexed, so this cell's core sources require a headless browser and should be treated as bot-defended-but-live, with national wire media and NBRM's social accounts as unblocked mirrors.

North Macedonia's public-procurement intelligence for banking and payments is heavily centralised: ЕСЈН (e-nabavki.gov.mk), operated by the Биро за јавни набавки inside the Ministry of Finance, is the mandatory publication point for annual plans, notices, awards and realised contracts across every contracting authority, and it is the one source this cell cannot do without — though it is bot-defended (403 on plain fetch) and best reached through its .asmx service endpoints and dossier UUID URLs, with data.gov.mk and the skopjehacklab civic-tech scraper as machine-readable auxiliaries. North Macedonia's enforcement-and-grievance layer for banking and payments is institutionally clear but unevenly published. For MK A3, the durable monitoring backbone is narrow but high quality: NBStat (nbstat.nbrm.mk) is a genuine PxWeb database with a standard API covering monetary, external and financial-accounts series, and ENER (ener.gov.mk) is the statutory consultation register that carries draft financial-sector laws and their comment deadlines before anything reaches the Official Gazette (slvesnik.com.mk), which remains the legally binding dating mechanism and offers a free-issue tier plus a queryable archived-issues index. Primary banking/payments legislation in North Macedonia moves through a legible four-stage chain — ЕНЕР consultation, Влада session adoption ('утврден Предлог-закон'), Собрание committee and reading stages, then binding publication in Службен весник — and each stage has its own official publisher, so the cell is best covered by four monitors joined on bill title rather than one feed. The gazette is the only legally operative source and the only place transition articles fix compliance deadlines, but it exposes no feed or API and resisted automated fetching (TLS chain error), while sobranie.mk returned 403; both are treated here as bot-defended-but-live and need a browser-grade fetcher. Coverage cannot rest on ЕНЕР alone: civil-society monitoring indicates only about half of government bills reaching Parliament in 2023 were consulted there, making vlada.mk session readouts and Assembly agendas independent necessities rather than confirmations. Practical mirrors materially reduce risk — nbrm.mk and ufr.gov.mk host maintained consolidated statute texts with amendment gazette numbers in the filenames, mchamber.mk republishes draft and gazette PDFs, and AKADEMIKA offers licensed consolidation if a paid route is acceptable.

North Macedonia's supervised-entity register landscape is almost entirely centralised at the National Bank (НБРСМ/NBRNM), which under the 2022 Law on Payment Services and Payment Systems maintains statutory public registers for payment institutions, e-money institutions and payment system operators, alongside longstanding lists of licensed banks, savings houses, exchange offices (менувачници) and fast-money-transfer providers. For North Macedonia, individual banks are a rich but fragmented source layer: there is no consolidated register of customer notices, so regulatory change must be observed bank-by-bank through Macedonian-language 'Соопштенија/Известувања' pages, tariff and General Terms PDFs carrying explicit effective dates, and the annual 'Извештај и податоци за Банката' (the local Pillar-3 disclosure).
